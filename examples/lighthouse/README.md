# Thrum lighthouse

A red-and-white lighthouse with a lit lantern on a sandy shore, three rocks at its foot, under a physically based
sky. Four sliders: Sun elevation and azimuth, and a camera that orbits the tower at a chosen distance.

```sh
./dev scene examples/lighthouse
```

- `assets/meshes/lighthouse.gltf` is generated by `python3 examples/lighthouse/generate.py` (stdlib only): a
  tapered 12 m tower, a gallery deck, a lantern room and a conical cap, origin at the base centre. Re-run it while the
  scene is open and the watcher cooks the new mesh. The rock and `white.png` are copies of the starter's.
- `material.wgsl` paints the bands, the dark gallery, the glowing lantern (emission 60 reads as lit after sunset;
  6 barely shows) and the cap. It runs for every mesh, so it only changes fragments within 1.8 m of the tower's axis.
- `lighthouse.params.json` holds the default slider values; edit it and press `L`.

Review a frame without a window:

```sh
THRUM_ASSET_STORE=target/scenes/thrum-lighthouse/store THRUM_PARAMS=examples/lighthouse/lighthouse.params.json \
  THRUM_MATERIAL=examples/lighthouse/material.wgsl THRUM_CAPTURE_PNG=tmp/lighthouse.png target/release/thrum-lighthouse
```
