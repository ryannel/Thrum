# Thrum work list

The first goal is a small game that follows one clear path: **a player acts, the game checks the action, the world changes, and players see the result**. Local and multiplayer play use the same game rules.

This is the central to-do list. A checked box means that particular piece is done; it does not mean the larger stage is finished. The [detailed construction plan](design/CONSTRUCTION-PLAN.md) gives contributors the full sequence and technical requirements.

**Current integration focus:** Turn the provisional source candidate and test-only SQLite proofs into one production admitted-source path, then cook, run, save and restore a small game from its pinned revision. The [holistic consolidation review](evidence/integration/FOUNDATION-CONSOLIDATION-2026-11-02.md) records what is working, what was measured and which wider claims remain open. The unchecked tasks under **Editable game and save** below are the completion gates.
The [deep review of merged main](evidence/integration/DEEP-REVIEW-2026-11-02.md) records cross-platform and source-boundary fixes and keeps the remaining evidence limits visible.

## Atmosphere and weather design preparation

This work can proceed alongside water design. The [environment family design](design/modules/ENVIRONMENT.md) and [shared environmental contract](design/CONTRACTS.md#c09-environmental-fields-and-physical-exchange--e1-with-p3) already assign common time, source fields and consumers. The [render specification](design/RENDER-SPEC.md#5-initial-atmosphere-fog-and-transport-operator) selects an initial air/fog operator and authored cloud attenuation; their runtime and device proofs remain open. Celestial, weather and air-quality source details need further design. None of these effects is required by the first small game, and a game can choose simpler authored providers.

- [ ] Design one coherent source for time of day, Sun and Moon position/light, wind, clouds and cloud shadows, temperature, humidity, pressure, precipitation, fog and atmospheric visibility. Specify units, spatial/temporal validity, authoring controls and what remains available when a visible sky or cloud renderer is off.
- [ ] Define air quality separately from visual haze: name any pollutants, particulates or breathable-air quantities a game needs, who owns them, and how gameplay/health/audio/visibility queries use them. An attractive sky or fog shader must not silently stand in for an exposure value.
- [ ] Implement and qualify the selected initial air/fog and authored cloud-attenuation operators; select compatible lightweight celestial and weather sources and any optional richer providers. Prove that shared Sun/Moon, wind and cloud causes agree across lighting, sky, vegetation, sound, water and surface response, while unused providers avoid their simulation, preparation and rendering cost. Record quality limits and transitions across desktop, browser and mobile profiles.
- [ ] Integrate a selected environment provider into the larger game, then qualify changing day/night and weather, two views, save/reload, multiplayer state, device visuals and sustained CPU/GPU/memory cost. Keep unbuilt phenomena and unmeasured platform claims open.

## Water design preparation

This D2 work can proceed alongside the foundation. It does not make water a dependency of the first small game.
The [water commission](design/modules/WATER.md) distinguishes the selected lightweight baseline from the wider
interactive-water target; all runtime and visual/device evidence remains separate.

- [x] Align the existing water ambitions and research with Thrum's owners, contracts, baseline limits and cost policy; obtain an independent coverage review. See the [alignment record](evidence/water/ALIGNMENT-2026-11-01.md).
- [x] Select and cross-review a bounded wider-provider proposal for coupled waves/hulls, finite falls, causal whitewater, optics and lifecycle, with an analytical cost account. See the [design review](evidence/water/DESIGN-REVIEW-2026-11-01.md) and [proof order](design/modules/WATER-INTEGRATION.md). No runtime or target qualification is implied.
- [ ] Complete the remaining full-family D2-water B/C mechanisms: advancing dry-slope runoff, traversable heterogeneous seams, overlapping hull pressure supports, richer sea/open-boundary coverage, thin-wall exclusion and contact-qualified breaking. Preserve these as design gaps rather than cosmetic fixes.
- [ ] When commissioned and dependencies permit, prove the bounded profile's least-established pressure/body coupling, then its finite fall and shared appearance/optical consumers. Numerical stability, ordinary motion, save/headless/two-view behavior and sustained target cost remain unrun.

## How a box gets checked

Passing `./dev check` shows that repository checks pass. It does not prove that a game works well, looks right, or performs well on a device. The [maturity record](design/MATURITY.md) tracks implementation, correct behavior, visual review, and device measurements separately. We check off a piece here when its stated behavior and relevant evidence are in place.

## Contributor reference

The [construction plan](design/CONSTRUCTION-PLAN.md) uses short stage names: **I1** is the headless foundation and early phone portability checks; **I2a** is source files, content, and saves; **I3a** is the first rendered game; **I2n** is real multiplayer; **I2b** expands content and creation tools; **I3b** integrates play and presentation; **I4** covers platform choices; and **I5** covers advanced capabilities. **D1/D2** are detailed design work needed before dependent features. Other codes point to specific technical areas or requirements: **F1** covers the runtime clock and coordination; **L4** in the [content lifecycle review](design/research/CONTENT-LIFECYCLE-REVIEW.md) says canceled or obsolete work must not reappear later. You do not need these codes to follow the work list above.

The [shared contracts](design/CONTRACTS.md), [foundation specification](design/FOUNDATION-SPEC.md), and [network specification](design/NETWORK-SPEC.md) govern the implementation. Keep one owner for each kind of state, and keep the public engine API independent of its private storage implementation. Use one small core crate until a real ownership or dependency need warrants splitting it.
