# Thrum work list

The first goal is a small game that follows one clear path: **a player acts, the game checks the action, the world changes, and players see the result**. Local and multiplayer play use the same game rules.

This is the central to-do list. A checked box means that particular piece is done; it does not mean the larger stage is finished. The [detailed construction plan](design/CONSTRUCTION-PLAN.md) gives contributors the full sequence and technical requirements.

**Current integration focus:** Turn the provisional source candidate and test-only SQLite proofs into one production admitted-source path, then cook, run, save and restore a small game from its pinned revision. The [holistic consolidation review](evidence/integration/FOUNDATION-CONSOLIDATION-2026-11-02.md) records what is working, what was measured and which wider claims remain open. The unchecked tasks under **Editable game and save** below are the completion gates.
The [deep review of merged main](evidence/integration/DEEP-REVIEW-2026-11-02.md) records cross-platform and source-boundary fixes and keeps the remaining evidence limits visible.

## How a box gets checked

Passing `./dev check` shows that repository checks pass. It does not prove that a game works well, looks right, or performs well on a device. The [maturity record](design/MATURITY.md) tracks implementation, correct behavior, visual review, and device measurements separately. We check off a piece here when its stated behavior and relevant evidence are in place.

## Contributor reference

The [construction plan](design/CONSTRUCTION-PLAN.md) uses short stage names: **I1** is the headless foundation and early phone portability checks; **I2a** is source files, content, and saves; **I3a** is the first rendered game; **I2n** is real multiplayer; **I2b** expands content and creation tools; **I3b** integrates play and presentation; **I4** covers platform choices; and **I5** covers advanced capabilities. **D1/D2** are detailed design work needed before dependent features. Other codes point to specific technical areas or requirements: **F1** covers the runtime clock and coordination; **L4** in the [content lifecycle review](design/research/CONTENT-LIFECYCLE-REVIEW.md) says canceled or obsolete work must not reappear later. You do not need these codes to follow the work list above.

The [shared contracts](design/CONTRACTS.md), [foundation specification](design/FOUNDATION-SPEC.md), and [network specification](design/NETWORK-SPEC.md) govern the implementation. Keep one owner for each kind of state, and keep the public engine API independent of its private storage implementation. Use one small core crate until a real ownership or dependency need warrants splitting it.
