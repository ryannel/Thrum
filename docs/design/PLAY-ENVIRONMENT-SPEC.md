# Play and environment specification: initial operators and limits

1 November 2026. **Selected baseline specification; no code or device qualification.** This is the smallest concrete P3/P4/P5/E1 set behind [C4 contracts](CONTRACTS.md), [architecture decisions](ARCHITECTURE-DECISIONS.md), [play](modules/PLAY-SYSTEMS.md), [environment](modules/ENVIRONMENT.md) and [N1](modules/NETWORKING.md). It selects algorithms, ownership and rejection behavior before construction. The first playable I3a can omit water entirely. The distinct mixed-coast qualification fixture has at most 128 × 64 active shallow cells, 16 incident waves and four floating bodies; these are proposed workload bounds, not measured capacity. The shared 60 Hz tick and CPU/GPU budgets remain F1's, not a private entitlement for these modules.

## P3: body and controller contract

World positions and saved transforms are f64 metres in W1's Y-up frame. F3's selected authored envelope is world positions within ±1,000,000 m and preserve-world authored TRS round-trip ≤1 µm over a ≤1 km object/subtree; this does not assert 1 µm physics contacts. Rapier's f32 bodies and query geometry use a P3 local origin chosen at a pre-tick barrier; each query/result carries world ID, solver origin epoch, content/collider epoch and `Q_n` or `Q_(n+1)`. Rebase converts positions/velocities/forces, rebuilds or translates the solver representation at a barrier and invalidates stale query results; the camera origin never drives physics. A selected 2048 m local radius including collider/query support implies about 0.24 mm f32 ulp near that radius (about 0.12 mm rounding), but contact and broadphase error are larger and must be measured; if a game tolerance fails, shrink the island or use a separately qualified f64 solver. This selected radius is not a certified extent and does not weaken F3/W1's separate authored TRS envelope. W1 owns committed body transform and authority mode; P3 owns internal body pose/velocity, collider and joint state. Only checked W1 runtime IDs cross modules.

Static triangle/heightfield colliders are cook products; dynamic bodies initially use validated convex/simple compound shapes with finite mass/inertia and a declared scale bake. No dynamic concave triangle mesh, sheared/unbaked collider, zero-scale body or arbitrary animated triangle collision enters this set. Select one 1/60 s integration per authority tick, eight constraint solver iterations, two CCD substeps at most, one internal stabilization pass, and capped accepted speeds of 50 m/s linear and 10 rad/s angular for this reference profile. A body whose swept motion can exceed feature thickness needs CCD; a shape/body exceeding these bounds rejects admission or uses another named profile, never a hidden time-step change. These profile numbers require target qualification, not assumed physical adequacy. P3 consumes ordered force/impulse requests once and emits identified contact/sensor candidates including substep transients. The integrated rejection workload is 128 active native bodies and 32 mobile bodies within F1's shared CPU cap; this is unmeasured. Rapier's [event and query docs](https://rapier.rs/docs/user_guides/rust/advanced_collision_detection/) and [integration parameters](https://docs.rs/rapier3d/latest/rapier3d/dynamics/struct.IntegrationParameters.html) motivate this boundary, not a promise that every short collision is automatically handled without CCD settings.

For a walking character, P4/game policy supplies **one** desired displacement `d_req` for the tick, including authored gravity/support policy. Rapier's [kinematic character controller](https://rapier.rs/docs/user_guides/rust/character_controller/) computes a slide/step/slope corrected displacement against named `Q_n`; P3 sets the kinematic body's next pose/velocity for the solve and returns achieved `d_act`, support/contact and `d_req−d_act`. The reference capsule is 0.35 m radius and 1.8 m total height, with 0.01 m skin/offset, 45° maximum climb slope, 30° downhill slide threshold, 0.25 m autostep height over at least 0.15 m top width (static geometry only), and 0.20 m ground snap. A differently scaled actor needs an admitted profile. It does not add code velocity and full clip root twice. The controller's query can see the previous physics pose of a moving platform; the initial platform policy predicts known **translational** kinematic displacement from its tick-n trajectory and sweeps the capsule against the conservative swept support. Rotating platforms, unknown acceleration/teleport or stale epoch yield `unknown support`/blocked movement; they are not treated as empty ground. This moving-platform policy is server/standalone only; N1's initial prediction excludes it. Rapier's own guide says the corrected movement must be applied by the caller and discusses moving-platform support; the bridge is ours and needs target qualification. [Setup](https://rapier.rs/docs/user_guides/templates/character_controller_setup/).

P3's query snapshot is a purpose-specific immutable collider/pose index, not a borrowed mutable Rapier set. Pre-step control reads `Q_n`; post-step hit/interaction can read `Q_(n+1)` after solve. Missing mandatory cell collider stops a crossing before W2 removes the old support. Structural body-mode changes occur at a barrier. On ragdoll entry, P4 maps committed joint poses and velocities to P3 dynamic bodies, retires the capsule and invalidates pose/motion histories; on exit, P3 validates support/clearance before kinematic reactivation. If the selected Rapier version cannot reproduce this transfer within declared pose/contact tolerance, that mode is not admitted rather than teleported silently.

P7's durable save uses stable IDs, mode, world pose/velocity, shape/product references, joint state and any solver-internal state required for exact continuation of the selected replay envelope. Rapier [documents serializable complete simulation structures](https://rapier.rs/docs/user_guides/templates_injected/serialization/), but their bytes are a pinned provider-private checkpoint, not the cross-version save schema or N1 replication format; portable game saves use F3 codecs plus explicit compatible reconstruction/migration. The server/standalone world is authoritative. [The selected N1 protocol](NETWORK-SPEC.md) permits initial prediction only for an isolated kinematic capsule against pinned static geometry, with complete controller/input state and no dynamic-body, moving-platform, water, damage/inventory or other-actor dependency. If a cast discovers one of those, or the static collision revision changes, discard that prediction branch and present confirmed state until resynchronized; do not publish a partial replay. Dynamic/ragdoll/water bodies use confirmed interpolation. Rapier's determinism conditions require matching insertion order and build/float behavior; no cross-platform bitwise lockstep is assumed. [Rapier determinism guide](https://rapier.rs/docs/user_guides/templates_injected/determinism/).

## P4: pose, root and prior-view sampling

F4 validates stable joint hierarchy, rest/bind and inverse-bind matrices, clip target IDs, sorted times/tangents, morph target IDs and root extraction policy. Each clip stores local TRS/morph tracks plus a separately cooked root displacement/rotation curve in metres/radians. Root extraction bakes that curve out of the skeletal root; P4 samples it over the full phase interval, including wrap, and sends exactly one request before P3. P4 stores graph state/phase/weights and the last committed pose inputs, not an independent W1 world transform. It blends local translation/scale linearly and rotations by shortest-arc normalized slerp; glTF cubic tracks use Hermite with tangent time scaling and quaternion normalization. Mask/additive use an explicit reference pose and order, never an implicit second root translation. A clipped joint scale/zero quaternion or missing required joint rejects import or clip admission with ID/path. The integrated native reference contains up to 32 skinned actors at 64 bones and mobile eight at 64 bones; these are rejection workloads, not a qualified pose budget.

The two ordinary clips yield one root request: integrate each extracted root curve over the full phase interval, rotate each displacement into the same actor-local tangent frame, then blend displacements and yaw with normalized nonnegative weights. The optional additive pose layer contributes no root motion. A clip crossing wrap integrates the tail and head exactly once; an event key `(clip,cycle,event)` prevents duplicate emission. After P3, P4 maps `d_act` and residual into phase/stride adjustment capped at 10% of the nominal phase advance per tick; a larger mismatch freezes foot planting and reports blocked motion. One analytic two-bone IK can plant each designated foot against `Q_(n+1)` with a support epoch: clamp the target distance to `[|l₁−l₂|+1 mm,l₁+l₂−1 mm]`, select the authored knee pole, and cap planted-foot correction at 0.25 m/tick. A farther target, invalid/old support or nonfinite limb lengths leaves the authored pose and reports the fault rather than moving W1. Gameplay hitboxes sample committed tick pose; per-view render pose may interpolate only between the two bracketing committed poses when root/event correspondence is valid. P4 retains 16 ticks of clip/graph/root inputs and two accepted presentation samples per view to reevaluate skin/morph pose at each view's last accepted time; if history falls outside retention or a content/ragdoll switch changes correspondence, V2/V4 get invalid motion and reset. Saves store graph/clip revisions, phases, weights, root accumulation policy and durable animation events, not GPU palettes. No ozz dependency or FFI enters this initial runtime.

## P5: ground routes, dynamic obstacles and bounded work

F4/Recast cooks one nav tile set per agent class from the same geometry revision as P3's required collider, with radius/height/max-slope/step and material-area flags. The initial ground class matches the 0.35 m/1.8 m capsule, 45° slope and 0.25 m static step; tiles cover 64 × 64 m with 0.25 m horizontal voxel cells and 0.10 m height cells before erosion. Recast's voxelization/erosion is an approximation; the cook validates clearance and border portal coincidence against neighboring tiles to 0.05 m and rejects a mismatch, without promising subvoxel exactness. W2 activates collider, nav tile, off-mesh link endpoints and required game state atomically. A border stays closed until both tiles are resident and version-compatible. Detour `findNearestPoly`/sliced `findPath` yields a versioned polygon corridor; `findStraightPath`/funnel yields waypoints. A query includes agent class, start/end, tile/obstacle generations, origin epoch, maximum nodes/iterations/age and request ID. A Detour partial path or node-pool exhaustion is **not** a completed route; it returns `pending/partial/unreachable` with its safe frontier and reason.

P5 permits at most 16 queued requests, eight active sliced searches, 2048 Detour nodes per active query, 32 search iterations per request and 256 iterations globally per tick, a ten-tick request age, and outputs at most 128 corridor polygons and 32 corners. Overflow yields a named pending/partial failure; actors stop at their last valid frontier. Priority may defer a request; it cannot let an actor follow a stale corridor past a newly closed portal. At a known change or unload, W2 closes entry first; P5 invalidates affected corridor generations and local steering stops/replans. Repath is limited to one attempt per actor per ten ticks unless the current path becomes unsafe, in which case it stops immediately and queues within the same caps. For a moving crate or other dynamic obstacle, P5 proposes 17 ordered candidates (eight headings at two speed fractions plus stop) over a 0.5 s horizon; P3 checks each with a capsule cast and predicted nearby-agent occupancy, then selects the lowest cost admissible candidate by progress, clearance and deviation from the corridor, with stable ID tie-breaks. If none clears, achieved motion is stop and replanning is requested. The short horizon is a gameplay approximation, not a proof that agents never collide; P3's final sweep remains authoritative. P5's route may lag until a named obstacle/area update. The initial set does **not** rebuild nav tiles every frame. A persistent topology change stages a replacement tile off-world; W2 swaps it with collision and matching links. DetourCrowd and TileCache can later supply qualified steering/obstacle rebuilds, but neither is a second movement authority. Flying, swimming and falling require a separately selected topology provider; they are not forced through the ground mesh. The integrated rejection workload is 16 ground agents native and eight mobile, within F1's shared CPU cap.

The selected Detour runtime uses f32 tile-local coordinates behind P5's origin epoch. The owned C ABI exposes only opaque world/query handles, copy-in tile blobs and query parameters, and copy-out bounded path/status records; C++ pointers, allocators and exceptions never cross it. Its poly refs are private, transient and salt/generation-checked; public paths carry stable tile/link IDs and revisions plus a bounded corridor, never bare refs retained across a tile replacement/save. P7 saves agent intent/goal and any durable policy state, then revalidates or recomputes the path after restore; a transient Detour search frontier is not a cross-version save. N1 replicates game-relevant achieved pose/goal outcomes, not the Detour heap or client-owned path authority.

## E1: analytic sea and shallow river

**Prescribed sea and still pond.** The reference physical profile pins `g=9.81 m/s²` and water density `ρ=1000 kg/m³` in its semantic manifest; different fluids require a named profile. E1 owns a mean stage `η̄(t)` from an authored constant/keyframed tide and a finite component list. For wave `j`, depth `H_j>0` and wave number `k_j`, `ω_j²=g|k_j|tanh(|k_j|H_j)` and `η_j=a_j cos(k_j·x−ω_j t+φ_j)`. The physical contact height is `η=η̄+Ση_j` inside declared support; slopes/normal and linear orbital velocity are analytic derivatives of the same components. The source file fixes `a,k,φ,H`, tide curve/phase origin, gravity and density; F4 hashes them and E1 samples at world tick time on CPU. Require a graph surface, sufficiently small `|k_j a_j|` (initial authoring bound `Σ|k_j a_j|≤0.1`), positive depth with a named uniform/slowly varying approximation, and no breaking/overhang. Tide stage change within one shortest wave period is limited to at most 1% of the smallest supported wave depth; faster tide is outside the fixed-depth dispersion/phase approximation. These conservative bounds are selected admission rules to be tested, not universal physics thresholds. The sea is an imposed reservoir: waves are not finite volume and body/river forces do not change it. A distinct still pond is the zero-component, zero-current instance with authored constant stage and polygon support; it has no finite storage or flood claim. A visual residual may change foam/normals within the specified contact error but cannot move the physical height used by P3.

**Shallow patch.** Use one active 2D finite-volume grid of at most 128 × 64 rectangular 2 m × 2 m cells in the initial mixed fixture. There are no cut cells or subcell shoreline geometry; walls follow full faces. Cell `i` stores piecewise-constant bed `z_i`, depth `h_i≥0`, horizontal discharge vector `q_i=h_i u_i` and area `A_i` in SI units. Admission bounds are `0≤h≤20 m` and `|u|≤20 m/s` for cells and boundary ghosts; a proposed state outside them rejects the staged tick. Surface stage is `η_i=z_i+h_i`. On a face of length `L_f`, outward normal `n`, use hydrostatic reconstruction at `z_f=max(z_L,z_R)`: `h_L*=max(0,η_L−z_f)`, `h_R*=max(0,η_R−z_f)`, `q_s*=h_s* q_s/max(h_s,h_dry)` with dry velocity zero. Apply a first-order HLL flux to `(h*,q*)` with characteristic bounds `s_−=min(u_n,L−√gh_L*,u_n,R−√gh_R*)`, `s_+=max(u_n,L+√gh_L*,u_n,R+√gh_R*)`. Use the same face mass flux with opposite signs in the adjacent cells; add the hydrostatic bed-pressure correction to each side's momentum equation. Explicit rainfall/inlet and evaporation/outlet are volume fluxes with named source/sink IDs. This is a first-order depth-averaged, hydrostatic approximation: no vertical profile, dispersive swell, water fall or hydraulic-jump quality claim. [Hydrostatic-reconstruction paper](https://publications.imp.fu-berlin.de/478/1/file_2004_siam.pdf).

The HLL flux for state `U=(h,q_x,q_z)` uses normal physical flux `F_n(U)=(q·n, (q·n)u+½gh²n)`. It is `F_L` if `s_−≥0`, `F_R` if `s_+≤0`, otherwise `(s_+F_L−s_−F_R+s_−s_+(U_R−U_L))/(s_+−s_−)`. A zero denominator returns the common zero/rest flux, not NaN. For a bed step, add the hydrostatic pressure correction `½g(h_s²−h_s*²)n_s` to each *side's* momentum flux, with `n_s` outward from that cell; mass uses one common face flux. Thus a lake at rest remains at rest, while the bed supplies the missing momentum reaction. Closed banks reflect normal velocity in the ghost state and transfer zero mass. At a river mouth, the **named still-sea/tide reservoir** supplies only its mean stage `η̄_sea(t)`, not an instantaneous wave crest. The boundary ghost has the mouth-face bed `z_f`, `h_g=max(0,η̄_sea−z_f)` and `u_g=0`; the same hydrostatic/HLL face operator computes the signed exchange. This is a finite still-reservoir approximation valid only for an authored subcritical/low-gradient mouth (`|u_n|/√gh ≤ 0.5` when wet, `|η_i−η_g|/Δx ≤ 0.05` at the mouth, no bore/breaking). Both-dry faces carry zero flux; a wet/dry face uses the same positivity-limited HLL operator. It does not model an imposed sea current or wave transmission. If wet interior flow exceeds the Froude or slope limits, or the source changes faster than the declared tide bound, the boundary reports `outside-regime` and blocks the next publication under F1's explicit fault/overload policy; it never substitutes a visual wave height or an unstated zero flux. The one mouth edge/tick ID integrates `ΔV=Σ_substeps Δt L_f F_h` with sign outward from river; the river's storage changes by `−ΔV`, and an equal ledger entry is assigned to the **external sea reservoir**, with no sea-state update. This proves only the accounted finite river balance, not global fluid momentum/energy conservation.

Select f64 cell state and flux arithmetic in a fixed face/cell iteration order; this aids the finite-volume ledger but is not a cross-platform bitwise replay promise. Set `h_dry=0.01 m` in the initial profile, pin it in the physical manifest, zero momentum for `h≤h_dry`, and do not divide by dry depth. Set authored Manning coefficient `n_M=0.03 s/m^(1/3)` by default, admitted within `[0,0.10]`; zero is allowed for frictionless fixtures. For each tentative substep, enforce `Δt ≤ 0.4 min_i A_i / Σ_faces L_f(|u_n|+√gh)_f` using reconstructed wet states. Recompute the bound after each substep, choose the next `Δt` up to the remaining tick interval, and stage at most four substeps before atomic publication. As an analytical envelope check, 2 m square cells with `h≤20 m`, `|u|≤20 m/s` and `g=9.81` have `Δt_limit≥0.4·4/[8(20+√(9.81·20))]≈0.00588 s` when each face speed is bounded by that admitted maximum; three substeps of `1/180 s≈0.00556 s` cover a 1/60 s tick. This check does not assert runtime cost or stability beyond the stated shallow model. If four cannot cover the tick, discard the staged state and enter explicit overload/fault or reject the scene/profile; never silently slow simulation time.

The selected positivity rule is a **face donor limiter**, including at the mouth. Add named nonnegative rainfall/inlet volumes to each cell's available volume `V_i=A_i h_i+Δt S_i+`. From the raw HLL mass flux, compute raw outgoing volume `O_i=Δt Σ_f L_f max(F_h,f outward from i,0)`. Set `θ_i=min(1,V_i/O_i)` for `O_i>0` and `θ_i=1` otherwise. Multiply the entire homogeneous HLL vector on each face by its **upwind mass donor's** `θ_i`, using the identical scaled vector for the two neighboring cells and the mouth ledger; the external sea donor has `θ=1`. If the face mass flux is exactly zero, use scale 1 for its homogeneous momentum flux; no mass donor is selected. Leave the two hydrostatic bed-pressure corrections unscaled. After shared fluxes, remove each named sink only up to the cell's remaining volume, record an unmet-sink diagnostic, apply Manning friction through `q←q/[1+Δt g n_M²|u|/max(h,h_dry)^(4/3)]`, and set `q=0` when dry. Rain/source water carries zero horizontal momentum in this initial model. For an interior face, one signed scaled mass flux enters one cell and leaves the other; for the mouth, its signed flux has an equal reservoir-ledger entry. The limiter, sink and friction are part of the same staged substep and are never applied twice. A `θ<1` is reported as a model stress event, not concealed as ordinary wave behavior.

**Discrete invariants.** Before sinks, a cell's exact-arithmetic new volume is `V_i−θ_i O_i+I_i`, with `I_i≥0` the sum of incoming limited face volumes. Because `θ_i O_i≤V_i`, this is nonnegative. The sink is capped at that remainder, so the published depth remains nonnegative. For an unforced lake at rest with constant stage `η₀`, each face reconstructs equal wet states `h_L*=h_R*=max(0,η₀−z_f)` and zero discharge; a wet/dry face with `z_f≥η₀` reconstructs two zeros. Hence HLL mass flux is zero, `θ=1`, and the homogeneous pressure flux plus each side's correction is `½g h_i² n_i` on every face of cell `i`. Since the closed rectangular cell has `Σ_f L_f n_f=0`, its net momentum update vanishes; friction also vanishes at `q=0`. At a mouth this statement requires the reservoir mean stage to equal `η₀`; a time-varying tide intentionally drives flow. It assumes full-face banks, fixed bed and no sources/sinks/body displacement. Thus the chosen discrete operator is positive and lake-at-rest preserving under these stated conditions; it is not a convergence proof or a model of subcell shore shape.

The committed ledger checks `R=V_(n+1)−V_n−Σ(delivered source volumes−delivered sink volumes)+Σ(mouth outward ΔV)−C`, where `C` is the signed sum of any explicit roundoff-only volume corrections (positive when a small negative cell volume is raised to zero). It uses the **same cached f64 face-volume value** on both sides, fixed-order cell updates and pairwise or compensated global summation. Let `W=V_n+V_(n+1)+2Σ|internal face ΔV|+Σ|source/sink ΔV|+Σ|mouth ΔV|+Σ|C|` across all substeps, `u=2^−53`, and `γ₂₅₆=256u/(1−256u)`. The initial numerical acceptance bound is `ε_V=10^−9 m³+γ₂₅₆ W`; it accounts for at most 256 rounding stages per contribution in this bounded four-substep/8192-cell update and reduction. For a single cell use `ε_i=10^−12 m³+γ₂₅₆[V_i+Σ|incident face ΔV|+|source/sink ΔV|]`. An implementation with a deeper arithmetic path must derive a larger bound before admission. Flux truncation/physical modeling error is **not** this residual. Nonfinite/subnormal state, a negative cell volume beyond `ε_i`, or `|R|>ε_V` rejects the staged tick. This gives a conservative finite-volume mass-accounting test under the selected arithmetic bounds; physical convergence, wet/dry behavior and target performance still require fixtures and measurement. A failure rejects this provider instead of silently switching numerical schemes.

Physical stage/depth/flow queries use the committed owning cell and a declared support footprint, with no smoothing between sea and shallow authority. River domain cells form a half-open support; the sea owns its complement, and a stable edge ID resolves exact-boundary tie cases. A query returns domain/model/product/commit epochs, validity, stage, depth, normal, horizontal velocity and a local face-stage spread used as an uncertainty bound for coarse cells. Initial floating contact admits at most 0.10 m local face-stage spread across each volume element's swept footprint; greater variation rejects this approximation. Render interpolation across the mouth can hide a visible seam but is explicitly presentation-only; it cannot grant hull passage or change force queries.

The shallow grid state and edge flux ledger are E1 authority, independent of W2 stream cells. The initial N1 server profile preloads the entire finite authoritative physical closure before a match and does not stream new authoritative regions or switch physical sources during it; W2 client interest and visual residency may change independently. In standalone/offline worlds W2 pins required grid/ghost/bank support while P3/P5 use it; unload/entry is blocked unless a separately qualified transfer/reduced-state mechanism exists. P7 captures `h,q`, bed/product hash, tide/source phase and edge cumulative ledger at a committed barrier. Headless/offline and server authority execute the same CPU operator. N1's required gameplay/query content is verified **before** capturing a fresh baseline; a peer missing it remains content-loading with no pinned stale baseline, and an applied water query is one complete epoch/dependency group. The reference N1 baseline sends confirmed body outcomes and only bounded physical water **query projections** needed by client consumers, each with model/domain/source/edge epoch, tick and quantity-specific error (initial stage ≤5 mm, horizontal speed ≤0.02 m/s); it does not send the entire 8192-cell f64 grid within its 256 KiB baseline cap. A client wanting authoritative local water simulation is a different prediction/provider profile. No client recomputes server mass flux from shader pixels or just a seed. Distinct target visual grids may be finer/coarser only as bounded presentation residuals. F2 may turn water off only when no retained rule, contact, nav or audio consumer needs its state/query.

## E1: moving-shore precipitation and the distinct capacity profile

The [moving-shore design](research/MOVING-SHORE-EXPOSURE-DESIGN-2026-11-02.md)
selects a bounded CPU/headless exposure and material-exchange operator for rain
on changing physical wet support. It does **not** change the initial rectangular
HLL state above. That solver retains `V=A*h`, a constant bed per cell, full-cell
wet/dry support and its own four-substep admission. A triangulated 1:20 bank
under a 2 m cell can differ by about 1 m in shore position at mid-stage; the
original HLL must use matching terrace source geometry, not a smoothed render
shore posing as its physical support.

F4 prepares one static arrangement of rain cells, top solid receivers, source
bed/response patches, water domains and boundary/halo ownership. E1 combines
it with the committed provider wet support during each admitted F1 interval:
each local horizontal rain area has one first-hit solid, water or named export
recipient and one identified mass receipt. Prescribed sea rain enters its
external-reservoir ledger; finite water receives volume under the selected
zero-horizontal-momentum source policy. PLAY's analytic sea includes spatial
waves. A tide-only exposure shortcut is admissible only when the entire wave
envelope cannot change wet/receiver ordering; otherwise the bounded wave
profile uses analytic phase extrema and certified space-time bands to account
for interior wet and dry excursions, or refuses the work/error request.
For an admitted tide-only prescribed scalar stage, F4 prepares per-triangle
first-hit area polynomials and E1 integrates them against interval rain across
stage/source/vertex events. This removes frozen-start direct-rain allocation
bias in the exact offline fixture; film/water state timing and floating-point
certification remain open. Durable receipt identity is the physical
source/stream interval and aggregated receiver payload, independent of the
number of internal algebraic or physical subdivisions.

The selected surface response has exposed film, submerged bound coating and
absorbed liquid as separate **mass** stores. Inundation and recession transfer
real mass by source patch/response-cell overlap; finite water can refill a
bounded coating under an explicit donor debit. Excess coating moves into an

substep or tick. Its stage/support effect and residence are bounded at

recursively change after it is used. W1/F1 publishes source, water, material,
transit and cursor results together, or leaves the entire interval uncommitted.
P3 reads X[n] traction, P5 invalidates a stale moving wet-area generation,
P7 saves every authoritative store and pending receipt. Rendering or rain
particles do not create a second wetness authority.

A separate **provisional** `CapacityHllShore` profile is the higher-quality
numerical proof target for finite water on the admitted fine bed. It stores
cell volume `V_i` and integrated momentum; `C_i(η)=∫cell max(η-b,0)dA`
provides stage, partially wet area and source-correspondent shoreline.
Pointwise shared-face HLL flux integrates over actual bed traces, with a

physical timestep change or visual smoothing of the discrepancy.

## Body–water approximation and the hull crossing rule

The first P3/E1 force law is **one-way sampled buoyancy and flow-relative drag**, not fluid–body conservative coupling. F4 cooks at most 16 **non-overlapping body-local tetrahedra** approximating the admitted hull interior; their union volume must match its validated convex hull within 5% and volume center within 0.10 m. The cooker assigns only **exterior** hull triangles to elements (no internal tetra faces); their body-local areas and normals define directional projected drag area. The reference drag coefficient is `C_D=1` (dimensionless, pinned source/profile data), or the hull is rejected. At each fixed tick P3 rotates/translates all tetra vertices and exterior face normals to world coordinates. For the analytic sea, E1 supplies a tangent water plane at each tetra's world centroid from its physical stage and gradient; for a shallow/pond cell the plane is horizontal at that centroid's physical stage. Every vertex and centroid of the **whole swept hull** must have valid samples under one physical domain authority, and actual stage at each vertex must differ from its tetra's chosen plane by at most 0.05 m; a domain seam or greater within-domain curvature/cell step rejects the hull contact rather than changing models mid-force. Clip each oriented tetra against its plane, compute the clipped polyhedron's volume `V_j,wet` and centroid `c_j,wet` by signed tetra decomposition, and apply `F_b,j=ρgV_j,wet up` at `c_j,wet`. This volume/centroid computation responds correctly to body roll and pitch within the piecewise-planar surface approximation; no unrotated vertical extent or linear immersion fraction is used.

Evaluate drag at `c_j,wet` as `F_d,j=−½ρ C_D A_j(v̂_rel)(V_j,wet/V_j)|v_rel|v_rel`, where `A_j(v̂)=Σ_exterior faces A_f max(0,−n_f,world·v̂)` and `v_rel=[v_cm+ω×(c_j,wet−c_cm)]−u_water`; set drag to zero when `|v_rel|=0`. For the shallow model `u_water` is horizontal depth-averaged velocity with vertical component zero, while analytic sea may supply its linear orbital vertical component. Scaling projected exterior area by wet-volume fraction is an empirical approximation, not pressure integration. Torque is `(c_j,wet−c_cm)×(F_b,j+F_d,j)`. Empty clips contribute zero; a clipped vertex or domain query failure fails the contact capability rather than taking zero. If computed total force exceeds `2ρgV_total` or total torque exceeds `2ρgV_total R_max`, the model reports outside-regime and rejects that staged tick rather than clipping an unaccounted impulse. The reaction ledger's displaced-volume estimate is exactly `ΣV_j,wet` under this geometry approximation. The remaining errors are tetrahedral hull approximation, planarization of the true surface, empirical drag and one-tick force sampling; the stated volume/centroid/stage limits bound admission, while measured force/contact accuracy remains qualification.

P3 emits one identified `body-water reaction` record per body/tick containing sampled domain IDs, force impulse `−ΣF_j Δt`, displaced-volume estimate, validity and work diagnostic. For the **prescribed sea**, E1 logs that reaction against an external wave/flow reservoir and may produce a separate cosmetic wake; it does not add the rendered wake back into body wetting or claim reciprocal wave energy. For the **initial shallow solver**, E1 likewise logs it as an unresolved external momentum/displacement exchange and does **not** inject the impulse or subtract displaced hull volume from `h`. Admit shallow-body contact only when `V_displaced / V_affected_cells ≤ 0.05` and predicted induced stage `V_displaced / A_affected ≤ min(0.05 m, 0.02 h_local)` throughout its swept support; these are conservative initial model-validity caps, not measured accuracy. A large hull in a shallow channel is rejected for this provider. No double wake momentum, no hidden mass subtraction, and no claim of coupled body/fluid conservation. A later pressure/displacement source requires a new E1/P3 joint operator and ledger.

At the sea–river mouth, the **water exchange is admitted; a boat crossing is not implied**. The initial physical crossing policy is closed for a hull whose swept footprint touches both model supports. W2/P3 must provide a visible authored sill/bank/obstacle or a declared impassable portal, with collision and nav agreeing; no invisible rendered seam turns into a navigable channel. A boat can operate separately on sea or within the small-body river regime, and the four-body fixture can place bodies on both sides. A future traversable mouth needs one explicit finite-hull handoff law: unique point/volume query owner over the swept footprint, bounded height/normal/current and integrated-force jump at the transition, compatible model time/phase, and pinning until the hull clears. If unavailable or outside tolerance, F2 rejects the traverse; it does not blend heights or let a hull pop. This is intentionally narrower than a universal heterogeneous coupling promise, while the initial sea→river reservoir flux is completely specified.

## F2 admission, traces and remaining proof

F2's initial physical model matrix has: dry/no-water (all water work removed); static authored pond (prescribed height/flow only, no finite storage or flood claim); prescribed analytic sea (surface contact under linear-wave support); 2D shallow patch (finite storage, flow, wet/dry and small-body contact); sea→shallow still-reservoir mouth (signed **river** flux only, no boat traverse); visual falls/pool (render-only seam, physically separate reservoirs). A swimmer, submerged cave, waterfall boat, rough breaking surf or large hull in shallow flow fails the relevant quantity/regime check. A target may change water mesh/foam/optics quality but not this physical model, bathymetry, flux, force or server manifest. A connected sea/river/pond requiring true equal-and-opposite momentum/energy, a finite receiving pool rising from actual discharge or traversable mixed-model mouth needs a separately derived operator/common solver; it is **unsupported in the initial set**.

**Normal trace.** At an admitted source/build revision, W2 activates the shallow grid, banks, P3 colliders, P5 ground nav and the external-reservoir mouth as one pre-tick closure. F1 samples tide at `t_n`, E1 computes substeps and internal/mouth HLL fluxes, logs one signed mouth volume, updates `h,q` and publishes X[n+1]. P3 has used X[n] to force two small river floaters and sea samples to force two sea floaters, emitting one tagged reaction record per body. P5 keeps the sill impassable. In the mixed-coast R3 fixture, an attempted **hull crossing** therefore returns a visible blocked/rejected result even when the calm still-reservoir water exchange is valid. Two cameras or a headless server consume the same committed physics; a mobile client may omit high-frequency visual waves yet receives the same authoritative body outcomes. P7 saves `h,q`, source phase, domain/edge IDs, Rapier/body state and stable event cursors at a committed barrier. A reconnect obtains N1's pinned semantic manifest and coherent baseline, then client-only presentation readiness.

**Adverse trace.** A source edit raises tide speed or river inflow so mouth Fr exceeds the admitted range while one client streams out its shore cell. The live match remains pinned to its old source/build; the edited source does not change the server. Within that match, if the physical state itself crosses the mouth regime limit, E1 reports outside-regime before committing the next tick; F1 enters its explicit fault/overload policy and does not silently clamp flux or remove solver state. W2 retains server-side river support despite the client's interest change. That client cannot substitute its sea texture for missing water state; N1 resynchronizes a coherent domain group. If it had only the initial isolated-capsule prediction, the water-contact body remains confirmed/interpolated. A save/restore requires the exact old river provider/bed/source closure or a reviewed migration; it cannot pair new tide source with old `h,q` by default.

**Remaining implementation qualification:** The positivity, lake-at-rest and mass-ledger arguments above close the chosen discrete operator under their stated assumptions. A code implementation still has to show it uses that operator, keeps the 256-stage arithmetic bound or derives a larger one, passes wet/dry/convergence fixtures, and meets visual contact/shore tolerances; none has been run here. Rapier/Detour target builds and licensing notices, wrapper invariants, moving-platform controller behavior, ragdoll transfer, source/cook schemas, P4 prior-pose retention and per-target integrated cost also need implementation/measurement. F1's native 5 ms and browser/mobile 8/7 ms proposed CPU critical-path budgets are shared across every system; the 8192-cell/16-wave/4-body fixture is a **rejection workload**, not proof it fits. Before public demonstration, measure full tick tails, snapshot/stream peak memory, offline recovery and actual displayed contact/shore images.
