# G3t Metal boundary: one graph, one device owner

6 November 2026. **Boundary selected; native encoding choice provisional.** This record implements the direction in [hardware-native](HARDWARE-NATIVE-ENGINE.md) and C04/C06/C07. It does not reorder G3b and G3t or qualify either gate.

## Why this boundary exists

The 10k/1080p V1 frame runs with a resident world thread and a render owner. [Current evidence](../../evidence/render/V1-BOUNDED-FRAMES-2026-11-04.md) owns the frame and allocation numbers; neither G3b's CPU nor zero-allocation budget passes. The owner-approved eight-second Metal System Trace shows drawable waits with little overlap with this app's GPU work. Pinned wgpu 30.0.1 records commands early but performs native encoding in `CommandEncoder::finish`, after acquisition. The portable scene-pre-submit experiment is parked at `30ae560` on `codex/g3b-scene-presubmit`: extra submissions increase allocations without a demonstrated complete-frame CPU win. A raw drawable bridge has not been selected; it must address measured native encoding and submission cost, not merely move acquisition. Offscreen rows and trace intervals do not qualify displayed cadence or latency. The current wgpu path remains the portable oracle; trace, exports and rejected runs stay in task-owned `tmp/` while this decision remains provisional.

## Selected ownership and data flow

The capability registry is built once per adapter generation. It selects a portable wgpu recipe or an Apple tile recipe before pipeline closure; no pass probes a device privately. Both recipes consume the same compiled graph, view contract, material/instance ABI, source generations and recorded pass identity. The graph owns dependency order, live-pass culling, attachment lifetimes, history transitions and named timestamps. Its executor derives load/store and memoryless eligibility from actual producer/consumer lifetimes. A depth or motion attachment read after the merged render pass must be stored; a last-use attachment may be transient or memoryless on a supported Apple recipe. Surface acquisition remains the last operation before the drawable consumer; no feature owns another surface or submission queue.

One isolated `apple_tile` backend below the graph may own mapped per-slot upload rings, attachment storage, native pipeline archives, MetalFX and EDR handoff. It must use the coordinator's device generation and submission ordering. The portable backend retains equivalent graph semantics and provides the fallback. An invalid cache, unsupported feature or failed optional pipeline selects the already prepared portable path; it cannot trigger compilation during play. Cache keys include adapter, driver, OS/SDK, recipe, shader and ABI revisions. Device loss discards native objects and rebuilds from retained cooked inputs. A raw call never publishes history or retires a resource before the graph's submission succeeds.

The local macOS 27.2 SDK exposes memoryless textures, `MTLBinaryArchive`, and Metal 4 command allocators/archives. Availability is a registry input, not a reason to assume all Apple devices can use them. The pinned wgpu-hal 30.0.1 Metal path uses `MTLCommandQueue`; wgpu's `CommandEncoder::as_hal_mut` forbids ending its command buffer. A Metal 4 allocator/queue therefore cannot be inserted into that encoder as a narrow escape hatch. The two credible experiments are (a) a legacy Metal shared-encoder call that respects wgpu's buffer lifetime and submit, and (b) one coordinated full-frame Metal 4 submission owner, OS/capability gated, with resources prepared for that backend and a portable fallback at generation boundaries. The latter needs graph commands that both backends can record; today's closures take a wgpu encoder. Neither experiment may create a rival device or queue for part of a frame.

## Time-boxed selection and rejection

Use the existing `v1-game` 10k/1080p frame, with identical scene, lights, history and output, on the M3 Max. Interleave portable and candidate runs under shared load; separately qualify a quiet run. Record p50/p95/p99 of the complete render callback, allocations on every frame thread, per-pass GPU timestamps, attachment traffic from Metal counters, image difference, pipeline hitches and one surface-loss/recreate cycle. First time-box the shared-encoder branch against the full frame. If it cannot remove the allocation and CPU gap, estimate the graph command representation and resource handoff needed for a full Metal 4 owner; do not land a parallel queue as a shortcut. Reject a candidate that needs a rival submission owner, weakens C07 history, allocates after warmup under G3b's zero-allocation row, exceeds portable GPU time, or fails to recover. Promote only with a measured win and image tolerance per the hardware-native rule. If the wgpu recorder cannot meet G3b's 300 µs and zero-allocation rows, keep G3b open and request the owner's gate-order steer before bringing native recording forward.
