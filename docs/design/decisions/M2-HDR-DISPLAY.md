# M2 scene colour and fixed SDR display slice

4 November 2026. Selected continuation of the running [M1 lit diagnostic](M1-RENDER-FOUNDATION.md) under [C06–C08](../CONTRACTS.md) and the [render specification](../RENDER-SPEC.md#4-scene-colour-and-light-quantities). This is one prepared offscreen interaction, not a clustered renderer or an F4 product.

## One view, two roles

The directional GGX/depth draw writes scene-linear Rec.709 radiance to an `Rgba16Float` attachment. It applies the first fixture's storage pre-exposure `p=1/1024` after material, direct-light and emission evaluation. The previous forward shader clipped every channel to one, so light values above display white could not be distinguished by a later transform. The second prepared role loads one exact scene texel without filtering, applies the selected fixed exposure and luminance tone/gamut map, and explicitly encodes sRGB to `Rgba8Unorm`. The final four-byte readback remains an SDR image. There is no automatic exposure, spatial AA or display-P3/HDR output claim.

One checked positive exposure `e` belongs to the prepared view. The existing ABI2 `ViewRecord` keeps `p` and reciprocal exposure `1/e` in their defined lanes; a separate private 16-byte display uniform carries checked `e/p`. The display shader evaluates the [specified curve](../RENDER-SPEC.md#8-r0-first-rendered-game-contract-and-proof) in a scaled form to avoid multiplying large radiance and gain before the denominator. It writes sRGB values into a non-sRGB UNORM attachment, so no attachment transfer repeats the encoding. Source texture decoding remains in the material inputs. Changing exposure prepares a new view and target binding; no runtime tick or world light authority is introduced.

The target retains the HDR scene texture, SDR output, depth and its display bind group. Both pipelines are prepared before the view is ready; resizing makes target resources and a bind group, not a new pipeline. The typed graph declares forward attachment write, display sampled read and output write, and optional final copy. Two timestamp pairs are checked together before the first pass, so an undersized batch cannot leave a partially encoded diagnostic. The executor still owns the device, queue, submission, polling and fence retirement. Generation and exposure matching reject stale target use.

## Cost and qualification

At 1920×1080, the extra HDR attachment is 16,588,800 logical bytes. HDR plus final SDR colour is 24,883,200 logical bytes, before depth, readback, allocation alignment and old/new overlap. The new fullscreen role evaluates 2,073,600 pixels. Nominal colour write/read traffic is about 41.5 MB per frame across forward HDR write, display HDR read and SDR write, compared with 8.3 MB for the prior single SDR write; tile caches/compression may reduce physical traffic. It adds no per-instance CPU bindings or frame-time pipeline creation. These are an account, not a device-memory measurement or whole-frame budget.

The GPU proof uses 65×33 padded readback and a 10,000-cube 1080p scene. Independent f64 checks cover the specified tone and sRGB transfer, background, light/material response, radiance above one at two exposures, depth order, stale target and timestamp capacity. The named Metal timing and visual inspection belong in the [M2 evidence](../../evidence/render/M2-HDR-DISPLAY-2026-11-04.md). The slice remains incomplete against [section 4's](../RENDER-SPEC.md#4-scene-colour-and-light-quantities) per-pass nonfinite repair/clamp counters and visible diagnostics. Clustered lights, Sun shadow, masked geometry, a general view packet, source/build binding, target fleet and sustained frame pacing remain open.
