# Shared environmental source research — 2 November 2026

Status: research recommendation for E1; no implementation, numerical, image or device qualification. This study proposes changes to the canonical [environment family](../modules/ENVIRONMENT.md), [C08/C09 contracts](../CONTRACTS.md), [render specification](../RENDER-SPEC.md) and [build plan](../../plans/BRIEF.md); it does not supersede those authorities. It follows the module-design blue-sky, performance and review workflows. Source research and analytical deductions below are distinct from executable proof.

## Independent target, recorded before reconciliation

The reusable target is one quantity authority at a position/time, with optional authored constant/keyframed and richer physical-data providers behind the same typed sampling envelope. F1 owns time, E1 owns the environmental forcing, V3 owns light transport, water/surface providers own accumulated response, P3 owns body integration, and A1 owns audio playback. No camera, cloud image, water shader or audio loop advances weather. Cloud shadows can remain when visible sky/clouds are absent. No mandatory atmospheric fluid solver or global Earth model follows from coherence.

R0 needs only an authored directional/constant environment source. R1 adds world-space wind, one fog region and two independent presentation histories; R2 preserves selected physical semantics while reducing visual work; R3 adds finite water/surface exchanges and cloud attenuation without visible clouds. Moon/astronomical time, pollutants and thermodynamic simulation are optional capabilities, never invented from a missing field. A complete source supplies declared values or typed absence plus an admitted substitute. A simple source may deliberately author unrelated fog, humidity and light; it must not claim their physical consistency.

The first selection is a bounded authored field provider: immutable spatial supports and piecewise-linear time tracks; directional lights in unit-vector form; bounded world-space gust basis; planar cloud optical-depth field with declared light/altitude range; liquid/solid precipitation as external mass flux; explicit temperature, vapour and pressure values; independent optical medium coefficients and optional pollutant concentrations. A physical-data provider may publish sampled forecast/observation volumes with conservative remapping where conservation is claimed. An online meteorology solver remains a separate provider commission requiring closure laws, boundaries and budget, rather than an implied implementation of this interface.

This target was saved before inspecting the Broadsides candidates for this study. Thrum's existing design/provenance already records historical Broadsides findings, so this is independent target selection, not a claim of complete historical blindness.

## Source and query envelope

A proposed `EnvironmentSample` is a conceptual contract, not a compiled Rust type. Its common metadata is:

- `WorldEpoch`, committed tick or exact interval, stable provider/domain identity, source/model/schema revisions and required cooked-content hashes.
- Quantity mask and role (`authoritative`, `prescribed external forcing`, `presentation-only`); quality/error is per quantity rather than a single misleading frame-wide “accuracy”.
- World-metre support, vertical datum and coordinate/origin epoch; spatial representation (constant, analytic, point sample, cell mean, face flux), spacing/filter scale and covered altitude.
- Source sample time, valid interval, temporal reconstruction, maximum admitted age/extrapolation and discontinuity generation. Store both observation/model issue time and world sampling time for imported products.
- Values or `outside_support`, `unavailable`, `stale`, `unsupported`, `ambiguous_owner`, `invalid_input`. Missing temperature never becomes zero kelvin; missing wind never silently becomes calm.

The router chooses exactly one authority for each requested quantity. A compound snapshot may reference several providers, but its dependencies and common tick are atomic. Local overrides select explicit stable priority/support. Scalar/vector *authored nonconserved* transitions may use declared blend weights; conserved fluxes partition support and transfers instead of summing two full sources. Equal priorities with overlapping authority fail admission. Boundary ties use authored topology/order, not view position or hash iteration order.

Batch consumers request related fields together at world points or region footprints. A sample cannot be retained after its immutable snapshot/provider leases expire. A renderer can upload a reduced f32 projection carrying a bounded conversion/interpolation error; it cannot become the CPU physical authority by being convenient to sample. F3 unit wrappers and typed quantity enums should prevent mixing direction, radiance, illuminance, flux density and scalar artistic controls.

## Time, celestial geometry and light quantities

F1 remains the only tick scheduler. Persist a phase mapping `theta_j(t)=theta_j0+integral r_j(t) dt` for each optional environmental driver: astronomical/day phase and authored weather phase. Constant/piecewise-linear admitted rate tracks have exact piecewise polynomial integrals; save phase/rate segment identity rather than accumulating render-frame deltas. The default is one physical second per world second. A short artistic day changes the source trajectory sampled at a fixed world tick; it does not change `dt` in P3, water or surface mass integration. Pause freezes the authority clock under F1 policy; resume does not apply wall-clock weather unless an explicit bounded external-forcing policy was selected.

A provider declares whether its direction is geometric or apparent, and whether refraction/air bending is already included. Baseline authored lighting uses straight rays and one consistent unit vector `s` pointing *from receiver toward source*; propagation is `-s`. For a local east/north/up astronomical adapter, convert once into the authored C01 world transform. Azimuth's zero, clockwise/counterclockwise convention and geodetic/geocentric latitude must be recorded. Origin rebasing never changes Sun/wind direction or source phase.

Direction tracks interpolate on the unit sphere; reject antipodal segments unless an intermediate direction or rotation axis is authored. Intensities use finite nonnegative linear tracks; angular radius is radians. Do not linearly blend wrapped azimuth degrees. A baked ephemeris pins site, calendar/time-scale conversions, kernel/table identity, correction policy, supported dates and maximum angular interpolation error. It fails outside that interval. With a bounded angular second derivative `A`, linear interpolation error is at most approximately `A*Delta_t²/8` before normalization away from antipodal geometry; certify with an independent ephemeris sample set including rise/set and high-latitude cases. An arbitrary-date runtime adapter is an optional later selection.

Sun/Moon source facts carry stable light identity, unit direction, angular radius and separate photometric and optional radiometric products. V3's baseline source input is perpendicular illuminance in lux with unit-luminance Rec.709 colour; it is *before* the air/fog/cloud terms V3 owns. Terrain visibility, cloud transmittance and air extinction are each applied once on a named source-to-receiver path. An authored already-ground-calibrated illuminance needs an explicit boundary/attenuation contract and cannot be passed as the raw top-of-air value. HDRI terminal background excludes the separately supplied Sun disk under RENDER-SPEC; an optional Moon disk follows the same identity partition. Zero direct light below the authored horizon is an authored light curve, not an ephemeris irradiance model.

Moon direction and phase are separate quantities. A provider can author both; an astronomical provider derives phase from Sun–Moon–observer geometry, with illuminated fraction `(1+cos alpha)/2` for phase angle `alpha` at the Moon. That is geometry, not a lunar brightness law. Realistic lunar intensity additionally needs reflectance/phase/opposition and atmospheric treatment. Therefore baseline Moon light is a declared authored intensity track; it cannot claim radiometric Moon calibration merely from illuminated fraction. Tide remains the water provider's named forcing: no implicit rule that a rendered Moon determines tide.

Thermal/plant consumers receive independently authored or spectrally integrated W/m², with direct-normal/diffuse-horizontal/longwave meanings declared. Broadband lux divided by 683 is invalid solar heat. A headless irradiance provider shares source direction, cloud/exposure facts and medium identities but runs its own declared radiometric operator; physical surfaces never read a shadow texture or rendered brightness. A profile may use constant temperature/melt/evaporation rates instead and explicitly waive energy-accuracy claims.

## Wind, clouds and precipitation operators

The authored wind floor is `u(x,y,z,t)=u_base(y,t)+sum a_j(t) b_j(x,y,z) sin(k_j dot x - phi_j(t))`, where basis support, amplitudes, frequencies and phases are immutable admitted content. Constant is the zero-mode case. Units are m/s; any imported meteorological “from” bearing is converted once to the engine's toward-vector. Amplitude sum bounds gust speed, and spatial/temporal derivatives bound sampling error. This is authored correlated forcing, not incompressible turbulence or a pressure solution. Vertical interpolation uses authored layers and rejects outside support; it does not extend a marine power law through forest canopies or the entire troposphere.

Water samples the provider's declared wind reference height or area average and applies its selected stress/wave response; wind alone does not define a physically developed sea. Vegetation samples the same field at plant/branch heights and uses its own inertia/stiffness/damping. P3 drag uses `u-v_body_point` with declared density and area/drag law. V5 streaks may sample cheaper correlated detail but do not control rainfall totals. A1 reads local wind and response facts near its selected listener; a second camera does not create another weather mix. Consumer filtering is explicit: integrating small gusts into foliage response is different from dropping them from authoritative sail force.

For the initial cloud field preserve RENDER-SPEC's authored `tau_s(x,z,t,s)` with angle/altitude validity and `T=exp(-tau_s)`. Sample optical depth, not painted RGB darkness. One field can be evaluated headless and uploaded as a texture. Its phase is integrated from wind history; `position = current_wind * absolute_time` is invalid after a wind edit because it teleports the whole field. For uniform piecewise-linear wind, use the exact integrated displacement. Spatially varying wind needs characteristic integration or explicit authored moving cells; rigid translation is labelled a different approximation.

A concrete optional planar recipe stores vertical optical depth `tau_v` on world plane `y=h`. At receiver `p` and upward unit source `s`, intersect `q=p+(h-p.y)/s.y*s`, then approximate `tau_s=tau_v(q.x,q.z)/s.y`. This is a thin horizontal slab approximation. It cannot supply cloud-side views, internal depth occlusion or self-shadow radiance; no visible 3D claim follows. Require `s.y>=0.1`, receivers below the slab and declared height/footprint support; lower angles return unavailable or an explicitly admitted alternate. This bound is a proposal for this recipe, not a replacement for the broader renderer contract. A finite slab of thickness `D` smears the projected footprint over approximately `D*length(s.xz)/s.y`; a thin-plane approximation must fit its authored shadow-edge tolerance. For smooth `tau_v` with Lipschitz bound `L`, footprint error `epsilon_x` contributes at most `L*epsilon_x/s.y` optical-depth error. This makes high-frequency sharp shadows and low Sun a reason to strengthen the provider rather than merely increase texture resolution.

Cloud/no-cloud switches are source changes when they change attenuation; disabling only visible-cloud presentation is a resource change. A richer coefficient-volume provider supplies `sigma_t(x,t)` and derives light-ray depth by integration, with CPU/analytic physical projection if required. Visible and shadow projections share density/phase revision; render-only fine erosion has a declared coarse-shadow error. Cloud coverage fraction is insufficient to determine optical depth or rain. The small provider may author these controls jointly in one front recipe, but it declares their prescribed relation rather than a water-conservation claim.

Precipitation exports liquid and solid water-equivalent downward flux `P_l,P_s` in kg/(m² s) per horizontal footprint, plus phase/type and, optionally, temperature/impact velocity. At water density 1000 kg/m³, `1 mm/h = 1/3600 kg/(m² s)`; “snow depth” needs snow density and is not interchangeable. Imported time accumulations become interval-integrated mass, never point intensity interpolated across arbitrary missing times. The bounded authored provider integrates piecewise-linear rates exactly over tick/substep intervals. A sharp front is either explicitly discontinuous or resolves the rate transition; temporal smoothing cannot silently change its total deposit.

The baseline deposition operator supports vertical precipitation on exposed heightfield receivers. Cooked projected footprint ownership partitions each weather footprint into first-hit roof/canopy/ground/water receiver weights `w_i>=0`, exported sink weight and residual in-flight storage if modeled, with `sum w=1`. Deposit `Delta M_i=w_i integral_A integral_t P dtdA`; do not multiply by sloped area again. A canopy interception provider can release its retained water as identified later drip flux; it cannot also send the original full rain immediately to soil. Moving roofs/objects require updated exposure or an explicitly admitted no-occlusion approximation. Arbitrary slanted fallout, splash rebound and multilayer porous interception are outside this first operator unless a provider supplies their first-hit/transfer law. Cosmetic rain can drift with wind without claiming wind-driven deposition on vertical walls.

For physical-data grids, remap a source cell-mean flux to disjoint target horizontal footprints using `P_i=sum_j area(A_i intersection B_j) P_j / area(A_i)`. Then `sum_i area(A_i)P_i` equals the source integrated flux over the covered partition, up to geometry/rounding; missing coverage is typed absence, not zero. Bilinear point interpolation remains appropriate for nonconserved visual queries, not a conservative rain-transfer claim. Source atmospheric storage is not debited for prescribed weather: deposit is a named external reservoir exchange. A simulated moisture provider must debit rain/evaporation with matching event/flux IDs and account for boundary transport and suspended condensate.

## Thermodynamics, air quality and surface response

The lightest thermodynamic record stores `T_air` K, local absolute pressure `p` Pa and vapour specific humidity `q_v=m_v/(m_dry+m_v)` kg/kg; condensate is separate. Do not silently substitute mean-sea-level pressure for local station pressure. The gas-only denominator is explicit and differs from a model convention including all cloud/precipitation phases. With `epsilon=R_d/R_v`, `e=q_v*p/(epsilon+(1-epsilon)q_v)`, and `RH=e/e_sat(T,phase)`; humidity fraction is dimensionless, editor percent is a conversion. The saturation law, water/ice reference and valid temperature interval belong to the selected thermodynamic provider. The first authored profile may directly author RH with T/p, convert once to q using that law, and publish q as the only moisture authority. Reject incompatible redundant q/RH inputs. Zero vapour has a valid q but an undefined finite dew point; no logarithm-of-zero fallback temperature is invented.

For the initial bounded authored profile, adopt the NOAA/NWS liquid-water Magnus fit `e_sat=611.2 exp(17.67 T_C/(T_C+243.5)) Pa` only for a declared ordinary-liquid range, proposed `T_C in [0,40]`; colder/supersaturated mixed-phase air selects a stronger provider or independently authored optical fog. This fit is an approximation, not a global equation of state. The [NWS calculation sheet](https://www.weather.gov/media/epz/wxcalc/rhTdFromWetBulb.pdf), saturation and RH equations, was inspected; it also makes pressure/unit conventions explicit. The bounded provider admits `0<=q_v<1`, positive finite p/T and `0<=RH<=1`; a physical cloud provider may represent supersaturation and condensation explicitly instead of clamping away mass. Fog is an independent optical coefficient under the authored profile; high RH alone does not determine droplet number, size or visibility. A physical condensation provider must supply latent-heat and water transfer, droplet closure and dissipation, not merely map RH to a density noise.

A richer physical-data provider imports T/p/q/wind/cloud/rain fields with their original heights, timestamps, grid support, missing-data masks and model provenance. Pressure-level products require a defined geopotential-to-world-altitude transform; treating pressure levels as evenly spaced metres is invalid. Humidity/RH is derived after interpolating its admitted thermodynamic state, not averaged independently into a contradictory triple. Ingesting data is not simulating meteorology: absent subgrid gusts, fog and canopy shelter remain explicit. A future physical simulation needs continuity/momentum, energy, vapour/condensate, boundary sources, turbulence/surface exchange, radiation, microphysics and stable numerical operators; that complete provider remains an open design commission.

Optical air owns extinction/scattering/phase/emission coefficients in V3's admitted RGB basis. Optional air quality instead owns named species and definitions: e.g. dry PM2.5 mass kg/m³, PM10 with explicit nesting (do not sum PM2.5 and inclusive PM10), CO or O2 mole fraction mol/mol, or fictional toxin mass concentration. Particle cutoff/dry-versus-wet convention and measurement/model support matter. There is no mandatory pollutant list or default “healthy air”; unknown is unavailable. Exposure/game rules belong to P6: an optional inhaled-mass estimate integrates concentration times authored breathing volume rate and protection efficiency over simulation time. It is game policy, not a medical predictor. A1 may cue coughing/wind/muffling from confirmed rule/events or environmental facts; it never infers concentration from fog pixels. Mapping pollutant mass to optics needs size/composition/hygroscopic growth and an optical model; inverse fog-to-pollutant is generally nonunique. Clear visible air can still carry a named gas hazard.

Surface state owns `m_liquid,m_solid` kg/m² and any thermal/storage variables, independently of source rate. On tick n, P3 uses committed surface X[n] for traction/contact. E1 samples interval-n weather and surfaces integrate reservoirs, then publish X[n+1] with the world. Newly produced contact/splash/drip deposits join the next designated update exactly once. Melting/freezing cancel in total water. Evaporation and runoff debit at most available water and name vapour/exported-sink or receiving-water identities. Water receives runoff once; the surface loses the matching amount. The rain shadow/exposure product may be shared with rendering, but physical authority cannot depend on whether that product was drawn.

An authored thermal provider specifies prescribed surface temperature and phase/evaporation rates; positivity-limited transfers close mass while making no energy-accuracy claim. A physical energy provider may instead integrate `C_A dT_s/dt=Q_SW_abs+Q_LW_net-H-L_v E-G`, where each W/m² term has a material/exposure/forcing producer and positive sign convention into the surface (latent evaporation is outgoing). Source irradiance is only one term. Surface albedo/emissivity, air T/q/p/wind, soil/conduction state and available water are required. This equation alone does not close turbulent H/E or soil G; those constitutive laws are explicitly still missing for a general physical surface provider. Do not mark physical thermal response build-ready until that provider is commissioned. The simpler prescribed-rate baseline is complete for its stated approximation.

## Developer lifecycle and supported profiles

An ordinary authored source file binds stable IDs to named quantity fields, supports, track knots, units, allowed substitutions and optional appearance products. F3 validates units/ranges/conflicts; F2 computes required consumer closure; F4 cooks track coefficients, overlap/exposure weights and target-specific optional projections from admitted immutable source. A future point/interval query inspector should show support, time, authority and approximation as well as the value. These tools and schemas are proposed, not existing commands. No new source admission pipeline is commissioned.

At play start the ready build pins provider/products; F1/W1 publishes initial environmental state before consumers activate. Source edits that alter gameplay/rain/wind/thermal/traction restart the run under current C4. An allowed purely visual quality change may invalidate/recreate textures and histories without changing field meaning or authority. Replacing a provider with accumulated state requires explicit migration or reset/restart; switching shaders is not that migration. A physical-data gap uses the admitted bounded hold, fallback or failure policy; after its maximum age it cannot pretend to be fresh.

P7 captures authoritative source phase/rate segment, input revision, stateful provider fields, exchange/event cursors and reservoirs at the same committed barrier. Static analytic providers can save their parameters/phase only within a declared replay envelope. No GPU history is saved as the weather truth. Restore loads the exact compatible source/build and provider closure before publishing any query. Retired source jobs carry world/source epochs and cannot publish after restart. Browser suspend resumes under F1 pause/limited catch-up policy; no unbounded offline rainfall integration is implied. For unloaded regions choose pinned active support, a specified aggregate integration, or paused deposition with declared boundary behavior. Unsupported catch-up is a real gap, not permission to drop stored water.

Off is demand-driven: disabling precipitation *emission* stops new deposit/streak emission, but water, existing airborne particles if modeled, wetness, snow and continuous runoff may persist. Disabling the last consumer can retire optional provider work after leases/fences release; retained reservoir state remains P7-owned as required. Disabling a visual cloud pass while rain deposition depends on the cloud field cannot delete that source. Physical owner absence with retained required queries rejects activation rather than selecting clear/calm without authorization.

For N1, pin physical source/schema/forcing semantics and required content in the gameplay manifest; do not force every client to ship the authority's full meteorological solver. Publish bounded confirmed query projections or admitted reproducible prescribed parameters with source revision/tick/validity, paired atomically with dependent body/surface facts. A client visual wind/rain field can be cheaper only within the game's declared cue/error requirements. Weather-dependent gameplay visibility is an authority query, independent of rendered fog/shadows. Reconnect discards old epochs/baselines, restores current confirmed source and dependent state, and resets affected presentation histories. Prediction stays off unless the entire relevant forcing, reservoir, P3 and event-ledger closure is captured/replayed.

## Worked paper traces

These cases explain mechanisms; none was executed.

1. **Clear R0.** Author one light `s=(0,1,0)`, 100000 lux D65 and constant environment luminance. These are fixture values, not measured daylight. Required consumers contain no weather/air/water/audio. Admission chooses constant provider; one immutable descriptor is reused each tick. V3 consumes it without invented cloud/air attenuation; V4 displays. Saving records the source/build reference and world tick, with no weather grid. The wrong implementation scheduling an invisible sky update fails the off-work counter check.
2. **Storm crosses forest and coast.** An authored front advects by integrated wind phase and changes co-located tau, wind and precipitation tracks. A 10 mm/h liquid flux over a disjoint exposed 100 m² patch deposits `100*10/3600=0.277777... kg/s`; over 60 seconds this is 16.6667 kg. A roof taking 40% leaves 10 kg for the exposed ground over that minute and 6.6667 kg on the roof/storage, not 16.6667 kg on each. Surface transfer and named runoff feed finite water; P3 sees the previous committed reservoir for one tick. Vegetation bends under sampled height-dependent wind; A1 reads wind/rain impact rate at its one listener; V5 draws optional streaks. V3 uses one cloud factor per direct-light path and jointly integrates overlapping fog/air. Rendering fewer streaks changes none of those masses/forces. Source-off stops new input, while existing wetness and runoff continue.
3. **Cloud shadows, sky off, two views.** The front source publishes one tick endpoint. A top-down main view and first-person second view sample the same tau/source time in world space. Main view shows ground shadows without a cloud volume. Second view may use an admitted appearance projection, but a planar tau provider alone does not promise fly-through clouds. Each history records the image sample it actually retained; the half-rate second view reevaluates prior analytic source phase or resets. Moving/rebasing either camera changes projection, not weather. Sun leaving the planar recipe's valid angular range yields the authored alternate/failure, not silent stale attenuation.
4. **Edit, restart and save/reload.** Changing a wind knot at source admission creates new revision B; live run A stays pinned. Restart publishes B's phase origin after old leases retire. A save from A restores only against compatible A source/build, including reservoir/event cursor. Reconstruct cloud displacement from its integrated phase rather than `new_velocity*old_time`, so the edit cannot retroactively move old clouds. Switching to a simulated provider requires migration or a fresh run, with no promise that q/thermal state can be inferred from old fog colour.
5. **Headless server and mobile reconnect.** Server advances storm once at fixed dt and P3 uses the same sample semantics as standalone. Mobile client can omit all visible clouds and receive confirmed body/surface/query facts, with matching source revision and tick. Old reconnect packets cannot update current source epochs. A GPU shadow map is never shipped as proof of physical rain/traction. Missing physical content prevents admission while local visual preparation may continue independently.
6. **Suspend or source gap.** Paused world records the last committed interval and phases. Resume either continues that time or performs a selected bounded catch-up with complete interval forcing; it cannot emit three hours of rain into one tick. A stale imported product reaches maximum age and takes its admitted fallback/failure path. Continuing rain audio while the source became unavailable without a declared hold is rejected.

## Cost account and quality reductions

Costs here are analytical counts, not measurements or subsystem entitlements. Whole-scene limits remain [BASELINE-SPEC](../BASELINE-SPEC.md#budget-allocation-and-failure-rules). At 30 Hz two due 60 Hz ticks share the simulation allowance.

For K track knots, B active basis modes, Q batched query points, N grid cells, F updated scalar components and f source updates/s: authored constant preparation is O(1), track stepping amortizes segment lookup and analytic queries cost O(QB). A sampled provider update costs O(NF) per endpoint plus decode/remap; per-query trilinear reconstruction costs up to eight vector/scalar corner reads, and temporal interpolation can double them. World count multiplies authority cost; view count does not. Consumer callbacks performing individual routing/lookup/allocation at every branch/leaf can exceed field arithmetic: group by provider/tick, deduplicate repeated support samples, cache only under complete point/phase/source keys, and use consumer-specific spatial filtering rather than allocating a universal dense atmosphere.

An illustrative optional 64×64×8 wind grid with three f32 components has 393216 bytes per endpoint; two endpoints need 0.75 MiB. A 128² f32 tau map needs 64 KiB per endpoint, 128 KiB for two. Two 128² liquid/solid rate maps need 128 KiB per endpoint, 256 KiB for two. Total selected field values are 1.125 MiB for two endpoints, before metadata, imported decoded data, immutable reader overlap, queues, uploads or consumers. Full 3D T/p/q/cloud/species can expand this rapidly; no such grid is required for an R0 constant or analytic gust profile. Two 256² R16Float shadow maps cost 256 KiB logical storage; adding a double buffer doubles it. Driver residency, alignment and pass scratch are separate measurements. Shared unified memory is counted once, not as independent full CPU/GPU allocations.

For a visible volumetric cloud alternative, work grows approximately with shaded pixels × view-ray samples × shadow-ray work, plus source bake/projection and temporal resources per active view. A map-only shadow update with R² texels and S source steps is O(R²S); the planar authored tau path instead has O(1) field samples per queried light path. Raising visible-cloud fidelity cannot be priced solely by its source grid memory. The critical path includes provider preparation → committed forcing → consumer integration → extraction/upload → shadow/media draw, and GPU readback into physical queries can insert a queue wait. No proof of sub-millisecond operation follows from small field bytes.

Use ranked reductions: remove duplicate source/routing work and inactive products first; share immutable source/projection data across views/lights where validity permits; precompute track/basis invariants and exposure overlap products; update slow fields only when their time/error budget requires; reduce optional visible cloud/reflection/streak work; coarsen visual projections with measured edge/cue error. Do not lower physical update rate, drop rainfall area or change force semantics merely to meet a visual tier. Lower source cadence is legal only if the same admitted temporal reconstruction bounds retained consumer error.

For a Lipschitz field with temporal derivative bound L_t and spatial bound L_x, a hold at age dt and point approximation at distance dx has error at most `L_t*dt+L_x*dx` within the bounded regime. Smooth linear temporal interpolation improves the temporal term to `A_t*dt²/8`; discontinuities invalidate that argument. Since `|d exp(-tau)/d tau|<=1` for nonnegative tau, absolute transmittance error is at most optical-depth error; relative radiance error can still matter in dark/high-contrast regions. Phase advection error and sharp front placement deserve their own bounds; more texture resolution cannot repair an absent evolving-cloud mechanism. Consumer mass errors accumulate over area/time and must be measured against the ledger independently of visual similarity.

Quality transitions reserve old/new products and outstanding reader/fence leases, count source replacement plus both view histories, and apply hysteresis. Source floor cannot be removed while its last physical/audio consumer remains. Device loss recreates derived products from pinned source without resetting physical phases/reservoirs. Counter instrumentation should report source steps, quantity query counts, source/projection upload bytes, bake texels/voxels, retained endpoints/leases, per-view history bytes, skipped work and failure/age counts. Measure CPU stages, GPU timestamps and synchronous waits separately, with cold/load and sustained transition tails on named devices.

**2024 particle optics.** Schneegans et al., [Physically Based Real-Time Rendering of Atmospheres using Mie Theory](https://serwiss.bib.hs-hannover.de/files/3478/schneegans_etal2024-rendering_of_atmospheres.pdf), Eurographics/CGF, 30 April 2024: inspected §§2.1–2.3, 3.1–3.2 and §4, including equations 1–6 and Table 1. ### Resulting design deltas and comparative decision

The authored provider remains selected as the bounded first source: it needs no ML runtime, planetary model, high-dimensional optical precompute or sparse volume residency. Modern alternatives earn specific roles: particle-optics cooking for composition-sensitive skies; imported/hybrid forecast products for large-scale changing boundary conditions; baked sparse volumes for local visual detail; generated HDRI for content creation. None independently supplies all of wind, thermodynamics, deposition, optical transport and accumulated surface response.

This sweep strengthens four concrete recommendations: (1) put dry/wet particle basis, interval accumulation, coordinate-specific velocity and source-cycle identity in the import envelope; (2) qualify HDR illumination and solar partition independently of sky appearance; (3) preserve explicit approximation labels for overlapped media and roof/precipitation presentation; and (4) retain committed source/time/forcing semantics even when an external example updates its Sun per render frame. The existing proof queue gains invalid imported-interval/negative-rain, Pa/s-versus-m/s, hydrated-sea-salt and generated-HDRI solar-energy cases. These are recommended checks, not tests run.

No recent source inspected here overturns the one-authority/tick, separate source/response, independent visible-sky selection or headless physical-query contract. No publication/talk/renderer closes the outstanding physical meteorology, turbulent/thermal closure, general exposure or GPU-authority water mechanisms. Repository checks remain separate from correctness, visual acceptance and sustained device performance.
