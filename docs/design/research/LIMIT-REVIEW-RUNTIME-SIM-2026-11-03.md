# Runtime, simulation, networking and terrain limit review

Reviewed 3 November 2026 against checkout HEAD `e3ce277492e3e43e1b48e214d26d703a4d4a421c`; the holistic policy identifies its own earlier inspected revision. Read-only review; only this report was written. Applied the repository engine-architecture and engine-module-design skills, including review/performance references. Prior exposure: the commission supplied the existing policy and implementation; this is an adversarial review, not a blind architecture selection.

## Recommendation

Keep the revised policy. No P0/P1 defect was established in this scope. Its separation of semantic invariants, numerical validity, capacity, operating policy and measurement targets is substantially sound. One concrete ordinary-host capacity gap remains, and a family-design sentence still permits a weaker network lifetime interpretation than C13. Other important items below are explicitly recorded implementation/proof gates, not newly discovered architectural defects.

Inspected AGENTS/README/build plan, C01/C02/C05/C09/C12/C13, BASELINE-SPEC, PLAY-ENVIRONMENT-SPEC, NETWORK-SPEC, WORLD-CONTENT, NETWORKING, terrain research/dogfood/final-triangle evidence, current clock/host and relevant authority/publication paths. The 4,000-line host includes substantial tests; inspection was focused on constructors, budget/callback paths, peer registration, publication ownership and their tests, not a full independent review of every test and storage helper. No transport, solver or integrated terrain runtime was run; none is present as a qualified engine path.

## Findings

### [P2] Ordinary peer registration has no finite aggregate membership limit

**Code/policy gap:** `crates/thrum-core/src/authority.rs:1657` checks peer count only when optional `fixed_credits` or `structural_credits` exist. The ordinary `HeadlessHost` branch at `crates/thrum-core/src/host.rs:814` forwards registration directly. Each additional distinct PeerId grows the registry and due scratch (`authority.rs:1672-1692`), while the eight-input and 256-result bounds are per peer (`authority.rs:1869`). There is no peer-removal API in these owners. Thus ordinary operation has no declared aggregate ceiling on registered peers, pending records or per-tick watermarks, even though each per-peer queue is finite.

This is not a claim of a network exploit: the existing host is an in-memory fixture and N1 remains unbuilt. It is a concrete hole if this ordinary path is used to satisfy mandatory finite overload under `docs/BUILD-PLAN.md:250` and the holistic policy's ordinary-host promise. Making precharged progress optional must not accidentally make aggregate membership bounds optional.

**Action:** add an ordinary owner-selected finite peer/membership capacity, independent of publication-byte credits, or explicitly mark the ordinary API as a prototype that requires a bounded outer membership owner before runtime promotion. A later disconnect policy needs an epoch-safe cleanup/tombstone/result-retention contract; an initial finite lifetime-membership cap is simpler and sufficient for a bounded match. Report the chosen count before run.

**Executable rejection case:** construct an ordinary local host with P peer capacity; register P distinct IDs, attempt P+1, and prove refusal leaves membership, pool-independent storage and input sequence unchanged. Repeat across fresh connection epochs and while P peers each retain eight pending/256 total pending-plus-completed entries. Verify that the sealed charged path retains its existing stronger guarantees.

### [P3] Networking family text still allows snapshot retention until peer pressure

**Design wording conflict:** `docs/design/modules/NETWORKING.md:118-121` says N1 follows the same bounded-reader discipline as other consumers and slow peers cannot retain snapshots *indefinitely*, followed by releasing leases on queue/age pressure. A straightforward implementation could retain W1 snapshots until ACK or a two-second sync deadline. That is materially weaker than C13 (`docs/design/CONTRACTS.md:565`) and the holistic policy (`docs/design/research/HOLISTIC-LIMIT-POLICY-2026-11-03.md:75-77`): remote progress must not retain authority snapshots.

Eight distinct snapshot bundles are enough to block the current authority; two seconds at 60 Hz is not compatible with treating network retention as ordinary required-reader leases. The policy already chooses the correct remedy; harmonize the family wording and the N1 adapter commission.

**Action:** explicitly require an owned bounded projection/baseline image, release the source W1 lease after copying, and ACK-retain only N1-owned projection/result/event storage. Local extraction may briefly lease a snapshot, but remote ACK must never be a release condition.

**Executable rejection case:** capture a baseline with all four peers holding ACKs for more than eight authority ticks; stall one peer's send path, force two resync attempts, and keep other peers active. Assert that W1 snapshot-lease count is independent of ACK age, ticks continue until genuine local CPU/backlog overload, and N1 queue pressure closes/resyncs only the affected peer.

### [P2] Aggregate due-batch CPU admission is still a proof gate, not a supported capacity

**Known implementation/proof gap, already tracked at BUILD-PLAN:248-250:** `docs/design/NETWORK-SPEC.md:387` gives N1 0.5 ms/tick inside the whole-engine budget; `docs/design/BASELINE-SPEC.md:89-93` assigns all due simulation ticks 30% of a callback's 5/8/7 ms CPU envelope. Four catch-up ticks at the N1 budget alone cost 2 ms, exceeding desktop's 1.5 ms simulation allocation, before rules/physics/publication. This does not prove every tick takes 0.5 ms or make four a promised service count: the current clock's four is a safety maximum, and recipes may deliberately rebalance. It does prove these independent maximum allowances cannot be advertised as a simultaneous desktop guarantee.

The important workload multiplier is also wider than the headline counts: P5's 17 candidates ×16 agents gives 272 P3 casts/tick, outside its 256 search-iteration cap (`PLAY-ENVIRONMENT-SPEC.md:39`). The 128×64 shallow grid has 16,576 geometric faces; four substeps imply up to 66,304 unique face evaluations/tick before reconstruction, donor passes, cell updates, rain exposure, snapshots and consumers. Two due ticks double that work. These are count derivations, not timings.

**Action:** the first F2/F1 composition must publish a maximum admitted due batch from a complete tick bound plus callback non-step work, and state separately what happens to already queued ticks. Include publication/projection copies, required local readers, serialization and optional providers only when selected. Measure total work and critical path, rather than summing parallel stage timings. No need to require water/nav for R0 or introduce a universal scheduler framework.

**Executable cases:** 30 Hz presentation with two 60 Hz ticks; a callback owing four ticks; full four-peer result/event bursts; 272 steering casts with dense geometry; optional 8,192-cell water/rain composition. Compare admitted count with actual callback elapsed and backlog. Reject/rebalance the recipe or enter explicit overload; never advance a partial physical tick or conceal discarded world time. Existing `HostBudget`/observed-overrun machinery (`host.rs:96-119,899-959`) is a useful narrow foundation, not a hard real-time guarantee.

### [P2] Terrain geometry evidence does not yet certify the activated consumer closure

**Known integration/proof gap, accurately labelled:** `docs/evidence/terrain/T1-FINAL-TRIANGLE-CERTIFICATE-2026-11-03.md:179-183` explicitly leaves full neighbour/halo preparation, F3/F4 binding, material patches, collision/nav clearance and runtime water open. Its exact final-triangle/error/encoding tests are valuable; they are neither a general remeshing certificate nor a successful physical activation. The achieved near-zero error derives from this aligned reference geometry and mostly exact preservation, not from measured arbitrary-terrain quality.

**Action:** preserve that narrow status. The first runtime product manifest needs independently checked source→geometry→consumer bindings and quantities each consumer can actually use: positional error/support for P3, swept cylinder clearance/step/headroom for P5, and bed/capacity/connectivity over the admitted stage interval for E1. A source-attached vertical error alone cannot establish these. Keep unsupported certificate classes as `certificate-unavailable`, not a sampled numerical pass.

**Executable rejection cases:** rehash a product after removing an interior sill; offset only its collider; change one neighbour halo; keep render vertices correct but change nav climb/agent profile; change geometry-patch assignment without updating response/exposure; use off-grid coordinates outside the exact certificate's profile. Fail activation before exposing the region. Positive cases should include both an identity/full-resolution fallback and a genuinely changed coarse product whose consumer errors remain within the combined budget.

## Sound choices and limits of the argument

- **Time:** C02 correctly distinguishes dt, render cadence, world seconds, wall callbacks and transport deadlines. The clock uses integer rational tick accumulation, caps due work, reports refused overload time, and requires explicit stopped-time resume. Arbitrary `ClockConfig.tick_hz` does not prove compatible physics or N1 windows; the unbuilt F2 pre-run compatibility check is correctly left open. Test 30/60/120 Hz owner combinations, including non-60 rejection, before promoting a resolved recipe.
- **Memory:** optional charged owner-byte progress is a legitimate specialized profile. Ordinary rules can allocate private memory; a publication pool cannot prove RSS. Snapshot and CPU/provider/GPU leases must retain their distinct lifetimes. The present callback publication wrapper keeps its vector permit through consuming iteration, and the authority's snapshot backpressure prevents reuse of live pages. Do not weaken those guarantees to make byte totals prettier.
- **Physical behavior:** positive mass and a small conservation residual do not prove spatial/temporal accuracy, contact quality or nonlinear stability. PLAY explicitly distinguishes them, stages refusal, keeps body-water exchange one-way and blocks mixed-model hull crossing. This is narrower but coherent. Preserve the provisional status of CapacityHllShore and derive any new arithmetic/error bound from the actual implementation. The cited hydrostatic reconstruction theorem is conditional and does not automatically certify the added sources, donor policy and coupling.
- **Availability:** explicit stop/fault on an outside-regime state is a valid safety outcome, not proof an ordinary coastal game remains playable. Required normal-use trajectories that repeatedly hit refusal need a wider model/profile; a visual tier change cannot fix physical validity.
- **Network:** finite full dependency groups, content-ready before fresh baseline capture, separate replica/GPU ACKs, no automatic uncertain one-shot replay, and no initial delta-history machinery are coherent simplifications. Browser queue limits must remain engine-controlled limits, with browser-internal buffers separately qualified.

## Current primary-source checks and applicability

Inspected online in this review, 3 November 2026. These references constrain mechanisms; they do not qualify Thrum devices or select Thrum's numerical constants.

1. [Godot Engine fixed tick and maximum physics steps](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/classes/class_engine.html#class-engine-property-max-physics-steps-per-frame) documents bounded steps and resulting slowdown when service cannot keep pace. This confirms the need for an explicit overload/time policy; it does not justify copying Godot's implicit time behavior into a server that promises stable authoritative timing.
2. [Rapier integration parameters](https://rapier.rs/docs/user_guides/rust/integration_parameters/) distinguishes dt, CCD substeps, solver iterations, stability/accuracy and length units. Therefore tick-rate and solver-profile changes are coupled semantic/numerical choices; eight iterations/two CCD substeps are not a complete cost or accuracy bound.
3. [Audusse et al., hydrostatic reconstruction](https://publications.imp.fu-berlin.de/478/1/file_2004_siam.pdf) derives well-balanced/positivity and entropy properties under stated conditions on the base solver. It supports the selected mechanism and separately motivates non-rest stability/convergence checks for Thrum's modified source/boundary operator.
4. [WHATWG WebSockets standard](https://websockets.spec.whatwg.org/) exposes complete message delivery and buffered sending, not application receive-backpressure control. [Tungstenite 0.30 WebSocketConfig](https://docs.rs/tungstenite/0.30.0/tungstenite/protocol/struct.WebSocketConfig.html) distinguishes eager read-buffer storage, frame/message limits and an unlimited default maximum write buffer. Explicit native adapter caps and UA-memory qualification are necessary; neither byte queue tables nor TLS alone bound all allocations.
5. [Recast rcConfig](https://recastnav.com/structrcConfig.html) defines interacting raster cell dimensions, erosion radius, voxel climb/headroom and contour/detail errors. Matching border portals to 5 cm cannot alone certify continuous capsule clearance. [meshoptimizer's simplification documentation](https://meshoptimizer.org/#simplification) provides practical simplification/error controls, not independent physical topology or water-capacity proofs; verify the final encoded product against each required consumer certificate.

## Simpler coherent alternatives

For the first ordinary game, prefer a finite preloaded match, one explicit tick recipe, owner-local count limits and measured pressure over compulsory future-credit reservation for every native allocation. Retain protected output credits only where progress under a competing bounded pool is actually promised. For N1, retain full bounded groups and owned projections instead of adding delta baselines or leasing whole world snapshots. For terrain, preserve full-resolution physical geometry and protected sills while simplifying render products; identity certificates are a valid initial fallback when changed-product certification is unavailable. For water, choose no water or a prescribed pond when those satisfy the game; choose the finite shallow operator only for actual finite-storage/flow consumers. These reduce required mechanisms while preserving semantics, rather than loosening error or silently dropping consumers.
