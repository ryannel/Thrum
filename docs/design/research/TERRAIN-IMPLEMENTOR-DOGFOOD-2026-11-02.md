## First complete fixture and deliberate limits

The fixture is a 64 m × 64 m beach region A, `x ∈ [-32,32]`, `z ∈ [0,64]`, and an adjacent same-size region B at `z ∈ [64,128]`. World frame is right-handed Y-up metres; first source objects have identity transform. A contains a 1:20 bank, a flat shelf with a small basin and a protected sill, a pitched roof over ground, and a bridge with distinct deck/underside patches above ground at the same XZ. B continues the bank and has matching source samples on `z=64`; its traversal boundary can be held closed while A's required halo is present. Both are authored sources rather than camera-centred terrain.

Reference landscape samples are at 0.25 m spacing: 257² vertices per region, 256² quads, a fixed SW–NE diagonal and 131,072 triangles. Source heights and coordinates use integer multiples of 0.0001 m in the fixture schema. The datum and step are explicit; quantization into that schema is the creator's admitted source choice, not error relative to surveyed geology. The bank is linear away from the shelf. Basin/sill geometry is defined by the same explicit source samples; its 0.25 m-wide sill with a 0.125 m crest is protected from coarse decimation. That dyadic crest also survives the first f32 encoding exactly. The fixture records the exact source mesh and sill crest, rather than relying on regenerating an undocumented noise function.

One selected static prescribed-water provider supplies constant H and zero flow over an authored shore support. Its bed is the selected landscape floor, never the bridge deck. It claims neither finite storage nor flood arrival time. The basin/sill level sweep uses separate runs with precipitation off; it measures geometry wet-set connectivity and storage curves independently and does not turn this provider into a connected flood solver. The first rain proof instead uses the E1 owner's fixed-footprint finite pond: the already selected CPU HLL/hydrostatic shallow-water family on a smaller closed reflective rectangular domain, flat liner bed -1 m, initial depth 1 m, admitted stage [-0.25,0.25] m and retaining-wall crest 1 m. The source geometry includes that cut/liner/wall; surrounding terrain and roof must preserve the pond's first-hit ordering over the entire stage interval. Pond grid/footprint and source-split/substep order are the E1 dogfood's pinned provider inputs. Its actual water update belongs to E1, not W2 or a new terrain-local solver. Rain/runoff exported to the prescribed sea is logged against its named external reservoir and does not change H. Advancing dry-slope transport, moving-shore exposure recomputation, erosion, swimming/submerged caves, body–water feedback and live physical source replacement remain outside this first fixture and return explicit unsupported outcomes when requested.

The first material response has film and absorbed liquid only, fixed prescribed temperature 293.15 K and vertical precipitation; snow/freezing/energy coupling are absent capabilities. The rain footprint is inland and includes roof, ground and the fixed pond; it does not cover the changing-shore geometry test. Static exposure keys geometry/receiver topology plus water-domain and stage validity. A changed wet footprint or receiver ordering invalidates it and blocks deposition before tick writes; no new wet patch can receive both its old ground share and a water share. Two controlled capsules test ground/bridge clearance, with two P5 ground-route requests. A main perspective view and an orthographic overview share state. The scene uses one directional light with cloud attenuation only; it needs no visible sky/cloud renderer or vegetation simulation. A few keyed static placement markers can verify that grounding uses the canonical source, not a view's LOD.

The flat west/northeast ground rectangles in the [E1 dogfood](ENVIRONMENT-IMPLEMENTOR-DOGFOOD-2026-11-02.md) are a separate analytic **rain subfixture**, not this 1:20-bank region's admitted source. Their closed-form projected areas and true areas supply a simple independent oracle. The integrated beach scene must cook exposure and surface true areas from its own actual source triangles, roof/bridge geometry and fixed-pond support; it cannot reuse the flat subfixture's weights, counts or hashes. Moving-shore level tests remain rain-off.

## F3 source records, identities and field meanings

128-bit stable IDs below mean F3 opaque authored IDs, never names, array positions or hashes. Hash fields are SHA-256 under FOUNDATION-SPEC. Decode rejects nonfinite/out-of-range values, bad indices, missing required IDs, duplicate IDs, invalid bounds and undeclared dependencies before allocating beyond profile limits. First fixture maxima are two regions, one landscape per region, 16 landscape patches per region, four mesh instances, 256 mesh triangles, 64 extra mesh response cells, eight drain edges and two capsules; exceeding them selects a different fixture/profile instead of growing unchecked.

The landscape has 16 stable 16 m × 16 m source patches; response cells are 1 m × 1 m in patch-local XZ. Each cell owns a list of source-triangle fragments and their summed **true** area. Material boundaries split cells where necessary. For a roof/bridge, use unique patch-local face coordinates and true triangle area. Ordinary repeated/tiled material UVs are not unique response storage coordinates. Source patch IDs survive triangle reordering and atlas repacking; render-product triangle IDs never become save keys.

**Previously missing choice resolved:** distinguish source provenance from reusable product content. `SourceRevision S` hashes the whole admitted file tree. `GeometryRevision G` hashes the canonical geometry/source-patch/material-assignment inputs and their declared dependency values. A build manifest binds `(GeometryId,S) → G`; all active consumers resolve through the same admitted build binding. **C05's requirement to identify one admitted source revision is fulfilled by this activation/build manifest binding S→G.** Content-addressed product bytes hash only true dependencies: G plus actual cook inputs/profile, not an unrelated whole-tree formatting edit. The activation binding still carries S/build/epochs. This preserves one admitted-source authority while allowing unchanged product bytes to be reused across different admitted trees. An implementor must not omit source bindings just because two product hashes match.

## F4 product DAG and readiness tuple

The first cooker reads admitted immutable bytes only. Canonical records use F3's selected I-JSON/JCS conventions; binary arrays have a versioned encoding, explicit endian/counts and checked indices. Source hash S remains exact-file provenance. A proposed task key is `SHA256(JCS({task_schema, canonical_input_hashes, sorted_dependency_product_hashes, cooker_version, toolchain_digest, consumer_profile, target_encoding}))`; output hash separately identifies actual bytes. Floating-point/compiler-dependent cook output must either be pinned/reproducible or be treated as non-shareable cache output. This record does not select a new hash system.

```text
admitted source S + schema/profile roots
  ├─ canonical landscape/mesh + patch membership G
  │   ├─ reference triangles + patch locations + boundary records
  │   │   ├─ P3 full-topology static collider product
  │   │   │   └─ P5 Recast tile + agent-area flags + neighbour portals
  │   │   ├─ E1 selected bed/shore topology + capacity/error certificate
  │   │   ├─ E1 patch true areas + response adjacency
  │   │   └─ E1 vertical-exposure footprint partition
  │   └─ V2 full/coarse render + source attachment + shadow products
  │       └─ geometry/error/boundary certificates against reference
  ├─ material recipes ── response law + V3 closure/textures/pipeline demands
  ├─ water/precipitation/drain sources ── physical provider configuration
  │   └─ fixed-water ground-area policy ── P5 area flags/shore frontier
  └─ region manifest ── complete consumer refs + certificates + dependencies
```

P5 uses the selected Recast 1.6.0 offline cooker and Detour runtime, not an invented nav generator. Its initial 64 m tiles, 0.25 m horizontal/0.10 m vertical cells, 0.35 m agent radius, 1.8 m height, 45° slope and 0.25 m static step follow PLAY-ENVIRONMENT-SPEC. This fixture's ground-navigation policy excludes prescribed-water wet ground and the pond interior; the fixed water-support/level revision is therefore a real dependency of nav area flags/shore frontier, in addition to collider geometry. A later moving wet frontier would require a separately admitted dynamic-area/invalidation policy. Geometry-only level sweeps do not silently qualify stale navigation. P3 uses the selected Rapier adapter and full-reference static triangle topology initially. The bed product evaluates the same triangle diagonal/interpolation; bilinear height interpolation is a different surface and is not silently substituted.

A proposed `ProductHeaderV1` contains logical product/geometry IDs, G, product kind/schema, dependency hashes, consumer/profile/version, target encoding, local coordinate datum, support AABB, source-patch mapping, boundary/halo refs, certificate refs, finite count/byte maxima and declared capability bits. A manifest `ActivationBinding` adds S/build hash, region ID/activation epoch, world epoch, current provider/model revisions, CPU consumer readiness and optional GPU device/pipeline readiness. A staged result also carries expected active-set generation and P3/P5 origin epochs. CPU/GPU outputs with stale device/origin/region/build generations are discarded before publication; their leases retire normally.

Required for this interactive fixture: P3 collider, P5 tile and portal data, E1 bed/patch/area/exposure/response/source products, V2 render/shadow products and V3 material/pipeline closure. Headless recipe omits V2/V3 readiness without dropping physical products. A visual-only observer does not force the authoritative headless world to wait for a GPU. Within the interactive fixture a missing required shadow pipeline is view-loading, not silent loss of shadows; source admission, physical activation and local view readiness remain different statuses.

## Error certificate algorithms an implementor can actually follow

Certificates are relative to the **admitted discrete source**, not the imagined smooth landscape or a real surveyed coast. Store reference/product hashes, support, metric, algorithm/version, protected-feature policy, arithmetic/error assumptions and achieved bounds. A requested threshold is separate from an achieved bound. First local position budgets proposed here are 5 mm collider–source, 5 mm bed–source, 10 mm render–source in the shoreline support; these are fixture rejection values pending content/visual acceptance, not engine-wide physics guarantees. Source-to-render, source-to-bed and coordinate conversion are charged separately.

1. **Construct the reference exactly.** Decode integer sample coordinates/heights as rational metres. Split each source quad SW–NE. Mesh roof/bridge triangles retain exact source indices and patch assignment. Reject degenerate or nonfinite inputs; mark holes and explicit mesh boundaries. The fixture has no procedural-function remainder to guess.
2. **Build one full and one coarse landscape candidate.** Full product preserves source topology. For the coarse candidate, process aligned 2×2 source-cell blocks: keep boundary-strip and protected-feature blocks fine; remove interior midpoint samples only in unprotected blocks. Where an adjacent retained fine block supplies an edge midpoint, include it in the coarse block ring and emit a centre fan; otherwise retain the fixed diagonal. The centre lies on the admitted source. This is deterministic known-grid stitching, not arbitrary mesh welding. Candidate render triangles retain patch-local XZ attachment; material is sampled by source location. Roof/bridge keep full topology in both views.
3. **Compute a true vertical difference bound.** Overlay candidate/reference XZ triangulations using exact rational orientation/intersection tests. The aligned 2×2 source-cell blocks are the broadphase: each block compares only its eight reference triangles and at most eight candidate fan triangles, rather than all source/product triangle pairs globally. On each overlay triangle the height difference is affine, so evaluate its absolute value at every overlay vertex; the maximum is the exact discrete vertical bound before output quantization. Coarse fans must be checked **after** final triangulation. Product f32 values are exact dyadic inputs to the checker or enclosed by directed-rounding intervals; add a conservative bound for output/local conversion. A checker unable to determine a predicate/bound within its finite work/bit-length limit returns `certificate-unavailable`; it cannot round an uncertain result into passed.
4. **Certify first mesh products by correspondence.** Same-topology roof/bridge source/product triangles have vertex displacement bound e; barycentric convexity bounds every corresponding point's displacement by the largest endpoint displacement. Verify each face orientation/material/patch assignment and explicit source boundary. This is a correspondence certificate, not a generic Hausdorff certificate for remeshed geometry. Such general simplification remains a gap.
5. **Certify seams and support.** Both sides reference the same integer-coordinate boundary polyline and vertex order; output rounding uses the same datum/encoding or explicitly checked world-space conversion. Count interior manifold edges and compare matching boundary segments/holes; no T-junction or skirt counts as a physical seam. Compare all halo input hashes and source locations; mismatched revisions reject the pair.
6. **Certify a nonflat shore.** For the declared water-height interval, overlay source bed and constant/linear water evaluator. Where a positive lower bound g_min on bed–water gradient through the uncertainty band is proved, use `horizontal displacement ≤ (e_bed + e_water + e_coordinate)/g_min`. For a 1:20 bank, 10 mm combined vertical error permits 0.20 m horizontal error; first shore limit is 0.10 m, so that candidate fails. Refine/use the reference or tighten constituent bounds; foam and material darkening are irrelevant to acceptance. Water render residual is initially zero.
7. **Treat shelf/sill directly.** Enumerate all source/product vertex elevations and protected sill critical levels. Between consecutive critical heights, clip every bed triangle by `b<H` to obtain wet polygons, connect shared wet edges under explicit boundary/seed labels, and compare component adjacency, wet area and volume curves. At critical heights inspect one-sided limits and equality under a declared `b<H` wet convention. Protected sill geometry is identical initially; otherwise any shifted protected topology-changing level or changed component adjacency within the admitted interval rejects the first candidate. Noncritical contour/area changes must fit their separately requested bounds. This deliberately avoids a meaningless slope division at a flat shelf. Compute `V(H)=Σ∫triangle max(H-b,0)dA_xz`; triangular clipping/polynomial integration supplies exact-rational oracle coefficients. Between critical levels area is piecewise quadratic and volume piecewise cubic; bound differences at interval ends and all derivative roots using certified root intervals, not only a few chosen levels. This is storage **geometry**, not evidence of actual flood dynamics.

For a smooth bank all bounds must include input/product quantization; 2 km encoded in unsigned 16-bit heights would alone spend about 1.53 cm rounding and fail this fixture's shore allowance. First products therefore use the admitted local datum and f32/f64 encodings with recorded local errors. Normals/geometric silhouettes, material coverage and nav clearance get separate validation; a positional certificate cannot certify them. P3 solver/contact tolerances and controller skin are separately qualified against the source geometry, not added to a cook error and labelled exact contact.

**Nav validation:** Recast's candidate is an approximation. Check its source/collider hash, agent parameters, tile borders and portal coincidence to the existing 0.05 m limit. Independently test source-ground headroom/step/slope and selected capsule sweeps along fixture routes, including deck versus ground, side rail/underside and shore blocker. Path queries carry tile/obstacle/origin generations; partial/exhausted is not complete. This first route validation is not a mathematical certificate for every possible interior path. A universal continuous free-space/clearance oracle is a real later design/proof obligation, not something voxel size proves.

## Boundary halos and source-patch correspondence

The first landscape source has an explicit 1 m canonical halo beyond each region edge, plus neighbour geometry needed by ray/shape queries. This covers one metre of prepared material/normal filtering and the first static source checks; each consumer declares its actual support. Recast additionally requires its selected border width from its cooker configuration, at least its agent/raster erosion needs; P3 includes capsule radius/skin and actual swept query range; directional shadows retain a separately bounded sunward caster closure. The cooked manifest takes the union. If a later kernel/step/caster range exceeds these supports, it invalidates the preparation and requires a larger declared halo. The number “1 m” is not a universal streaming safety distance.

A's neighbour halo may be leased while B is inactive; B's traversal portal remains closed. At activation of B, both manifests must agree on the boundary record and dependent halo revisions. A request to move beyond a closed frontier is blocked before leaving current swept support. A nav tile's private Detour references and collider handles never supply durable patch identity.

A render point maps to `(GeometryId,ObjectId,PatchId,PatchRevision,local coordinate)` through its product correspondence. Landscape local XZ is unique within the patch; roof/bridge local face coordinates are unique within their separate patches. Full and coarse render triangles reconstruct that attachment without sharing their runtime vertex IDs. The first LOD switch resets correspondence/history for switched triangles unless current/history source-point reconstruction is actually implemented. A correct source attachment does not automatically provide a valid prior screen point. Atlas repack/triangle reorder leaves response cell identity unchanged.

On an explicit source migration, matched response cells transfer **mass**, then divide by new true area. A source patch topology split needs reviewed overlap/ownership weights that partition every donor mass exactly once, including explicit removed/exported mass. New material capacity must admit retained absorbed mass or reject migration; silently clamping it loses material. First physical edits use stop/restart and old-save compatibility refusal, so this broader migration is not presumed implemented. An atlas-only visual recook is a different case and preserves all physical state.

## First vertical-rain reservoir and handoff

The E1 exposure cook partitions the inland horizontal weather footprint into disjoint first-hit roof/canopy/ground/pond pieces, plus explicit exported/no-receiver pieces. There is no canopy in the first content. For static solid faces, project upper-facing source triangles into XZ, intersect them with response/weather cells, and split overlap polygons along triangle boundaries and equal-height lines; the highest valid surface is the first receiver. Vertical walls have zero direct vertical-rain projected area. The pond's fixed horizontal interface footprint is another receiver; its entire admitted stage interval must preserve that first-hit order. Roof/bridge undersides and the ground beneath them cannot also receive direct rain. Tie ownership on measure-zero edges uses stable receiver ID order; shared edges must not acquire finite duplicate area.

`ExposureProductV1` carries G, exposure revision, source/receiver topology hashes, weather-footprint revision, water-domain/model revision, pond stage interval `[H_min,H_max]`, permitted static-object transforms, projected receiver polygons/weights and residual sink. For every weather-cell footprint, nonnegative receiver/export weights sum to one within the recorded area-arithmetic bound. Decoding checks complete, nonoverlapping coverage; missing cover is unavailable. A stage outside that interval, moved roof, changed pond footprint or mismatched geometry invalidates the product **before** E1 writes any next-state stores. Dynamic wet-front/tide first-hit recomputation is a separate W2/water/surface mechanism and is not silently filled by a stale mask. The water owner must stage its proposed next stage and all deposits together; if next state would violate the exposure range, reject the candidate step or enter the declared blocked/fault policy without partially debiting surface donors.

Shared receiver references are `SurfaceReceiver { GeometryId,ObjectId,PatchId,PatchRevision,ResponseCellId }`, `WaterReceiver { DomainId,ModelRevision,ReceiverRevision }` or `ExternalSink { SinkId }`. A deposit carries world/source/forcing revisions, exposure revision, interval tick and mass kg; a delayed transfer additionally carries `(DrainEdgeId, producer_tick, bounded_sequence)`. Runtime slots cannot substitute for these IDs. The same interface is used headlessly. The finite pond's substrate is an impermeable liner in this proof; no unmodeled absorption from pond to underlying soil occurs. A material requiring that exchange needs an admitted transfer operator or is unsupported.

Surface state stores **mass** `M_f,M_a` in f64 kg per response cell, plus true area A in m² from the geometry product. Densities exposed to material response are `m_f=M_f/A`, `m_a=M_a/A`. For each interval, integrate the piecewise-linear precipitation exactly over time and clipped footprint area; `M_rain=P_integrated × A_projected`. Do not multiply the result by A again. The selected pond receives liquid through its finite source register as `Δh=ΔM/(ρ A_water_cell)` with zero horizontal source momentum, under the E1 owner's stated source-split/substep order; solid precipitation is unsupported. Contact deposits already committed for this update enter once. First synthetic recipes use nonnegative authored absorption/evaporation rates and film-retention thresholds; their numerical values and response curves are pinned fixture input, not a claim about real stone/soil.

The first finite material operator is:

1. Add interval direct deposits and receipts from previously queued transfer IDs to `M_f`.
2. Transfer `ΔM_abs=min(M_f, max(0,C_a A-M_a), a A Δt)` from film to absorbed liquid.
3. Evaporate `min(M_f,e_f A Δt)` and `min(M_a,e_a A Δt)` to the named external vapour sink. No thermal-energy accuracy is claimed by these prescribed rates.
4. Release `max(0,M_f-r A) × (1-exp(-Δt/τ))` along the donor's declared drainage edges. Debit the donor once, partition by normalized nonnegative weights and append an identified next-tick transit record. Newly received water is not forwarded through arbitrarily many cells during the same tick.
5. Publish the entire surface next state, transfer queue and sink/exchange ledger with X[n+1]. The pond/water owner consumes its matching admitted receipts through the joint commit; it never receives water whose donor failed to commit.

An edge to another patch or finite pond remains material in a bounded in-flight store until delivery; external exports are cumulative named sink entries. `ΣM_f+ΣM_a+M_transit+M_finite_water+exports−external_inputs` is the global material balance for the chosen assembly. Internal receipt cannot count again as external rain. For the imposed sea, transfers terminate in its external-reservoir ledger; no equal simulated sea storage is claimed. If the finite pond provider cannot reserve its incoming records or accept the proposed stage, the shared tick candidate is unavailable/fails before any donor write. A later render particle can illustrate the transfer but is not the receiving mass authority.

P3 uses committed X[n] material response for tick n→n+1. Its contact events enter the next designated E1 update once; it cannot see partly updated film. V3 reads the same committed densities/material ID with a declared interpolation policy; synthetic traction and gloss curves are checked for consistent conditions, not numerically equal values. Rain off sets future direct input to zero while queued transfers, drainage and evaporation continue. Turning rain drawing off changes none of those physical quantities.

## W2 activation, ordinary step and typed outcomes

A proposed state machine is `unrequested → products_verified → consumer_prepared → overlay_restored → ready_to_commit → active → entry_closed → retiring → inactive`. Failure at any staging state releases candidate leases and preserves the current active run/previous save. F4 availability is not W2 active state.

**Off-world prepare:** resolve one build manifest and source binding; verify every product's bytes/hash/schema, G, patch correspondence, feature/error certificates, neighbour/halo revisions and physical provider compatibility. Reserve decoded products, consumer construction, states/queues/readers and GPU upload/pipelines at their simultaneous peak. Build P3 collider objects and P5 tile data in unpublished consumer candidates; initialize E1 response/pond/provider state from the matching save or explicit new-run values. Prepare render/shadow instances, material products and demanded pipelines for the device generation separately. Candidate IDs/handles are not visible in live queries.

**Pre-tick commit:** recheck world/build/region/active-set/origin epochs and readiness; if any new dependent appeared, include/reprepare or abort. W1 publishes the new region ownership, P3/P5/E1 candidates, source/product bindings and overlay together under one coordinator barrier. P5 traversal/region entry opens last. GPU work publishes only through the corresponding V1/V2 extraction/device barrier. A local view still loading cannot counterfeit physical readiness or halt an independent headless authority. A body remains on old support until either the new closure is active or movement is explicitly blocked.

**Ordinary tick:** F1 supplies one common interval. P3 reads X[n]/Q[n], P5 reads its validated corridor/generations, E1 samples interval forcing and previous receipts, and consumers stage their next results. Validate exposure/stage range, incoming/outgoing queue admission and once-only transfers before the common commit. V2 extracts immutable committed state for both views. V3 combines solid visibility and cloud transmittance at actual 3D receivers once, using the shared light/source time. The roof's geometric shadow/exposure does not multiply cloud attenuation into albedo. P7 captures a separate immutable save state only at a committed boundary.

| Proposed result | Meaning and observable behavior |
| --- | --- |
| `SourceAccepted { S, root_generation }` | Durable source admission succeeded under F3 adapter guarantee. Does not imply cook, physical/view readiness or saved play state |
| `CookReady { product_hashes, certificate_hashes }` | Complete verified candidate bytes exist. Does not open traversal |
| `Active { world_epoch,region_epoch,tick,build_binding }` | Required physical closure and matching overlay committed. Local `ViewReady` additionally names device/pipeline/extraction epochs |

These are semantic distinctions to implement using existing F1/F3/F4/W2 error/publication conventions, not a second generic result framework.

## P7 save, unload and source edit walkthrough

1. At committed tick n, P7 reserves a purpose-specific capture and records S/build/manifest, geometry/patch/provider revisions, region overlay ancestry, cell film/absorbed masses, pending transfers and applied receipt cursors, water continuation state required by its provider, capsule stable IDs/poses/state and source rate segment/phase. Texture atlases, GPU buffers, render LODs, Detour pointers/private poly refs and transient searches are excluded. P5 saves intent/goal and revalidates paths after restore.
2. Serialize from that immutable capture while the world continues only if the capture/old reader leases fit. A failed serialization/root transaction preserves the previous save. A completed save is a new durable save root, separate from source admission. Replaying pending transfers after restore must not redeliver an already applied receipt.
3. Unload closes A entry/portals first and invalidates affected corridors. Existing capsule support, traversals, pond/drain dependencies, save readers and submitted GPU work pin required products. The first fixture pins coupled surface/pond/transfer state while it is physically active; it does not freeze half of a drainage graph. When the complete small run is stopped and no live physical dependents remain, persist the endpoint and retire consumer state, then release leases after CPU readers/fences. GPU eviction alone does not unload authoritative liquid.
4. Restore verifies the exact compatible old build/provider/source closure, reconstructs physical and required consumer products off-world, restores masses/queue/cursors and commits as above before queries become available. No renderer is needed for that operation. Offline elapsed time does not imply rainfall catch-up; F1's pause/run policy is explicit and the first fixture resumes at its saved simulation endpoint.
5. Edit the bank samples, move the roof or alter material capacity in ordinary project files. Admit a new immutable source S2 using F3's expected-generation/writer handoff. A watcher merely reports pending changes. The cook invalidates all changed G-dependent collider/bed/render/shadow/nav/exposure/patch-area/placement outputs and both sides of affected halos. A material colour-only change can reuse geometry content through its new build binding; it cannot secretly change absorption/friction.
6. First physical source changes stop and restart under the selected baseline. An old save still binds S1; loading it against S2 returns `MigrationRequired` unless an explicit migration has been selected/proved. Keep S1 products/save pins recoverable. A failed S2 cook leaves S1 runnable and the admitted S2 draft inspectable; no half-new geometry appears in S1. Live physical source migration and generic geometric correspondence remain later commissions.

## First fixture oracles and tests to implement independently

Every row below is a proposed full interaction check. The later [offline
geometry certificate](../../evidence/terrain/T1-FINAL-TRIANGLE-CERTIFICATE-2026-11-03.md)
runs the vertical/planar-bank and protected-sill subsets on one synthetic
source/product; the remaining consumer and assembled rows are unrun. A checker
sharing the candidate's wrong triangulation/receiver omission is not
independent. Retain fixture source/build hashes and report exact artifact
identity in any later proof.

| Check and independent formulation | Concrete expected result and rejection |
| --- | --- |
| Geometry decode/topology | Manually specified bridge deck/underside/ground ray hits at identical XZ must remain distinct; full-source triangles use the declared diagonal. Reject triangle-order IDs, wrong floor, degenerate faces or omitted holes |
| Vertical error certificate | Independent exact-rational checker versus candidate cooker; include a thin one-cell ridge, diagonal crossover and a coarse fan whose centre differs from the initial diagonal. Reject a sampled-only “max”, an unverified final triangulation or a certificate referring to another product hash |
| Analytic planar coast | Pure 1:20-plane subfixture gives shore `x=20H`, wet area `64(20H+32)` m² and volume `640(H+1.6)^2` m³ for the unclipped level range. Compare source, bed, render and independent formula; inject known vertical bias to verify the horizontal-error gate actually fails |
| Shelf/sill topology | Source crest/protected openings and one-sided critical-level component graph are the oracle; a deliberately decimated sill must fail even if RMS height/capacity error is small. Compare wet polygon area/volume, not only one moving edge pixel |
| Rain area/occlusion | Hand-built roof/pond/ground rectangles/triangles provide closed-form projected fractions and total `P×area×Δt`; bridge underside/vertical wall are no-direct-deposit controls. Reject full roof rain plus full ground rain, slope-area double multiplication, missing weights interpreted as zero or stale-stage acceptance |
| Reservoir/exchange | Independent scalar spreadsheet/analytic constant-rate cases for absorption saturation, evaporation, exponential drainage and one-tick transit; zero-input source-off and repeated receipt IDs. Track local and total mass, not only total. Initial candidate balance tolerance proposed `1e-9×max(1 kg,total supplied mass)` plus a recorded arithmetic bound; qualification may reject the arithmetic/limit but cannot hide mass loss with visual fades |
| P3/P5 consumer agreement | Controlled capsule stands/walks under/on bridge, near bank and at closed region boundary; compare achieved support/contact/clearance with direct source triangle queries. Recast portal mismatch, stale corridor, partial path or exhausted node budget must stop at a safe frontier rather than be declared complete |
| Two views/history | Perspective main plus orthographic overview, then swap LOD/skip/resize/rebase one view. Check source material/response cell identities and query outputs unchanged; histories keyed independently. Unknown prior attachment resets instead of a fabricated zero velocity. Shadow/probe caster coverage is tested outside the main frustum |
| Atomic lifecycle/adverse products | Remove one collider/bed/exposure/nav tile/pipeline, corrupt a hash, change a halo/source revision, complete an old job after restart, fail cook, hold a save reader and GPU fence. Each stage must preserve old world/save or report unavailable/loading; no partial floor/wetness/state publication |
| Save/unload/source edit | Save while transfers are in flight; stop, restore exact S1 and compare masses/cursors/physical queries. Attempt S2 restore without migration and verify refusal. Old reader holds bytes until release; release/fence completion permits retirement, not earlier |

Numerical source/product/reference checks precede ordinary-motion visual acceptance. For images/motion inspect a close grazing shore, under-bridge floor, roof drip boundary, wet versus absorbed material tail, geometric shadow and cloud-shadow receiver position, then native-resolution crops and uninterrupted motion from both views. Reject visible contact separation, silhouette/material popping outside the declared envelope or rain appearing under shelter without a causal transfer even when all hashes/ledger totals pass. Physically plausible material calibration remains separate from agreement with the fixture's synthetic curves.

## Whole-scene cost, bounded work and first build order

This is a bounded geometry/response fixture inside the [whole-engine envelope](../BASELINE-SPEC.md#budget-allocation-and-failure-rules), not a terrain allocation or capacity claim. One 257² source region has 66,049 samples, about 258 KiB of i32 heights, 131,072 reference triangles, about 1.50 MiB of u32 indices and about 774 KiB of f32 XYZ vertices. P3, water, render and CPU reference data may share immutable allocations only where their encodings/lifetimes actually alias; count independent copies where they do not. A full-reference draw in two views plus shadow passes multiplies submitted triangles, not authoritative terrain updates. Terrain textures, collider BVH, Recast output, certificates, halos, upload staging and old/new versions are additional.

At 1 m response cells, 4096 cells × two f64 masses is 64 KiB per endpoint, 128 KiB for current/staged endpoints before area/material/queue/reader storage; 64 mesh cells add at most 1 KiB per endpoint. A third save reader is another owner unless shared immutable storage genuinely avoids copying. Transit capacity is finite: eight authored edges, at most one coalesced mass record per edge/next-tick interval and a separately bounded receipt cursor; reject before release if those reservations fail. The fixed-footprint precipitation partition is prepared once; constant/piecewise-linear rates integrate once per forcing interval, not once per view/pixel/drop. Cook exact overlays can be expensive but remain offline bounded tasks with count/time/scratch admission and cancellable candidates.

Each 30 Hz callback may owe two 60 Hz world steps. Surface updates scale with active cells/edges/ticks; P3 casts/solves, P5 queries, water updates, source/extraction and all views/shadows share the baseline budgets. Render LOD removes triangle/pass work under its error gate; it cannot lower P3/bed/reservoir fidelity to meet a visual budget. F4 peak reservation includes live state, source/product decoding, candidate consumer structures, retained save/readers, upload and old/new GPU fences. If the new closure exceeds staging/headroom, the first recipe stops/releases old run before loading or presents explicit loading; no uncharged second world is hidden behind “atomic”.

Build order when implementation is commissioned: (1) decode/source bindings and the independent reference/checker; (2) full/coarse products, final-triangle/seam/shore/sill certificates and P3/P5 consumers; (3) static exposure plus bounded material/pond receipt interaction with source-off; (4) staged activation/save/unload/failed-cook/old-lease loop; (5) two-view render/shadow/material/history; (6) assembled cold-load/edit/steady/sustained measurements. The first rendered walkthrough still requires a real V1/V2/V3 host and selected platform adapter; this document cannot pretend those foundations already exist.

## Implementor-invention audit and remaining gaps

| Missing choice encountered | Disposition in this paper walkthrough |
| --- | --- |
| What “same source revision” means for cache reuse | Proposed build binding S→G above; root architecture owner must review before encoding it in F4. Different consumer product hashes remain normal |
| How to find ground with a roof/deck above it | Explicit ray/shape context and source patch identities; water bed selects a named floor. Universal XZ world-height query rejected |
| How to prove terrain approximation rather than sample it | Exact discrete overlay plus output/conversion bound; full-topology mesh correspondence initially. Generic remesh Hausdorff/topology certification remains open |
| What protects a shallow/flat shore and sill | Positive-slope bound only where valid; direct critical-level wet-set/component/area/volume checks and protected source feature geometry elsewhere |
| Where rain hits as shore/pond stage changes | First rain proof uses fixed pond footprint/order and validity interval; stale or crossing-stage product blocks before writes. Moving-shore exposure recomputation is a **joint core gap**, not tuning |
| How runoff reaches its receiver without double use | Stable surface/water receiver refs, delayed finite transit store, joint commit, once-only edge/tick receipt IDs and explicit external sinks. The finite pond operator is E1/water-owned and must meet this handoff |
| Whether visibility-off erases wetness or water | It does not. Required physical consumers/source/state remain; optional renderer products retire through leases/fences |
| What happens to mass after source/material edit | First restore refuses incompatible S2; later explicit migration must conserve donor mass and validate capacity/patch mapping. No automatic nearest-geometry reset |
| Whether nav validation proves all traversable geometry | No. Selected Recast approximation/portal limits plus independent fixture route checks; general continuous clearance certification remains open |
| Whether LOD source attachment proves temporal history | No. First switches reset affected histories until prior-source-point reconstruction is implemented and qualified. Transport mappings/Jacobians are optional later work |
| How to unload a coupled wet region | Pin complete physical support/transfer closure or stop/save the small run. Unqualified aggregates, background climate catch-up and live source swaps remain unsupported |

The first recipe is sufficiently concrete to expose source, product, location, timing and ownership obligations; it is not a claim that every broader terrain capability is build-ready. Exact robust overlay implementation, codec/task integration, the executable binding to the selected finite pond provider, source/cooker determinism, P3/Recast target wrappers, hardware/view memory peaks and ordinary-motion quality remain unimplemented/unrun. Runtime erosion, arbitrary volumetric/mesh joining, dynamic exposure and generic conservative patch migration still require designs beyond this fixture. This author performed the walkthrough/self-check; independent verification belongs to the parent programme. Documentation validation proves only repository consistency.
