# Terrain and surface research — 2 November 2026

**Status:** research recommendation for W2 geometry and E1 surface identity/response; no implementation, numerical, visual or device proof. This supporting record proposes changes to the owning [world/content](../modules/WORLD-CONTENT.md), [environment](../modules/ENVIRONMENT.md) and [water](../modules/WATER.md) designs, under [C01/C02/C05/C07–C10](../CONTRACTS.md). It does not replace those authorities or promote maturity. Scope follows the [architecture brief](../ARCHITECTURE-BRIEF.md), [baseline](../BASELINE-SPEC.md) and [build plan](../../plans/BRIEF.md).

## Independent target recorded before historical code inspection

This section was saved before inspecting Broadsides code in this commission. Existing Thrum family designs were read, so this is independent of the historical implementation, not blind to existing Thrum decisions.

Select heterogeneous world geometry with one source/revision authority and explicit consumer products. Landscapes that are single-valued over XZ may use a tiled heightfield; caves, interiors, vertical walls, bridges and overhangs use authored triangle meshes or explicitly selected volumetric-source extraction. Do not make heightfield queries the world surface API. W2 constructs/activates geometry; F4 builds/delivers immutable products; P3 owns collision representation; P5 owns navigation; V2/V3 own draw, shadow and transport representations. E1 owns mutable material response anchored to source surface patches. Render LOD, cloud drawing, camera count and physics quality cannot create independent ground or wetness authorities.

Required outcomes: common bed/bank/solid geometry and correspondence at shore contacts; supported surface queries for arbitrary 3D geometry; stable material/patch identity through cook and view LOD; distinct geometric and material error bounds; collider/nav clearance and seam validity; deterministic vegetation placement and persistent changes; cloud-shadow receivers at actual 3D positions; precipitation deposition with roof/occlusion support; source-off drying/runoff; transactional source/cook/save/region transitions; two views with independent histories and shared physical state; viable whole-scene active sets, staging and regeneration costs. Optional runtime erosion/deformation must rebuild the affected physical closure; ordinary weathering does not imply moving geometry. A small first proof can reduce extent while retaining these boundaries.

Selection before reconciliation: error-bounded tiled heightfield render products plus static mesh products; stable source-patch mapping; conservative per-patch liquid-film/absorbed-liquid/snow reservoirs and named exchange edges where physically requested; prepared runoff routing only for its declared approximation domain. Full advancing dry-slope hydrodynamics, sediment/topology evolution and cross-representation geometric welding are open mechanisms, not tuning tasks. Reject missing support at activation rather than inventing flat ground, dry state or zero flow.

## Source, products and shared authority

Use a geometry-set stable ID and admitted source revision as the root. It declares coordinate frame/metres, support, intended solid/surface role, patch IDs, material assignments, holes, boundaries and optional physical attributes. Mesh triangles are a source discretization, not durable object IDs. Heightfield cell coordinates can identify locations within a fixed source lattice; a resample changes the source revision and needs correspondence. Source functions need bounded parameters and explicit evaluation/support; infinite noise is not an infinite resident world.

Each cooked product carries `(geometry ID, admitted revision, product hash, cooker/schema/profile versions)`, bounds including deformation/displacement, error kind and bound, source-patch correspondence, required neighbours/halos, and surface/material mapping. Rendering has LOD/transition/shadow products; P3 has validated static heightfield/triangle/convex products; P5 has agent-profile nav tiles/portals; water has bed/solid/shore support and capacity information; vegetation has placement/exclusion support; E1 has patch area, exposure and response adjacency. Those are different views of one admitted solid revision. Product hash equality is not a requirement between consumers, but compatible source identity and declared error are.

A general surface query is a ray/shape/contact query with origin/direction/range or explicit surface reference and vertical context. Return a checked geometry/patch ID, source location (triangle+barycentric or chart coordinate under its revision), position/normal/material, support/time/epoch/error, or typed unavailable/ambiguous/outside-support. A convenience landscape `height(x,z)` is a requested capability. At a bridge/cave stack it cannot select physical ground without context. Water's single graph bed may use a selected floor layer only within its domain; unsupported vertical layering rejects the domain rather than flattening the cave roof into its bed.

E1 persistent response attaches to `(object or geometry ID, patch ID, response schema/revision, patch-local cell)`. Shared mesh instances need object-local response unless the game explicitly shares a reservoir. Render triangles carry source patch/chart mapping; morphing interpolates position while sampling the same source attachment. Atlas repacking changes only an acceleration/texture mapping, not saved identity. Overlapping UVs used for ordinary material tiling cannot store unique physical wetness. A separate unique response chart or patch-cell data is required. Chart cells store true surface area/Jacobian; XZ projected area fails on walls and steep slopes.

Material IDs describe authored composition; E1 state describes changing condition; V3 closure describes shading. Contact response is a declared P3 material law keyed by material/condition, never inferred from RGB/normal maps. Blend regions need a game policy (e.g. dominant solid contact or an explicit calibrated mixture); normalized shading weights do not prove friction mixtures. Hard material/contact boundaries and small cliffs/sills can be locked during simplification. Texture normals/displacement below a declared render residual bound do not move collider/nav/bed. A displacement larger than the supported contact envelope must become geometry and rebuild the consumers.

## Error, seams and topology

Separate source approximation, quantization, consumer proxy, origin conversion, render residual and temporal errors. For two compatible representations, triangle inequality gives `e_AB ≤ e_A_source + e_B_source + e_coordinate`; a visually close render proxy is insufficient if the collider already spends the entire tolerance. Units are metres for position, radians for normals, area/volume for coverage/capacity, pixels for a particular view. Each bound names its support and norm; RMS or a few sampled maxima are not supremum bounds.

For a discrete piecewise-linear heightfield reference and piecewise-linear product, overlay their triangulations in XZ. Their height difference is linear on every overlay triangle, so its maximum absolute value occurs at an overlay vertex. Testing all such vertices supplies an exact vertical bound up to stated arithmetic tolerance. Merely testing original grid vertices misses diagonal crossovers. For analytic sources, sparse samples need a derivative/Lipschitz remainder bound or interval refinement; otherwise label them sampled evidence and preserve unqualified extrema. An error threshold at maximum refinement depth is a request, not guaranteed success: refine further, retain the reference, or fail cook with the achieved bound. For arbitrary meshes use independently bounded adaptive distance checks plus protected features/topology/clearance tests; simplifier output metrics alone remain estimates.

At a smooth shoreline `g(x,z)=bed(x,z)-water(x,z)=0`, a local normal-to-contour displacement estimate is `|δs| ≲ (e_bed+e_water)/|∇g|`, provided the slope remains bounded away from zero through the uncertainty band. Thus 2 cm total vertical mismatch on a 1:20 bank permits roughly 40 cm shoreline movement. A flat shelf/sill has no useful bound from this formula: small vertical changes can merge puddles, flood a large area or open a channel. Preserve/certify sill elevations and connected components and measure wet area/capacity over the required level interval. GPU water residuals must taper or be masked by compatible geometry near contact; a shader-only clip at a different bed is another shoreline.

For a heightfield reference/proxy over projected area `A`, a uniform vertical bound `e` yields a fixed-level capacity error at most `A e` (integrating the Lipschitz positive-part function); topology/flux errors still need separate tests. Near narrow channels compare width/clearance and sidewall occupancy, not just capacity. For a collision proxy pair each bounded by `e`, inflate exclusion/clearance margins by the sum of relevant uncertainties. P5 must budget rasterization, contour simplification, collision proxy and placement error against agent radius/headroom/climb; do not advertise the raster cell size as the full safety margin. Nav rasterization can retain multiple spans, but requires explicit portals/layers to distinguish floors.

Tile boundaries are canonical shared edge records in source coordinates with a single owner and hash. Height/normal/filter/response halos refer to exact neighbour revisions. Neighbour LOD differences are constrained (initially ≤1) and compatible stitch indices/morph boundaries use those same anchors. Boundary changes invalidate both sides, their halo-dependent products and any cross-boundary water/nav/response edges. Skirts are an optional far render crack cover: they add false vertical geometry, affect shadows and cannot certify a traversable/collider/water seam. For mesh/heightfield joins author a common welded boundary or an explicit overlap with certified cover/exclusion and one physical owner; generic welding of arbitrary independently decimated meshes is an open cook feature, not promised automatically.

Render selection uses perspective `e_px ≈ e_m f_px/z_nearest` with conservative nearest depth and near-plane handling, or orthographic `e_px ≈ e_m s_px_per_m`; include projection/orientation/silhouette effects in qualification. A triangle selected for the main camera may be insufficient for a Sun cascade, probe or second view. Select/validate caster geometry by shadow texel footprint and protected silhouette; extend caster relevance sunward beyond receiver view bounds. Shared meshes may satisfy the strictest active view, or independently draw LOD products, with both costs charged. Hysteresis and morph bands reduce popping; topology/material discontinuities reset history where correspondence is absent. No camera LOD changes P3, P5, water storage or reservoir cells.

## Surface reservoirs, forcing and response

The physical response option stores true-area densities `m_f` surface film, `m_a` absorbed liquid and `m_s` snow/ice in kg/m², with material capacity `C_a` and physical patch area `A_i`. A distinct `m_f/m_a` split is a proposed refinement of C09's liquid reservoir, with `m_l=m_f+m_a`; it is not an extra copy of water. Optional deposit/soil species get their own mass stores only when their transport is actually modeled. A simple authored wetness parameter remains a valid cheaper provider but cannot report conserved liquid volume.

For interval Δt, use integrated masses in kg rather than mixing rates/area conventions:

```text
Δ(A_i m_f,i) = rain_i + contact_i + melt_i + desorb_i
               - freeze_i - absorb_i - evaporate_f,i - drain_i
               + Σ(edge_in - edge_out)
Δ(A_i m_a,i) = absorb_i - desorb_i - evaporate_a,i
Δ(A_i m_s,i) = snow_i + freeze_i - melt_i - sublimate_i - remove_i
```

Internal absorption/desorption/freezing/melting cancel when summing stores. Each edge has one signed interval transfer and one stable event/edge ID; the donor loses exactly what the receiver gains at commit. Water receives runoff mass/ρ as volume through a named receiving domain, at its declared contact location and interval; any associated momentum/energy or deposited solute needs its own payload/law. Absorption is not disappearance into an unnamed ground sink. If groundwater infiltration is outside the selected model it is a separately logged external export.

For the proposed bounded authored material-rate provider, saturate absorption by both available film and `A(C_a-m_a)` and by material rate `a_i Δt`; cap evaporation/removal by donor availability, and phase transfers by prescribed temperature-dependent rate and donor mass. Deterministic operator order is precipitation/contact admission → phase transfer → absorption/desorption → evaporation → edge drainage; later operators see the staged local stores, while P3 still sees X[n]. Document this splitting error and measure Δt refinement. Multiple outgoing requests share a single donor scaling when their sum exceeds available mass, preserving nonnegativity and ratios; process edges from the same staged state rather than sequentially forwarding newly received water arbitrarily far in one tick. This is a complete conservative bucket/rate mechanism for authored response, not a shallow-water momentum solver or a validated dry-front model.

A minimal authored drainage graph prepares nonnegative downhill weights `w_ij` summing to one and residence time τ_i; outflow is the available surplus film above retention `r_i`, multiplied by `1-exp(-Δt/τ_i)`, then distributed by weights. Terminal edges name a surface patch, finite water receiver or external drain. Flat basins must have explicit retention/overflow topology; no invented downhill direction. The operator has finite per-tick handoff and source-off tails, but its path timing depends on patch layout and authored τ. It cannot claim advancing dry-slope sheet flow, backwater, pressure-driven uphill flow, capillary spreading or moving flood boundaries. Those remain the water family's open physical mechanism. Do not quietly enable this approximation for a game requesting such behavior.

Rain must define its reference area. The initial deposition operator follows the [environment-source proposal](ENVIRONMENT-SOURCE-RESEARCH-2026-11-02.md): vertical liquid/solid water-equivalent flux per horizontal footprint, partitioned by cooked first-hit roof/canopy/ground/water receiver weights. These nonnegative weights, any explicit sink and any modeled in-flight storage sum to one. Each receiver gets its share of the interval-integrated footprint mass; do not multiply by sloped surface area again. Convert that received mass to true-area reservoir density only after assignment. Roofs/canopies can retain water and release identified later drip flux, but cannot also pass the original full rain immediately to ground. Dynamic blockers need updated exposure or an explicitly admitted no-occlusion approximation. Visual rain particles may drift with wind, but neither their direction, count nor culling determines initial physical deposition. Vertical walls receive no direct vertical-rain deposit; their drip/runoff needs a named transfer. A snow provider additionally needs compaction/coverage and phase rules; present rate equations alone do not claim drifting snow.

Arbitrary angled fallout is a separate future operator and proof. A candidate represents precipitation mass-flux vector `j` in kg/(m²·s), pointing with the falling material, and deposits `A max(0,-j·n) Δt` on a surface with outward normal n, subject to a qualified directional first-hit/transfer partition. A meteorological horizontal rate `P_h` with drop velocity v can give `j=P_h v/|v_y|` only within a declared nonzero vertical-speed regime. That local formula alone does not supply the cross-surface exposure partition, interception or transfer law. No angled wall deposition, splash rebound or multilayer porous interception is admitted by the initial vertical operator.

Thermal input is prescribed E1 temperature/rate initially. A stronger energy provider would need absorbed short/longwave radiation, emission, sensible/latent exchange and conductive/substrate closure, and `L_f Δm`/`L_v Δm` energy bookkeeping for phase/evaporation. Cloud attenuation affects shared irradiance, but a render shadow map is not headless thermal authority. Retain the named authored-rate fallback without an energy-accuracy claim. Different soil/stone/metal coefficients and hysteresis can map `(m_f,m_a,m_s,T,material)` to coverage, roughness, darkening and traction; calibrate each response. Wet materials do not universally darken or reduce friction. P3 receives a declared contact response at X[n]; V3 receives physical condition plus the selected material closure and applies illumination exactly once.

Cloud-shadow receiving is a V3 lighting query at the actual 3D position, shared source/time/light direction and origin epoch. A 2D authored map declares its receiver plane/height/Sun regime. Projecting an XZ map indiscriminately onto mountains, cave walls and bridges is outside that regime. Let V3 combine solid visibility and cloud transmittance under C08; E1 does not multiply a second cloud darkening into albedo. Geometric occlusion and cloud extinction are different factors. If cloud drawing is off while attenuation/thermal queries remain, keep their source and required projection but retire visible-cloud work. A provider absent everywhere yields the explicit clear identity; missing/stale required coverage is a typed unavailable/fallback policy, not silently clear.

Runtime erosion is a different authority change. Offline erosion cooks a chosen final bed plus masks/deposits as source content. To claim active erosion, connect bedrock/soil mass removal → entrained sediment store → transport → deposition → geometry displacement/topology, with density/porosity, a closure for erosion/deposition and stable positive fluxes. A solid-height change `Δb` over area A implies a material-volume change `A Δb`, with dry solid mass depending on density/porosity; a wetness scalar cannot supply it. W2 must publish new collider/bed/nav/placement/surface correspondence together, and E1 migrate material stores conservatively. No runtime sediment/geometry model is selected here. Its missing laws/recook admission are core design gaps if that capability is commissioned; ordinary weathering/stain maps are a separate optional visual outcome.

## Authoring, lifetime and operating cases

Proposed authoring schemas/tools below are design obligations, not existing `./dev` or SDK commands. A creator can import a height master/mesh/blockout, attach region/source/patch/material IDs, add authored road/river/bank curves and holes, choose water support/response profiles, and provide vegetation/exclusion masks. Show units, quantization, source bounds, resolved surface identities and actual consumer proxies together before admission. Curves modifying a river cannot alter water bed without the solid/collider counterparts. External tools retain editable recipes/masters; import flattens only the declared product, never the only recoverable source. A reproducible cook records exact tool versions, settings, dependencies and any nondeterminism; retain selected masters/evidence under repository lifecycle rules.

**Shore, cave and two bodies.** Admit a beach tile, mesh rock/overhang and finite pool support with one geometry revision. Cook P3 collision, P5 walkable ground, E1 true-area patches, water bed/occupancy and V2 draw/shadow proxies. Check shore displacement/area over all admitted levels, sill connectivity and clearances. Tick n queries X[n] for traction/water forces; P3 integrates bodies and emits identified contact/deposit facts for the next E1 update. E1 applies prior admitted facts once, evolves surface/water states and stages X[n+1]. Render interpolates committed state and its bounded residual; bank geometry clips the same interface within its declared error. Test both overhead and low grazing first-person views. A reflection may see a bank offscreen; retain that geometry/visibility support. Entering the cave selects its actual floor/volume context; an unsupported graph-water layer rejects activation. A foam edge hiding a 40 cm bed disagreement fails this case.

**Storm, shelter and source-off.** Vertical precipitation is partitioned over horizontal footprints into first-hit roof/canopy/ground/water receivers. Roof film saturates/overflows along named edges; sheltered ground receives only identified later drip/runoff, not the original full rain twice. A vertical wall receives no direct deposit from this operator; any wall wetting needs a named transfer. Stone with small absorption retains film/gloss; porous ground stores absorbed liquid with a longer drying tail. P3 contacts read the same committed material conditions with declared one-tick lag. Cloud attenuation changes direct lighting once; a headless source supplies prescribed thermal/rate forcing independently of view pixels. Stop rain: accumulated film drains, absorbed liquid dries, in-flight named transfers finish. Hidden rain rendering leaves all this physical work intact. Removing the physical response provider must reject a retained traction consumer or select an admitted authored substitute; toggling drawing alone cannot erase mass. Angled deposition and splash rebound remain separate future operators/proofs.

**Cross-region travel and unload.** Relevance is the union of authoritative actor/contact/nav demand, physical water/drainage dependency closure, active views and Sun/probe caster support. A region cannot unload a bed/edge still leased by an active pool, a nav portal under a path or a required runoff receiver. W2 stages required products and surface state before opening the frontier. Absent readiness holds movement/load visibly; no flat/dry fallback. A remote view adds visual relevance without advancing authoritative weather twice. When a patch truly becomes inactive, choose one declared policy: continue its physical support at a reduced but qualified rate, transfer to a qualified aggregate, or freeze with recorded last-time/forcing history and catch up before queries become usable. Catch-up is only analytic for supported piecewise-constant rates/uncoupled stores; coupled runoff/nonlinear overflow cannot be reconstructed from the last rain value. Pin active coupling or require bounded replay/loading. Stream cells do not become the water/response solver boundaries.

**Source edit, cook failure and save.** Edit a bank curve and roof material. Admit immutable source bytes under F3/F4; invalidate both bank halo sides, water capacity/topology, affected collider/nav/response/placement and render/shadow products; the material change also invalidates response-law and shading dependencies. Cook into a candidate closure with new hashes. Failed seam/clearance/error checks leave the old ready run usable. First portable R0/R2 follows the baseline stop/load/restart path after releasing old world/provider leases; it does not promise live replacement. A wider live recipe must reserve old/new peak owners and stage all required dependencies before one W1 barrier commit, generation-check stale jobs and close/reprepare if new dependents appeared. Publish the new geometry/material/state mapping atomically or reject; never move the drawn bank while the collider remains old.

Save `(geometry/admitted revision, provider/response schema, patch IDs/cells, true-area stores, last tick, pending transfers and once-only ledger)` at a committed C02/C10 boundary; GPU atlases, mesh LOD and cloud histories are rebuildable. Restore matching support before exposing P3/water/response queries. Atlas-only recook preserves attachment. Source topology edits require explicit old→new patch correspondence: conservative overlap weights redistribute **mass**, with each old mass partition summing to one including explicitly removed/exported material. Missing/ambiguous patches reject migration or require an explicit reset/new-run decision; nearest triangle to old position may jump from cave floor to roof. Changed patch area changes density after mass transfer, not total mass. Two concurrent saves/readers keep old leases charged until retired.

**Two views and quality change.** A first-person player and orthographic overview read the same immutable X[n]/X[n+1] and source revision. Each has its own origin/projection/previous geometry/LOD morph/temporal invalidation. Reuse source and ready tile bytes; charge separate extraction lists, shadows when coverage differs, histories and pixel passes. A secondary view every other frame uses the actual sample time and history validity rather than an implicit previous-frame assumption. Reducing render detail removes distant triangles/material texture residency or optional histories while collider/nav/water/response fidelity remains unchanged. A high-frequency render displacement switch invalidates velocity/history where correspondence is absent. A second camera must not rebuild a whole forest or duplicate physical reservoirs.

## Whole-scene cost and quality account

Counts are analytical estimates, not measured capacity. [BASELINE-SPEC](../BASELINE-SPEC.md#budget-allocation-and-failure-rules) remains the sole allocation authority. Its browser/mobile R2 first recipe preloads authoritative gameplay and uses stopped replacement; live streaming is a separate gate. The 60 Hz tick may run twice in one 30 Hz callback. Geometry work competes with actors, skinning, water, weather, UI, audio, network and all active views; a terrain-only millisecond budget would spend the same envelope twice.

Illustrative 512 m square at 1 m spacing has 513² = 263,169 height samples: f32 heights alone are about 1.00 MiB; a 2D full mip pyramid approaches 4/3 of the base. Four byte weights add roughly another 1.00 MiB before mips/halos. A full unindexed f32 position/normal/UV grid is much larger; regular vertex reconstruction avoids storing XZ and repeated normals. Two triangles per cell give 524,288 triangles **before** caves, objects, shadow passes or vegetation, already above R2's 500k visible-triangle fixture. LOD/culling is required, not optional after a detailed bake. A second view can reuse resident samples but not its fragment/visibility work.

Illustrative response at 1 m patch-cell area over the same 512² m² stores 262,144 cells; three f32 water stores alone are 3 MiB, two staged endpoints 6 MiB. Areas, routing, materials, snow/thermal fields, events, snapshots and allocator overhead are additional. At 0.25 m resolution counts grow ×16: two endpoints of only these three stores reach 96 MiB, exceeding R2's complete 76.8 MiB world-state share before any actors/water. Sparse allocation helps only where activity/support is actually sparse; wet weather over the whole region defeats that assumption. This rules out universal fine response grids and requires explicit coarse/near/optional response profiles with visual/traction/error qualification. No per-view copy of physical cells is acceptable.

For a source height quantization range R and b-bit uniform integer, rounding contributes at most `R/[2(2^b-1)]` vertical error. A 2 km range at 16 bits costs about 1.53 cm before interpolation/proxy errors, already roughly 30.5 cm horizontal shoreline uncertainty on a 1:20 bank. Choose tile-local datum/range or f32/greater precision for critical shore products. f32 local conversion contributes roughly `2^-24 |p-O|`; carry origin epoch and reserve its portion of the error envelope. Half-float absolute precision depends on magnitude and cannot be certified by a single near-zero test.

Streaming lookahead needs at least `v_max × (fetch+decode+consumer preparation+publication latency)` plus braking/query support and safety margin; teleport uses explicit loading unless complete destination closure is ready. Multiple remote cameras can make the relevance union nearly additive. A shared archive is not a shared working set. Whole peak is `live + staged replacements + old reader/save leases + decode/upload scratch + consumer rebuilds + reserve`; charge CPU/GPU allocations once in the physical ledger and report logical claims separately. Replacement memory must fit before starting work.

Reduction order: eliminate duplicate source evaluations/copies/uploads, reuse static products and bounds with correct invalidation, coarsen distant render geometry/textures/optional shadow detail under projected/silhouette error, reduce optional response presentation resolution, then select a cheaper **declared** physical profile only at a compatible restart/migration boundary. Never lower physical water/traction semantics just because the viewport shrank. Validate ordinary-motion bank silhouettes, horizon/normal aliasing, material transitions, shadow contacts and wetting tails separately from numerical conservation. Measure integrated CPU critical path, work, GPU passes, memory peaks, cold cook/load and sustained transitions on named targets before promoting any tier.

## Proposed owning-document and shared-contract changes

Return these to the architecture owner for sequential integration; this commission edits only this research record. Existing contracts already state many owners, so add narrow clarifications rather than a duplicate terrain service framework.

1. **C05, W2/F4 with P3/P5/E1/V2/water:** geometry products must identify the same admitted source geometry revision, source-patch/material correspondence, support/boundary dependencies and declared error **kind**, bound and approximation domain. Activation/replacement stages the required consumer closure atomically. A render skirt or sampled threshold does not establish physical seam validity. Attach dependent water capacity, nav portals, response and placement invalidation to the same closure.
2. **C09, E1 with W2/P3/water:** split physical liquid internally into film and absorbed stores when the selected material response requires it; expose total liquid semantics without double ownership. Declare true-surface versus projected precipitation reference area, exposure producer, once-only patch/water runoff edges, units, committed time and unavailable behavior. Source absence/provider-off policy preserves retained consumers and state tails.
3. **C09 error handoff with C05:** water bed/contact/render proxies carry absolute positional and shoreline/coverage limits at the admitted interaction scale. A height bound converts to shoreline displacement only where the bed-water slope has a declared positive lower bound; flat shelves, sills and connected wet areas need direct topology/area/capacity qualification. A missing bed/support is unavailable, never dry/flat/calm.
4. **C07/C08, V2/V3:** view LOD and shadow caster/receiver products preserve source surface attachment; each view owns projection/history, while physical state remains shared. Cloud visibility is applied by transport once at the actual 3D receiver, with receiver-plane/height validity exposed for 2D approximations. Do not add cloud shadow directly into material albedo.
5. **C10, P7/F3/T1 with W2/E1:** saves pin geometry/provider/response revisions and patch identities; topology/resample migrations require explicit correspondence, conservative material-mass redistribution or explicit reset/restart. Atlas packing, render triangle order and ephemeral LOD IDs cannot be save keys. Preserve ledger and in-flight transfer identity across unload/restore.
6. **WORLD-CONTENT / ENVIRONMENT / maturity:** add TS01–TS09 capability/proof references and specific supported landscape/mesh products, response profiles and their open mechanisms. Retain unbuilt runtime/quality/device statuses. No new crate or workspace dependency is warranted by this research.

### Revised selection and proof consequences

The recent alternatives preserve the heterogeneous source/product architecture while widening credible internal choices. **Add multi-layer height columns as a distinct optional source family**, alongside single-valued heightfields, authored meshes and selected SDF/volume sources. They can be more economical for layered geology with frequent edits, but have bounded layer count/orientation/precision and still need non-render queries and error. For a fully deforming-column game, direct rays over that source may be preferable to rebuilding triangles every frame; that is a game/profile selection rather than a universal terrain API.

**Add analytic/parametric patch tessellation as a candidate render product**, rather than require every smooth source to arrive as all pre-expanded vertices. A stable base-patch ID and local coordinates are an especially natural surface identity. Its displacement/boundary agreement, finite output pool, whole-source error and P3/water proxy remain explicit. Mesh and heightfield product defaults stay reasonable for the initial preloaded portable fixture; none of the reviewed sources justifies replacing every landscape/interior with one universal representation.

**Strengthen C07 correspondence selection for the architecture owner:** carry source instance/patch identity and declared local correspondence across render LODs; preserve partial/unavailable correspondence and use it to invalidate the affected history. Optional transport reuse may need invertible mappings and area Jacobians beyond ordinary motion vectors. Do not assume a per-object same-LOD result extends to independent views or per-ray LOD. Rendering correspondence is also not a license to migrate saved material mass through nearest UV/triangle guesses.

Extend Q1/Q2 with a representation challenge fixture: the same bank, sill, cave underside and sharp material boundary from an ordinary ground-level camera and orthographic overview; compare prepared mesh, height tiles and an optional layered/parametric candidate using source-based coverage/depth/normal/material reference checks. Preserve thin openings/sill connectivity and actual edit maintenance. Reject a candidate that wins top-down timing by losing ground-level coverage, closes a tunnel during remesh, or measures only smooth source geometry while hidden edit buffers dominate memory.

Extend Q4/Q6 with dirty-region rebuild/cold preparation and retained edit-state peaks, including layer splits, source/response correspondence and overlapping old reader/products. Extend Q5 with known seam displacement, opposite edge directions and LOD changes in both views; reset only unsupported histories or prove a mapping. Keep physical queries unchanged by graphics adaptation. For native-only work-graph/cluster candidates measure repeated generation, backing store, shadow/ray geometry, extra views and source-off transitions as assembled work. Qualified reductions may change render representation; they cannot drop collision, exposed precipitation mass, active body support or saved surface state.
