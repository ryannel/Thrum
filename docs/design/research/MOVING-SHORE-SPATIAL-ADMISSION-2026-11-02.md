# Fixed-column shore spatial admission — 2 November commission, 3 November 2026 result

**Decision: reject the fixed-column challenger as the general ordinary-coast target under this studied 8192-column/error profile.** It preserves local point chronology, but useful stocked shore coverage exhausts the column cap because final film/coating labels have an inventory-dependent spatial mismatch floor. Retain the point proof and exact geometry/source oracles; do not promote the column representation into C09. Small restricted fixtures pass offline. No engine/source admission, F4 product, finite-water runtime, image or device qualification follows.

Owned files are this report and `tmp/moving-shore-spatial-admission/`; its selected six-file source/fixture/result/measurement set is retained in the [evidence archive](../../evidence/water/MOVING-SHORE-SPATIAL-ADMISSION-PROOF-2026-11-03.tar.gz). ## What is actually tested

Original stdlib Python constructs a **fresh-cook, fixed** adaptive triangle partition, then reuses it for rising/falling tide samples, a one-second accumulated-history path and one four-second moving-wave crest. Refinement occurs offline, never during material simulation. No saved liquid is remapped into new columns. This is an admission feasibility study, not a material simulator or a proposal to let per-frame remeshing evade the prior fixed-state budget.

| Input | Exact definition |
| --- | --- |
| Source geometry | Square `[0,L] x [0,L]`, affine bank `b=x/20`; roof at 10 m, bridge at 12 m, both above all admitted water stages |
| Static boundaries | Roof footprint `[L/4,L/2] x [L/4,L/2]`; bridge `[3L/4,7L/8] x [L/2,3L/4]`; explicit export strip `x>=15L/16`; soil boundary `x=L/3` |
| Material layers | Bank remains under roof/bridge with positive true area and zero direct-rain eligibility; top roof/bridge have separate columns/identities. Export has rain mass but no material state |
| Fixed-column construction | Overlay all above edges first; split rectangles into triangles; choose each representative at its triangle centroid; bisect the edge with greatest X span, deterministically, until the worst stage-envelope error fits or 8192 total columns are present |
| Tide envelope | Shore contour X anywhere in `[L/4,3L/4]`, not just the tested instantaneous contour. Slope is 1:20. Stage speed magnitude `0.00015 m/s`, contour speed `0.003 m/s` |
| Tick and material | 1/60 s, refill 0.02 kg/(true m² s), coating cap 0.1 kg/m²; nonbinding-cap restricted law, no absorption/drainage/phase changes |
| Stocked/heavy case | Initial surface density 0.05 kg/m²; rain `1/36 kg/(horizontal m² s)`, equivalent to 100 mm/h under the fixed 1000 kg/m³ water density |
| Dry/quiet control | Zero initial film; rain `1/360 kg/(horizontal m² s)`, 10 mm/h |
| Prior-error counterexample | 4 m side, initially dry quiet case, **0.001 kg/m² pre-existing state error density**, preserved through partitioning and every subsequent certificate |

Area coverage and eligibility are checked exactly: eligible rain triangle areas plus named export equal `L²`. The bank's projected physical area is `15L²/16`; roof adds `L²/16`, bridge `L²/32`. Total material projected area is therefore `33L²/32`, larger than the rain footprint. Counting the floor as a second rain receiver would fail that equality. Material and source identities inherit their static source region through every split; a leaf never crosses the soil, shelter or export boundary. Triangle areas are exact rational values. The bank true-area ratio is enclosed by the checked rational upper bound 1.002; roofs use ratio one. This conservative ratio is a bound, not an exact rational square root.

## The inequality driving refinement

For a column of projected area A, direct-rain eligibility e in `{0,1}`, true/projected ratio upper bound beta, representative mode q(t), true wet set W(t), define:

`U = integral_I area_xz(W(t) XOR [q(t)*whole_column]) dt`.

For the eligible top surface this is the source mismatch as well as material-mode mismatch; a sheltered lower bank has no source term but retains its material U. Endpoint mismatch `E` is the projected area where final representative mode and true wet classification disagree. These are **actual representative-versus-footprint mismatches**, not Taylor uncertainty. Exact affine geometry has zero Taylor error and can still have positive U/E.

Runtime local acceptance in the prototype uses the conservative absolute subrequest:

`prior_i + 2 P e U + 2(P e + K beta) U + 2 rho_H beta E <= 1e-5 kg`.

The terms are respectively retained state uncertainty, receiver-vector source allocation, nonbinding material-rate chronology, and final film/coating-label inventory. The prototype intentionally uses the absolute floor of the proposed `1e-5 + 1e-3*processed exchange mass` request, so it is a stricter **declared subprofile**, not proof that every conceivable use of the relative allowance fails. The material upper density `rho_H = rho_initial + prior_error_density + (P+K)*1 s` covers the one-second test horizon and must remain below the cap. It is not reset to initial film for later ticks.

Offline preparation uses `U<=A*dt` and the exact maximum possible E over the **entire contour envelope**. For a centroid whose shoreline can pass through it, the one-sided mismatch at that instant is at least A/2. The endpoint term consequently has a floor at least `rho_H beta A`, regardless of the tide's tiny per-tick displacement. Changing temporal quadrature or reducing Taylor remainder cannot remove it. The stocked-heavy rho_H is about 0.09778 kg/m², so this preparation bound demands active-triangle area of order 0.0001 m². Bisection anisotropy helps avoid refining distant permanently wet/dry regions, but cannot eliminate the inventory floor throughout the supported moving-shore band.

There is also a representation-based diagnostic lower bound under the absolute request: even using the initial density 0.05 alone, covering a swept ground band of area B with one representative label per column needs approximately `N >= 0.05 B / 1e-5`. Here B=L²/2, giving `N >= 2500 L²` before boundary/layer overhead. At L=2, that already exceeds 8192; at L=64 it is about 10.24 million. This is a lower bound for this fixed-point label/error model, not a theorem against cut-cell fields or all alternative acceptance norms. The tested conservative one-second bound is tighter still. Simply removing the source-rate terms does not fix the dominant endpoint inventory term.

Patch acceptance adds a 0.001 kg sum-of-local-bounds request for each sampled tick and 0.01 kg accumulated request after the one-second path. Prior surface-state error is carried by stable leaf ID: each tick adds its nonnegative source/material rate enclosure to the previous radius. Endpoint label error is recomputed at the current support; it is not a permanent transfer of L and does not justify resetting L's retained error. Refining an initial error density preserves its integral; it cannot manufacture a fresh zero error budget.

## Independent geometry and integration checks

The affine candidate works **time first**: clip the actual triangle at each relevant contour, split at all vertex and representative crossing times, and exactly integrate the piecewise-quadratic wet-area/complement law with rational arithmetic. It also integrates the continuous true water first-hit receipt and compares the representative receipt against its mismatch bound. Constant-mode triangles use the analytic zero-mismatch branch.

The independent affine oracle works **space first**: sort triangle vertices by X, derive the piecewise-linear vertical width directly from its three edges, and integrate `width(x)*|clamp(t_cross(x))-clamp(t_cross(representative))|`. True wet area-time is integrated separately from point wet duration. It calls neither clipping nor prepared wet-area samples. Rising, falling and constant-stage cases are covered by 15 additional exact triangle tests, including a nonzero-area negative-coordinate triangle. Every dynamically cut triangle in the reported tide samples/histories is compared exactly. This does not prove a generic rational polygon overlay/cooker, which is deliberately reduced here to explicit rectangular boundaries and bisection inheritance.

For the wave, `omega=pi/2`, `k=omega²/9.81`, amplitude 0.05 m, midpoint X=L/2, and

`eta(x,t)=x_mid/20 - 0.05 cos(pi/480) + 0.05 cos(k(x-x_mid)-omega(t-T/2))`.

The bank remains `x/20`. At the midpoint, dry endpoints surround an interior wet pulse with roots T/4 and 3T/4. Since `a*k<1/20`, f=eta-bed is strictly decreasing in X, so it has one spatial shoreline at each time. Candidate contour boxes use rational cosine polynomial bounds, a pi enclosure, and this derivative sign. A global derivative bound first certifies the root within 1 mm of the midpoint; the smaller phase range then tightens that derivative bound. Sixteen global time slabs enclose W by clipped contour lower/upper areas. A representative not classified against a slab's contour box charges the **whole footprint** for that slab; a representative endpoint left unresolved refuses. It never substitutes the thin contour-box difference for U.

The independent wave oracle works space first using actual cosine/acos wet-time intervals and the triangle's edge-derived width. It splits at independently solved endpoint-contour, crest-threshold, vertex and representative X coordinates and performs adaptive Simpson integration at two requested tolerances. This oracle uses floating point, has a finite depth cap and is **not a formally enclosed reference**. Its refinement differences and depth-exhaustion counts are retained. Agreement inside the candidate enclosure is numerical supporting evidence, not exact-wave or f64 certificate qualification. The affine oracles remain the exact hard gate.

The stocked 1 m² fixture consumes about 88% of the entire 8192-column allowance. Its sixty-step accumulated bound is about 0.000261 kg; the smaller stocked case about 0.000116 kg; the dry/quiet 4 m² control about 0.000359 kg. No prior radius is discarded between those steps. The prior-error fixture carries more than 0.016 kg across its layered material support before new numerical error, exceeding 0.01 kg even if arbitrarily subdivided.

The single-crest candidate U upper bounds are about 1.25 times the independent numerical U estimates in the retained run. Only 68–87 columns lie near its narrow moving critical band. That specialized crest is therefore much easier than certifying stocked cells throughout a useful tide range. Larger cases can fail a local bound while their global sum is small; global conservation and an attractive global error number cannot override the local refusal. Wave diagnostics use the tide-prepared partitions; they are not an optimal wave-only partition study or a full wave-cycle history. Diagnostics on a cap-refused tide partition are explicitly not whole-profile success. The numerical oracle exhausts its depth cap in 8 panels for the 16 m-side case and 16 panels for the 64 m-side case; these remain labelled numerical comparisons, not formally certified wave passes.

**Ordinary-coast implication:** a general coast needs metres to kilometres of supported shoreline and persistent nonzero film/coating, not a one-square-metre demonstration or a partition tailored to one lucky crest. The tested stocked 4–4096 m² profiles already fail. Under this budget/norm/model, the challenger is infeasible for that required ordinary-coast scope. Better root isolation, faster Python or a cheaper shader cannot remove the spatial inventory floor. The result does not forbid a separately admitted tiny interaction patch, permanently dry/wet region, or a game choosing a materially different error/physical profile.

## Next candidate: proposal, not selection

The next feasibility target should retain **source-aligned spatial liquid fields/cohorts with exact wet-set integration**, rather than one wet/dry label and one density at a point. At an endpoint, integrate L(x) over the actual wet/dry source cut to derive M_d/M_w. This removes the point-label inventory floor while retaining local chronology. Merely calling the same P0 point state a finite-volume cell does not fix it.

For an initial restricted tide-only proof, use height-aligned cohorts on each actual source triangle. With constant rain/refill and affine stage segments, crossing time is affine in source height, so retained surface density is piecewise affine in height. Store its coefficients and cohort height intervals, clipping against the shared exact source wet-area law. Wet-entry caps split cohorts at the actual density threshold; deposition stays attached to the affected source interval. No end-of-step whole-patch remix is permitted. Absorption can remain off in the first independent proof; richer constitutive laws must explicitly close their state representation.

A conservative proposed cost is at most eight height cohorts per 8192 source fragments: 65536 cohorts, about 64 B each for interval bounds, surface/absorbed affine coefficients, error and flags = 4 MiB/endpoint, **16 MiB for four owners**, plus prepared geometry/polynomials and receipt scratch. Bounded count is not guaranteed: repeated unequal fronts/deposits can grow cohorts; merge only with a separately proved local field/receipt error or refuse. This is more state than columns but can describe large smooth film regions without one microcolumn per tiny wet-area label error.

General wave fronts are not height-parallel. A possible extension is spatially clipped cohorts with affine/polynomial density in source barycentric coordinates and certified geometric/time residual. At eight cohorts per source fragment, a conservative 224 B including bounded polygon storage is already 14 MiB/endpoint, 56 MiB for four owners. Grazing sinusoidal crossings can create nonpolynomial crossing-time fields; they require a proven approximation/remainder or a curved cohort, not division by a vanishing time derivative. That much richer path is a proposal needing a new feasibility commission, not adopted architecture or free hidden children under the 8192 count.

The next exact independent proof should replay the existing two-fragment rain and three-fragment opposite-motion adversaries over actual triangles, then compare cohort density integrals and each exchange kind/destination with an **independent space-first event-history integral**, including rising/falling stage, timed deposition, binding caps, dry→wet→dry and wet→dry→wet. Require identical local/total mass and source receipts in rational arithmetic, reject a deliberate cohort-remix mutant, and bound cohort growth under repeated reversals before any wave extension. A single ordinary-frequency crest then tests curved history and accumulation separately. The exact continuous first-hit source stays authoritative throughout; the new material representation must consume that history, not silently replace it by point exposure.

It does not establish uniform performance at this shore's grazing cases. No implementation/code licence was established or reused. The inspected [Basilisk `fractions.h`](https://basilisk.fr/src/fractions.h), `fraction_refine` and linear-interface reconstruction, reinforces that cut fractions are geometric quantities distinct from a point label. This study's implementation is original and imports none of those files. No talk/video was needed to settle the counted failure.
