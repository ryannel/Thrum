## Required behavior and supported regimes

Rain crossing a roof edge, a rising shore or a newly overtopped sill has exactly one first receiver. A dry bank can acquire film and absorbed water; inundation can retain a coating, exchange excess film with water and feed bounded infiltration; retreat leaves actual retained liquid, not a shader fade or water created from a wetness scalar. Finite water rises from delivered volume and crosses only admitted hydraulic faces. Prescribed sea remains an external reservoir. Source-off stops new rain while water, film, absorption, drainage and already queued transfers continue. Headless, one view and two views execute the same physical transaction. Saves retain liquid and receipt identity across retry/restart.

| Regime | Selected semantics and limits |
| --- | --- |
| Prescribed moving shore | Tide-only profile: static triangulated solids/bed and piecewise-linear scalar stage, affine clipping. Analytic-wave profile: PLAY's CPU mean-plus-sinusoids, with the bounded space/time interval method below. Both use vertical liquid rain and external-reservoir accounting. No wave-breaking, overturning surface or finite-sea conservation claim |
| Existing rectangular HLL | Keep PLAY's `V=A*h`, constant bed per 2 m square, full-face banks, hydrostatic/HLL/donor limiter and four-substep admission. Wet support is the complete cell when `h>0`, empty at `h=0`. Overtopping is flow over full-face bed steps into a receiving finite grid, not discharge into a visual pool. Source/collider/render bed must actually be that terrace geometry |
| Capacity-HLL shore profile | Distinct first-order finite-volume profile derived below: source-triangle capacity, partially wet cells, integrated face flux, conservative sliver agglomeration. Selected higher-quality proof target for the sloping finite R3 bank; provisional until independent numerical review/qualification. It retains HLL as a flux kernel, not PLAY's constant-bed state/guarantee |
| Excluded | Moving/deforming shelters, oblique/porous interception, snow/ice, erosion, submerged cavities, flow through buildings, hidden hydraulic links, unconstrained live geometry migration and physical offline catch-up. Each requires a declared provider/operator; none becomes zero rain or a graphics fallback |

The original 1:20 bank changes height by 0.10 m across a 2 m cell. At a mid-cell stage a full-cell wet mask can put the visible/contact shore about 1 m from the source contour, exceeding the terrain recipe's proposed 0.10 m shore allowance. A correctly conserved terraced fixture therefore **does not satisfy the attractive sloping interactive shore**. Rendering a smooth contour over that finite terrace does not repair physics or precipitation. Retain the terrace as a conservative lower-complexity profile; select capacity-HLL for the smooth finite-bank proof. A prescribed sea clipped to source terrain already supports a smooth imposed shoreline, with its separate external-reservoir limitation.

Coverage is explicit: E1 owns interval forcing, finite water and material exchange coordination; terrain owns patch film/absorption law and state; F4 prepares geometry/capacity/exposure products; W2 pins and activates the complete physical closure; W1/F1 owns the clock, candidate transaction and fault/retry; P7 captures committed state. P3 and P5 read committed support/material conditions; V2/V3 and water optics consume that same wet set. This is not a new universal environmental solver.

## Evidence that changes the choice

The following were inspected as primary material on 2 November 2026. Published methods support components; the assembly and numerical bounds below are Thrum deductions/design choices.

- [CGAL 6.2.1 arrangement manual, “Overlaying Arrangements” and exact-kernel examples](https://doc.cgal.org/latest/Arrangement_on_surface_2/index.html): inspected the documented overlay/DCEL/traits operations and examples. This supports preparing static projected intersections with labelled faces. It does not supply a moving-water or conservative rainfall solver. No CGAL runtime dependency is selected; an independent offline reference may use it under a separately checked licence/tool boundary.
- [Shewchuk's primary predicates page and linked C implementation](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~quake/robust.html): inspected the description and implementation availability, not a complete line-by-line code audit. Adaptive orientation solves predicate sign robustness, not the exactness of constructed polygon intersections. Therefore use filtered predicates **and** certified/exact construction when needed; a robust `orient2d` alone is insufficient.
- [Delestre et al. 2012, §§2.2–3](https://arxiv.org/html/1206.4986v2): inspected the reconstruction equations and slope/depth/mesh counterexample. Positivity and lake-at-rest preservation do not establish accurate thin flow over a coarse bed step. Include thin overtopping and mesh refinement tests; a global rain ledger cannot qualify these hydraulics.
- [Ersing, Goldberg and Winters, revised 8 October 2026, §§2–3](https://arxiv.org/html/2406.14119v2): inspected the hydrostatic reconstruction, well-balanced/positivity sections and wet/dry discussion. The paper's path-conservative/high-order assembly is a credible replacement, not a licence to attach a fine wet mask to the existing HLL state. Do not transfer its theorems to the operator below.
- [Bitsch et al., 5 January 2026, §§3–4.5](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.01895v1): inspected coarse/fine state representation, mass-preserving reconstruction, face treatment and gravity source. This directly supports considering explicit finite-volume subgrid bathymetry. Its bathymetry is piecewise constant on fine cells; §3.1 also mentions negative-depth neighbor redistribution. Our affine-source capacity, donor limiter, quadrature and agglomeration are distinct authored choices requiring proof, not reproduced published results.
- [Volp et al. 2013, full article, §§1–2](https://agupubs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1002/wrcr.20324): inspected the disclosed coarse-grid/subgrid volume and section-area formulation. It supports keeping nonlinear volume–stage relations and face hydraulics together. [Casulli's institutional record](https://iris.unitn.it/handle/11572/79798) was accessible only as an abstract/metadata; its restricted full text was not read. A mildly nonlinear semi-implicit subgrid method remains a credible stiffness alternative, without a claimed implementation derivation from that abstract.

No production talk was found necessary to settle this conservative CPU geometry/state boundary; a rain-particle demonstration would not prove it. No external code is imported or added as a dependency. Broadsides is not used as an implementation authority or dependency.

Source quantized coordinates and decoded f64 values have the already declared meaning, not a fictitious real-world exactness.

## The spatial first-hit operator

For atom `a` and staged interval-start water state `W_j`, evaluate water existence and stage from that provider. Let `s_a(x)` be the top solid and `b_a(x)` the selected floodable bed. For tide-only prescribed/capacity water, `wet(x) ⇔ η_i>b_a(x)` in admitted support. Analytic-wave water instead uses the actual `η(x,t)>b_a(x)` evaluator and certified interval subdivision below; it cannot enter the scalar-plane clipping branch. For original HLL, `wet ⇔ h_i>0` over the complete cell, using its matching constant bed. At `η=b`, bed is dry; positive-volume water is wet even when `h≤h_dry`. The momentum cutoff never deletes water or changes exposure.

Clip `a` by `η_i>s_a(x)` where water is admitted. Its wet-above-solid polygon receives water rain; the complement receives the top solid. Where a roof is above water it keeps the ray, while the selected floor may nevertheless be inundated beneath it. Level bounds initially keep water below roof/deck tops; a request to hydraulically flood a building is unsupported. Missing floor/water/support identity is `NotReady`/`Unsupported`, not a generic ground receiver.

For a subinterval `I_j=[t_j,t_j+δt)`, compute one scalar `J_kj=∫_Ij P_k(t)dt` per source cell. **Tide-only prescribed stage** delivers each receiver `m_rkj=∫_Ij P_k(t) A_rk(η(t))dt` kg from the certified time-varying horizontal first-hit area. The analytic-wave branch uses its separate certified spacetime integral below. Original HLL and provisional capacity-HLL deliver `m_rkj=J_kj A_rk(W_j)` relative to their frozen-start **discrete numerical** water state; no continuous-shore accuracy is inferred for that finite-water branch. Store a local row per receiver; total mass alone is insufficient. Terrain converts mass into its own true-area densities. Do not multiply rain by the sloping true area a second time. Water source volume is `m/ρ`, with zero incoming horizontal momentum.

Each complete source cell satisfies `Σ_r A_rk=A_k`, hence `Σ_r m_rkj=J_kj A_k` in exact arithmetic. Include explicit footprint exports in that sum. Runtime uses fixed ordering, compensated reduction and an error enclosure for polygon areas; choose one residual recipient within its nonnegative area enclosure only for arithmetic reconciliation. Never normalize away missing geometry, renormalize a stale row, or hide a negative area. Candidate rejection includes local per-receiver enclosure failure as well as global sum failure.

## A distinct finite subcell-capacity water operator

This section is an authored first-order finite-volume derivation, not a claim that the baseline HLL or a cited paper already implements it. Its public profile/state/product revision is different. It is sufficiently specified to build a numerical proof; convergence, accuracy of transient overtopping and target cost remain unrun.

### State, storage and topology

Base cells remain bounded rectangles for the first profile, initially up to 64×32 cells of 2 m × 2 m. The immutable source bed is piecewise affine on its actual 0.25 m triangles. Each cell stores liquid volume `V_i≥0` in m³ and integrated horizontal momentum-per-density `p_i=∫_cell h u dA` in m⁴/s. Define

`C_i(η)=∫_cell max(η-b(x),0)dA`, `A_w,i(η)=dC_i/dη`, `u_i=p_i/V_i` when wet.

F4 clips each triangle at its vertex critical heights and prepares exact/certified piecewise-cubic storage and piecewise-quadratic wet-area coefficients; f64 coefficients carry an absolute evaluation enclosure. They refer to the same triangle diagonal as collision/render/source. No flat-cell `h=V/A` is used as the physical shoreline stage. `V=0` returns Dry and `η=b_min`; for `V>0`, invert strictly increasing C above `b_min` using a bracket `[b_min,b_max+V/A_cell]`, safeguarded Newton and bisection with at most 64 refinements. Require both stage enclosure ≤0.1 mm and capacity residual within the numerical volume enclosure. A failure is a rejected candidate, not an unconverged height. V remains authoritative; reconstructed C(η) is checked, not written back as an unlogged mass correction.

One stage must not teleport water between disconnected pockets within a cell. F4 proves connected sublevel wet sets and face connectivity over the admitted level interval, by the terrain critical-level component method. Align/split protected ridges/sills into cell faces, or create separately owned hydraulic compartments in a different admitted mesh product. This profile initially rejects a base cell containing disconnected wet pockets. The cook certificate explicitly includes the initial state and **every** attained stage in the admitted interval, with one-sided checks at critical levels; a later state outside it is refused. Counterexample: two pits in one coarse cell, separated by a ridge, start with water only in the left pit below the ridge. A single C inverse would wet both without a face flux. The certificate must reject/split that cell; a matching total capacity curve does not qualify it. Fine capacity alone does not resolve a buried narrow channel, a barrier wholly inside a cell, velocity variation below the base grid, or hydraulic loss through an unresolved opening.

### Shared face flux and bed reaction

On each outer face, use its actual piecewise-affine bed traces `b_L(s),b_R(s)` and `b_*(s)=max(b_L,b_R)`. At each face coordinate use `h_L*=max(η_L-b_*,0)`, `h_R*=max(η_R-b_*,0)`, `q_s*=h_s* u_s`. Apply the same HLL normal flux and characteristic speeds as PLAY, pointwise. Its integrated homogeneous flux `Φ_f=∫_face F_HLL ds` has mass units m³/s. Cache one signed value for both cells and any reservoir ledger.

For side i define `Π_if*=∫_face ½g(h_i*)² n_if ds`, an outward vector. On a tentative substep include known positive sources in `V_i+`, compute outgoing `O_i=δt Σ_f max(Φ_h,f outward,0)` and `θ_i=min(1,V_i+/O_i)`. Use its mass donor's θ to scale the **whole homogeneous** Φ, identically on both sides; zero mass flux uses scale 1. Update

`V_i' = V_i+ − δt Σ_f (θ_donor Φ_h,f outward)`,

`p_i' = p_i − δt Σ_f [θ_donor Φ_m,f outward − Π_if*]`.

This is hydrostatic correction plus bed reaction in cancellation form. To derive it, let `Π_if=∫½g max(η_i-b_i,0)² n_if ds`. For constant reconstructed η within a cell, `−g∫h∇b dA=Σ_f Π_if`, including declared bed-jump reactions. The usual side correction is `Π_if−Π_if*`; inserting it into flux and adding bed reaction cancels Π_if, giving the expression above. It is not a pressure term added twice. At an unforced lake with one η and zero u, homogeneous HLL mass is zero and its momentum is exactly Π_if* on every face; use the same cached hydrostatic integral for this equality branch, making the update vanish face by face. This also covers a wet/dry interface whose crest is above η. Interior mass and homogeneous momentum flux cancel in pairs; bed reactions need not conserve fluid horizontal momentum.

For general face fluxes, split at bed-trace knots, trace crossings and wet endpoints. Use interval-enclosed adaptive quadrature with fixed face-node ordering, at most 32 leaf panels per face and a global tick evaluation cap. A panel encloses the pointwise HLL flux over its segment; refine until the integrated mass/momentum interval meets its allocated tolerance. Identical constant-rest states take the analytic pressure-integral branch. Square-root/dry cases and uncertain sign must be enclosed, not epsilon-snapped to zero. A failed face integral rejects the whole candidate. Positivity of the limited V update does not depend on quadrature being physically exact; accuracy and the flux enclosure still require qualification.

Before capped sinks, exact-arithmetic `V_i'=V_i+−θ_i O_i+I_i≥0`; sinks cannot exceed the remainder. Dry momentum is zero with its explicit ground/reservoir momentum ledger. Use the selected semi-implicit Manning damping with an admitted representative wet depth `V_i/A_w,i`; no division by zero. Zero incoming-horizontal-momentum rain leaves p unchanged. A water→surface mass withdrawal removes proportional p and logs the ground/material momentum transfer. This profile retains the one-way small-body limits; it does not claim reciprocal body/fluid displacement or sea momentum conservation.

### Slivers, conservative agglomeration and the actual 60 Hz claim

A partial wet wedge can make an explicit scheme stiff. Use the admitted sufficient *design* CFL estimate `δt ≤0.4 A_w / Σ_outerfaces L_wet (|u·n|+√(g h_max))`, recomputed on source-updated states. It is a conservative scheduling candidate, not a proven nonlinear stability theorem for this new operator. A strictly dry recipient (`V=0`) does not contribute a zero numerator/positive incoming-face denominator to the timestep minimum: its outgoing mass is zero under the donor limiter, and the existing wet donor/face speed controls that first incoming flux. Reconstruct the newly positive recipient and check velocity/depth/support immediately after that step, then apply its partial-wet estimate/agglomeration on the next step. This is a specific wet-front activation rule to test, not a proof of stability from the volume limiter. Four substeps and the original rectangular-grid analytical envelope do not transfer automatically. The new profile admits at most eight water substeps per 1/60 s tick, at most four base cells per temporary aggregate, and explicit reject/overload if it cannot cover the complete interval.

For a quiescent 1:20 shore strip of width d inside a 2 m cell, `A_w≈2d` and the dominant wet-face length is about 2 m. Thus the estimate approaches `0.4√(d/(g/20))`: at d=0.01 m it is about 0.057 s, and at d=0.0001 m about 0.0057 s. Nonzero along/normal velocity can dominate and tighten it sharply. Arbitrarily small newly wet strips therefore have no universal fixed-substep guarantee, even with an exact capacity curve.

Use **conservative bounded agglomeration**, not dry deletion or a finer physical timestep hidden from F1:

1. Before each flux step, identify a cell whose own estimate cannot meet the proposed remaining tick division. Select its largest-wet-area face neighbor whose stage is compatible; stable cell ID breaks ties. Grow a nonoverlapping connected aggregate of at most four cells. Internal connecting faces must be wet, not a protected sill/closed bank. A fully isolated puddle with no wet outer face has no face-flux CFL restriction.
2. Set `V_G=ΣV_i`, `p_G=Σp_i`, find `η_G` from `ΣC_i(η_G)=V_G`, and use `u_G=p_G/V_G`. Admit only if each previously wet constituent's stage changes by ≤0.5 mm, predicted newly wet support moves by ≤0.01 m under its certified source slope, and no protected component/portal changes. On flat critical bands compare the exact wet-set change directly; slope division is forbidden.
3. Compute only aggregate outer-face fluxes with the same pressure/source formula and combined capacity/wet area. Internal faces are removed. After the step split by `V_i=C_i(η_G')`, `p_i=V_i u_G'`; assign the tiny certified arithmetic residual once to an eligible constituent and log it. All source/response IDs remain base-cell keyed. Sum V and p are preserved by merge/split; velocity mixing dissipates `½ρ[Σ|p_i|²/V_i−|Σp_i|²/ΣV_i]≥0`, recorded separately from mass and external work.
4. Recheck every constituent's final stage/support error against the same profile limits and contact/geometry certificates. Additionally require every wet constituent `|u_i−u_G|≤0.02 m/s`, each merge loss `ΔK≤1e-3 max(K_before,ρgV_G×0.0001 m)`, and cumulative tick merge loss `ΣΔK≤1e-3 max(K_tick_start+positive admitted external work,ρgV_tick_start×0.0001 m)`. These are proposed numerical-profile acceptance bounds, not measured hydraulic accuracy. A merge that would move shore farther, cross a sill, exceed these velocity/energy limits, exceed 20 m/s/depth support, or still fail CFL is rejected. In particular equal-stage opposite fast currents cannot be merged merely because their stages agree. Do not reinterpret a large error as numerical roundoff. Retry a smaller admitted step while the eight-step cap permits it; otherwise no publication.

For the exact planar bank at a lake-at-rest stage all compatible cell stages already agree: merge changes neither stage, shoreline nor momentum, while an adjacent wet cell supplies finite aggregate wet area. A sliver adjoining a fully wet 2 m cell at rest has roughly 4 m² aggregate wet area and O(8 m) outer perimeter; for h≤0.1 m and |u|≤0.25 m/s, the estimate is O(0.1 s), comfortably above a 1/60 s tick. This is an analytical representative case, not a guarantee for a moving bore. Near-zero source-created isolated puddles have no open wet outer face; prescribe a fully wet apron at any external-reservoir mouth so a dry boundary sliver is not an unmergeable special case.

The 0.25 m source mesh is retained inside the 2 m hydraulic cells, so on the exactly planar bank capacity and wet contours are exact relative to source before arithmetic. The proposed ≤0.1 mm inversion plus ≤0.5 mm admitted aggregate-stage change costs at most 0.012 m horizontal shore error at slope 1:20; add the separately certified source/product/contact errors. This can fit the proposed 0.10 m shore envelope. A transient with steep η gradients may fail the merge rule or eight-step cap; reference overtopping accuracy, stress frequency and whole-tick cost must be measured. If ordinary required motion repeatedly fails, select a separately derived semi-implicit/subgrid provider or revise the profile, rather than widening the error limit until the proof passes.

## Film, bound coating, infiltration and retreat

First-hit assignment alone is insufficient. A response cell holds three **mass** stores: exposed film `M_d`, submerged bound coating `M_w`, and absorbed liquid `M_a`, all kg. Its committed dry/wet source subsets and their true areas D/W are derived from the owning endpoint. Film/coating are uniform within their respective subsets under an explicit finite-cell homogenization approximation. Absorbed liquid is uniform over the whole response cell; it is not a second hydrology solver. Cell sizes/material boundaries are selected physical-profile data, not a render resolution control.

On any old→new wet-set transition, compute exact/certified true areas `A_DW=|D_old∩W_new|` and `A_WD=|W_old∩D_new|`. Transfer `m_DW=M_d A_DW/|D_old|` and `m_WD=M_w A_WD/|W_old|`, with zero denominators requiring the corresponding mass to be zero. Set `M_d←M_d−m_DW+m_WD`, `M_w←M_w−m_WD+m_DW`. Within each new subset homogenize conservatively. This explicitly bounds what is represented; a tiny advancing shore cannot repeatedly remove a fraction from an unrelated patch. Smaller physical cells are the available refinement, not a global wetness scalar.

The first synthetic constitutive profile has fixed 293.15 K, authored bound-coating retention `r_w` kg/m², refill rate `k_w` kg/(m²·s), absorption capacity `C_a`, dry/wet absorption rates `a_d,a_w`, exposed-film/absorbed evaporation rates and existing drainage residence/edges. Proposed initial values `r_w=0.10 kg/m²` and `k_w=0.02 kg/(m²·s)` give an actual retained 0.1 mm water coating when supply/duration permits; these are test-material controls, not calibrated soil physics.

- On inundation, coating above `r_w|W|` transfers to an identified **pending water-exchange inventory** for the same base cell, consumed at the next water substep (or next tick after the last substep). A prescribed reservoir receives it immediately because its stage is externally fixed. The inventory is real liquid mass, included in the ledger/save and never counted in free-water V until delivery. This explicit lag prevents endpoint wetting → excess-film addition → raised stage → further wetting from becoming an unspecified fixed-point solver. On delivery, reconstruct support once; any resulting new excess becomes a new pending receipt rather than recursively delivered in that substep. Retained coating remains material-bound liquid, separate from free-water V. Its ignored geometric thickness is bounded by `r_w/ρ=0.1 mm` in the first profile and charged to contact/shore error. No optical wetting scalar creates this mass.
- Before a finite flux step, refill a coating deficit by `min(deficit,k_w|W|δt,ρV/4)` from its finite water donor. A prescribed reservoir supplies an equal named input ledger. Debiting at most a quarter of current positive V prevents refill alone from toggling the donor dry and triggering a circular reclassification; multiple response fragments share one per-cell quarter-volume allowance. Remove proportional horizontal momentum to the material/ground reaction ledger. If water is scarce, coating remains below target; never borrow future rain/flow.
- Absorption transfers from the corresponding film/coating into `M_a`, limited by the dry/wet exposed capacity share `(C_a A−M_a)×|D or W|/A`, remaining available donor mass and `a_d|D|δt` or `a_w|W|δt`. Apply dry then wet in declared order, recomputing residual capacity; this is a bounded synthetic law. Water→coating refill enables infiltration during inundation. No energy-balance claim is made.
- Evaporation acts on exposed film and the exposed share of absorbed liquid only; no underwater evaporation sink. Existing dry-film retention/drainage releases along declared next-tick edges, with donor debit and finite transit storage. Submerged excess joins its local pending water-exchange inventory; it does not pass through an unbounded drain graph or recursively raise the current endpoint stage.
- On ebb, `m_WD` becomes exposed film, carrying the **actual** retained coating. Absorbed mass remains. A freshly flooded dry patch with insufficient water/time can emerge with less film than the target, including zero; this is a source-limited result, not a visual reset. Future wave contact can replenish it. A profile requiring saturated wetting after arbitrarily brief contact must select a different explicit exchange rate/law.

Pending exchange has a quantitative admission test: for each finite base cell compute the stage interval enclosing `C_i^-1(V_i+M_pending/ρ)−C_i^-1(V_i)` (or the aggregate capacity if one is proposed). Require it ≤0.5 mm and its exact source wet-set displacement ≤0.01 m, with protected topology unchanged; otherwise reject/refine the physical response profile before publication. Original HLL requires the equivalent depth bound and no unresolved dry→wet whole-cell transition from deferred exchange; in that case shorten the source interval or use the capacity profile. Pending residence is at most one admitted water substep, or one F1 tick across a barrier, with no expiry/discard. Thus the lag is bounded in time and stage/support, not merely conserved globally. High film densities may fail this rule; use smaller physical material cells/intervals or an independently derived joint free-water/film solver, not a hidden recursive add.

Coating refill withdrawals use frozen start-of-subinterval contact support for their finite prescribed-rate operator. Reconstruct source-updated water stage for the actual face flux and apply the endpoint dry↔wet coating map afterward, including any recession caused by withdrawal. Bound the withdrawal-induced stage/support shift by the same 0.5 mm/0.01 m limits in addition to the quarter-volume cap. A coating placed on a formerly wet fragment that emerges is transferred to exposed film by that endpoint map; it is not deleted or refilled again recursively. The rain/source ordering remains explicitly first order; its local timing bound includes this withdrawal shift.

Homogenization can spread liquid within a physical response cell at a transition. Its maximum displacement is that cell's diameter; record it as model error and select cells fine enough for the material cue/traction requirement. It cannot spread mass between roof and ground, across a material barrier, across water cells or across regions. Existing dogfood two-mass surface state is a schema predecessor; adoption needs an explicit initial migration (all old film goes to dry film only if the old cell is certified dry; otherwise partition by an approved old-state model) or a fresh run. Never attach it automatically to the new three-store model.

## Tick order, temporal assignment and no rainfall loss on rejection

One W1/F1 tick owns `[t_n,t_n+1)`. P3 reads X[n] traction and water/contact state for its selected one-way force update. E1/terrain/water stage a complete next-state transaction. Neither views nor audio callbacks update rain or water. The durable delivery key is `(world_epoch,source/control_revision,stream_id,physical_interval,kind,donor_id,receiver_id)` with a source-cell/edge discriminator where needed. Subinterval and atom ordinals are internal work keys, not another deliverable interval identity: a retry with a different valid partition compares the canonical aggregated receiver payload digest and deduplicates if equal or refuses a conflict without depositing again. The applied-interval cursor advances only with the complete tick.

For each admitted water/exposure subinterval:

1. Apply previously committed next-tick water/surface transit receipts and the prior substep's identified pending water-exchange receipts once in candidate state. Delivery of a within-tick receipt is a candidate operation whose cursor commits only with the whole tick; a failed tick rolls it back together with its donor. Water receipts can wet a dry cell. Reconstruct its new wet set, transfer old film/coating ownership and cap inundated excess before forming current first-hit areas. This is causal input from a known earlier interval, not current rain predicting itself.
2. Use the provider-specific first-hit receipt integral above. Tide-only prescribed stage integrates the changing area across this interval; original/capacity HLL freezes the current discrete partition, and analytic waves use their certified spacetime branch. Apply the resulting local wet-first-hit and dry-first-hit masses once at the declared physical response barrier; prescribed-reservoir water rain is an external ledger entry, while finite-water rain adds V with zero incoming horizontal momentum. **Dry direct rain does not nucleate free-water depth by itself**: ponding requires an authored runoff/inlet edge into a finite water cell, or a separately specified ponding law. This choice preserves the surface/finite-water authority boundary.
3. Refill bound coating within the shared finite-water withdrawal allowance, then choose/recheck CFL on the source-updated state. If source changes invalidate the proposed duration, recompute a smaller candidate interval and its exact rain integral; no receipt cursor has advanced. Select compatible capacity aggregates if necessary. Original HLL uses its unchanged scheme and four-step cap; capacity-HLL uses its distinct eight-step/aggregate rules.
4. Apply one shared limited face flux, prescribed sinks, friction and boundary ledger. Derive the endpoint wet set. Perform conservative dry↔wet film/coating repartition; excess coating joins the pending water-exchange inventory. A cell dry at start but wet after flux therefore receives its onset-subinterval rain first as film, then retains a bounded coating and sends any excess to the pending local water-exchange inventory **once**, with no absorption before that endpoint sweep. The water receives it at the next substep, with a matching receipt; no current-endpoint V change triggers recursive inundation. Recheck stage/depth/support and the next step's CFL; do not secretly rerun the current flux with the pending volume.
5. Run the declared bounded film/absorption/evaporation/drainage response using this endpoint support. Coating cannot grow beyond its cap from absorption; outgoing drainage is next-tick transit. Coalesce finite edge receipts under reserved capacities. Recheck that no unresolved support change or mass capacity remains.
6. After the complete tick interval, validate local/global ledgers, geometry/exposure/error enclosures, stage and merge certificates, receipt cursors and all consumer candidates. Publish one X[n+1] group. No water candidate can publish before discovering a missing ground receiver, and no donor commits a transfer whose receiver cannot accept it.

For a **tide-only prescribed scalar stage**, split physical response at every stage-track knot/reversal and further only as required by film/coating/exchange timing, at most 16 physical exposure subintervals per tick in the initial profile. Rain-receipt integration has its own algebraic pieces; they do **not** advance water/material state or consume a physical slice. Within each monotone stage piece, split the polynomial integral at source-rate knots and every attained triangle vertex-height level. On an affine projected triangle with distinct ordered heights `h0<h1<h2` and area `A`, wet area is `A(H−h0)²/((h1−h0)(h2−h0))` for `h0<H<h1`, and `A−A(h2−H)²/((h2−h0)(h2−h1))` for `h1<H<h2`; outside those levels it is zero/full. Equal-height cases use their direct degenerate limit and strict dry-at-equality classification. Linear stage makes each area quadratic in time; piecewise-linear rain makes the receiver integrand cubic. F4 prepares the coefficients/critical order once, and E1 integrates those pieces in fixed receiver order with a certified floating-point enclosure. A source/geometry crossing not represented by the cooked arrangement refuses the product. Constant full/dry atoms take their direct area branch. The [exact-rational proof](../../evidence/water/MOVING-SHORE-FIRST-HIT-2026-11-02.md) exercised this model, including nonzero quadratic/cubic terms; it did not certify a production f64 implementation or physical response.

The prior frozen-start rain approximation remains an independent diagnostic and the discrete finite-water branch's declared operator, not the tide-only target. Its conservative bound for a monotone interval is `B_k=J_k S_k`, where `S_k` is changed projected area, with receiver-vector L1 error at most `2ΣB_k`. Identical endpoint masks around an interior reversal cannot justify `S=0`; a 16-slice frozen tide-only stress tick failed the proposed `1e-5 kg + 1e-3×local interval rain` receipt request even while conserving total mass. The analytic tide-only source receipt removes that model split error in exact arithmetic, but f64 coefficient/integration error and the **separate film/water chronological response error** still need local enclosures and rejection limits. If 16 physical slices cannot cover material exchanges, refuse the interval. Do not solve the problem by advancing stage without rain. HLL support is a discrete numerical trajectory: its rain split is exact under that declared operator, but it has no claimed bound to an undisclosed continuous shoreline. The onset-substep excess-water source has at most δt delay and mass bounded by that dry receiver's interval rain plus pre-existing excess film; record it separately from arithmetic residual and finite-volume truncation error.

### Analytic waves and interior wet intervals

PLAY's prescribed sea is `η(x,t)=η_mean(t)+Σ_j a_j cos(k_j·x−ω_j t+φ_j)`, not a scalar tide. Its up-to-16 physical components, phase/datum and CPU evaluator are exactly the P3 source. A mean-tide mask while contacts see waves is inadmissible whenever the wave envelope can cross bed, top-solid or a protected shore feature; the disagreement can be as large as `Σ|a_j|/g_min` horizontally and is not a cosmetic residual. The tide-only fast path requires certified unchanged receiver/wet ordering over the **whole** wave envelope, or zero wave amplitudes.

Select a bounded interval branch for wave-contact shoreline. Its spacetime receipt integration uses the actual varying wave height rather than the tide-only area polynomial or finite-water frozen `J×start_area` operator; the material operator still uses its explicitly bounded chronological endpoint split. For each convex source/response atom and time slab, interval-evaluate phase ranges, including all cosine extrema in those ranges, plus mean-stage bounds and affine bed/solid extrema. The resulting interval for `f=η−b` or `η−s_top` certifies full-wet/full-dry only when its sign excludes zero. Otherwise use a space/time affine Taylor approximation at the slab centre and bound its remainder by `ε≤½Σ|a_j|(|k_x,j|r_x+|k_z,j|r_z+|ω_j|r_t)^2` plus the mean-track remainder. Clip against the affine upper/lower bounds. Their difference is an explicit ambiguous spatial/time band, not an undocumented half-weight. The source owner exposes these analytic interval inputs; renderer texture displacement is insufficient.

Subdivide the largest-error atom spatially or bisect the time slab, reserving at most 16 global time slices, 65536 total spatial leaves and 1048576 interval-node evaluations per tick in this profile. Evaluate actual endpoint water with the same analytic source. The chosen receipt estimate clips the leaf's triangle×time prism by the **centre Taylor plane** `f_aff>0` (equality is dry), with source/top-solid/domain boundaries retained; it does not use a free choice of half weight or random sample. Triangulate the resulting convex polyhedron into oriented tetrahedra and integrate its volume and first time moment. On each source-rate segment `P(t)=α+βt`, its receiver mass is `α×space_time_volume+β×first_time_moment`; split the integration prism at source knots, without creating an additional physical update. Source knots retain the environment plan's finite 256-knot bound. Use the same deterministic prism partition for water and solid so their masses sum to the exact source integral. Upper/lower Taylor planes enclose the true receiver region, giving local mass error `P_max × ambiguous space-time volume` and the earlier per-receiver split tolerance; polyhedral clipping/integration has its own certified arithmetic enclosure. Interval bounds, not endpoints alone, must certify no missed crossing. A wave covering and uncovering a patch inside one F1 tick therefore subdivides even if both endpoint masks are dry. If the cap cannot bound it, the full tick refuses; frequency is not silently lowered.

Rain error alone is insufficient for material exchange. The material operator remains the chronological global-slab endpoint coating map/refill/response, not a second continuous solver. Its cheap **instantaneous-transfer-only** certificate requires every material-support space/time leaf to have either a fixed sign of `f=η−b` or a fixed sign of its time derivative (zero may be included, but not both derivative signs), all changing leaves of a response cell to move in the same wetting or drying direction, and **no rain/deposition, refill, absorption, evaporation, drainage, pending delivery or other mass change that can interleave with that support movement**. Under those conditions each point has at most one transition and the cell's endpoint map captures its directional film/coating transfer under the selected uniform-subset model. Endpoint equality is dry. Exact integrated first-hit rain masses alone do not certify their chronology in material stores: deposit-before-endpoint-wetting can differ from depositing on the moving dry set. A single-wave run-up turning point can also defeat the sign/monotonicity certificate even as its unresolved area shrinks; this fast certificate is not an unconditional admission rule.

For a cell without that certificate, use a sound conservative chronology envelope before accepting or refining. Form a joint output vector containing `M_d,M_w,M_a`, its pending inventory and every outgoing receipt/sink by stable destination. Let `B` be its starting **total** material and pending-exchange mass plus certified upper bounds on all possible incoming solid rain, transit, finite/prescribed coating refill and other admitted deposition in the slab. Nonnegative conservation makes the L1 distance between the endpoint operator and **any** admissible event chronology at most `2B`, including opposite-direction transitions, arbitrarily brief wet or dry excursions, cap releases and uniform-subset homogenization. Charge this whole-cell bound in addition to source and arithmetic enclosures; a swept-area-only bound on endpoint-hidden changes is insufficient when two observed fragments change in opposite directions. A tighter chronology bound may replace `2B` only after independent proof. If the resulting local error exceeds `1e-5 kg + 1e-3×processed exchange mass`, split the global physical time slab (with a material update at its new endpoint), select finer **independently stored** fixed response cells in a newly cooked profile, or refuse the full tick at the 16-slice/cell cap. Spatial clipping leaves alone do not undo shared-cell homogenization.

For diagnosis and any future tighter proof, enclose true **wet and dry** area-time separately for each response cell and compare both with the support/time used by the endpoint operator. Rate-times-uncertain-area-time estimates alone cannot certify the current uniform-subset operator when timed deposition and homogenization interact; the proven `2B` envelope remains the acceptance bound for those cases. Require the applicable chronology, source and arithmetic bounds to meet the local request, boundary displacement uncertainty ≤0.01 m away from critical topology, and exact protected-feature/topology classification. Interval geometry, derivative certificates and transient masks are charged to the same 65536-leaf/16-slice caps. This conservative fallback may reject ordinary rainy run-up on a mass-bearing cell because its starting-inventory term does not shrink with time; that workload needs a tighter chronological operator/proof or finer independent material state before promotion.

These bounds establish an implementable conservative approximation with declared local errors, not exact sinusoidal spacetime integration or a promise all PLAY waves fit the shore profile. Wave plots/normal detail within permanently wet support can use the cheaper branch. Headless executes this same CPU classification and exchange timeline; a GPU foam mask cannot certify an interior wet interval.

A candidate rejection leaves X[n], the world tick and every forcing/receipt cursor unchanged. Retain or deterministically reconstruct the exact `[t_n,t_n+1)` forcing plan/control revision for retry. Ordinary transient lack of products/scratch returns explicit backpressure/loading under F1. A terminal numerical/outside-regime fault stops the physical world with that interval still uncommitted; the game can restart or choose a reviewed provider/state migration. It must not skip to the next interval, call the failed rain delivered, or save an advanced source cursor with old water. Rain during a paused world follows F1's simulation-time pause policy; there is no hidden wall-clock precipitation debt.

## Conservation and local meaning

For the selected finite assembly define

`M = ρΣV_water + Σ(M_d+M_w+M_a) + M_transit + M_pending_water_exchange`.

Let `R` be direct rain input, `I/O` named external reservoir/boundary sources/sinks, and `E` vapour/export. A tick satisfies `M_new−M_old=R+I−O−E+C`, where C is the signed, explicitly recorded arithmetic-only mass correction. Internal dry↔wet reassignment, water↔coating, absorption, drain transit and finite-cell face flux cancel in pairs. Prescribed sea input/output is cumulative external ledger only; its infinite storage is not included in M. Do not count sea mouth transfer twice as rain and internal delivery.

The same identity is checked per source cell/first receiver, per material response cell, per water cell/aggregate and per boundary edge, including all incident exchanges. A globally balanced implementation that sends bank rain into a remote pond must fail. Record delivered volumes and use the same cached f64 transfer on donor and receiver; fixed-order/compensated totals accompany independently derived local arithmetic enclosures. Carry PLAY's rectangular-HLL tolerance only for its original arithmetic path. Capacity integration, quadrature, merge/split and coating add operations; derive and validate their own enclosure before admitting implementation, rather than pretending the old 256-stage bound covers them. Proposed initial scalar floor is `1e-9 kg` per material exchange and `1e-10 m³` per cell/step plus a derived rounding term; quadrature/model/split errors are separately reported quantities.

P3 traction reads committed material IDs and densities from X[n], including actual emerged film/absorption. New endpoint water/coating becomes authoritative with X[n+1]; P3 uses it next tick. The declared lag is at most one 1/60 s tick plus the within-water-substep split, not a camera-dependent delay. P5 closes wet/unsafe area entry using the committed dynamic-area generation; it may conservatively close earlier, but cannot continue through a stale wet frontier. Existing static Recast source-water masks are insufficient for moving R3 and require this generation/stop/replan path. New water/shore conditions do not grant swimming or a mixed-model hull passage forbidden by PLAY.

A coarse capacity cell still has one η, so adjacent flowing cells can have stage jumps even though their bed cut is fine. V2's default physical surface is the piecewise horizontal cell/aggregate plane clipped by the exact owning bed support; V3 optical rays/medium selection and CPU queries use that same branch/cell identity. Internal vertical stage seams require explicit side geometry/media ownership where visible, or rejection of a promised smooth-water presentation. Capacity detail alone is not a continuous free-surface reconstruction.

## Bounded cost and aggressive exact work removal

These are proposed workload limits and operation/byte estimates, not device timings. Reserve them before activation and charge old/new/save overlap. Initial combined closure: 64×32 capacity cells (2048), ≤32768 capacity polynomial records, ≤32768 convex exposure triangles, ≤8192 response fragments, ≤32 source rain cells, ≤64 drain edges, ≤16 prescribed **physical response** slices and ≤8 capacity flux steps per tick. Tide-only source-polynomial integration gets a separate proposed ≤131072 crossing triangle-segment evaluations/tick cap, excluding O(1) certified full/dry area branches; exceeding it refuses the interval rather than adding hidden physical updates. Its coefficient table, event cursor and f64 error enclosure need explicit F4/F2 byte and operation measurements before this cap can be called viable. Original HLL keeps its separate 8192-cell/four-step cap; running both maxima together requires a new combined admission, not two independent budget promises.

Store capacity records in a packed structure of sorted critical height, polynomial coefficients and error data, proposed 48 B/record =1.5 MiB. Atom geometry/IDs/classification, proposed 96 B/atom =3 MiB, plus indices/critical-level tables. Response three f64 masses at 8192 cells =192 KiB/endpoint; water V/p at 2048 cells =48 KiB/endpoint. No UUID/hash lookup, heap allocation, asset lease acquisition or virtual provider discovery per atom/face/receipt. Prebind source/product/consumer tables under the validated epoch.
- For repeatedly sampled **fixed** source positions, optionally precompute `cos(k_j·x+φ_j)` and `sin(k_j·x+φ_j)` and combine with `cos(ω_jt),sin(ω_jt)` evaluated once per component/requested source time. Positions are selected by actual repeated-query use and the cache key includes the immutable wave source. Derivatives reuse the same sin/cos pairs. Adaptive new positions/arbitrary contacts evaluate directly unless a valid fixed entry exists. This replaces transcendental evaluation with multiplies at repeated samples, but may lose to cache traffic; measure it. Interval extrema/derivative bounds remain analytic and independent of these samples. Share the small component/time table across CPU contact/rain extraction and provide the same source constants to optical evaluation rather than recomputing source phase per view.

## Creator and implementer walkthrough

A creator authors a 1:20 source bank, explicit basin/sill and roof/bridge, one liquid rain footprint and physical material recipes. Select prescribed sea for an imposed shore, the original matching terrace profile for a simple finite fixture, or the capacity profile for a finite smooth bank. Source admission rejects a capacity cell with disconnected bowls/protected internal sill and explains the offending source feature. F4 builds one pinned bed/patch/exposure/capacity/face/consumer closure and its error certificates; this is proposed tooling, not a current SDK command.

At first activation all physical products and response/water state are prepared off-world. W2 pins the closure and commits it under W1; P3/P5 entry opens last. Start dry film/coating/absorption at zero and finite water with a closed reflecting reservoir. Tick rain at constant source first, then raise volume through an authored inlet until water overtops the protected face sill. Flux fills the receiving cells; endpoint wet geometry transfers real film into coating/excess water. Stop rain while inlet/ebb continues. Newly exposed ground retains the actual coating; absorbed liquid and declared drainage tails continue. A main perspective and overview view show the same progression, while the headless variant records identical source/mass/support results.

An implementer can build the first fixture from these inputs without inventing a rain receiver, subcell storage law, wet/film exchange, inverse lookup, source split or retry policy.
