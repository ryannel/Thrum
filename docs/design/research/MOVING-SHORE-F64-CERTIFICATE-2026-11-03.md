## Restricted law and what is certified

The actual source triangle has height `h=x/20`, nonnegative source heights and true/horizontal metric `beta=sqrt(401)/20`. Surface liquid is represented by cohorts carrying projected density `q(h)=c+m h`, in kg/horizontal m². Canonical cap `r_w=0.1 kg/true m²` and refill `k_w=0.02 kg/(true m² s)` become `C=beta/10` and `K=beta/50`; rain remains per horizontal area. Initial density and resolved affine deposition use the same beta conversion. Static direct-first-hit eligibility gates direct rain; it does not gate true-area wet refill. Deposits occur at declared source-span endpoints. Wet crossing, dry accumulation, cap release and wet refill retain their earlier order. There is no absorption, evaporation, drainage, wave motion or finite-water grant.

For one receiver, the seven certified mass quantities are film, coating, solid rain, direct-water rain, refill, release and resolved deposition. These are cumulative final values over a fresh source episode. Each retains its kind rather than cancelling refill against release. The local field norm is

`E_density = integral |q_f64(h)-q_exact(h)| dA_horizontal`.

Because `rho_true=q/beta` and `dA_true=beta*dA_horizontal`, this is also the true-area density L1 mass error. It detects a local remix even when total inventory and exchange masses remain unchanged. The certificate does not bound pointwise maximum density or prove that a very small physical event is exactly absent. The candidate's separately rounded `total` convenience value is not an eighth certified quantity; total inventory should derive from the two certified stores with its own summation enclosure.

Let `[l_j,u_j]` enclose each exact mass, `m_j` be its candidate binary64 value, and `B_density` bound the field norm. Acceptance requires the **outward-rounded** sum

`B_mass = sum_j max(|m_j-l_j|,|m_j-u_j|)` and `up(B_mass+B_density) <= tolerance`.

The retained tolerance is the binary64 value produced by `1e-7`, in kg. It is a local experiment budget, not an allocation of the earlier whole-coast error budget. Multiplying it by 8192 receivers permits approximately `8.192e-4 kg` aggregate error; a world owner must allocate its own global budget and carry prior error before adoption.

## Candidate, verifier and independent falsifier

`float_candidate.py` translates the earlier bulk cohort formulas and polygon first moments to binary64. It discovers numerical cap roots, clips source triangles, sorts/splits cohorts, and merges only exactly equal adjacent functions. It uses no epsilon merge. Nonfinite roots, negative/nonfinite final masses, failed internal validity assertions and a ninth cohort refuse. Candidate rounding or even a wrong cap branch is not trusted: the independent verifier must bound its resulting state and exchanges.

`proof.py` separately replays point event histories using outward-rounded interval-affine fields on source-height panels. It does not call the exact oracle to construct acceptance intervals. Its scalar interval operators widen every nontrivial basic operation by `nextafter` in both directions, reject nonfinite endpoints, and refuse division by an interval containing zero. Exact-zero algebraic identities avoid needless subnormal widening. The documented [nextafter operation](https://docs.python.org/3/library/math.html#math.nextafter) supplies adjacent representable values; [Fraction conversion](https://docs.python.org/3/library/fractions.html#fractions.Fraction.from_float) exposes the exact rational value of a stored float. The implementation's soundness is conditional on correctly rounded binary64 basic arithmetic, adjacent `nextafter`, and gradual underflow on the executing target. The radix/mantissa/underflow checks are sanity checks, not platform qualification or a formal proof of the interpreter/compiler.

Rational inputs are enclosed by comparing their exact value with the exact rational represented by the nearest float and adding an adjacent endpoint where necessary. The sqrt result is merely a starting guess: rational squared comparisons prove lower² <= 401 <= upper², with at most eight adjacent refinements, before outward division by 20. The retained beta enclosure is `[1.0012492197250389, 1.0012492197250398]`. Cap, refill, initial state and deposition all use this same enclosed metric. This accounts for authored-to-binary64 conversion relative to the rational mathematical fixture; it does not assert that current F3 accepts the rational fixture strings.

The verifier's topology predicates are **exact rational source-cut comparisons**. They are proof-only until a bounded runtime encoding or independently qualified interval/predicate equivalent is selected. Panels begin at triangle critical heights, stage endpoints, deposition band boundaries and the exact rational values of numerical candidate boundaries. Constant stage is recognized from exact source equality and never divides by speed. For a genuine crossing, the affine crossing-time interval divides by the enclosed exact nonzero rational speed; a denominator enclosure touching zero refuses. The verifier does not guess numerical root order. It replays `min(q,C)` and `min(q+K dt,C)` directly: an interval sign proves the branch, or a range hull encloses both possibilities. Adaptive spatial refinement reduces the ambiguous band. Thus even a numerical root that falls on the wrong side of a cap is checked through field and mass error, rather than inheriting the candidate's branch assertion.

After an ambiguous min, a field enclosure need only be pointwise: `q(h)` lies between the interval-affine bounds; it need not have one fixed coefficient pair throughout that panel. Integration therefore uses proved **nonnegative source moments** `M0=integral dA` and `M1=integral h dA`, then encloses `c*M0+m*M1`. For a panel `[a,b]`, width `w(h)=w0+w1 h`, `d=b-a`:

`M0=d*w(a)+w1*d²/2`, `M1=a*M0+d²*w(a)/2+w1*d³/3`.

Both are outward evaluated and intersected with nonnegative bounds justified by the source geometry. This is not a clamp of physical mass. The source's height/width support is checked to be nonnegative. General negative-height geometry needs a different or shifted moment proof and is outside this executable.

The verifier bounds candidate-minus-enclosed-density over the entire panel and integrates its absolute upper bound against area. A global heap refines the panel contributing the largest width/field bound. There are at most 4096 panel evaluations and a `2^-80 m` height refinement floor; either exhaustion refuses. The final acceptance sum is outward rounded even though the heuristic used to select the next panel need not be. Unsupported source history, nonfinite arithmetic, unresolved denominator, failed error budget, coverage failure and cohort overflow leave no certified result. This standalone experiment emits a status; it does not mutate engine state or test an engine rollback.

Only after this decision does the exact archived `Q(sqrt401)` reference falsify it. That reference uses exact algebraic branch/root ordering; its independent space-first point-history oracle integrates the source-triangle width, rather than consuming the numerical candidate's moments or cohorts. All seven exact masses must lie in the generated intervals. A separate exact root-split integral of `|q_f64-q_oracle|` must lie below the field bound, and exact mass error must lie below `B_mass`. Failed oracle checks are hard failures outside the refusal handler. The exact arithmetic layer is shared with the archived reference, not independently reimplemented here; the archive's separate scalar-order witnesses remain relevant.

## Cost, source folding and residency

Fresh history is restricted to at most **16 compiled source spans**. Adjacent spans fold only if their exact stage slope, endpoints and P/K rates match and no deposit barrier intervenes. There is no approximate fit. The 60-step ordinary ramp folds from 60 to one; the two count-failure prefixes fold 361→7 and 240→4. A source with more than 16 irreducible spans refuses. This helps a cooked episode and is not a general 60 Hz streaming solution: changing rain, reversals, contacts, checkpoints, publications or independently consumed intermediate receipts can preserve barriers and make history grow.

Candidate cohort visits and geometric-integral counts are recorded separately in semantic results. Interval-operation counts include the verifier's arithmetic, but exclude source rational parsing/sorting, heap overhead and the exact falsifier. An interval multiply itself evaluates four endpoint products plus widening; an interval-operation count is not a CPU instruction count. Original and folded exact timings are both recorded, avoiding crediting the arithmetic backend for source folding. On the recorded Python/macOS arm64 host, the representative ramp costs approximately 0.6 ms candidate + 2 ms verifier, versus about 3 ms folded exact and 118 ms unfurled exact; mixed cap/deposit costs roughly 0.9 + 27 ms, versus 71 ms exact. These single-run illustrative timings are separate from deterministic results and are neither benchmark statistics nor Rust/device budgets.

For 8192 identical fragments the ordinary verifier alone scales to about **8.52 million interval operations per episode endpoint**, or 511 million/s if repeated at 60 Hz without additional reuse. The mixed fixture scales to about **178 million interval operations and 442368 panel evaluations** per endpoint. The 4096-evaluation guard is a safety bound, not a feasible all-fragment work budget. Independent geometry/field products may be reusable, but no runtime cache or batching result is proved. The numerical candidate is much cheaper than its certificate; this proof does not establish that per-fragment, every-tick replay is practical.

A prospective packed 64-byte numerical cohort budget gives `8192*8*64 = 4 MiB` per endpoint, 12 MiB for old/staged/save overlap. A value-plus-radius six-scalar layout with 16 bytes metadata would be 112 B/cohort: 7 MiB per endpoint, 21 MiB for the same three owners. These are proposed payload accounts inherited from the bridge, not this Python program's measured allocation. They reserve slots beyond the single active liquid law without proving additional stores. Five numerical receipts plus two endpoint masses cost 56 B/receiver; seven interval bounds another 112 B, about 1.313 MiB across 8192 before IDs, receipts' transaction metadata, scratch or owner duplication. Scratch can be processed one fragment at a time, but rational source cuts, Python objects and heap records have no claimed fixed-size production representation or measured RSS bound here. A practical implementation must separately charge cooked geometry, old/new world products, maximum 16 crossings/source spans, transient splits and all concurrent save/publication owners.

## Adoption boundary and next gate

The experiment achieves a conditional a posteriori certificate for the named **fresh, uniform-initial-state, bounded episodes**. It does not achieve a sound runtime certificate for arbitrary retained cohort state. No previous error enclosure is transported through a saved boundary; no per-tick receipt subtraction/cursor/deduplication or P7 serialization is verified. Fresh replay avoids those obligations rather than solving them. Rational topology predicates and rational source folding remain validator machinery until their encoding/cost and runtime equivalent are chosen. Untrusted source parser limits, encoded parameter admission, source revision authentication, engine failure atomicity and production numerical coverage checks remain separate requirements.

Before adoption, select an incremental representation with enclosed old density and boundary positions, carry each receiver's prior mass/field error, and prove its cap/refill update and interval receipts through reversal, save/restart and retry. Bind certificates to the complete source/material/geometry revisions, exact interval, candidate coefficient bits, all kind/destination exchanges, tolerance policy and algorithm version; do not use a final-inventory digest alone. Retain a finite global budget and explicit refusal before committing state or water grants. Then measure an independently implemented bounded runtime backend on the ordinary workload. This report proposes those gates; it supplies no canonical product or receipt format.

No sound extension to waves, finite water, nonuniform arbitrary initial fields, general slopes/negative heights, absorption, or a whole-coast streaming workload is claimed. The existing eight-cohort failures remain a reason to restrict admission or redesign the representation, even if a better numerical backend is later qualified.
