## World source, identity and authored controls

### State owners and units

`EnvironmentFrame` supplies world/epoch/time, light directions/irradiance and wind in m/s. The selected authored weather
source additionally supplies fronts/potential tracks and optional rain/lightning causes. One authority owns each
quantity; a cloud renderer neither advances weather nor invents thermodynamic state.

E1's cloud snapshot contains:

- immutable recipe/product revision, world seed, weather-storyboard revision and current source time;
- persistent formation IDs, family/master variant, birth/branch/death times, base/cap/size/optical controls and trajectory;
- low-layer region/cell identities, column amount/thickness/base and local creation/drying tracks;
- storm core/outflow lineage and ice-filament emission/cohort IDs;
- conservative support, source group/partition rules, source/material correspondence or explicit invalidity.

All geometry is in metres and source time in seconds. The canonical product is nonnegative extinction `sigma_t` in
m^-1 plus liquid/ice scattering weights, albedo and optional valid material identity/velocity. It is **not** image
alpha, water mass, electrical charge or rain flux. Authored sources are labelled optical proxies. A physical provider
may supply conserved fields through the same contract, but does not follow from naming an optical concentration.

For independent constituents add extinction/scattering once. For several products representing **one condensate**,
compose their source geometry/partition before publishing coefficients: adjacent same-layer cells use one continuous
field; a storm's core/outflow overlap has nonnegative weights summing to one. Two quality levels are never added as
two clouds. Independently authored overlapping air/cloud constituents may genuinely add, according to their declared
group semantics. Source IDs persist through visual merging without requiring a new topological object ID every frame.

### Weather and population producer

The initial proof profile uses an immutable, editable **authored storyboard** with spatial fronts and family controls;
play, pause, seek and1x/16x/64x sample that same source timeline. Parameter edits validate/cook/restart, following
Thrum's initial stop/load policy. General seamless live model changes are not silently promised.

A front is a moving world corridor, with position `p_f(t)`, direction, width and local potential contribution
`P_f(x,t)=A_f smoothstep(-w/2,w/2,n_f dot(x-p_f(t)))`. Wetting and drying tracks are separate signed influences.
`P(x,t)` controls local formation conditions. It never multiplies the final sky image or every existing density.

Stable world cells have jittered candidate sites from `(world_seed, layer, integer_cell)`. Candidate birth episodes
use `(cell, episode, slot)` identities and hashed times/variants; no camera participates. A candidate is admitted when
its local forcing at its birth time allows it. Lifetimes/renewal/drying are event-local, not one synchronized sky clock.
For authored linear fronts and constant/height-linear layer winds, arrival/departure times can be solved from relative
front/source motion. More general stories cook bounded event/forcing tracks before play. Seeking evaluates these tracks
or deterministically replays the source cursor; it does not infer old births from the current image.

Coverage is an outcome of support. For isolated populations, `c≈1-exp(-lambda A_mean L_mean)` is a planning estimate
for Poisson births, not an image-opacity law. Actual source/sky union coverage is measured. As cover approaches one,
spawning isolated Cu is structurally unsuitable: the connected low-layer state closes gaps instead. Candidate capacity
is an explicit admission limit; overflow rejects/defer new requested work, never silently kills an existing retained
formation. Virtual far candidates remain computable from the same storyboard/cell IDs without allocating every source.

Existing material/event state is preserved when forcing changes. A convective source can take a deepening branch from
its current head/body; a low layer changes its local amount/topology continuously. Incompatible source replacement
restarts a validated epoch. Replacing arbitrary endpoint skies with a blend is not a supported weather transition.

### Wind and motion, exactly once

Shared wind is evaluated at the material's height. Internal plume rise/outflow and ice fall speed are residual source
motion; they are not a second application of reference wind. In a local source map `x=a(t)+A(t)xi`,
`v_world=a_dot+A_dot xi+A v_source phi_dot`. A baked reference-wind contribution is subtracted before the runtime anchor
adds the actual shared wind. A tall storm cannot use one rigid base-height drift as its entire shear model.

Proof winds are bounded constant or piecewise-smooth height-linear tracks. Source trajectories use analytic integrals
where possible, otherwise cooked Hermite/RK trajectories with explicit position/error bounds and C1 joins. No view
integrates its own position/age. Changing wind in an authored story bends trajectories from their existing state;
reseed/teleport is rejected. Exact global inverse motion is not claimed: surviving cohorts provide local correspondence;
new/eroding/overlapping-dominance regions declare invalidity rather than zero or fabricated rigid velocity.

## Content producer and recoverable input

The editable authoring unit is a formation/layer/filament **with a lifecycle and causes**, not a volume plus fade curve.
A recipe records schema/seed, family/variant, dimensions/base/cap, source/loss tracks, material-detail scale, optical
calibration, allowed controls/branches, source group/partition, reference wind, bounds and permitted representation levels.
Cook-time admission requires H_max>0, M_star>0, a positive detail/core normalization Z for M>0, width w0>0 and a
positive width law, nonsingular anisotropic transforms, nonnegative emission rates and0<=loss<=1. Emission integrals
are causal: s lies in [max(s_start,t-a_max),min(t,s_stop)] with a=t-s>=0 and exactzero after the bounded retirement age;
an empty interval emits zero. Every family uses this domain. Values outside the admitted coefficient range reject/
recook the recipe or require a new range/precision profile; silent sigma clamping changes optics and is prohibited.
Cooked products preserve recipe/detail dependencies and hashes. The current Q1 source/master remains recoverable and
unchanged; it is a certified small-Cu comparator/provider, not the new whole-sky source format.

The chosen first producer is an evaluable **authored control graph**. It can be sampled/cooked into final density
fields or retained as a compact recipe. Difficult content can instead adopt an actual offline fluid/artist master
through the same coefficient interface, after its defining lifecycle passes. The first graph is not declared proven
because its equations are compact. Its characteristic risk—smooth blobs/shells/artificial cuts—has explicit rejection
witnesses before large sky assembly. Bakes cannot supply a missing family mechanism by themselves.

The initial procedural branch has a finite grammar and concrete tracks: a small filled parent, overlapping staggered
daughters, compact growth/hold/terminal curves and a spatial drying front. Seed ranges, maximal node count and smallest
accepted feature are cook-time constraints. This choice does not erase the earlier warning that lobe kernels can look
like smooth blobs: they are a **candidate producer** whose visual rejection precedes adoption. Prepared final-density
masters remain a replacement at this seam if the complete connected graph fails, not an excuse to leave lifecycle
unspecified. The defined first graph is what the isolated proof tests.

One fixed shared detail volume provides filtered erosion/roughness in persistent physical material coordinates.
A source seed selects offsets/orientations and graph structure, not a new random texture each frame. Growth exposes new
material through a spatial front; detail is not uniformly stretched with the whole formation. Geometry-support and
local source/loss operators provide creation, merging and breakup. A minimum core/body fill prevents a noise-only hollow
birth scaffold. Fine detail is reduced only under an admitted footprint/error regime, never to replace gross family form.

## Evolution and motion equations

### Convective Cu and towering/Cb master

A connected graph has a persistent lower condensation body and staggered daughter billow cohorts. A cohort records
birth time, parent/material attachment, reference shape/orientation, trajectory, radius-growth track, hold interval,
exposed-edge/drying-front track and detail coordinates. Different seeds vary graph branching, lobe count/offsets,
width/height, asymmetry and timing; four instances must visibly differ in gross morphology, not only yaw/scale.

In physical local coordinates let
`m_i=clamp(1-|diag(R_i(t))^-1(q-c_i(t))|,0,1)` and
`rho_i=sigma_0 smoothstep(0,e_width,m_i-eta_i(q,t)(1-m_i)^p-D_i(q,t))`.
Here `eta>=0`, `p>0`, `e_width>0`; no inverse is evaluated when any radius component is zero. A zero-radius
birth contributes zero; integrated optical support tends continuously to zero, without claiming pointwise C1 at
its centre. New children are embedded inside an existing filled parent before they emerge.
`eta` samples fixed material detail plus a bounded local drying front. The deep core is protected during birth;
erosion is strongest at the boundary. Birth core protection is explicitly released by a **separate spatial drying
threshold**: `D_i=(1+e_width)*smoothstep(t_stop+delta_i(q),t_stop+delta_i(q)+duration_i,t)`.
The material/exposure-time field delta is bounded and duration is positive. By the last departure time this threshold
exceeds every m, including the centre, so density is exactly zero everywhere. Its moving spatial front creates local
loss rather than multiplying the finished formation by one opacity. The old protected-centre-only rule was invalid.
`R_i` grows from a small filled core, with a fixed reference detail metric rather
than normalized-radius texture stretching. Parents overlap daughters before daughters emerge from the existing body;
a child starts inside its parent's support. This rejects disconnected sphere popping. A max/union of same-condensate
cohorts creates no phantom positive bridge between disjoint supports. A smooth base mask clips the condensation level.

Radius alone is not the lifecycle: new cohorts are born at distinct positions/times, head trajectories rise, older
cohorts renew/erode and exposed spatial fronts cut support into fragments. Mature sources emit staggered new lobes
while older edge material disappears. When feeding stops, a spatial drying/entrainment front and local detail remove
older/exposed regions first; detached remnants have their own terminal tracks. Density reaches exact zero at finite
retirement. Global opacity decay is not the breakup mechanism. This is authored condensation/entrainment, not buoyancy
or water conservation. The implied local residual `D rho/Dt` has creation/loss; it is not hidden in shading.

Fair Cu has a shallow cap and comparatively broad lower body. A deepening event retains the same lower body and ID;
the prescribed rising head and future child births continue above the former shallow cap. The head approaches the
new cap with a continuous velocity track. No transform stretches all old density upward. Tower clusters have different
ages/widths and height-dependent wind tilt. Typical source lifetimes are minutes/tens of minutes; normal witnesses
inspect meaningful subintervals, while complete accelerated histories expose topology and repetition.

At cap arrival, the **same event** emits upper outflow. The first producer uses **forward emitted cohorts**, not
an inverse radius lookup under arbitrary shear. For launch time s and sector angle theta, a cap outlet launches a
finite-width ring/strip from its actual position. Radius grows as `r(a)=r0+v_out*a` for age a; r0 is positive. Each
sector follows the shared height-dependent wind plus its prescribed horizontal outflow, with a declared cap/thickness
track. Cooked C1 trajectories integrate moving/tilted outlets and shear. A compact normalized swept kernel deposits
each cohort's optical amount; integrate continuous emission or a quadrature with an explicit spacing/error bound:
`sigma_out(x,t)=int Q_sigma(s) K_normalized(x;launch(s),t-s) loss(s,x,t) ds`.
Q_sigma has units m²/s, the 3D kernel has units m^-3, and the result has units m^-1. The precise ring kernel is
`K_normalized(x;s,a)=int_0^2pi f_theta(s,theta) K3(x-X(s,theta,t);A(s,theta,a)) dtheta`, where f_theta>=0 integrates
to1 and each anisotropic compact K3 integrates to1 using its determinant factor. X is the forward centre trajectory.
This integrates emitted amount, not inverse radius; expanding rings automatically dilute through their longer arc.
Folds/multiple launches add their actual transported amount rather than requiring a unique inverse. A segment/strip
optimization uses the launch-to-arclength Jacobian explicitly and must match this kernel integral. Multiple overlapping launches accumulate their emitted
amount once within the outflow producer. Emit/sector spacing stays below half the narrowest accepted kernel width;
independent fine sampling checks gaps/beads and quadrature convergence.

Emission starts continuously when the tracked head reaches its cap. Seeded sector rates, asymmetric outlet tracks,
fibres and edge widths produce an irregular spreading anvil instead of an instant disk. Outflow inherits source/
material lineage. A special fixed co-moving radial case can use an analytic inverse only when that restricted mapping
and its Jacobian are independently verified; it is not the general producer. After feeding stops, older emitted
material continues outward/shears and retires locally, rather than deleting the anvil with the core.

Core/outflow partition weights sum to one in their shared junction. An authored ice fraction increases through the
upper transition; it splits scattering weights without adding duplicate extinction. Feeding shutdown erodes the core
before the longer-lived outflow, and outflow can leave provenance-preserving high-cloud filaments after the lower tower
is gone. Cb must show core→vertical tower→cap-driven spreading anvil→differential decay in its isolated proof. If the
cohort master cannot do that convincingly, replace this content producer with an accepted connected transition master;
keep the optical/consumer contracts and reject the failed source.

### Broken Sc and layered St/overcast master

A persistent reference layer chart chi has a **height-dependent wind map** F_h(chi,t), supplied by the same
wind authority as other material. At world query (x,h), invert the admitted analytic/cooked map to chi_h; coefficients
use that material column. Within-layer shear is therefore not replaced by base-height rigid motion. Proof winds have
no vertical low-layer flow and use analytic height-dependent rotations/translations; more general maps require a
bounded inverse/error product before admission. Source-driven thickness/base changes remain explicit creation/loss,
with correspondence invalid where a surviving material mapping cannot be established.

A cell/roll pattern and localized wetting/drying fronts produce reference-column optical amount M(chi,t)>=0,
base b(chi,t) and thickness H(chi,t). The concrete producer is
`M=max(0,P(F_base(chi,t),t)-theta+A_cell C(chi)+sum_j source_j-sum_k dry_k)`.
World forcing is pulled back through the base map, not sampled as P(chi,t), which would incorrectly add front/wind
speeds. Cell/region IDs remain fixed when coverage changes. Cell-size/reseed edits require a new validated recipe.
M is the normalized optical amount in the reference column; under appreciable vertical shear, a world-vertical ray
crosses different material columns and its actual optical depth must be integrated, not assumed equal to M.

Choose `H=H_max sqrt(clamp(M/M_star,0,1))` and a nonnegative compact vertical profile `P_z` of unit integral. For `M>0`,
`sigma=M/H P_z((h-b)/H) d(chi,h-b)/Z`, with `Z=int_0^1 P_z(s)d(chi,Hs)ds`; at `M=0`, define zero without division.
The fixed physical-height detail `d` has a positive core floor. This normalization makes M the intended reference-column optical amount before shear, rather than changing it arbitrarily as thickness varies. It is a specified column closure,
not per-frame normalization of an unrelated animation. Nodes can be cooked into anisotropic maps/slabs; the definition
is the source, not whatever samples happen to fit a texture.

Sc uses stronger cellular/roll relief and irregular connectivity: wetting fills flattened domes and joins neighbouring
regions; drying opens/widens holes and leaves shallow banks. St uses weak relief and a more uniform base/connected
sheet. Sc→St keeps M/material IDs, changes local source/drying/relief continuously and fills existing gaps; it does not
spawn a second opaque sky over the first. St breakup lowers local amount/thickness and opens true finite holes/fractus.
A mature hold requires asynchronous compact source/dry episodes per persistent cell, not only a translating fixed C.
For episode j, `source_j=A_j K_j(chi,t) sin²(pi*u_j)` for0<u_j<1 and zero outside; u_j=(t-t_j)/duration_j.
K_j is a moving compact spatial kernel with a seeded trajectory/size. Dry episodes use the same bounded envelope
with independently staggered onset/duration/position, typically following a wet episode locally. Storyboard seed/cell/
episode hashes pin the tracks. The first recipes use300–900s durations and staggered pairs, maintaining roughly steady
aggregate cover while individual holes/thickness renew. All world-defined tracks use the same F pullback.
Exponential decay alone is insufficient for exact termination; compact terminal fronts set cleared regions to zero.
Where a column cannot represent an undercut/overturned local feature, use a resolved 3D local source under the same
layer owner. Do not force deep convection into this single-valued sheet model.

### High Ci master

An emitting head/tuft trajectory continuously releases ice cohorts, with birth time, source lineage and a smooth
reference curve. For cohort age a, `dX_h/dt=U_h(X,h,t)`, `dh/dt=U_vertical-v_f(a)` and
`w(a)^2=w0^2+2D_a*a` for the declared authored support-width law (not a microphysical diffusion claim). Shared wind
shear plus prescribed settling creates trailing hooks/fall streaks. The selected evaluator integrates emission time:
`sigma(x,t)=int Q_sigma(s) K3(x-X(t;s);w(t-s)) loss(s,x,t) ds`.
Q_sigma is m²/s. Use the finite normalized spherical kernel
`K3(r;w)=315/(64pi*w^3)*(1-r²/w²)^3` for r<w, zero outside; anisotropic support divides by its actual determinant.
Thus no infinite Gaussian hides outside conservative bounds. Local sublimation/drying and compact terminal tracks
reduce different emitted sections independently.

A separately calibrated line-kernel authoring mode may use
`K2(r;w)=3/(pi*w²)*(1-r²/w²)^2` for r<w, zero outside, with line optical amount A_sigma in metres.
It is not automatically the identical continuous-emitter source: converting requires the launch-time-to-arclength
Jacobian `A_sigma=Q_sigma/|dX/ds|` and a checked transverse profile. Degenerate launch speed uses the continuous3D
kernel, never a division by zero. The first proof pins the continuous-emitter mode and its convergence oracle.

Polyline/cohort spacing must be below half the narrowest accepted transverse width, with a smooth longitudinal join.
Bundles have different head paths, widths, ages, rates and settling/shear, not just rotations of one streak texture.
Seeded material perturbations are attached to surviving strands. Stopping a head does not delete its old tail; local
sublimation/drying separates and retires sections at different times. A source-wide alpha fade is rejected. This is
an authored optical ice model, not nucleation, ice microphysics or halo/phase-function validation. A Cb anvil can feed
these cohorts with the same parent lineage; detachment never recreates a random unrelated high sky.

## Shared current field and portable GPU layout

`SourceOptics(world_position, source_time, footprint)` returns sigma, liquid/ice scattering weights, support, identity,
representation/error/validity and optional correspondence. The canonical cause is independent of camera. Footprint
chooses a qualified approximation, not new events or phases. Source groups union/partition once; independent groups
sum once. A shared spatial index includes all main/reflection/light/environment/semantic requirements.

Resolved convective content uses an atlas of current scalar fields/mips, with source-specific anisotropic extents.
Low layers retain column maps/profile parameters; Ci retains a segment/curve hierarchy and optional sampled resolved
patches. The initial source graph is evaluated at the requested source time; it does not require a huge dense4D clip
library. Existing prepared clips decode through their own admitted evaluator. Source field rebuilds and shader/texture
sampling must use the same product generation before any view consumes them.

**Portable scalar candidate:** RGBA16F storage packs four consecutive logical Z cells per texel, rather than storing
one useful channel and copying a packed buffer to an R16 texture. XY filtering is hardware-linear; Z interpolation
loads the appropriate channels at the two centred Z-group slices. Do not hardware-interpolate packed groups as if each
were one voxel. Each logical mip is built by averaging the actual eight parent coefficients with explicit half
rounding; atlas guards and cross-group Z boundaries are tested independently. Logical voxel centres are integer i+0.5: for logical boundary-space coordinate u (voxel centre i+0.5), set q=u-0.5, z0=floor(q), z1=z0+1 and interpolation weight fract(q);
for each neighbour use group=floor(z/4), channel=z-4group, and sample each group at its physical Z centre with XY filtering. Outside logical
support contributes zero through explicit guards. Negative indices use mathematical floor, not truncation. Physical
Z length is ceil(logicalZ/4), plus guards; unused channels/odd-size padding are zero. Each level has its own dimensions,
XY one-voxel filter halo and group guards. Child dimensions are ceil(parent/2); world voxel pitch doubles at each level. An odd last block is zero-padded,
so the child sampling extent grows to childDimensions*childPitch instead of compressing those samples back into the
un-padded parent extent. Tiny/odd dimensions use explicit logical parents with zero-support boundary rules; never native mipmapping of packed channels. Level0 plus two additional logical levels is the three-level profile.
This uses ideally2logical bytes/voxel and two filtered fetches per integer mip before guard/padding overhead. It is a selected implementation candidate, **not yet a measured improvement**.
The current one-channel RGBA16F reference remains the golden fallback until exact sampling and image witnesses pass.
Rounding is IEEE half round-to-nearest/ties-even at every declared store, checked against CPU half across the admitted
0..0.1m^-1 coefficient range and subnormals. Point optical queries use level0 unless a separately admitted error envelope
permits coarsening. Coefficient-average mips serve filtered queries only after coverage/transmittance witnesses pass.

Field resolution is selected against the smallest defining feature and the admitted view/light footprint;128³ is
not a universal limit. A large close formation may require256³ or direct canonical evaluation. A source-brick candidate
list permits a fine query to evaluate the actual local graph/detail when a coarse field cannot meet its error envelope.
A coarse field cannot recover missing grain by trilinear upsampling. The shared source recipe remains the cause and
all approximations report their admitted domain; semantic queries never depend on the camera-selected cache.

Pool handles contain logical dimensions, physical atlas offsets/guards, mip offsets, source bounds/transform and
source/time/revision. They are not event identity. Field/cache sharing keys include every quantity affecting values;
referencing one recipe at two ages does not mean one current field. Dirty support includes newly born/vanished density
and filter halos. Admission/lifetime follow V1/F4 leases; no overwrite of in-flight pages.

The inner128km workload is not the entire horizon population. Far candidates use a deterministic world-cell/episode
namespace and cooked forcing tracks; only cells intersecting a ray/support request are materialized. Source shell
intervals bound low/high layers before cell traversal; a conservative hierarchical cover bound skips clear regions.
Virtual lookup includes **birth-to-current reach**, not only current kernel radius: each admitted recipe records
maximal lifetime/displacement/residual outflow/support. A source-cell/time hierarchy conservatively expands birth-region
bounds by the full trajectory reach, or backtraces the request under the shared wind and expands by bounded residual
motion/shear/support. Traverse all intersecting birth cells and active episode times, including face neighbours, then
deduplicate canonical IDs. Epoch/story tracks bound active age (initial master cap7200s); no camera births or omission
of a source that drifted in from another cell. Tests include a moving source born outside the queried cell/face,
source support spanning a face corner and zero-opacity periods followed by regrowth. Request/candidate limits are
visible admission failures rather than arbitrary truncation of the birth window.
At high opacity, a derived radiance/transmittance bound can stop further integration, but a truncated tau is returned
as a bound/status, never mislabeled exact. Virtual-source evaluation and candidate/capacity counters are measured.

For distant source queries, stable virtual cells/episodes are evaluated on demand and coarse source modes omit only
sub-footprint detail. Analytic shell/layer bounds skip empty atmosphere before traversing candidate source cells.
Convective far evaluation keeps gross cohort structure; low layers keep their actual column profile; Ci keeps its
swept filaments at an admitted footprint. No density average may replace clear/cloud mixture without checking
transmittance/coverage (`E[exp(-tau)] != exp(-E[tau])`). Use deterministic cone samples or a separately qualified
coverage/transfer summary when unresolved gaps matter. Near/far transitions refer to the same event/material and count
one representation contribution. Camera movement cannot reseed a field, move weather fronts or reveal a hard cache edge.

## Camera, reflection and numerical envelope

### SKY-G extended ground-sky profile

The baseline short-range air and local4km RC proof stay valid in their own domains. The required full-sky profile
selects a shared terrestrial **sky frame**: planet centre/radius, local tangent transform and radial altitude, with
cloud sources, atmosphere, far ground and Sun/planet visibility using that same frame. This is a visual/environment
profile, not a new planetary gameplay engine. Main full-sky cameras support0–1536m altitude and perspective/orthographic

## Light projection and joint transport

At a sample, sum all constituent extinction and source terms before the Beer affine update. Separate liquid/ice
scattering weights permit their declared phase closures; do not assign the nearest cloud family's phase to all overlap.
For each light `E_direct=E_light V_geometry exp(-tau_air-tau_fog-tau_all_cloud)`. Surface receivers, air/fog and cloud
primary source use the same incident light. Light disabling does not remove density/source age. All illumination and
emission zero produces zero radiance while extinction persists.

The first extended cloud optical profile uses a **fixed energy-budgeted mixture of effective scattering paths**:
`j_cloud=sigma_s [E_noncloud sum(n=0..3,w_n b_n p(g_n) exp(-b_n tau_cloud)) + L_external]`.
E_noncloud is source irradiance times geometry/air/fog attenuation, **excluding** cloud attenuation already in the sum.
All cloud constituents use the same fixed weights w=[.7,.2,.07,.03], sum1, and b=[1,.5,.25,.125]; these are explicit
starting appearance parameters, not fitted physics. Liquid phase starts g=[.86,.43,.215,.1075]; ice starts with the same
declared ice phase for each branch rather than imposing liquid isotropization on thin wisps. Phase functions normalize
to one, sigma_s<=sigma_t, and ordinary extinction/direct-receiver transmittance remain unchanged.

The b_n prefactor matters: along a beam, each branch's integrated generated cloud-scatter power is bounded by w_n
of incident power (`int b_n exp(-b_n tau) d tau<=1`). Fixed common weights across cloud mixtures avoid arbitrary
local weight changes allocating the beam twice. This replaces the unbounded added-orders/B_local proposal: its thick
homogeneous slab emitted more than twice incident upward power at5degree Sun in independent quadrature. The new
mixture weights its primary term instead of claiming physical single scattering plus free extra energy. Its thin-limit angular-integrated source is sum(w*b)=.82125 of ordinary single scattering (about17.9% loss).
For equal ice phases the directional ratio is also.82125. Liquid phases differ: the directional ratio is
sum(w*b*p_n(theta))/p_0(theta), with both gains and losses by angle; front/backlit thin-Cu witnesses must qualify it.
It can underlight or redistribute optically thin cloud and remains an appearance approximation; air/fog interactions and diffuse-source
energy are not made physically exact by the cloud-only bound. Analytic thick/thin slab, all-media energy/reference,
low-Sun and native thick/ice/overcast witnesses must qualify it. The historical Cu b=.2/unconditional-order profile
remains reproducible unchanged. No claim of converged multiple scattering follows.

All branches use the same ordinary cumulative cloud tau, with branch scaling; this avoids intrinsic lighting changing
with a camera-selected density mip. A future world-scale filtered/scattering field requires its own source/error/cost
qualification. If the energy-bounded candidate fails required appearance, correct this closure at its seam using an
accepted reference rather than changing extinction or relabelling a failed storm base as complete.

supports, not one receiver plane or independent per-family shadow maps. Each curve stores depth and ordinary cumulative cloud tau; it can answer a sample inside a cloud, below an anvil or between layers. All layers along the same Sun
ray contribute. Cascades each estimate the entire source-facing path; overlap selects/blends estimates, never adds
identical upstream extinction twice. Outside a projection is clear only if conservative source projection bounds prove it.

samples into sorted depth knots. The fit metric bounds primary `exp(-tau)` **and** `exp(-b_n tau)` for every effective
branch, not only primary T that is already black. The bounded streaming builder appends integration endpoints, then removes the interior knot with the smallest
maximum deviation in those transformed terms when capacity is exceeded. Evaluate deviation at interval endpoints
and any interior extrema of the difference of exponentials of the old/new linear-tau segments. Accumulate removed
error with the triangle bound, plus separate quadrature/storage error; do not claim the area heuristic alone bounds
