# Cloud POC acceptance and definition of done

2 November2026. Required companion to [the checked build plan](CLOUD-MIGRATION.md), not another execution queue.
The [selected baseline](CLOUD-PRODUCTION-BASELINE.md) fixes mechanisms; optional experiments are not acceptance gates.
All new migration POCs remain unbuilt/unrun. A component or planning checkbox cannot promote family quality, watched
motion, target performance or engine maturity. The [capability ledger](CLOUD-CAPABILITIES.md) owns those outcomes.

## Common proof packet

Before constructing each P stage/substage, pin a short proof card in its evidence package:

1. Capability/decision and question; predicted visible result; plausible wrong substitute the test must reject.
2. Prerequisite passed scopes, executable launch/replay command or URL, immutable source/build/scenario/seed and actual
   loaded identity. Record domain/capacity, source/weather event IDs and requested/displayed product time.
3. Scene, cameras/projection/DPR/resolution, exposure/light/profile, source clock/rates and source/consumer controls.
   Match these across comparisons; pin reference stills/motion spans and comparability limits. Keep a held-out
   comparator per family/weather story; stochastic cloud geometry is judged by cues, not photographic pixel equality.
4. Native beauty and necessary diagnostic views, tagged local regions/events and negative-control toggle. Numerical
   norms, ROIs/footprints/tolerances, timing scope and pass criteria are declared before inspecting the candidate.
   Distinguish model/appearance error from quadrature/codec/history error; do not relax an observed failed gate.
5. Required stills and contiguous motion spans. Observe actual playback at1x and accelerated rates for motion gates:
   normal spans cover meaningful growth, mature renewal and breakup; extend until the named cue can be judged, not a
   brief frozen interval. Watch the full accelerated lifecycle/transition. Record observer, source and playback times,
   duration, speed and actual verdict. A saved recording, sampled frames or contact sheet is not watched motion.
6. Mechanism and consumer receipts, same-place/time identity, numeric/reference results, visual cue verdicts, cost and
   physical-memory scope, adverse cases and failures. Separate capture/probe runs from exclusive timing runs.
7. Disposition per capability: pass/fail/unrun/unqualified, exact scope and next correction. Each check names evidence.
   If available tools only expose sampled frames, say so and leave continuous-motion acceptance unrun.

Show ordinary beauty beside diagnostics, not instead of it. Averages cannot compensate for a failed defining cue.
The agent may supply engineering judgement while the user is away; user aesthetic acceptance remains distinct.

## Status vector and advancement

For each stage record **implementation | contract/numerical | visible quality | observed motion | coupled consumers |
cost-risk screen | actual-device admission**. Use pass/fail/unrun/unqualified/not-applicable with evidence. Required
columns vary by stage, fixed on its proof card. A helper may have a checked numerical task while the P stage remains
partial. A whole stage is done only for its declared scope when all required columns pass. Unavailable hardware is not
an automatic failed algorithm, but target admission remains unqualified and cannot be included in a green cost box.

Small source/reference/conformance stages can advance on their explicit scoped outputs, with remaining risks visible.
P4a needs passed coupling/visual/motion scope plus a quantified cost-risk disposition before broad variants. A structural
failure blocks dependent production and changes the governing design. P7 can accept full-sky fixture quality/cloud cost,
not the actual engine or real water; P8 owns integrated product/frame/memory admission.

## Visual decision scorecard

Record each indispensable cue separately: family recognition/scale, local structural birth, mature renewal, local loss,
continuity/advection/shear, silhouette/internal light, clear-air/horizon, source-shadow/ambient/reflection alignment and
temporal stability. Use pass/fail/unrun plus a short observed reason and source-time span. A failed required cue blocks
that capability; an aggregate similarity score cannot conceal it. Reference fit may be approximate due to FOV/exposure/
weather differences, which are documented rather than repaired by retrospective threshold changes.

For every variant/story, preserve at least its declared beauty/key diagnostic/native images, relevant personally
watched motion intervals and failed negative control where feasible. Not every variant needs the entire adverse Sun/
camera Cartesian product; the representative full matrix plus basic per-variant consumer routes prevents four-times
content from multiplying unrelated benchmark work while still exposing variant-specific defects.
