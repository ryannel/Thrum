# Production cloud technique study (2026-11-02)

Scope: independent research on cloud content, lighting, rendering, reconstruction and downstream consumers. No prototype or existing cloud design was read before this initial shortlist. All choices below are provisional until primary-source and source-code inspection.

## Initial independent target and shortlist

Required outcomes from commission: Cu, Cb including anvil, Sc, St and Ci with recognizable silhouettes and optical character; growth, maturity and breakup; coherent wind/weather; a main camera plus independently timed reflected and environment consumers. Browser WebGPU/M2 candidate envelope: 1280×720, clouds 5 ms within 16 ms frame and 512 MiB whole browser. Native candidate: clouds 3 ms within 10 ms. These are targets, not demonstrated performance.

1. **Candidate production core:** authored, weather-driven 2.5D cloud fields and cloud-type-specific vertical profiles, with sparse/clipmapped or region-cooked density and macrostructures; low-resolution physically motivated single-scattering render, cached multi-scatter/ambient approximation, temporal reconstruction and separate low-frequency shadow/environment products. The state/weather authority must remain camera independent.
2. **Challenger:** true sparse volumetric cloud objects/tiles with authored growth graphs and type-specific density generators. Better local towers and anvils, but residency, traversal and independent view costs may dominate.
3. **Challenger:** procedural analytic/implicit primitives with ray-cone filtering. Could reduce storage, yet layered banks, organic continuity and multi-consumer quality may demand as much expensive evaluation as a field representation.
4. **Reference/fallback:** impostor/billboard and artist-authored cards or shell rendering for distant Ci/Sc/St. This can dominate some profiles but does not alone establish coherent transitions or near-view parallax.

Provisional system concern: optimize the *number of density evaluations over all views and history invalidations*, not one isolated ray evaluator. Cloud-class morphology, weather evolution and shared light/shadow data may matter more than a locally clever scattering integral. This hypothesis will be challenged against production sources.

## Recommended production model: weather authority + cooked class morphology + bounded render products

**Decision status: provisional recommendation, research-supported and unmeasured.** Keep the original independent 2.5D core for far sky, but upgrade it from a single coverage/type noise sheet to **typed, evolving cloud populations**. Make sparse prepared voxel objects a specialized near Cb/Cu path, rather than a mandatory globe-scale representation. This hybrid captures the strongest demonstrated economies: 2015 weather/authoring, 2023 prepared morphology and acceleration where needed, 2020 BSM, and 2019/2021 shared environment products. It does not claim any one shipped engine uses this exact assembly.

**Authoring/cook.** A content recipe per genus contains a horizontal organization field, altitude span and vertical extinction envelope, anisotropic base/detail noise or a prepared coarse 3D field, erosion/detail type, optical coefficients, and staged lifetime curves. Cu: separated cauliflower lobes with mostly flat base and discrete updraft towers; Cb: growing vertical core with broad sheared anvil and dark precipitating underside; Sc: connected shallow rolls/cells with gaps; St: broad smooth lamina with soft variable base; Ci: high sparse wind-aligned wisps. Use measured photo/reference silhouettes to accept these recipes; a shared Perlin/Worley threshold is only a detail operator. For Cb, prepare a small library of editable fluid/mesh/particle-derived 3D core/anvil archetypes (Nubis³ modeling precedent), sampled through compressed low-frequency fields plus procedural detail. Offline cook class-specific conservative occupancy/distance bounds and mip min/max extinction. Preserve source recipes and exact tool/license pins; no bulk content is commissioned here.

**World state.** Weather owns coarse fields in world coordinates: horizontal wind by altitude, humidity/convective potential, front/lift, precipitation propensity and high-level shear, plus authored biases. Each persistent cloud population/feature has stable ID, genus, position/extent, age, moisture/condensation budget, maturity, precipitation and seeded morphology. Advect centers and coarse fields using shared wind. Evolve *structure*: for Cu add/remove child lobes from updraft and entrainment, alter vertical reach and connectivity; for Cb grow tower, spread anvil in upper shear, then rain-out/erode core; for Sc merge/split cell boundaries; for St vary sheet depth/coverage; for Ci stretch/shear/tear filaments. Smooth envelope transitions conserve identity and prevent popped clouds. This is an authored, meteorology-informed state machine, **not** a resolved fluid solver; directability is permitted. An age scalar alone cannot produce topology or vertical growth.

**Density reconstruction.** For feature `i`, use coarse authored profile/field `P_i(x,t)` and prepared base noise `N_i` with weather-dependent threshold `b_i`: `rho_i = sigma_i(t) * smoothstep(b_i-w_i, b_i+w_i, P_i - erosion_i(N_i,detail_i))`, `rho = sum_i rho_i` with explicit overlap ownership/cap. Class envelope specifies altitude and shape. The exact operators are proposed; independently validate silhouettes and motion. Evaluate low-frequency occupancy first, then detail only where needed. Mip/LOD selection follows projected footprint, and near cloud special cases use conservative SDF/occupancy skips. Because erosion can add density outside a coarse bound, the bound must include maximum detail displacement or skipping creates holes. Compression error `epsilon` requires skip `max(0,d_sdf-epsilon-step_guard)`, never raw sphere tracing through a density field. Step length must also honor smallest optical/silhouette scale and screen footprint.

**Lighting/radiance.** Use Beer–Lambert transmittance `T=exp(-integral sigma_t ds)` and front-to-back in-scattering `L += T * (1-exp(-sigma_t Delta s)) * (sigma_s/sigma_t) * L_incident * p(theta)`, updating `T *= exp(-sigma_t Delta s)`. Cache/approximate multiple scattering and sky irradiance by genus/altitude and sun/weather state; start with dual-lobe or depth-varying phase and compare to a slow reference. Maintain a sun-space BSM/optical-depth product for ground, cloud self-shadow, aerial perspective and atmosphere, with a short local ray only where coarse shadow lacks near-surface definition. BSM response `tau(d)=min(tau_max, sigma_mean max(0,d-z_front)); T_sun=exp(-tau)` is Hillaire slide 37's approximation. Include finite cloud thickness and spatially varying extinction; invalidate when sun angle, weather or morphology changes beyond bounds. Reflections reuse *world density and light products*, not main-view color/depth history.

**View/consumer schedule.** One authoritative weather/morphology state and source cache, multiple view products. Main view has its own low-resolution march, depth/velocity, jitter and temporal history; use ~1/4 linear resolution (1/16 pixels) only when motion/quality proves it, with immediate higher-rate/quality refresh around silhouettes, new towers, camera cuts and fast features. Independent reflection view has its own projection, depth and history; lower spatial resolution and lower cadence are allowed if reflection roughness/importance supports them. Environment sky cube/paraboloid and SH/irradiance refresh incrementally by direction/region and sun/weather change, with bounded stale age; regenerate newly exposed/changed cloud sectors promptly. Cloud shadow map is shared spatial product, updated independently of view histories at a cadence set by shadow movement and visible ground footprint. Represent each product with world-state epoch, sun epoch, region revisions, timestamp, camera transform where relevant, and confidence/stale bound. Retain old product during rebuild and blend where perceptually valid; camera cuts or disocclusion reject history. With clouds off, retain a zero-density/clear-sky contract and release cloud-only caches after consumers finish, rather than continuing hidden marches.

**Operating case.** During a forming Cb, weather raises lift and moisture; the stable feature adds vertically connected towers, then upper shear grows an anvil while rain-out erodes the core. Density bounds and light cache update from changed regions. The main view marches changed tiles immediately and selectively reuses valid history elsewhere. Sun-space shadow changes at the same world position/time, while environment and reflection products refresh their affected directions using independent histories. Terrain darkening, aerial perspective and reflected sky then describe the same event with tolerated time lag, recorded by epoch. A pure scroll/fade would fail this case by preserving old connectivity.

## Cost model and risk ordering

At 1280×720, the main output has 921,600 pixels; 4×4 interleaving traces ~57,600 rays/frame before any independent reflection rays. Let `R_v` be rays/view, `S` coarse steps/ray, `q` fraction entering occupied regions, `D` detailed density cost, `K` local light steps and `C` cached light fetch cost. The dominant shading work is approximately `sum_views R_v S [C_weather + q(D+K D_light+C)] + R_shadow S_shadow C_density + C_reconstruct + C_prepare`. An SDF skip saves work only if its lookups and divergent control cost less than the density samples it avoids; a faster analytic density function that doubles `S`, `K`, or view count can lose end-to-end. Shared lighting/shadow preparation pays when `(number of reused camera/light samples) × saved local ray work > cache build + bandwidth + invalidation`; this condition must be measured in the assembled scene.

Logical memory illustration, *not measured browser residency*: a 512²×64 BC6 3D field is 16.8 MB; one BC4 SDF is 8.4 MB; a 128³ RGBA8 reusable noise volume is 8.4 MB; four full-size RGBA16F history products would be ~29.5 MB before shadow cascades, weather, scene and compositor. Two or three independent histories and prepared cloud objects can exceed a safe cloud share of the 512 MiB whole-browser ceiling quickly. Budget residency for source fields, staging/upload duplication, views/histories, shadows and reflection/environment buffers together; browser process memory is not just GPU texture arithmetic. Native 3 ms and browser 5 ms remain **unqualified**, including tails and simultaneous consumers.

Ranked reductions to test: (1) exact shared weather/light/shadow caches with validity; (2) occupancy and conservative SDF skipping; (3) object/region culling and projected-footprint LOD; (4) fewer ray pixels via temporal/interleaved reconstruction with fresh-detail masks; (5) fewer light samples through precomputed lighting; (6) lower reflection/environment cadence/resolution based on consumer error; (7) remove expensive local ground bounce/shafts; (8) reduce cloud morphology/detail fidelity only after silhouette, type and evolution tests. This ordering is a hypothesis, not measured on M2. Class identity and growth topology are below the quality floor and should not be removed to reach timing.

### Challengers, unknowns and decisive proof

- **Full Nubis³-like voxel core:** reject if compressed 3D formats unsupported/slow on target WebGPU or residency and multi-view cost breach envelope. Accept for near Cb/Cu only if 0.5–1 m-like silhouette detail, fly-through parallax and growth stages decisively improve quality at bounded residency. Prepared clips need state interpolation and conservative bounds; the talk does not prove editable live topology.
- **Pure analytic SDF/primitive core:** reject if Sc/St layer continuity, Ci wisps and Cb anvil topology require so many primitives or evaluations that ray work beats prepared fields only in isolated scenes. An exact local distance function does not automatically yield believable morphology or cheap multi-scattering.
- **Pure 2.5D weather/noise core:** reject if close Cb and independently moving reflection views expose repeated/noisy silhouettes or absent vertical development despite high-quality main stills. Keep as far-sky baseline even if near challenger wins.
- **Pure time-sliced sky texture/cards:** reject as sole core if reflected clouds change from wrong location/time, local parallax fails or shadow mismatch is visible. Keep as very distant/rough environment representation.

The first executable proof should show Cu → Cb tower/anvil → breakup, adjacent Sc/St and Ci, with weather shear, a main camera, independent planar reflection/environment capture and ground/cloud shadow in one world-time sequence. Compare rendered views and shadow from identical world coordinates; include fast time, camera cut and sun motion. Capture per-pass GPU time, p50/p95/p99 cadence, resident/peak browser memory, occupied-ray fraction, detailed density fetches, light fetches and history rejection. Opposing outcomes should change architecture: if field misses morphology, use more prepared class objects; if cache rebuild or multi-view dominates, decouple cadence and regions; if temporal artifacts dominate, promote fresh silhouettes to higher resolution. No GPU/build measurement was performed in this research commission.

## Gaps and nontransferable constraints

The Guerrilla 2017/2022 decks are now inspected locally; 2023 remains a slide study and 2024 a limited still-frame video study, not watched narrations. RDR2's 2019 232 MB deck was inaccessible, so only its stated cross-consumer system goal informs architecture. No Decima/Frostbite/Rockstar production shader or scheduler source was available. Unity HDRP source is publicly visible but restricted by the [Unity Companion License](https://unity.com/legal/licenses/unity-companion-license), whose section 1 excludes competitive analysis and developing a competing product; it was deliberately excluded from source inspection/copying for this reusable engine. Takram is MIT, pinned and inspected, but WebGL-specific and its README discloses artifacts; it is a reference, not proof of this assembled system. Guerrilla presentations convey concepts and results without a general source-code license or runnable implementation. Epic's [public cloud documentation](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/unreal-engine/volumetric-cloud-component-in-unreal-engine) is an approach reference, not a license to transfer Unreal source. There is no measured M2/WebGPU/native result or evidence that the full five-genus growth/evolution problem is solved by any one cited system.

## Selection delta after retained research (2026-11-02)

The initial shortlist above was saved before any current design/prototype review, as required. After that independent selection, I read the retained [cloud system source study](../../design/research/CLOUD-SYSTEM-SOURCES.md) and [complete-sky source study](../../design/research/CLOUD-SKY-SOURCES.md). Those studies contain material our initial proposal did not yet reconcile: Smolder 2026's offline time-varying VDB animation/packing with whole-animation residency cost, Stormscapes' offline/reference formation physics, five-family procedural shape literature, and explicit rejection of static shear/fade and smooth lobe-cutting as sufficient structural development. I did not re-inspect Smolder's full deck here; the root study owns its update. The retained studies are research support, not an executable proof.

**Revised selection:** the reliable **source-content core** for the commissioned five-family lifecycle should be *prepared, time-varying final optical-density sequences* (or a producer proven equivalent), with stable feature identity, world-space weather arrangement and bounded flow-assisted interpolation. The original hybrid recommendation's hand-designed runtime lobe birth/erosion grammar remains a challenger, especially for responsive Cu/Cb, but is no longer the default absent convincing moving evidence. The reason is representational: a static coarse profile plus advected/rotated noise preserves its topology except for threshold effects; a time-varying optical master can encode genuinely changing connected components, tower height, anvil spread, sheet opening and filament decay. The source master can be offline authored, fluid informed or procedural, with genus-specific tools. Its final optical remap and content cadence must be verified, not inferred from a simulation label. One full sky need not be one enormous VDB: finite local clips/populations, hierarchical weather placement, selected distant profiles and sparse/packed source residency are distinct scales.

The 2017 deck makes this boundary explicit: coverage inflation and scrolling detail were coupled to an art-directed formation that deliberately persisted during ordinary evolution (p.63/printed64). Its weather scheduler *does* change formation at transitions (pp.70–72), but a 10–30 s state blend (p.71) is a scene-level transition, not proof of individual Cu growth and breakup. The 2022 deck extends 2.5D sky, near envelope models and superstorm rotation/reprojection, yet its sky density still scrolls a noise sample (p.37), while its VFX motion is a special vortex transform (pp.147–157). The 2023/2024 Nubis³ material shifts to modeled low-resolution 3D cloud data and render-time detail up-rez, which strongly supports prepared volumetric *content* but does not itself demonstrate live structural lifecycle. The retained 2023 deck inspection notes its final comparison calls voxel evolution pseudomotion (physical p.198); we should not relabel that as formation physics.

**Render-system selection remains:** prepare geometry/detail/bounds offline where their cost is reused, skip certified empty regions, use weather-first/detail-later evaluation, cache direct/ambient light and optical-depth shadow data, render the main view at a controlled low or mixed resolution with independently valid temporal history, and provide separate reflected/environment products from the same world source. This uses transferable *system* principles from Nubis 2017/2022/2023, Hillaire/Patry and inspected Takram code. It does **not** imply one shared view history, a giant persistent 3D field, a 60-frame response for fast events, or wholesale transfer of a console timing number. The first proof still must render lifecycle, main/reflection/environment and shadows together; otherwise a convincing single-camera still could hide source and consumer mismatch.

Remaining decision-sensitive uncertainties: Can a small, finite set of prepared source clips cover all five families and stages without visible repetition under normal/accelerated clocks? What source resolution, frame cadence and correspondence error preserve topology without excessive memory? Does the browser admit the compressed/storage formats and peak upload footprint that make the Nubis³ strategy affordable? Does a shared light/shadow cache amortize its rebuild cost when sources evolve at the required rate? How much history can each view safely reuse at Cu/Cb boundaries and independent reflection cuts? The planned visual and device measurements should reject or revise the corresponding representation, rather than treating any technique as accepted because a cited engine shipped it.
