# Cloud and atmosphere proof programme — independent first pass

**Scope and provenance.** This first programme is derived from the commissioning brief before reading the existing cloud design, implementation or proof records. It is a proposed acceptance programme, not a claim that any proof has passed. Research and evidence reconciliation follows below.

## Observable target and discriminating references

The target is a beautiful, convincing evolving sky: Cu, Cb, Sc, St and Ci retain distinct silhouettes, internal light, altitude, motion and lifecycle. At normal speed, a cloud must develop **new towers, holes, mergers and breakup at identifiable places** as air, moisture and solar conditions evolve. Watch both a cloud-relative view and a fixed world view so advection cannot masquerade as growth. Identity persists; apparent motion cannot be only rigid translation, UV wobble, uniform scale, global opacity or cross-fading between canned shapes.

Use a small curated reference set before tuning: native-resolution daylit stills for each type and a real-time or documented-time-lapse sequence for Cu/Cb development, Sc/St sheet opening and closure, and Ci shear/dispersal. Record camera, field of view, approximate interval and what each reference can prove. A still establishes form and lighting only; a time-lapse establishes order and topology change but needs its time ratio stated. Include clear sky, horizon/aerial perspective, sun approach, overcast and broken-cloud references. Choose references by visual cue, not by technique used to make them. Keep held-out references to judge whether authoring merely copies the chosen exemplars.

Specific reference roles: [NOAA's five genus photographs](https://www.noaa.gov/jetstream/clouds/ten-basic-clouds) and [WMO identification](https://cloudatlas.wmo.int/en/identifying-clouds.html) anchor family form, altitude, anvil/fibre/base and lighting cues; they are still/description comparators. [UCAR's dated cold-front sequence](https://scied.ucar.edu/weather-timelapse-lyons-colorado-may-8-2017) is a promising weather-progression comparator: its [teacher guide](https://scied.ucar.edu/sites/default/files/documents/globe_weather_ls2_teacher_guide.pdf) labels high cloud, lower layers, Cu birth and Cb development by time. [NCAR's timestamped cloud time-lapse dataset](https://data.ucar.edu/dataset/cloud-time-lapse-videos) is a candidate pool for separate family motion clips. These motion sources were **located, not watched or classified by me**; their usable spans and speed ratios must be checked before use. A future reviewer should watch raw reference and generated motion side by side, not infer behaviour from source descriptions.

Minimum visual diagnostics: ordinary beauty view at game-like camera/FOV, cloud-locked and world-locked time series, orthographic density/condensate and vertical slices, source/sink/velocity fields, cloud-ID/connectivity map, optical-depth/depth map, direct/ambient lighting contributions, cloud shadow map, reflection-only view, clear-air-only view, and combined scene. Show the same event/time and world point across views. Each diagnostic must make a falsifying observation possible; it is not a replacement for ordinary output.

## Dependency-ordered POCs and rejection gates

1. **Causal source, isolated and cheap.** One Cu cell, fixed camera, prescribed changing lift/moisture/wind or explicitly authored equivalent causes. Show optical/source budget and cloud-relative development at normal and accelerated playback. Tag emergent towers, hollowing, shedding and death with sections and topology/volume/height traces. Reject if change is a global envelope, scrolling noise, shape morph, fade, or unrelated to supplied forcing. An optical proxy must be labelled as such; do not demand false water-mass conservation from it. Establish a stable input→state→source/sink→render chain before content multiplication.
2. **Early coupled sentinel.** Use that *one* source with one moving camera, Sun, terrain/water receiver, independent reflection, clear air/fog, direct and ambient light, cloud shadow and surface environment at one time/world frame. Include a clear-air-only and main-cloud-hidden run. Walk a sunward/backlit cloud edge, horizon route and shadow arrival. Reject double air, wrong depth/order, halo/temporal artifacts, detached shadow/reflection/environment, or clear air vanishing when visible clouds are off. Measure combined work now: postponing this connection until after five families would allow expensive incompatible content to accumulate.
3. **Structural family, same contracts.** Add Cu→Cb development and Sc/St/Ci cases, each with its own expected morphology and motion regime, using the accepted sentinel consumers. Test a wind/shear change and eventual two-layer overlap, rather than five independent hero shots. Reject if types are palette/height variants of one generator, layers share rigid motion, or a class cannot be authored without bespoke per-shot animation. Keep one representative exemplar per regime until the mechanism passes, then test meaningful variants and full lifecycles before accepting a family.
4. **Shared weather and spatial coherence.** Drive cloud field, clear-air extinction/fog and light with one timestamped weather/solar state. Sample camera motion and a moving receiver over cloud and layer edges; compare cloud location, shadow, Sun visibility, ambient change and fog/air across the same positions. Test clear→scattered, increasing cover, Sc→overcast and developing/approaching storm with tracked survivors. Reject duplicate weather authorities, unsupported latency, reversed wind, sky crossfades, synchronized resets, light without an occluder or detached shadows. Demand reproducible seeds and authorable conditions.
5. **Scale and adverse conditions.** Increase cloud count, layers, screen coverage and view distance to a representative full sky; test overcast, broken high contrast, fast camera movement, changing weather, cold start, long run and quality changes. Preserve lifecycle diversity and causal coherence. Reject repetition, popping, unstable history, memory growth, stalls or a lower tier that silently removes required dynamics. Include source-off, resource recreation and disabled-cloud cases; disabled visible clouds must save their actual work while supported clear-air/environment consumers remain.

| Family | Minimum positive motion witness before family admission | Plausible false pass to reject |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cu | Persistent, level-base body raises distinct billows, replaces mature heads and erodes into uneven remnants; multiple formations differ in more than scale/yaw. | Convincing still with frozen maturity or only filling a prefigured silhouette. |
| Cb | A tracked Cu body grows separate towers, reaches a cap, feeds a spreading/sheared anvil, then core and anvil decay at different rates. | Tall stretched Cu plus an independently fading disk. |
| Sc | Shallow connected banks/cells move with a common layer while openings form, merge and close locally. | Tiled puff field with frozen holes or whole-layer opacity control. |
| St | Broad sheet has visible thickness/varied underside; cover grows by local connection, then thins/opens locally without a finite stage edge. | Flat grey sky overlay or sudden sheet replacement. |
| Ci | Thin high fibres emerge from feeding heads, shear/taper into fall streaks, disperse while some tails outlive heads. | Stretched opaque Cu texture or camera-locked streaks. |

For every row, show at least two genuinely different formations and front/side/underside or relevant elevated views in the first mechanism screen. The canonical four-variant isolated-family gate remains the later acceptance scope. The coupled witness must include source-on/off, main-hidden, all-light-off and clear-air-only cases with same world/time. Record a scorecard per cue (pass/fail/unproven) and reason, plus held reference; an average beauty score cannot override a missing defining cue.

Each gate records the exact build, seed/weather path, reference frame/time, camera, resolution/DPR, visual judgement, counters and failures. The reviewer must inspect **actual full-size images and watched motion** at real and accelerated rates while the user is away; contact sheets and frame samples are screening evidence only. Rejection is allowed without a suggested cosmetic patch. If a gate fails because the model cannot produce the phenomenon, revise the governing design/contract before polishing the renderer or preparing a large asset library. Engineering proof and user aesthetic acceptance remain separate.

## Cost qualification paired with the gates

On the stated M2 WebGPU browser target at 1280×720, cloud work must fit **≤5 ms GPU/frame** within **≤16 ms whole frame** and **≤512 MiB browser memory**; native targets are **3/10 ms** (clarify which workload/profile each covers in the governing design). Measure cloud-state update, preparation/upload, sky/cloud draw, lighting/shadows, reflection/environment views, temporal reconstruction and clear-air work separately, then the **assembled** critical path. State which costs are shared and which are charged to clouds; avoid double-counting shared passes. Track p50/p95/p99 and worst relevant frames over sustained runs and weather transitions, CPU/GPU overlap, display cadence and allocated/resident/peak memory. Pin loaded artifact, device/backend, browser, viewport/DPR, quality, scene/weather seeds and warmup. Isolated pass microtimings cannot certify the frame or memory budgets; desktop native cannot certify browser behavior.

Before implementation, estimate pixels×samples×views×layers, weather grid cells×updates, shadow queries, history/cook residency and upload bytes, including full coverage, low sun, overcast, reflections and quality-tier transitions. Keep an explicit reduction ladder with predicted savings and cues at risk; validate at fixed required appearance, then accept fidelity changes only with visual review. Seek shared work, bounded support, temporal reuse and scheduling before deleting lifecycle or coupling. Establish the irreducible source/update floor and feature-off costs. Stop a POC when it has answered its discriminating question, including a decisive failure, rather than expanding content to make the result attractive.

## Evidence reconciliation

The [commission](../cloud-sky-design-2026-11-01/COMMISSION.md) and [capability ledger](../../design/modules/CLOUD-CAPABILITIES.md) already express most of the required outcomes and correctly label design versus proof. The [canonical design](../../design/modules/CLOUDS.md) names separate Cu/Cb, low-layer and Ci producers, persistent source IDs, world-cell events, shared wind, one coefficient/lighting path, far-sky evaluation and independent consumers. This is a substantial proposed operating model, not a demonstrated complete sky. Its F1–F5/M/W/S queue puts isolated families before combinations; my early coupled sentinel sharpens the risk order without reducing later family acceptance. The planned full-sky populations are useful **adverse workload hypotheses**, not mandatory content production before causal proof.

**Demonstrated, bounded:** [Q1](../cloud-source-2026-11-01/REVIEW.md) retains one licensed, recoverable authored Cu density lifecycle with numerical/codec and local breakup evidence; it is an optical proxy with static mature structure. The [coupled Q2/Q3 proof](../cloud-coupled-2026-11-01/review/final-review.md) retains a finite source, bounded fog/air, receiver attenuation, independent planar mirror and sampled motion, but reports direct-light approximation error and no full-sky or engine binding. The [Cu correction checkpoint](../cloud-cu-correction-2026-11-02/README.md) shows a held light-fit improvement, main-hidden shadow/mirror and all-light-off behaviour. I directly inspected native retained Q1 near-90s, fitted coupled, rejected hierarchy-front and later Sun-depth 1280×720 PNGs: Q1 has useful irregular billows; the coupled scene clearly remains a local plane/mirror/sphere diagnostic; hierarchy geometry reads as rounded buttons on broad bodies. The [appearance review](../cloud-cu-correction-2026-11-02/review/review-fit-appearance-motion.md) inspected sparse lifecycle frames and reports faint prefigured birth, static maturity and rounded late remnants. I also viewed full16× samples at ages 0, ~120 and ~240s: support appears then clears, but those three stills cannot establish between-frame smoothness or convincing mature renewal. I have **not watched the retained WebMs**; neither I nor that appearance reviewer should be credited with continuous playback.

**Still failed or absent:** the first coarse Cu and subsequent hierarchy [fail native form](../cloud-cu-correction-2026-11-02/review/review-hierarchy-appearance.md); the prepared Q1 master is a comparator, not four accepted Cu variants. Cb, Sc, St, Ci, their isolated full lifecycles, meaningful variants, shared shear, mixed layers, weather transitions and four complete skies have no accepted output. The [Sun-depth checkpoint](../cloud-sun-depth-2026-11-02/README.md) improves a bounded light path but fails its lateral point-query gate; external full SKY-G air/fog/ambient/surface environment is not established. The later [depth index](../cloud-depth-index-2026-11-02/README.md) is rejected for runtime selection after inconsistent ABBA performance; finite GPU correctness is narrower than an end-to-end gain. The current `cloud-air-sun` directory contains loaded-profile files but no review/visual/cost acceptance record yet; it is active work, not a pass.

**Cost contradiction:** retained one-source GPU brackets are already 11.960/15.073ms p50/p95 for the Cu correction and 12.976/17.075ms after the later crop, versus the 5ms browser allocation. The latter remains above 16ms p95 before a complete frame. They use a local M3-class fixture with only logical allocation accounting; M2 browser, actual 512MiB process/driver peak, native 3/10ms and complete multi-family sky are unmeasured. A causal sentinel should expose full-graph scaling and attribution now; repeated micro-optimizations that leave the composed budget failing do not justify a content campaign.

**Review decision:** retain the verified optical/consumer seams and selected reference master; require an actual source with identifiable local renewal, an early clear-air/lighting/environment/receiver/mirror coupling witness, then family diversity, weather and scaled sky. Use small controlled sources to disprove representation assumptions, not months of master authoring. The first pass here is a self-derived independent input; later reconciliation read the old design and selected evidence. No code was run or changed, no GPU claim was reproduced, and no motion was watched in this review.

## Review of migration and technique drafts (2 November snapshot)

Read the 304-line [migration draft](../../design/modules/CLOUD-MIGRATION.md) and 95-line [technique map](../../design/research/ATMOSPHERICS-TECHNIQUES.md) after the independent programme above. The drafts accurately keep optical proxies distinct from fluid thermodynamics, separate checked boxes from implementation and user acceptance, preserve recoverable masters, expose the current cost failure, and name no paid tool as an installed dependency. They offer an actionable module split and source/light/view flow. The following corrections are required before treating the written plan as a dependable build queue:

| Severity / exact section | Finding and actionable correction |
| --- | --- |
| **P1 — `Checked build queue: P1–P4` and status dependencies** | The supposedly early P4 sentinel waits for a Cu **and continuous Cb branch**, a complete four-LUT atmosphere, far-cloud/source atlas, production lighting and then adds two fogs, a low layer and temporal reconstruction. This risks a major content/renderer campaign before the first complete causal scene. Split P1 into one locally renewing/breaking Cu source plus portable optical sample, then Cb branch after the first coupling witness; split P4 into **spatial-only P4a** with one source, minimal validated clear air, a receiver, independent mirror/environment, common Sun/shadow and overlap, followed by P4b temporal reconstruction, extra fog/layer and full P2/P3 refinements. Make P4a a hard rejection gate before four-variant production; update status dependencies to match. The full P2 atmosphere and production P3 remain required before final family/sky acceptance. |
| **P1 — `Mathematical and content closure`, `One frame and one weather transition`, P1/P6** | Prepared evolving frames, branch markers and “creation/loss residual” do not yet define how a mid-life forcing change chooses a compatible continuation without replacing the visible cloud, nor how source interpolation preserves truly new towers/holes. Add a concrete P1 cook/runtime control contract: drivers, phase/branch selection, correspondence plus invalid regions, local birth/loss residual, continuity/error bounds and restart condition. Demonstrate a changed driver during an existing cell, with source and shadow/reflection staying on the same ID/time. A fixed clip may pass its local lifecycle yet fail weather controllability; let that result change the content route rather than burying it in P6. |
| **P2 — P0/P1/P2 visual reference deliverables** | The family motion-reference checkbox is good, but clear air, horizon, low Sun, overcast ambient, fog and reflected/environment look have only named views, no held photographic/observational comparison or acceptance cue. P2/P4 should state the specific observable cues each comparator tests, plus camera/exposure/time matching limits. The UCAR/NCAR sources above are candidates, not already-watched evidence. |
| **P2 — P5 and progress table** | “All isolated families, then variants” lists Cu and Cb as four distinct formations in the first bullets, then says add four variants per family; the stage has no per-family completion boxes. Split P5 into five named checkbox groups or a status table, each with **one mechanism screen → four-variant full lifecycle/lighting/shadow/reflection/view acceptance**. | **P1 — P3/P4 cost gate and `Budget account`** | The draft correctly measures throughout and gives a 5ms allocation, but “reject a structural budget overrun” needs a decision rule before content expansion. At P4a measure a matched assembled spatial-only graph on the actual M2/WebGPU target if available, with CPU/GPU/cadence and logical versus process peak memory; project full-sky costs using occupied work, active unique source phases, Sun/shadow/reflection/environment multipliers and full-coverage limits. Record an explicit outcome: viable reduction path with measured headroom, representation revision, or unqualified hardware. Do not infer full-sky admission from one source, and do not defer the first device reality check to P7 if the target is available. P4b then measures history's quality and net savings under births/cuts. |
| **P2 — P4/P6/P7 rejection witnesses** | Add an explicit paired same-location/time cross-view receipt: cloud coefficient and Sun depth at a world point, receiver shadow, environment/ambient term, main/reflected ray and clear-air segment; hold source/time while toggling main visibility and camera. This rejects a plausible but wrong scene with individually attractive views whose shadow/env/reflection sample stale or different source generations. **Disposition:** the technique map is appropriately a selected synthesis with source-access limits, not a false claim of production proof. The migration draft is directionally strong and practical, but its first complete coupled witness comes too late and its prepared-content control seam needs an executable continuity test. These are build-order/design corrections, not requests for a fluid solver, a larger bibliography, a paid tool or a large asset library. No source code, GPU run or new image/motion capture was performed for this review.

### Bounded delta verification of the revised plan

Re-read the revised [migration plan](../../design/modules/CLOUD-MIGRATION.md) against the **six findings above only**. All six have actionable written dispositions:

1. **Early coupling:** the status/dependency table and P1a/P2a/P3a→P4a queue put one renewing Cu, bounded validated air, receiver, independent reflection, environment and spatial-only integration before Cb, full LUTs, production renderer, history or family variants. P4b now owns reconstruction/extra overlap.
2. **Prepared-source control:** the control contract specifies monotone phase, compatible branch windows, same-boundary continuations or measured local residual, positive frame interpolation with union support, direct master interval tests, invalid correspondence and explicit rejection/restart. P1a tests mid-life phase/loss changes; P1b tests deepening. This is a concrete initial authored-control programme rather than an unsupported promise of arbitrary weather edits.
3. **References:** P0 pins family and air/light/fog/reflection comparators with cue/exposure/FOV limits; P1a requires watched motion spans and time ratios. The plan does not claim those views have already been watched.
4. **Family progress:** P5 has distinct Cu/Cb/Sc/St/Ci checkboxes and one mechanism screen→four-formation/full-consumer acceptance for each, with separate CF evidence.
5. **Cost decision:** P3a screens multi-source/far/64× structural slopes before production caches; P4a requires assembled M2 measurements if available, separates reference cost, and records viable reduction/revision/unqualified-target outcome before content expansion. P3b repeats the stress with production paths.
6. **Cross-consumer receipt:** P4a pins same-point/time medium, Sun depth, receiver, environment, main/reflected and air measurements under visibility/camera toggles; P6 tracks IDs/local topology through transitions; P7 retains adverse routes.

**Verdict on this delta:** no remaining planning blocker among my six findings. P3a's early far/many-source checks are explicitly nonacceptance architecture stress, so they do not imply early family content or full-sky admission. These are planned gates, not passed evidence; I did not rereview the complete research base, run code/GPU, or watch motion. Earlier failure/unproven statuses remain unchanged until executable proof.
