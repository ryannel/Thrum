# A04 decision proposal — water domains and edges

1 November 2026. **Selection for coordinator reconciliation; architecture only, unbuilt.** This closes the architectural *policy* missing in [A04](../../../design/ARCHITECTURE-AUDIT.md), while leaving individual solver operators and qualifications to E1's module commissions. It inherits [C01/C02/C04/C09/C10](../../../design/CONTRACTS.md), the [environment design](../../../design/modules/ENVIRONMENT.md), [world/content transactions](../../../design/modules/WORLD-CONTENT.md), [P3](../../../design/modules/PLAY-SYSTEMS.md) and [model audit](../../../design/research/AUDIT-MODELS.md). It does not turn existing Broadsides water experiments into the target engine.

## Selection

Use **authored, disjoint physical water domains** in one authoritative world. Each domain names one physical model and its valid quantities, scale, state, source terms and query support. Domain edges are explicit. The initial useful modes are **closed/disconnected**, **prescribed reservoir boundary**, and **visual-only adjacency**. A fourth mode, **conservative coupled**, is admitted only when an actual joint interface operator, source terms and substep schedule are supplied and validated. There is no generic heterogeneous exchange implied by two surfaces touching. A river can accept prescribed sea level or flow as an open boundary without updating the sea; the sea is then an external reservoir and the river's exchange is recorded. A falls/pool/fountain may use an authored inlet/outlet with the same declaration. If a game requires the river to change the sea or to conserve water across a falls and pool, it must select a specified coupled operator or a suitable common solver. Otherwise that connection is unsupported for physical gameplay.

The engine exposes a narrow **common envelope**—domain identity, time, support, units, validity/error, and typed unavailable result—rather than a mandatory fluid payload. A graph-like free surface can offer height/normal/velocity/depth. Volume occupancy, underwater current, pressure, force integration, wet/dry front and breaking are separate capabilities. A game selects the physical model and required queries; F2 may vary a presentation tier, or substitute a physical provider only among explicitly declared semantically equivalent models. Target cost cannot silently turn flooding into a flow texture. Domain/source/edge semantics belong to E1 and authored data, not the renderer.

### Model and edge admission

| Case | Physical model and edge policy | What is claimed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Still decorative canal or isolated pond | Authored level/current, closed banks or named external reservoir | Queryable prescribed surface within its support; no mass balance of an infinite fixed level. |
| Sea with swell and ship contact | Prescribed incident wave/current field, with explicit body-response model and optional separate visual residual | Wave timing/forces within the selected approximation. The sea is a reservoir; a ship wake or river outflow does not change it unless a coupled response operator says so. |
| Shallow flood/river | Finite water storage and flow solver, with authored solid banks and named upstream/downstream/precipitation boundaries | Solver-local mass accounting plus declared boundary sources/sinks; wet/dry, velocity and contact only if the provider supports them. |
| Sea → river mouth | Prescribed sea/tide *boundary data* drive an admitted river boundary condition; river computes its own signed exchange | River balance includes exchange with the sea reservoir. No equal/opposite sea-state change or global energy/momentum claim. Incident spectral elevation alone is not a valid discharge law. |
| River → falls → pool | Initially either a visual-only seam with no physical through-flow, or named prescribed export/import reservoirs | A continuous physical water path is **unsupported** until a common solver or specified transfer operator debits and credits represented storage. A falls is not assumed to be a graph-height surface. |
| Two finite dynamic domains | Conservative coupled edge only after one shared flux/traction operator and schedule are supplied | Only the material and momentum budgets actually represented by that operator, over its qualified regime. |

The provider's regime descriptor declares surface topology, bathymetry/geometry, relevant scale ratios (for example `kh`, steepness `ka`, depth/horizontal scale and Froude number where applicable), permitted boundary forcing, conserved variables, interactions, query latency/error, state residence, work and memory law. F2 checks content and all retained consumers, including P3, P5 and audio, against that descriptor. The [shallow-water model survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2001.09655) supports different equations by regime; it does not make one family interchangeable at an edge. The [wet/dry finite-volume study](https://arxiv.org/abs/1412.3580) illustrates why positivity and flux handling are numerical properties to prove, not consequences of a water-body label.

## Minimum domain/edge contract

An authored domain has stable ID, model/provider ID and version, world-space support and topology, bottom/bank/solid representation, boundary segments, physical parameters, required query capabilities and tolerances, authoritative state location, seed/forcing provenance, update cadence, save codec and required cooked products. It explicitly distinguishes finite storage from prescribed/infinite reservoir. No fake `volume = ∞` number is needed. Dynamic domains own their state and physical sample; presentation owns only derived draw products. Two cameras never create two domain simulations.

An edge has stable ID, ordered endpoints and geometry/orientation, one of the modes below, spatial/temporal validity, transferred quantities and units, source/sink ledger mapping, update/cook dependencies, crossing query policy and a failure outcome. Multiple edge segments between the same domains are distinct and cannot each count the same flux. Neither F4 nor W2 infers an edge from mesh overlap or neighboring stream cells.

| Edge mode | Required semantics | Failure/out-of-range behavior |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Closed/disconnected | Each side has its own solid/closed boundary or separated support. Transfer is zero by the selected boundary law. | Reject a physical crossing if its collision/query support is absent. |
| Prescribed reservoir/open | A named external or authoritative prescribed source publishes stage, velocity/discharge, pressure or wave forcing **only as the receiving solver's admitted boundary law requires**. Receiver computes or receives one signed exchange and records the reservoir ledger; donor is not implicitly debited. | Missing/late/out-of-regime forcing blocks activation or the tick under a declared bounded fallback; do not reuse stale boundary values as new physical truth. A fixed authored fallback is a new named source revision. |
| Visual-only | Render seam/refraction/foam may connect geometry; physically the domains are separate, closed or externally prescribed as stated. | No inferred flux or traversable buoyancy path. A crossing consumer fails admission unless independently qualified. |
| Conservative coupled | One interface owner computes a common integrated material flux and both sides use its opposite signs at the same world-tick commit. It also defines momentum/pressure traction, wet/dry rules, subcycling, and any model conversion. | Unsupported until the full operator exists. If an operator later fails validity/stability/readiness, retain the previous complete configuration or enter explicit overload/fault; never switch to a visual seam or silently drop flux. |

For a finite domain with stored volume `V_i` and common density, the auditable tick balance is

`V_i[n+1] − V_i[n] = Σ_e ΔV_(e→i) + ΔV_sources − ΔV_sinks + ε_i`,

where `ε_i` is measured numerical residual, not an unnamed drain. For variable density or phase/species, use mass and account for each transfer separately. At a truly coupled internal edge, `ΔV_(e→A) + ΔV_(e→B) = 0` to the operator's numerical tolerance; the same flux is entered once per side under one edge/tick ID. At a prescribed reservoir boundary only the finite receiver changes; the opposite entry belongs to the named *external reservoir ledger*, not a second simulated sea state. Therefore the finite-domain balance can close while the whole scene is not a closed hydrodynamic system. Momentum conservation additionally needs compatible advective momentum flux, pressure/traction, boundary and body impulses. Equal heights, blended normals or even equal mass flux alone do not supply that. Energy conservation is not claimed for friction, breaking, imposed waves or prescribed forcing. [Primary finite-volume work](https://arxiv.org/abs/1509.07096) demonstrates the role of a numerical interface flux for local mass/momentum; the exact operator for mixed model families remains to be designed.

## Time, force and query authority

F1/W1 fixes one world tick. Pre-tick region/profile transaction installs the complete domain/edge/query configuration and source revision. E1 evaluates prescribed boundary data at declared tick/substep times, advances domain state, and publishes one immutable X[n+1] with edge ledger and source IDs. If an edge needs smaller steps, its operator owns a schedule whose integrated flux register is applied once at world commit; independent domains must not each estimate the shared flux at different times. P3 samples the declared X[n] water query for its step and sends identified body impulses/disturbances once to the next specified water update; tighter body/fluid feedback needs one explicit coupled substep schedule. No render frame, camera, GPU readback or effect history advances authoritative water. For a game requiring same-step wave/body reaction, the separate lagged scheme is not silently upgraded.

One E1 query router selects the **unique physical domain** at world position, vertical context if needed, snapshot/time and requested capability. Disjoint support uses half-open ownership or an authored boundary/tie rule stable under origin rebasing; an apparent overlap requires explicit layered topology and a selector (for example upper/lower volume), not priority guessed from draw order. The result includes domain ID, state/source revision, exact sampled tick/time, support/scale/error/latency and value, or typed `outside`, `ambiguous`, `unavailable`, `stale`, `unsupported capability` or `invalid regime`. Adjacent domain values are never averaged to manufacture a physical height. A finite hull footprint may sample both sides only under a qualified crossing contract and one P3 force integration; the seam must meet the game's allowed height/normal/velocity gap and buoyancy/impulse error. Otherwise the physical route is closed or activation fails. A visually blended seam may hide a bounded image discontinuity, but its blended height cannot define contact, volume flux or momentum. A multi-valued waterfall, submerged passage or swimmer requiring a volume cannot be served by a single `height(x,z)` merely because a mesh can be drawn.

Physical state and fixed-tick P3 queries are CPU-owned or analytically reproducible in the authoritative world for the baseline. A provider declares CPU/GPU residence, any duplicated evaluation and phase error, transfer cadence, query age and device-loss recovery. GPU detail may add bounded presentation ripples/foam or optical displacement; an effect at a hull/shore contact must either remain within a declared image/contact mismatch tolerance or be excluded there. V2/V3/V5 consume the authoritative domain sample plus a labelled visual residual. They do not write back buoyancy, wetness or navigation. Presentation interpolation is per quantity and may reset at source/topology/domain revisions; it never creates a new saved state or mass. Headless and installed offline play use the same authoritative domain/edge semantics without GPU or network services. Neither a visual-quality switch nor device loss changes the physical law or ledger.

Future multiplayer keeps this owner **per authoritative world/server session**. Clients receive versioned committed snapshots/events and may render different visual detail; they cannot supply physical force truth from local pixels. Client-side prediction is optional per provider and only where its snapshot, forcing and correction contract is explicit; a nonpredictable solver is interpolated/corrected under game policy. Seeds alone do not imply equivalent trajectories across peers, and no cross-platform bitwise lockstep is promised. Save/replay records the authority's durable state and external boundary inputs needed by its declared reproducibility envelope.

## Streaming, source products and save

Solver domains and W2 stream regions are **orthogonal**. A large sea can span many cells; a tiny pool may sit within one. W2 activates authored geometry, source/edge descriptors, collision/navigation and required E1 query/solver products atomically before a boundary becomes traversable. It cannot unload a cell that owns live water state or a required query/boundary stencil merely because no camera sees it. It pins the needed solver support, or a provider implements a proved reduced aggregate and conservative transfer/reconstruction policy. If neither fits the budget, W2 delays activation/unload or closes the traversal frontier visibly. A stream-cell edge is not a physical flux edge unless the same solver explicitly accounts for its internal numerical faces.

F3 schemas and F4 cook DAG carry stable domain/edge IDs; topology/bank/bathymetry; boundary forcing recipes and provenance; flow/wave and query representations; collision/nav/underwater/visual products; regime/error metadata; provider versions and exact dependency hashes. W2 requires compatible neighbors and transition products before publication. Changing an edge/model/forcing is a staged transaction with old/new memory headroom and an explicit state migration or reset; it never splices a new boundary into a running tick. A visual LOD change may retire only visual products if all retained physical consumers remain served.

P7 saves at a committed barrier: durable finite-domain water state (including storage and any necessary momentum/internal variables), authored/prescribed source revision and required forcing history or replay IDs, edge/cumulative exchange ledger if gameplay or recovery depends on it, pending once-only body/source events, and schema/provider/content version. It excludes draw meshes, foam history, GPU buffers, runtime slots and borrowed query handles. A prescribed analytic sea may save its parameters, phase origin/time and seed rather than a grid if the provider proves replay under its stated envelope; a seed alone is insufficient for a stateful solver or uncaptured external inputs. Restore loads the exact compatible cooked closure and boundary source before publishing query availability. An incompatible or absent provider/product fails restore with a specific dependency path; it does not coerce stored water into another model. Offscreen catch-up uses a declared source integration or persisted reduced state; unbounded wall-clock catch-up is disallowed by C02.

## Paper trace and rejection cases

**Normal, limited claim.** An authored coastal scene has prescribed sea swell/tide, one shallow river with finite storage, a decorative falls and a fixed-level pool. Sea tide (not arbitrary instantaneous spectral crest) supplies the river mouth's admitted downstream stage. The river computes mouth discharge at each admitted substep and logs signed reservoir exchange; upstream rain/inlet and runoff have IDs. The falls is initially a visual-only end with the river's water leaving to a named external sink and pool supplied by a named external inlet. F2 reports that river-to-pool global conservation and boat passage through the falls are unsupported. At pre-tick activation, W2 installs river bank, query data and P3 colliders. P3's boat samples the river at X[n]; its force enters once. E1 commits X[n+1] and the flux ledger. Both cameras render the same committed sea/river state with separate visual histories; one can omit water detail without changing boat forces. Save captures river storage and boundary/source revisions. This is coherent, although it deliberately does **not** claim that the visible falls transfers the exact river discharge into pool storage.

**Adverse.** A designer changes the falls to a traversable physical connection and asks pool level to rise from river discharge, but provides only a render mesh and static pool height. F2/T1 reject the content change: no receiving storage or interface flux/momentum/crossing operator exists. The old active revision remains in play; required P3 crossing remains blocked. If a valid edge had been installed but its river query product later failed to load at a streamed frontier, W2 keeps the previous complete configuration or closes the frontier and reports the missing product/epoch. It does not let the boat sample a stale river height, interpolate sea and pool heights, or turn a missing flux into zero. A later conservative edge would need an explicit donor debit, pool credit, pressure/momentum policy and synchronized integrated flux; until then the case remains unsupported rather than a promise to fix after renderer work.

## Alternatives and closure

| Alternative | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| One monolithic 3D/free-surface solver for all seas, rivers, puddles and falls | Rejected as baseline: forces one topology and volume-resolution cost onto simple authored water and planetary/open seas; it still needs boundaries, source terms, contact and rendering policy. A game needing continuous falls/pool exchange may select a qualified common solver for that connected component. |
| One shallow solver everywhere | Rejected as universal: useful for resolved depth-averaged flow/flooding, but does not reproduce dispersive short waves, vertical structure or arbitrary breaking/overhangs by naming. Can be chosen for a compatible connected basin. |
| Independent models with automatic height/velocity blending at seams | Rejected: state averages do not define a unique flux/pressure law, can fabricate/remove mass and momentum, and can change P3 force with render quality. |
| Universal heterogeneous adapter promised now | Rejected: no model-independent conversion of spectral waves, shallow flow and falling 3D sheets conserves all relevant quantities across regimes. Admit individually derived operators only. |

**Coordinator revisions needed:** C09 should name domain/edge modes, reservoir ledger and query selection; C02 should specify edge substep flux integration and committed publication; C04/F2 should reject unsupported physical substitutions and crossings; F3/F4/W2 should require typed edge/source/query products independent of stream-cell borders; P3/P5 should request a capability at an explicit snapshot and respect `unsupported/stale`; P7 should capture provider state and external forcing; V2/V3/V5 should treat seam blending and fine ripples as presentation only. Future N1 should replicate authority snapshots and make prediction provider-specific. This is a small shared contract, not an E1 megaplugin or a universal hydrology simulator.

**Architecture closure:** coordinator and affected owners agree on these edge categories, single query authority, explicit reservoir accounting, tick/stream/save behavior and unsupported combinations, then revise the canonical documents together. **Detailed work remaining before an actual model is admitted:** derive each selected solver's boundary and wet/dry law; specify interface flux/traction and stability if conservative coupling is wanted; establish body-force and wave reaction policy; define source/cooked schemas, CPU/GPU phase and error budgets; qualify crossing continuity, mass residual, numerical stability, visuals and device cost on named workloads. A paper trace is a design check, not running qualification or visual acceptance.

## CROSS-REVIEW — N1, sessions/source and shader/media decisions

Reviewed [N1](../../../design/modules/NETWORKING.md), [sessions/source](SESSIONS-SOURCE.md) and [shaders/media](SHADERS-MEDIA.md) after this proposal. Their authority and product splits agree with A04. The remaining gaps are narrow:

| Gap exposed | Minimal shared-contract fix |
| --- | --- |
| N1's gameplay manifest says physical content must be compatible, but a water render package could appear compatible while the server uses different bathymetry, boundary forcing, domain graph or wet/dry law. | Include the E1 physical model/provider/schema revision, domain/edge topology and exact gameplay-relevant cooked/source hashes in the session's gameplay-content compatibility declaration. Local water mesh, foam, shader and optical tier may differ. A changed physical graph requires explicit versioned migration/renegotiation, never an ordinary visual-profile switch. |
| N1's interest and W2's cells are independent of solver domains. A client can receive a river surface sample while missing the mouth forcing or an upstream state needed to interpret it. | Define one versioned water replication dependency group per required query/interaction: domain ID and generation, authoritative tick/sample time, physical state projection, source/edge revisions, relevant boundary data and query validity/error. Send a coherent baseline and deltas before marking that query usable; partial interest remains `unavailable`, not dry/empty. Server solver state remains active independently of any client's interest or W2 cells. |
| Generic prediction capture/restore might rewind a boat without its contacted water, source phase and returned disturbance ledger. | Default water-contacting bodies to confirmed interpolation. Opt-in prediction only when the selected P3/E1 pair captures and replays the complete affected domain/boundary/body state and once-only impulses under N1's bounded budget; otherwise correction discards the speculative branch. A seed or locally higher-resolution water mesh is insufficient. A client-side water query is labelled replica/predicted and never commits server force, flux or save state. |
| Different visual water data can shift a shoreline or underwater transition even if gameplay content matches. SHADERS-MEDIA correctly allows separate visual products, but does not set the water-specific geometric limit. | V2/V3's local visual residual and optical interface must declare a displacement/contact/occupancy error against the server's physical query at interactive shores and hulls. If it exceeds the game tolerance, reduce/mask detail there or reject that visual tier; visual blending never changes P3 water ownership. Underwater medium selection uses the declared physical domain/interface or a labelled view-only approximation, with no gameplay occupancy claim. Correction/source revision invalidates water and underwater effect histories. |
| SESSIONS-SOURCE pins builds and save roots per authority, while N1 permits headless servers and replicas. A water edit could otherwise appear to mutate a live server or client save. | Water source/model edits follow the pinned build and staged F4/W2 activation rules. Server/standalone P7 alone saves durable E1 state and external forcing; clients receive replication projections, not recovery roots. An editor play instance has its own water state/ledger and cannot apply simulated river storage back as authored source by default. |

N1 already rejects universal lockstep and arbitrary full-world rollback, supplies committed snapshot groups, and keeps client presentation separate. These fixes specialize its declared compatibility, grouping and correction rules; they add no new networking subsystem or mandatory water simulation to the local baseline.
