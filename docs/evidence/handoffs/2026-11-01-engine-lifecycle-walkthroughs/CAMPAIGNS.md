# Storage dogfood: one AI optimization campaign

Paper walkthrough, 1 November 2026. No worker, benchmark, collector or cleanup was run. The values and trial results below are examples, not measured engine evidence or selected limits. This applies [storage lifecycle](../../../design/STORAGE-LIFECYCLE.md) to the [AI performance commission](../../../design/AI-PERFORMANCE.md) and the forest case in [performance walkthroughs](../../../design/PERFORMANCE-WALKTHROUGHS.md). The repo [storage guidance](../../../ASSET-LIFECYCLE.md) governs present manual work.

## Commission and admission

The coordinator proposes the O01 forest: one pinned Mac/OS/backend, 1440p output, moving vegetation and Sun, character, shadow, UI/audio and region changes. The proposed success target is a 25% lower p95 render-completion time while preserving the agreed visual and response criteria and a selected memory cap. The evaluator must define the actual machine, baseline, quality envelope, timing protocol and useful-gain threshold before any result can win. A faster culling pass alone is insufficient.

Two candidate workers explore distinct hypotheses: A changes F4/V2 instance layout and conservative visibility; B changes V2/V3/E1 shadow demand/reuse. The evaluator owns pinned workloads, reference checks, clocks and results outside their editable candidate areas. The coordinator owns the joint transient-memory budget, candidate combinations and final integration. A separate agent looking at the same writable tree is a reviewer, not a trusted evaluator boundary. Until isolated execution and result publication exist, an accountable human/operator must verify the actual launched binary and loaded shader/product identities.

For a **hypothetical** local run, the coordinator allocates two private worker roots, one evaluator staging root, at most eight cheap revisions and three expensive finalists, plus fixed device hours. Suppose the disk has 25 GiB free and the chosen floor is 8 GiB. Before launch, the coordinator estimates simultaneous peak space, including each worker's source closure and mutable build, evaluator capture, cold/warm products, unpacking, and old/new staging overlap. For example, if those peaks total 19 GiB, the run is **not admitted** merely because each worker individually fits: 25 − 19 is below the 8 GiB floor. Reduce concurrent jobs, selective LFS materialization or capture size, then re-estimate. These figures explain the rule; they are not proposed defaults. Check live free space during the run because reservations are estimates and another process can write to the same volume.

The minimum run record is one campaign section and one row per attempt, not a per-file registry. Campaign: owner/run ID, objective and versioned evaluator/workloads, baseline source/build/product identities, target configuration, source authority, owned roots, peak reservation/floor, limits and review/stop trigger. Attempt row: hypothesis and parent, source/dirty-patch identity, evaluator identity, actual build/loaded product IDs if built, result or failure, disposition and retained-evidence link or explicit expiry. Group large artifacts by root and storage class. Record the exact bytes and recovery location only for a result whose continued availability is claimed. Candidate hashes intentionally differ; a hash of deleted bytes is provenance, not a restore method.

## Twenty proposals, three finalists, one selected result

1. Start from B0, preserving its exact source and input closure, build recipe and a representative raw baseline measurement set. Make the evaluator's version and fixture manifest immutable for this round. Worker A and B each take a private root; only verified immutable products can be shared. A measurement lease serializes the contended Mac while both workers can reason or compile elsewhere. Cheap compile/correctness checks screen proposals before GPU time.
2. Across twenty **illustrative** proposals, A tries several bounds/layouts and B tries cache invalidation policies. They may make many local revisions, but only eight enter the bounded cheap-evaluation allocation; the rest receive a compact “not submitted” reason. A's fourth candidate saves traversal work but drops offscreen tree shadows. B's third looks fast only while the Sun is frozen. Both fail and cannot become “wins.” Keep one decisive shadow/motion failure capture and its exact fixture/source if it will substantiate the rejection; release repetitive frames, profiler dumps, private build trees and cooked products after the evaluator has finished reading them. If the capture or source is released, mark that failure as a historical observation rather than a runnable reproduction.
3. Maintain B0 and a *verified* current best. A faster but quality-failing attempt never replaces the best. Candidate records remain append-only at the decision level; deleting bulk does not delete the fact that an idea was tried. If two candidates are close and still needed for the choice, they remain under review. Do not label every intermediate attempt a finalist or keep all its compiled outputs “just in case.”
4. The coordinator selects at most three expensive finalists, independently rebuilds them from pinned inputs, and compares B0, A, B, and a coherent A+B only where the shared bounds/schema and validity rules actually permit combination. It records each integrated source and loaded-product lineage, not a synthetic multiplication of A and B speedups. Full-scene runs include animation, streaming, turning/teleporting camera, wind/Sun changes, cold preparation and an indoor/sparse held-out scene. The evaluator retains the raw timed run blocks needed to recalculate the claimed p95, uncertainty and memory peak, plus selected quality/motion evidence. Same-binary noise controls and independent final runs are part of that retained set. A profiler trace used merely to choose a hypothesis can expire once its conclusion is recorded.
5. Suppose A+B wins on this Mac but raises cold shader preparation and the memory overlap above the agreed cap. The integrated candidate is rejected or revised; neither separate local win authorizes promotion. If it passes, integrate sequentially into the owning code, run the exact combined revision again, record the Mac-only claim and fallback, and keep the prior known-good source/release closure until its rollback condition ends. A visually changed result still awaits the required appearance review. The final record says which criteria were measured, accepted by a person, or remain unqualified.

At selection/handoff, keep: baseline; exact integrated winner source, required generated/original inputs, build/cook recipe and dependency/toolchain identity; evaluator/workload version and selected raw run blocks; decisive quality evidence; current known-good rollback closure; and any genuinely unresolved alternative with a named next decision. The winner's build cache need not survive if a cold rebuild and restore from durable retained inputs actually succeeds. For a generated irreplaceable input, a prompt/service ID is not a rebuild chain. A source commit or small patch is useful only with its base, dependencies and verified recovery path. A local ignored file or LFS pointer alone does not prove recoverability.

| Losing material | When it can go | What remains |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rejected private compiler outputs, staging, generated shaders and cooked products | Evaluator readers/processes have released them and a retained source can rebuild anything still claimed | Attempt row with source/product identity, result and rejection reason; no claim that deleted binary is runnable |
| Repeated captures, GPU readbacks and profiler traces | A selected decisive subset supports the decision and raw timing evidence for the final claim remains | Representative capture/settings or concise diagnosis; expired artifacts explicitly marked unavailable |
| Dominated candidate source/worktree | The decision is closed, no unresolved comparison or unique input remains, and the owner has inspected dirty/untracked/ignored contents | Compact attempt history; optionally a small text patch with exact base if later rebuild is valuable; otherwise mark source non-rebuildable |
| Shared immutable cache object | No protected release, evidence, active reader or build needs it and its source closure is recoverable | Cache key/provenance may remain; cache bytes need not |
| Previous known-good release | Explicit rollback/support and saved-content obligations end | Its historical manifest/result; never infer expiry from winner publication alone |

“Keep the history of failed ideas” means the small hypothesis, identity, result and reason record. It does **not** require every failed checkout, sample frame or trace. Conversely, a summary graph without the final raw run blocks cannot support a precise measured improvement, and a deleted input cannot be recovered from its checksum. Claims narrow when evidence expires.

## Disk pressure, abandonment and races

Mid-search, suppose an unrelated writer lowers free space below the admitted stop threshold while A has a half-written build and B is awaiting the Mac. The coordinator stops new admissions, cancels A at a safe boundary, waits for tool/process termination and evaluator readers, then lets A inspect and remove **A's own** incomplete build/staging. It may evict verified, unleased reproducible cache objects under the cache policy. It does not purge B's root, old releases, unique ignored inputs or an unknown worktree. If space still cannot be recovered within the owned scope, the campaign pauses or ends with the best verified result and a clear unmeasured remainder. Disk pressure does not authorize expanding the resource budget or deleting someone else's work. Free bytes and directory footprints are measured again because APFS sharing and concurrent writes can differ.

If B crashes or the machine sleeps, an expired heartbeat makes its root *suspect*, not disposable. The coordinator checks host/boot and process/job epoch, reconciles with B's owner, and preserves unique dirty/ignored files. The worker root is reused only after readers and processes end, the candidate result is published or intentionally abandoned, and private incremental/build state is cleared or invalidated. On reassignment, verify the checked-out source plus dirty patch, evaluator version, fixture, target flags and actually loaded products. Stale work in a reused root can produce a false win even if the source commit looks right. Do not hard-link mutable workspaces or let two workers write the same cache without supported locking.

For future automatic collection, a dry run must report roots and reasons. A finalist or reader must register protection **before** publication/open; retiring an object must reject new readers and wait for existing ones. Recheck roots and leases before removal so a newly selected finalist cannot race a collector that saw it as dominated. A timestamp-only sweep cannot provide this. The exact atomic protocol and recovery journal are D1 design/implementation gaps. Until they exist, use owner-scoped inspected manual cleanup, not a pretend collector.

If the campaign is abandoned for poor results, retain B0 and the best verified source/evidence, concise failed-hypothesis history and any unique feedback. Remove the run owner's idle builds/captures after inspection; mark final qualification “not achieved” or “inconclusive.” If the premise changes (different output quality, device or workload), version a new commission and baseline. Do not reuse old scores as though the target were unchanged. No run begins or resumes automatically from this paper design.

## Minimal skill and tool split

The existing `repo-structure` skill and its storage reference tell contributors how to classify, retain and manually clean owned scratch; `code-conventions` and `testing` govern any eventual implementation. Existing `AI-PERFORMANCE.md` and `PERFORMANCE-WALKTHROUGHS.md` supply campaign judgment, evaluator separation and promotion criteria. A short performance/diagnosis skill pointer could remind agents to use the commission and storage budget; copying a long workflow into every mandatory skill would add context and drift. No dedicated optimization skill is needed until real repeated runs reveal a distinct routing problem.

Present tools: Git worktree/LFS operations, repo scripts and manual handoffs can identify and inspect specific roots; existing tests can check current code. They do **not** implement campaign admission, trusted evaluator isolation, hardware leases, source/product/loaded-binary lineage, coordinated publication/reader leases, raw result retention, cold restore, or safe automatic collection. Future T2/F4/T1 work should first provide a local per-run record, inventory, reservations and owner-scoped dry-run, then one end-to-end baseline/candidate/finalist with cold restore. Add an automatic collector or remote worker only after publication/reader races and recovery are proved. The schema should express these group-level facts, not enumerate every temporary file.

**Actionable gaps exposed:** (1) choose a real disk floor, peak accounting and stop/cancel policy on the selected machine; (2) isolate evaluator authority and verify loaded artifacts; (3) define atomic root/lease/retire ordering before automated deletion; (4) demonstrate cold recovery of selected inputs and a final rebuild with caches empty; (5) require results to distinguish retained raw evidence, historical summaries and expired, non-rebuildable trials. A single modest local campaign is enough to test this design before scaling search.
