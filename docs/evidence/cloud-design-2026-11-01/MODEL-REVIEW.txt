# Focused cloud-model review

1 November 2026. Reviewed the assembled `docs/design/modules/CLOUDS.md`, including the parent's live clarification that normal centroid wind uses world seconds and timelapse is a source/trajectory authoring preview. This review preserves the initial MODEL-STUDY.md. No maintained files, source data, bake, GPU work or runtime tests were changed/run.

**Judgement:** the 512m/128³ selection does not invalidate the prepared final-density representation. The primary operators, bounded full-source residency and current-field amortization are viable analytical choices. The midpoint/SNORM derivations are valid under the stated hypotheses. No switch of representation or further variant is recommended. Three local contract corrections/completions follow; numerical/visual/device acceptance remains unrun.

## Findings requiring resolution

### 1. History must include implementation/mapping uncertainty in coefficient validity, not treat the ideal residual as the complete change

Location: “Shared current field”, residual reduction; “History and local change”.

The identity `D_s sigma = r` is exact for the ideal stationary flow. A single explicit midpoint map is not a flow group: `Psi(h,Psi(k,q))` generally differs from `Psi(h+k,q)`, and `Psi(-h,.)` is not the exact inverse of `Psi(h,.)`. Reconstructing/resampling into an R16F trilinear grid adds another difference. Therefore the available `abs(b-a)/d` is a useful ideal-carrier residual proxy, but is not a conservative bound on all material change of the actual implemented current field.

Consequence: a low residual and low distributional motion spread can coexist with a wrong correspondence at a sharp cloud edge. A 1m local source gate alone does not establish subpixel history validity for entry/near views, especially under lambda=2. The parent has now added the right explicit rejection for projected mapping uncertainty >0.5 media pixel. Complete the definition of that uncertainty and its coefficient counterpart; do not report the old residual as a sufficient birth/death/warp-change bound.

Resolution condition: define per-query or conservative product-local `E_map` including midpoint, quantization, coarse/stationary fit and inverse/composition error, then transform it to world/pixel units. A safe projection test is the maximum projected displacement over the error ball; near the camera plane, simply reject. Also account for coefficient uncertainty, e.g. `E_sigma <= G_sigma*E_map + E_grid + E_quant` where a valid local gradient bound exists, and reject when `(abs(r)*abs(delta_s)+E_sigma)/(sigma_floor+sigma_now)>0.2`. At support/topology discontinuities without a valid bound, use direct coefficient checks or mark reactive. This is one sufficient operator, not a requirement for a new elaborate estimator. Resetting optional history remains a conforming conservative fallback.

Parent resolution supplied during review: compiler endpoint gradient bounds with `G*=max(G_i,G_j)*exp(L*d)`, propagated map/grid/half uncertainty, independent coefficient and projected-error rejection, and spatial-only fallback **close this operator issue analytically**, conditional on their explicit inclusion in the maintained design. The endpoint gradients must bound the actual quantized/interpolated fields. For one-step midpoint, the spatial Lipschitz factor is at most `1+L*abs(h)+(L*abs(h))²/2 <= exp(L*abs(h))`, so a valid runtime gradient bound does not require globally smooth flow curvature. Current sampled-field versus prior encoded-reader differences remain in the reconstruction-error term. Local bounds may improve reuse later; unhelpfully conservative bounds simply disable optional history. This review is not an objection to that completed closure.

The coarse maximum extinction is conservative for the *actual represented trilinear current texture* if its fine-voxel reduction includes the filter halo and outward half-rounding. Keep that valid empty-space result distinct from the residual proxy above. Prior swept bounds/tombstones must trigger a current-clear versus previous-nonzero rejection, including pixels with no current cloud samples; retaining a tombstone without using it cannot clear a ghost.

### 2. State the coordinate/scale convention of the residual ratio and the 1m gate

Location: world scaling equation and `R=|r|max*|delta_source_time|/(sigma_floor+sigma_now)`.

`r` and the product quantization floor naturally live in local optical units, while the ray marcher consumes `sigma_world=(k/lambda)*sigma_local`. If sigma_now in R is world extinction but the numerator/floor remain local, rejection changes incorrectly with scale and optical multiplier. Likewise the 1m fit gate is ambiguous between local source metres and world metres; lambda maps one to as much as two of the other.

Resolution condition: evaluate the ratio entirely in the contributing stamp's local units, **or** explicitly use `r_world=(k/lambda)*r_local` and `floor_world=(k/lambda)*floor_local` with world sigma. Do not use the summed multi-stamp extinction as the denominator for an individual stamp's birth/erosion test. k=0 contributes nothing and bypasses that ratio. Declare the source correspondence gate “1 local metre”; world/pixel history gates then apply lambda and the retained camera. If 1 world metre was intended, tighten the local gate per scale instead.

### 3. Prefix-subtraction quantization statement has the inequality reversed

Location: light cumulative-depth half-storage paragraph, sentence “absolute uncertainty at least eps(A)+eps(B)”.

For stored prefixes with absolute errors bounded by eps(A),eps(B), the rounding contribution to the difference is **at most** their sum, not at least. Actual errors can cancel or be zero. The sum is the conservative uncertainty radius to reserve when correlation is unknown; it is not a lower bound on actual error. The existing fallback when this bound exceeds 0.05 optical depth is a sensible policy. Change only that mathematical statement/term; the finite-path operator need not change. Separate quadrature/filter errors still remain, as the design already says.

## Analytical checks that passed at design level

- **Spatial bandwidth:** 512m/128 is4m local sampling, 2–8m world cells over lambda0.5–2. Baked optical detail finer than the actual sampling is not guaranteed; morphology/source optical tests decide that. A15m interval shift is3.75 density cells but is not inherently a failure of flow-guided reconstruction. The32m zero collar leaves at least17m after the worst15m endpoint displacement. The stated first four reconstructed voxel centres remain zero, allowing a zero edge sample through selected mips0/1/2, provided the cooker verifies the actual filtering/quantization path.
- **Midpoint bound:** for a carrier with norm<=V and Lipschitz L over the whole sampled path, `|x(t)-x0-t*v(x0)|<=LV*t²/2`. Integrating `L*(V*|t-h/2| + LV*t²/2)` gives `LV*h²/4 + L²V*|h|³/6`. With V2,L0.02,h7.5 this is0.61875m. It is conservative first/second-order error reasoning under only Lipschitz regularity; no globally C2 trilinear field or extra curvature assumption is needed. Validate V and L after quantization/interpolation/extension, not only at exported sample values. A practical certificate may bound the spectral norm of each trilinear-cell Jacobian from its component derivative ranges, including boundary extension.
- **Velocity quantization:** SNORM8 nearest rounding with component range[-2,2] has component error<=2/254; vector error<=sqrt(3)*2/254. Gronwall's flow perturbation bound is `delta_v*(exp(L*abs(h))-1)/L`, approximately0.110m. This is separate from the0.619m integration bound and external-source fit. Do not let the1m external fit gate quietly exclude those other errors if the advertised gate is intended to bound their total.
- **GPU source/layout:** the finite high-cap17.75MiB word-buffer source avoids portable3D depth/RGB-format assumptions. Packed scalar scratch plus R16F sampled textures avoids assuming R16F storage writes. Atlas slot dimensions and three density levels are consistent with the stated per-phase/capacity arithmetic. Five cloud texture bindings do not by themselves qualify the complete renderer, but the design explicitly requires whole-binding validation. No unbounded endpoint/prefetch lifetime is hidden.
- **Time semantics after clarification:** ordinary `dc/dt=u_world` uses world seconds, while internal material velocity correctly multiplies local carrier by `ds/dt`. Preview may sample a recorded source and recorded centroid path at64x without claiming force/physics/world wind ran64x. Use the preview's actual source age and recorded trajectory sample for history; never infer it from the preceding host frame.
- **Birth/death:** finite zero-endpoint density, source-interval reset, prior bounds and optional history rejection are a complete conservative direction. The moment thresholds are proposed calibration values and cannot be called visual acceptance. Full encoded residency enables old-age access but does not establish correspondence; the stated bounded history horizon remains necessary.

The main remaining risk is empirical: whether the actual external volume sequence passes local morphology, interval interpolation, optical precision and entry-view tests at the chosen bandwidth, and whether full-field reconstruction plus all consumers fits the integrated device budget. Those are explicit unrun qualification obligations rather than missing formation operators.
