## S1 — Complete preview trajectory has no explicit readiness producer

**Location:** CLOUDS “Evolution and motion equations”, scheduler/startup and diagnostic-timelapse paragraphs; “Normal update, transitions and authority”. **Kind:** bounded missing data/preparation contract.

The ordinary producer advances the centroid through fixed-tick wind integration and stores trajectory samples. The workbench opens at source age 90, can seek to birth, and diagnostic timelapse samples a **complete recorded centroid trajectory** together with source density. Complete encoded density residency supplies arbitrary optical ages, but recording the ordinary world as it runs only supplies the evaluated past; it does not supply a future centroid trajectory or necessarily the pre-start interval. The design has not selected whether this finite trajectory is precomputed from authored wind, analytically evaluable for constant wind, or a separately captured/readied recording. “Recorded” states the input without establishing its startup availability.

**Concrete case/consequence:** start a fresh workbench at age 90 and immediately seek to age 180, then back to 0. Optical density is available from the encoded clip, but matching world centroids cannot be recovered from a trajectory recorded only since this launch. An implementer must invent a preparation/replay policy, silently keep the wrong centroid, or run hidden catch-up. This can break shadow/reflection alignment or the no-hidden-time/no-startup-stall claims while density playback itself works.

**Resolution condition:** choose a finite preview trajectory producer and readiness requirement. For example, a constant/authored-wind preview can prepare the full bounded trajectory before enabling arbitrary seek; or a recording can be an explicit pinned input and seek remain unavailable until its range exists. State the interval, source-age↔trajectory-time mapping, interpolation and daylight mapping/default, ownership/version and bounded bytes/preparation cost. Initialize age 90 through that same path. Ordinary interactive worlds can retain their existing forward-only tick integration; no dynamic-weather forecasting or arbitrary reverse world simulation is requested. Verify the fresh-start 90→180→0 case by a paper trace, then Q4/Q6/Q7 when executable.

## S2 — The quoted source-traffic range mixes operation counts with unstated reuse

**Location:** CLOUDS “Shared current field and portable GPU layout”, read-only packed flow description, and “Cost and profile admission”, work-multiplier paragraph. **Kind:** cost-account correction; not a measured performance failure.

The stated full-field workload is 128^3 = 2,097,152 output voxels, with three trilinear carrier queries/voxel. The same design defines each manual trilinear carrier query as eight neighbouring packed four-byte values. Thus the unsuppressed carrier **logical operand-load bytes alone** are:

`128^3 × 3 × 8 × 4 = 201,326,592 bytes = 192 MiB/update`.

Two endpoint density interpolations add 16 scalar operands/voxel: at least 32 MiB if counted as ideal one-byte values, or up to 128 MiB of four-byte word operands before any sharing, plus indirection/occupancy access. The paragraph instead gives “32–128 MiB source traffic/phase/frame before cache effects”. That can be a plausible estimate only with unstated swept-occupancy omission, load sharing or cache reuse; it is not the logical full-field count implied by the declared work. Sparse endpoint byte caps do not by themselves prove the reconstructed swept-occupancy fraction.

**Consequence:** the design's bandwidth/critical-path comparison can underestimate or mislabel the repeated source work, obscuring what a proposed optimization actually saves. No contradiction with the 3/5 ms desired targets is established because those are explicitly unmeasured.

**Resolution condition:** separately state logical operations/operand bytes, the admitted or estimated evaluated-occupancy factor, and expected physical memory traffic after reuse/cache effects. Label 32–128 MiB as a conditional estimate if retained; include the unsuppressed case and indirection overhead. Keep measured bandwidth/timing as later Q7 evidence. This does not require an implementation or another optimization programme now.

## Qualification and remaining limits

The finite source product and cooker/reader are specified but unwritten. Valid external fine-time reference samples, recoverable rights-bearing content, a plausible 240-second lifecycle, 1x/16x/64x pacing, no invented bridges/contours, quantization/cache/source-ray errors and acceptable correspondence all remain to be proved. Loose history bounds can legitimately select spatial-only output; no correspondence claim is rescued merely by lower variance. The camera/fog optical extension is proposed rather than baseline-qualified.

No full-scene target measurement exists; 0.5 ms CPU/3–5 ms GPU and memory/startup figures are targets/planning arithmetic. The declared fallback work, ambient coefficient positions, secondary/probe consumers, sustained thermal state, pipeline readiness and actual loaded hashes must be present in Q7. Windows/mobile await their own evidence; a working Mac/browser slice cannot close those columns. The current evidence REVIEW correctly marks pending review/qualification, so no maturity finding is raised solely because those updates await this commission.

Stop here: resolve S1/S2 in the design and verify those changes sequentially; no new feature or physics programme is needed from this review.
