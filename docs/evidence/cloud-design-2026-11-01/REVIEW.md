# Cloud design review and integration

1 November 2026. Analytical design evidence only: no Thrum cloud runtime, bake, visual test or device measurement.
The author had prior exposure to the Broadsides systems. Fresh Sol High workers derived requirements and studied
content/transport from the original cloud brief and Thrum contracts, without old cloud implementation or an expected
inventory/verdict. Source/technique decisions and the resulting design are engineering recommendations.

source format and whole-clip residency. A transport study derived C08 composition, cache formats, off-consumer and
portable work/residency rules. The parent reconciled them into [CLOUDS](../../design/modules/CLOUDS.md), including the
512 m fidelity choice, slot-capacity atlas layout, finite local-light policy, all-media history validity, half-prefix
error/fallback and explicit ambient constituents. Parent derivations/recommendations are not attributed to the studios.

## Final integration and limits

The governing design is [CLOUDS](../../design/modules/CLOUDS.md); its [capabilities](../../design/modules/CLOUD-CAPABILITIES.md)
map28 required scoped outcomes plus4 explicitly optional extensions to owners and Q1–Q8 proof groups. Root synthesis
aligns E1 with V1/V2/V3/V4, F1/F2/F4, P7/N1, composition, initial specifications and workload admission. The original
attenuation-only baseline and active foundation construction order remain intact. The proposed mechanism is a detailed
recommendation; no provider/API/code/content has been promoted into a running Thrum renderer.

The parent performed arithmetic/reference/hash checks and reviewed the assembled edits. Independent design agreement
supports scoped analytical closure only. It cannot establish source availability, perceptual success, actual GPU
traffic, stable frame pacing, mobile support or user acceptance. Those dimensions remain explicitly unrun in maturity.
