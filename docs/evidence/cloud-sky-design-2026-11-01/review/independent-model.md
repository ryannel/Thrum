The experience implies five different structural organizations: localized buoyant plumes; plume plus thin spreading outflow; a cellular boundary-layer sheet with holes; a laterally coherent weakly varying sheet; and thin anisotropic ice filaments. Source content must represent actual local condensation/erosion, split/merge, vertical development and support changes. Independent candidate families:

1. Prepared final optical clips with fitted local motion and residuals. Actual topology change and authored density are representable; retained byte cost scales with resolved volume, temporal bandwidth, number of independent source variants and the range of controls. Deep convection, anvils, sheets and cirrus require different anisotropic source domains or composition.
2. Evaluable procedural forms with persistent growth elements: buoyant plume parcels and local erosion for convection; evolving thickness/coverage level sets for sheets; material-space filament curves/swept profiles for cirrus. ## Decision and evidence boundary

**Recommend a layered world/event model with family-specific evolution masters, cooked to a common optical query.** Use actual prepared 3D evolution for convective cores, anisotropic layer products for low sheets/anvils, and persistent filament products for cirrus. A complete master contains local source, transport and local loss; cooking may retain a field recipe or sample final density with motion/residuals. These are interchangeable private representations only after they preserve the same accepted optical evolution. The existing renderer's coefficient integration, common lighting and reference tools can remain consumers. Source content, rather than the integrator, must supply lifecycle.

This is a proposed authored model, analytically complete enough to identify its producers and falsify its claims, not a newly demonstrated meteorological model or a qualified content library. The prescribed optical/condensate drivers below are **our synthesis**. No cited studio presentation discloses this combination. Source authoring, visual quality, cadence and complete-sky performance are unrun. A physically derived offline master may replace the authored master while retaining the source product contract.

The minimum useful authoring unit is **an event or connected layer patch with a lifecycle**, not a density cube and an opacity curve. Its recipe stores stable event/source IDs; world support and altitude; reference wind frame; formation/maturity/breakup intervals; local source/loss tracks; correlated subparts; finite variation choices; optical calibration; source extent/resolution; and permitted transformation/phase-rate ranges. These are actual cooked dependencies, not descriptions left for an implementer to invent.

## 1. Source producers, quantities and equations

Use world metres, seconds, Y up, cloud liquid/ice proxy concentration `q_l,q_i >= 0` in kg/m³ (or an explicitly dimensioned optical proxy), and extinction `sigma_t` in m⁻¹. If the producer is authored, C/E are prescribed external creation/loss and do not claim water-vapour or energy conservation. If a physical producer is selected, it owns the corresponding vapour, heat and phase exchanges. No renderer invents either closure.

A useful offline authored master evolves:

```
∂t q_l + div(q_l v_l) = C_l - E_l - F
∂t q_i + div(q_i v_i) = C_i - E_i + F
F = k_freeze(y,t) q_l
sigma_t = kappa_l q_l + kappa_i q_i
sigma_s = omega_l kappa_l q_l + omega_i kappa_i q_i
```

`kappa` is a calibrated mass-extinction coefficient in m²/kg; `omega` is dimensionless single-scatter albedo. Concentration is not opacity. Sources `C` have kg m⁻³ s⁻¹ and losses use bounded nonnegative rates `E=k_e(x,t)q`; finite transport must remain nonnegative. Liquid-to-ice transfer F contributes exactly once to each side. The authored product labels q as an optical proxy if its mass interpretation is unsupported. These equations identify complete source/sink ownership without commissioning runtime thermodynamics.

**Actual producers:** authoring stores compact spatial masks as moving signed-distance volumes or spline sweeps, emission pulse functions, cap/base heights, velocity fields and local loss fields. Each source track is a compact smooth function with a location, support, amplitude, start/end and seed. Birth turns on local C in previously clear support; growth advects that newly created material; breakup shuts off C and activates local erosion/fragmentation masks, which can open holes and disconnect support. A fixed source domain is only storage; occupied support changes. Initial q is clear for formation sequences; inflow boundaries supply declared concentrations, and outgoing material is retained by a linked product or explicitly exits the source support. Zero/clamped boundaries cannot cut off an anvil or wisp invisibly.

The master can be produced with a cloud-aware offline solver, a DCC fluid tool plus these authored drivers, or a deterministic reference advection/reaction authoring tool. A raw smoke simulation mapped globally to cloud opacity is not an accepted source. The acceptance witness must show cloud-specific development; naming a tool is not evidence that its output passes. The selected first implementation should use the cheapest master path that produces those witnesses, and cook its **final** optical field to the retained runtime source format. A missing master remains an unfinished capability.

For bounded convection without a new live fluid solver, one explicit authored flow candidate uses an axisymmetric streamfunction about an updraft centre:

```
Psi(r,y,t) = A(t) r² exp(-(r/R(t))²) f(y,t)
v_r = -(1/r) ∂y Psi
v_y =  (1/r) ∂r Psi
```

At r=0 use the analytic limit. A is m/s and f is dimensionless; Psi is m³/s. f rises from the lower support and declines to zero at the stable cap. Positive centre updraft and the negative vertical derivative near the cap produce radial outflow. Off-axis return flow plus compact curl-field perturbations provide entrainment/roll-up in the master, with source pulses injected only in the moist core above cloud base. The velocity is divergence-free away from authored boundaries; q still has named creation/loss. This is a **testable phenomenological candidate**, not a claim of buoyancy or cloud thermodynamics. If it produces smoke rolls, smooth columns or repetitive toroids, use stronger offline simulation or sculpted source controls; do not promote it on equation correctness.

For stratiform layers, use advected horizontal material coordinates chi, a column amount M(chi,t) in kg/m², base b(chi,t), and thickness H(chi,t):

```
∂t M + div_h(M u_h) = C_M - k_M M
H = H_max min(1, sqrt(M/M_star))   (one authored closure)
q(chi,y,t) = M/H * P((y-b)/H) * d(chi,y-b) / Z(chi,t)
Z = integral_0^1 P(eta) d(chi,H eta) d eta
```

P is a nonnegative compact vertical profile with unit integral; d is bounded persistent material detail with a positive floor wherever M>0, and Z normalizes the column. When M=0, define q=0 without division. C_M is local cooling/moistening prescription, k_M local drying/entrainment; neither comes from global coverage. Exponential loss alone never makes exact zero in finite time: an explicit bounded drying sink `min(M/dt, D_M)` or compact finite-duration terminal track must remove residual columns to create true holes/termination. Formation therefore increases local column amount **and thickness**, while breakup thins and erodes connected support. H and P differ for St and Sc; neither is an unrelated opacity knob. Use material height y-b in d to avoid stretching every detail as H grows. d can be baked from accepted master texture; do not evolve independent 4D noise to substitute for structure. If billowing tops need non-columnar geometry, cook actual 3D local master patches under the same layer owner.

This column closure is suitable for thin, mostly single-valued sheets and broad cell tops. It cannot describe deep convective overturning, multiply folded clouds or anvil-core junctions. Use the 3D master in those regions. Do not extrapolate the layer closure there merely because the renderer can display it.

Cirrus uses continuously born filament cohorts, rather than stretching a cumulus source. A head trajectory h(t_b) emits ice cohorts at birth time t_b with local concentration/line mass. For cohort age a=t-t_b:

```
d x_h/dt = u_h(x_h,y,t)
d y/dt   = u_y(x_h,y,t) - v_f(a)
w(a)^2  = w_0^2 + 2 D a
m(a)    = m_0 B(a) exp(-integral k_sub dt)
```

B is a compact smooth birth/terminal envelope, not the opacity of the whole formation; `v_f` is prescribed crystal fall speed, D an authored diffusivity in m²/s, and k_sub local sublimation rate. A swept compact transverse kernel around the resulting curve converts line mass to q_i with its cross-section normalization. A continuous stream or fine cohorts avoid point beads. Different fall speeds and altitude-dependent shared wind generate trailing shear/curvature; local birth stops before existing tails thin/sublimate. A terminal compact track retires the final support. Seeded variation affects head paths, pulse spacing, widths and fall-speed cohorts, while each cohort retains identity. This supplies filament growth, fall streaks, head/tail evolution and local dissolution. It does not claim nucleation, realistic crystal size distribution, halos or correct ice phase functions; those are optional stronger content/lighting scope.

Dimensions below are **proposed authoring envelopes**, not universal atmospheric constants or measured resolution sufficiency. Select actual master voxel scales by the smallest defining cue at the admitted camera distance. A 512 m product is a useful *small Cu content class*, never the universal size.

The Cb outflow and core are **one event with multiple products**. Split a shared master using nonnegative partition weights w_core+w_outflow=1 within the shared support, so their optical sum reconstructs the master rather than double-density overlap. Do not independently bake/re-light two overlapping volumes and add final images. An anvil detaching into a high-cloud event transfers source ownership/products at a matching state/time; it is not destroyed and respawned with a new random density.

For Sc→St or St→Sc, retain the layer's M, base, material coordinates and event identities; change local source/loss/cell-top controls continuously under an authored conversion interval. Recipe morphing alone can still fail quality: if the required actual layer breakup cannot be produced by the column closure, replace the **content producer** with a prepared connected transition master. An accepted transition master is the compatibility bridge; two unrelated endpoint clips plus a crossfade are not.

## 3. Runtime source, world and weather model

### State and responsibility

- E1 weather owns a world-position/time-indexed wind field u(x,y,t), altitude/base/cap drivers, front trajectories and authored formation conditions. Constant/keyframed drivers are valid. E1 cloud lifecycle owns the event schedule and each source's event ID, birth time, age, branch, transform and source recipe. No camera owns events.
- F4 owns immutable master/cooked hashes and residency. The cloud provider owns active source products/current caches and publishes nonnegative sigma_t, sigma_s, phase/material ID, bounds, timestamp/generation and motion validity. V3 owns joint transport for main views, receivers, atmosphere and reflected views. V4 owns per-view history/display.
- P7 saves the authored world weather cursor plus active event/source identities, phases and branches. Derived GPU fields are rebuilt. Network projections carry the finite event/weather state when visual agreement is required; a headless authority need not retain source voxels unless a gameplay query actually needs them.

A local source in a co-moving reference frame maps to world position with a(t)+A(t)xi. Shared wind contributes anchor motion; source-internal motion contributes only residual billow/outflow/sedimentation. Never apply the reference wind in both the bake and the runtime transform. With source phase phi(t), its material velocity is:

```
v_world = da/dt + dA/dt * xi + A * v_source(xi,phi) * dphi/dt
```

Use an actual invertible deformation map and derivative when wind shear deforms the source. A single constant anchor drift does not represent vertically different winds in a deep storm. The reference master declares its supported wind/shear class; bounded shared shear can deform it, but an extreme change selects a compatible future branch/source regime rather than arbitrarily stretching its density. Nonuniform scale changes cloud aspect ratio and optical path length, so expose finite admitted variants. If sigma is left unchanged, a geometric scale s multiplies optical depth by s; a 1/s remap preserves depth but changes the content's density meaning. Author and qualify the intended policy.

**Prepared clip graphs:** each edge is actual time-varying final density plus correspondence/residual; junctions have an exact common endpoint density, material map and required derivative compatibility. Continuation/maturity/breakup suffixes are authored from the same junction state. Weather can request a branch at the next junction, with a declared maximum response delay; arbitrary mid-clip crossfade is not continuity. A mature loop must have a matching start/end field and motion with a motion witness; otherwise use finite-duration mature content and retire it through its breakup edge. A source restart must not recur every few seconds to hide a finite clip. Phase rate is bounded by source content's believable motion range and shared time semantics; normal and accelerated inspection use the same continuous source clock.

**Full-sky composition:** place persistent source events from world-space recipes with a stable hashed seed and a finite event cursor. A front is an authored moving corridor (position, width, direction and progress), not a global blend parameter. It modifies local future source intensity, event-birth rate, persistence and breakup requests. Existing events retain identity and their already accumulated material/source phase. Population caps are planning/admission limits: reaching them delays/rejects new authored events or selects a qualified larger source patch; it cannot cull existing visible/shadowing events by camera distance.

Birth-rate coverage control needs closure. For isolated sources with mean footprint A_bar and lifetime L_bar, a first estimate is cloud fraction `c≈1-exp(-lambda*A_bar*L_bar)` for a Poisson placement process, where lambda is births per area per second. Thus `lambda≈-ln(1-c)/(A_bar*L_bar)` only for weakly correlated isolated cells. The count and memory diverge as c approaches one; this calculation specifically rejects “make overcast by spawning more isolated Cu.” High coverage selects a connected Sc/St layer recipe with preserved transition sources. Use deterministic blue-noise/clustered event placement where appropriate and measure actual projected union coverage; the Poisson expression is a planning estimate, not the authoritative coverage field.

Sharing has three distinct levels: immutable source bytes shared by many instances; evaluated local fields shared only by instances at the same source phase/variant/deformation; world lighting/depth caches shared across all consumers where their validity allows. Different event ages do **not** become one current field merely because they reference the same clip. Phase bins can save current-product memory but introduce synchronization/error; keep their phase/shape error explicit and qualify them. Variation must include several accepted masters and asynchronous source schedules, not only uniform scale and rotation.

### Required weather sequences

| Sequence | Concrete changing causes and preserved state |
| --- | --- |
| Clear→scattered | Front/diurnal author track raises moist-source event birth locally, with staggered births; old clear support remains clear until each source actually forms. Existing Cu continue their lifecycle. |
| Increasing cover | Increase new-cell birth/survival locally; grow neighbouring low-layer M sources and retained cell footprints. Existing cells merge through a connected authored low-layer transition. Cross a declared morphology regime into Sc rather than silently overpopulating Cu. |
| Overcast development | Local cooling/moistening C_M closes gaps and thickens an already connected Sc/St layer. Front edge moves across the world; holes disappear by edge movement/condensation. Source identity and material coordinates persist behind the edge. |
| Developing thunderstorm | A selected existing deepening Cu event takes a compatible growth branch; new upper material follows its core into the cap and outflow. Neighbouring cloud events persist; their illumination changes through the common shadow field. |
| Approaching thunderstorm | Existing distant storm event translates under the shared layer wind while retaining phase, outflow extent and projected ground shadow. The front's rain/lightning seams approach from the same event space/time. No new storm spawns at the camera horizon. |

A weather designer chooses a coherent timeline/driver recipe, not arbitrary independent sliders with a meteorological claim. Immediate coverage requests that contradict current content can return “transitioning” with the completion time. Required transitions are real paths with accepted connected source content, not a promise that a universal parameter blend will discover them.

### Layers, distance and horizon

Use independent low/mid/high altitude bands with a shared atmosphere and shared wind field sampled at their own heights. Bands describe source selection/placement, not exclusive optical partitions: a tower crosses them. The far sky must come from the same world field/time as nearby events, using coarser optical products or an environment capture at a declared place/time. Do not keep one detailed local sky plus an unrelated painted horizon.

Prefer anisotropic finite slabs and sparse blocks for sheets, and a curve/filament hierarchy for Ci. Use a shared spatial index over all active source bounds for view/light/receiver rays, and union bounds for early skipping. “Offscreen” is not dead: receiver and reflection queries can require a source absent from the main camera frustum. Retain/query that source or an admitted source-derived projection.

The admitted world extent and curvature need a decision. For a terrestrial radius R, planar-layer height error at horizontal distance d is about d²/(2R): about 196 m at 50 km and 785 m at 100 km for R=6371 km. That is comparable to thin-cloud thickness. A long-range profile should place clouds on shared spherical altitude shells (storm/local tangent patches map into them), with atmosphere sharing the planet centre/radius. A planar bounded game may instead declare a finite extent whose margins are hidden by an error-qualified atmospheric/far-field continuation; camera movement, reflected rays and Sun rays must stay within that validity. Merely applying a fog fade to a sharp clip boundary is not a full-sky model.

Source LOD changes representation of the same event, not its phase, birth or location. Prepare coarse extinction and fine products with a once-only contribution switch, matching optical summaries and transition bounds. Mean density is not an exact footprint optical equivalent: `E[exp(-tau)] != exp(-E[tau])`; thinning a broken layer into mean density can darken every gap. Qualify transmittance/coverage as well as density errors, including near-horizon paths and low Sun. Distant source caches need world/epoch, source generation/phase, light and footprint validity; per-view radiance history is not a world source cache.

## 4. Common light, shadows, reflections and weather seams

At position/time the joint integrator sums extinction and scattering across all overlapping source products. A phase closure is scattering-weighted, not chosen by the nearest family label. For direct Sun:

```
tau_cloud(x,omega_s,t) = integral_0^s_exit sigma_t(x+s omega_s,t) ds
T_cloud = exp(-tau_cloud)
E_direct = E_sun * T_air * T_cloud
```

All layers and storm parts participate in the Sun path, including offscreen sources. One source/light registry supplies direction, spectrum and irradiance; V3 applies air/cloud attenuation once. The same source coefficients supply camera extinction, receiver shadow and reflected appearance at the same t. Multiple-scattering/ambient terms are a named V3 approximation under the same combined illumination, not independently lit family images. Thick overcast and deep storm interiors are the hard witnesses; thin fair-weather Cu is insufficient lighting coverage.

Receiver projection can be coarser than the visible field if its broad world mass/coverage and timestamp error are qualified. Reflection paths integrate the source from the reflected origin/direction or use a source-derived cache/probe with explicit location/time/age; mirroring main-view cloud pixels cannot establish offscreen sky. A low-layer source can shadow a high cloud depending on Sun geometry, while a high anvil can shadow lower clouds/ground; independent per-layer ambient light would miss those interactions. C08's overlapping-media rule remains authoritative.

Source correspondence supplies motion only where the same material structure survives. New condensation, disappearing edges and splice/discontinuity regions report low validity/reactivity and reject inappropriate temporal history. Their lack of correspondence is not zero motion. Each view uses its actual prior image time; accelerated inspection, pauses/seeks and source restore invalidate affected caches by event/region/generation.

Precipitation/lightning remain external implementations. The cloud event publishes stable event ID, active time/support, cloud-base/cap, advecting storm pose, precipitation-source region/rate **if authored by the selected weather producer**, and permitted lightning source region/activity window. Optical density cannot infer rain rate or electrical charge. Authored rates/windows are labelled authored and have one weather authority; a physical provider would supply its own conserved precipitation/charge state. Lightning flash emission is evaluated by V3 through the same cloud extinction when that consumer exists. Rain shafts, flash channels, wetness and audio do not die when the visible cloud draw is disabled. This is a seam, not implementation of those effects.

## 5. Analytical representation/cost comparison

Let V_s be source voxel count, F_s frames, b encoded bytes/voxel, f_s occupied fraction, G simultaneously evaluated **phase groups**, N world instances, P_v shaded pixels for view v, K_v medium samples, C_q cost of coefficient lookup, K_l light-path samples and H receivers/shadow texels. Ignoring compression metadata:

```
encoded source bytes ~= sum_s V_s F_s b f_s
current field bytes  ~= sum_g V_g b_current
source evaluation    ~= sum_g V_g * source_eval_cost * update_rate_g
view/light work      ~= sum_v P_v K_v (C_q + light_eval_cost)
shadow work          ~= H K_l C_q * shadow_update_rate
```

World instance transforms/indexing scale with N; immutable encoded source residency scales with unique source content. Sparse acceleration adds metadata, padding and lookup cost and only saves empty support. Dense overcast has little empty space to exploit. Sun caches, atmosphere/reflection consumers, history, uploads, CPU masters and replacement overlap are additional costs. Extra views multiply their ray work, not world source evolution; reflection probes may add several directions/views per update.

| Candidate | What it saves | Consequential multiplier / failure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dense prepared final clips | Bounded runtime source evaluation, actual accepted local evolution, straightforward common optical field | V×frames×variants×branch alternatives; active different phases also multiply decoded/current fields. Large sheets/anvils dominate bytes. Fast Sun/camera motion still requires transport work. |
| Sparse prepared clips / bricks | Empty exterior and coherent low-density blocks, exact immutable sharing | Occupancy over all frames may fill the source support; page/brick metadata, temporal union occupancy and sampling indirection remain. No magical savings for a thick filled layer. |
| Evaluable family recipes | Small control state, finite age evaluation, no frame sequence residency, cheap Ci curves/column maps | Field evaluation may repeat at every view/light sample unless materialized once. Noise/kernel counts, dynamic index updates, material-detail correspondence and true morphology still need complete mechanisms. Smooth inflatable blobs are the characteristic Cu failure. |
| Layered hybrid (recommended) | Family-appropriate dimensionality, world sharing, reusable local 3D content, one optical integration | Both content and compatibility machinery must be real. Need connected cross-family transition masters, phase-group accounting, source-derived far field and one owner at joins. Not automatically faster than clips at a small workload. |
| Live moist/ice simulation | Response to forcing with physical state/control and fewer prepared branches | 3D state fields, solver iterations/substeps, resolution and domain size, authority/save/network/feedback contracts. No primary source inspected establishes Thrum's complete frame budget on target devices. Not required by this brief. |

A 128³ R8 source frame is 2 MiB, and a 512 m cube gives 4 m voxels. Holding resolution while enlarging to a 2 km cube costs about 60× more voxels (500³); holding 128³ instead makes voxels 15.6 m and removes smaller billows. The scale is a content decision, not a free transform.

Illustrative uncompressed R8 **one-frame** anisotropic domains:

| Domain | At 40 m voxels | At 4 m voxels | Consequence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tower core 4×4×10 km | 100×100×250 = 2.38 MiB | 1000×1000×2500 = 2.33 GiB | Coarse shape can fit; fine final density cannot simply inherit 4 m everywhere. Sparse structure and scale-dependent detail are necessary. |
| Anvil 20×20×2 km | 500×500×50 = 11.9 MiB | 5000×5000×500 = 11.6 GiB | Use a slab plus local junction/high-detail zones; 128³ stretched to 20 km gives 156 m lateral voxels and loses filaments/edges. |
| Low sheet 32×32×0.8 km | 800×800×20 = 12.2 MiB | 8000×8000×200 = 11.9 GiB | A few column fields cost O(Nx Ny), while 3D clip cost also multiplies by vertical samples and frames. |
| Ci patch 20×20×0.4 km | 500×500×10 = 2.38 MiB | 5000×5000×100 = 2.33 GiB | Even 40 m can miss narrow strands; curve/cohort content preserves filament location without dense empty volume. |

These are logical byte estimates, not selected runtime allocations or quality claims. A 24-frame R8 sequence multiplies each by 24 before sparsity/metadata; 8 complete variants multiply by 8. Two-frame decoding reduces current residency but requires whole encoded content or actual deadline/seek/streaming design. A few-MiB Cu source result does not justify a full-sky clip library under the browser's 512 MiB whole-process envelope or the cold ≤25 MiB payload target. Conversely, evaluating a complicated recipe per ray sample can lose to one shared current field even when its source bytes are tiny.

The existing budget owners remain authoritative: baseline desktop CPU/GPU 5/10 ms, browser 8/16 ms, mobile 7/16 ms at its declared cadence; these include all consumers. Cloud-specific allocation must be taken from the governing selected profile rather than invented here. No new milliseconds or source/residency cap is certified by this note. A capacity worksheet must include all five sources at independent ages, a low overcast layer plus high storm outflow, direct/self-shadowing, a receiver and reflection while camera/Sun move. Measure source preparation separately, then complete-frame cadence/tails and peak replacement residency.

## 7. Critical gaps and discriminating proof order

These are design obligations derived from the original request, **not findings attributed to uninspected current CLOUDS/proof code**. I have deliberately not opened those artifacts in this commission; the parent should reconcile against their actual contents.

1. **A universal 512 m isolated clip cannot close all five families or full sky.** Resolve actual family domains, source dimensionality, master production and resource sharing before extending its content count. First rejection: tower-plus-anvil with genuine vertical growth and connected outflow, plus a thin filament patch; these stress the opposite dimensionalities. A nice Cu still does not answer this.
2. **“Final density clip” transfers lifecycle responsibility to source content; it does not supply that content.** Need retained masters/recipes whose local source/loss/transport creates every family's defining behaviour and accepted physical cadence. A library of noise-morphed shapes can pass density-change checks while failing the request. Reject with native motion at normal and accelerated time, fixed-camera silhouette/contact-sheet plus material landmarks.
3. **Weather continuity needs an actual active-source response path.** Specify and author connected branch endpoints, maximum response delay and layer conversion state. Drive coverage mid-lifecycle and require existing identities/geometry to continue. If only future birth rate changes and no active source can mature/break up appropriately, leave the corresponding weather capability open.
4. **Sharing cannot collapse independent age.** Count current evaluated groups, clip pairs, variant/deformation groups and simultaneous consumers. Test 20–50 visibly different phases, two views and a reflection; then an overcast layer plus storm. Repetition/synchronized boil or memory proportional to hidden age groups rejects an unqualified sharing claim.
5. **Connected layer/anvil transfer needs once-only optical ownership.** Compare joint source extinction to the sum at junctions, crossing cameras and moving Sun. Doubled density, seam gaps, detached outflow, independent lighting or disappearing shadows reject the assembly.
6. **Distant representation must preserve coverage/transmittance and world identity.** Horizon/wide views at two translated camera positions, reflected rays and low Sun must show the same formations. Planar extent, curvature and far-field cache validity need explicit support. A camera-centred recycled sky cannot satisfy persistent world weather.
7. **Lighting qualification must span thick and thin regimes.** Use front/side/back Sun, low Sun and twilight; Cu relief/silver edges, thick overcast interior/receiver dimming and translucent Ci must remain plausible under one declared closure. Validate all-layer direct transmittance against a high-sample oracle. Lighting a single Cu under midday does not qualify the cloud families.
8. **Source methods remain proposed until actual content exists.** The column and filament recipes above have closed drivers/operators but may fail perceived cloud motion; the prescribed streamfunction may produce smoke/toroids. Their rejection replaces the responsible content producer with a stronger master, preserving optical contracts and qualified transport. Do not polish the renderer to conceal a bad source.
9. **Complete performance remains open.** Time/frame allocation, peak resident bytes, source preparation, cold payload and thermal/device profiles need actual full-sky measurements with all required consumers. A representation can be selected analytically, but no resulting milliseconds, library memory or platform support is certified here.

Proof slices should follow the consequential uncertainty: connected deep convection/outflow and thin Ci dimensionality; actual low-layer formation/breakup; isolated full lighting/shadow/reflection; continuity through active-weather branches; heterogeneous complete sky; full workload and platform off/restore/cold-start. This does not require building precipitation or lightning, only preserving their declared source seams. Parent integration owns final canonical selection and acceptance. This bounded commission is complete; no runtime or source quality is marked passed.

## Addendum: controller closure, author operations and lifecycle ownership

Added after the independent draft, following the parent's request to make mature renewal, forcing-off, ownership and replay more explicit. This clarifies the same recommendation; no existing cloud design/proof was consulted.

**One condensate, one field.** A connected low layer has one M/base/thickness state (or one prepared field timeline) per world location/time. Its content patches are a partition-of-unity reconstruction of that state; overlapping patch borders do not add two copies of it. Likewise, core/outflow crops and different LOD products are representations of the same source, so switch/partition their contribution exactly once. Primitive/cell kernels used to reconstruct one cloud are normalized/blended within that source before producing sigma. Separate physical/proxy condensate cohorts and genuinely overlapping low/high layers instead add their nonnegative coefficients. Renderer `max()` cannot fix duplicate source ownership, and globally clamping summed density would incorrectly remove real overlap. Topological merge joins connected support; retain the set of original event/cohort IDs as provenance, with a stable resulting group ID, rather than respawning a new random cloud. The source layer/event owner resolves the distinction before V3 sees coefficients.

**World potential is authored forcing, not a second density.** One concrete weather controller carries a slowly advected formation potential P and authored instability/cap/base parameters. A front is a moving forcing corridor R(x,t):

```
∂t P + u_h · grad_h P = R_front + R_local - P/tau_relax
lambda_birth = lambda_max * smoothstep(P_on,P_high,P) * suitability
```

P is a dimensionless authoring state, explicitly neither humidity nor condensate. R has s⁻¹. The front recipe supplies its path, speed, width and forcing amplitude; terrain/diurnal influence is optional authored `R_local`, never inferred from rendered ground. Event suitability selects an admitted family/altitude/cap/shear regime. Integrate birth hazard over fixed world cells/material patches with seeded thresholds and persistent event IDs; do not draw a fresh random spawn decision per camera frame. Use `P_off < P_on` plus a sustained-time condition for breakup requests, so noisy threshold crossings cannot repeatedly restart sources. P controls actual local source tracks/next junction requests, not final optical opacity.

For source families driven as layer fields, P controls `C_M` through local connected cooling/moistening masks and `D_M/k_M` through drying masks; moving front edges produce spatially staggered formation/breakup. Cell-specific maturation delays and independent source phases prevent a globally synchronized response. For prepared sources, the same controller chooses birth, permitted growth/mature continuation, and next compatible erosion branch. Return the active transition/estimated response time when the requested weather cannot be instantaneously realized. A weather potential change does not relabel a shallow Cu into a thunderhead without an admitted connected growth path.

| Cu | Successive local source pulses create new upper billows as exposed sides/base remnants erode; mean footprint may be steady while material/support changes. | Stop new moist pulses; local exposed-edge losses carve gaps and separate wisps; explicit terminal loss reaches clear support. Detrained fragments retain source/cohort identity. |

| Ci | Heads/tufts continuously feed cohorts while older tails spread/shear/sublimate; old and new filaments coexist. | Shut off new heads/cohorts while existing tails continue their actual trajectories and local sublimation. Heads may disappear before distant tails; terminal loss ends each cohort separately. |

**Concrete author operations:** create a source family recipe and physical domain; set base/cap and its reference wind/shear class; paint or spline local condensation/injection tracks; author pulse timing and upper/edge erosion tracks; draw low-layer thickness/condensate islands and their joining/hole-opening sequence; draw Ci head trajectories with cohort/fall/width settings; bind the source to a world potential/front regime; construct growth/mature/breakup graph edges from exact shared checkpoint states; preview isolated and combined evolution under selectable Sun; inspect final density/optical depth, material correspondence and source ownership; cook selected source representations and pin hashes/quality ranges in the scenario. Variation seeds, local track edits and accepted master alternatives are visible controls. Arbitrary opacity/scale curves can remain diagnostic controls, but cannot serve as the accepted lifecycle authoring operation.

**Replay/seek:** advance weather/event state once on the world clock, independently of view count. Save the selected weather recipe/hash, seed, event cursor, active IDs/branch/phase, source revision and reference-frame transforms. An analytical/prepared source is directly sampleable at a requested timestamp; source caches rebuild from that state. A stateful runtime layer/physical provider additionally saves its actual state and forcing cursor; seek restores a valid checkpoint and deterministically replays the finite remainder under the declared replay support. It cannot reconstruct M from the coverage target or an approximate current image. A large seek presents loading rather than hiding dropped source time. Scrub/seek/source replacement increments generation and invalidates affected receiver/reflection/temporal products. Pause holds cloud/weather time; normal/accelerated controls change the explicit timeline mapping, never per-view update count. A seeded event schedule/checkpoint allows the same named weather sequence and normal/accelerated inspections to be repeated exactly within that provider's numerical determinism scope.
