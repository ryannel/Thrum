# Cb material-filter candidate review

2 November 2026. Read-only review of the new 64³→1³ RGBA8 noise mip chain, Cb carrier metric, optics LOD, and same-q diagnostic controls. The parent is still running images; this candidate has no visual or cost acceptance here. No shader/source files were edited.

## Resource and binding path

- `Gpu.texture` creates seven 3D mip levels and counts every level. The 64³ RGBA8 chain is 1,198,372 bytes versus 1,048,576 bytes for mip 0 alone, an incremental 149,796 bytes (~.143 MiB). The six compute passes read only the preceding mip view and write only the next mip view, ordered after base-noise generation in one encoder. The 4³ workgroup dispatch covers each halved size down to1³. Source-less providers create one mip and skip the chain. The pinned build `93bbb8f6f05c765c` GPU `nativeMipMean` check passes an exact synthetic 1³ mean with zero reported GPU errors; this establishes basic chain execution, not image filtering quality.
- Presentation writes one filter/material selector into `p.weather.y`; source updates still receive physical `dt=.25` there. The selector encodes world/advected and unfiltered/filtered as 0/1/2/3. The light cache, receiver field, main and reflected view use the same presentation uniform and hence the same `coefficients` selector. `p.front.z` independently selects 96/192/384 steps for main/diagnostic view integrators; cached light rays retain their 32/20-step paths. The controls permit same-q comparisons without resetting the source.

## Metric and units

Cb stage 4 backtraces the half-resolution carrier and samples its old unwrapped rest coordinates. For each axis it central-differences `oldRest.xyz` at `back±one carrier cell`, multiplies the three rest-coordinate components by their world scales `(4800,3200,4200)` m, then divides by the corresponding world step. The resulting `dx/dy/dz` are dimensionless columns of the **old** world-rest Jacobian. `sqrt(3)*max(column length)` is a conservative spectral-norm bound for that sampled old Jacobian because `||J||₂≤||J||F≤sqrt(3)*max|column|`. It is clamped to [1,128] and stored in carrier `.w`; seeding/missing backtrace uses `sqrt(3)`. Optics linearly interpolates this metric with the carrier states.

The metric is **not an upper bound on the newly advected map**: for `R_new(x)=R_old(x−v(x)dt)`, `J_new=J_old(back)*(I−dt∇v)`, and the implementation omits the backtrace velocity-gradient factor. Central differences of a trilinear old texture also cannot bound stretch/folds within every cell. In strong Cb cap shear this can underfilter even when the reported metric is finite. `.w` is now a scale estimate, not a validity flag; folded/missing carrier correspondence remains unhandled. The filter may hide a damaged carrier but cannot establish its correctness.

## LOD and visual-risk interpretation

`footprint=64 m * metric`; the unstrained advected seed has metric `sqrt(3)`, so its initial effective footprint is about111 m, whereas the wind-following world reference uses64 m. For the 50 m base-noise texel size, these choose about LOD1.15 and LOD.36 respectively. The fine-noise LOD adds `log2(4.3)` as required by its 4.3× coordinate frequency. LOD is clamped to the actual [0,6] chain. This is internally dimensionally consistent, but world-versus-advected images also differ in filter strength at zero strain, so that switch alone is not an isolated carrier-validity test.

The fixed 64 m world floor does not account for the **view ray's integration interval or pixel cone**. At the pinned Cb camera, the center ray crosses ~14.9 km of the box: 96 steps are ~155 m apart, 192 ~77.6 m, and 384 ~38.8 m. Even the unstrained advected111 m footprint can leave content finer than a 96-step integral can sample reliably. Conversely, the light cache's 32/20 complete-box samples have still larger gaps. A robust production LOD needs the relevant ray/voxel footprint in the common coefficient query, or occupied-interval integration must resolve the retained detail. This candidate is a useful isolation/tuning experiment, not a proof that default96 is alias-free.

Mip-averaging noise *before* `shapeMatter`'s clamp/threshold changes mean optical mass. For example, q=.2 with filtered low noise=.5 gives threshold `(1−.5)*.65=.325` and zero body density, while unfiltered bright low-noise samples can remain opaque. The lattice can disappear by erasing a thin cap. Compare same-state raw-q and shaped opacity, cap coverage and integrated tau to the 384-step reference; require the cap silhouette and optical depth to survive, not merely a smoother beauty image. If a fixed-state high-step image still has a world-locked pattern, inspect carrier validity/Jacobian and world-reference coordinates separately before adopting the filter.

## Checkpoint

No binding-size, mip-allocation, pass-order or obvious dimensional unit error prevents the candidate from running. The consequential open issues are new-map Jacobian underestimation, absence of carrier validity/reset, ray-footprint mismatch at96 steps, and nonlinear opacity bias. Retain the default-filter choice as unaccepted until the parent’s same-q image and optical-column comparisons establish shape and cost at the selected cadence.

## Build `5adeb34ec663e925` delta closure

The earlier **missing backtrace-gradient factor** finding is resolved at the sampled finite-difference scale. `backtracedRest(x±step,dt)` evaluates velocity separately at each of six neighboring positions, backtraces each, then samples `oldRest` (or the deterministic seed for an outside donor). Differencing these six *new-map* samples includes the spatial variation of `x−v(x)dt`, equivalent to the chain-rule factor at that stencil scale. The scaled columns remain dimensionless, and `sqrt(3)*max(column norm)` bounds the spectral norm of the **central-difference matrix**. It is still not a certified maximum over every subcell or a carrier validity/fold check; `rest.w` remains a scale estimate. Six additional velocity evaluations plus texture samples at each Cb half-resolution carrier cell are new source cost and belong in the normal/64x measurements.

The previous Cb **threshold-after-averaged-noise erasure** example is superseded. Cb now takes `Q=min(q_l+q_i,1)` and multiplies it by `(.65+.35*low)*(1-.12*(1-fine)*detail)` before splitting by `q_i/(q_l+q_i)`. For `low,fine∈[0,1]` and admitted `detail∈[0,1]`, shaped total lies in `[.572 Q,Q]`; if q total is positive, the factor cannot delete its support. Both phases receive the same factor, so liquid-to-ice transfer at fixed total does not introduce a second morphology threshold. The final optical extinction still reflects the distinct liquid/ice κ values. This bounding argument holds even when q total exceeds1, because Q is capped; it does not claim optical mass conservation relative to uncapped q or that filtering the product exactly equals averaging the product. The remaining low×fine covariance/footprint and ray-step error require image/tau comparison.

The new GPU `cbPhaseCoherentMorphology` fixture returns `[.1616800,.1616800,.3233600,.3233600]` for mixed, liquid-only and ice-only q at fixed total, within `1e-6` of the Cb formula; build `5adeb34ec663e925` reports pass and zero GPU errors. This is a pointwise material invariant, not cap-integrated tau or motion proof. Cb's seeded lower body now has a radial taper from3 to4.2 km, leaving a margin to the source box; the clear-start q evolution has no seed and is not changed by that t=0 adjustment. The mip/selector/resource findings above remain valid. The 64 m material floor still does not incorporate the view's ~155 m default step at the pinned camera, and a valid-looking filter image alone remains insufficient to adopt the default; the parent's 96/384, source-off, motion and cost runs are the relevant pending gates.
