# Connected Cb source card — bounded mechanism review

2 November 2026. Read-only review of `storm-source-proof.md` against the current `shaders/source.wgsl`, `shaders/optics.wgsl`, `src/weather.js`, and `CLOUD-PRODUCTION-BASELINE.md` P1b. This reviews the proposed pilot, not a visual or performance result.

## Disposition

Proceed as a P1b source-risk pilot after tightening two proof conditions below. The mechanism matches the selected baseline: the Cb initializer is confined to about 1.1–2.8 km; base pulses supply liquid; the updraft remains positive while outflow starts in the 9.0–10.3 km cap band; conversion transfers bounded liquid to ice in the same cells; no high disk is sourced independently. The current shader uses the weather wind once in velocity and moves the Cb emitter/core center by the corresponding base-wind integral. Upper-level shear deliberately moves material relative to that core. The 100 m/s internal cap plus 19.2 m/s admitted x wind and 1.1 m/s z drift imply less than 30 m per 0.25 s step, under the Cb cell pitch. Full high-wind support remains outside this pilot as the card states.

## Consequential proof conditions

1. **Treat base density and cap supply as measured, not established by the card arithmetic.** The actual Cb source is `S=.13*moist*min(lift,1)*pulseSum*band*(.45+.55*basisMask)` per second. At default moist .85 and one fully active pulse, its local maximum is about .11/s, but the basis factor ranges .45–1 and the pulse window varies. Ten seconds gives approximately `q=1-exp(-S*10)` of .39–.67 before loss in that one-pulse example; .6–.8 therefore needs favorable mask, overlap, or longer residence. The 280 m Gaussian band means effective residence varies with updraft and cell trajectory. The climb is roughly 8.4 km; 170–240 s at favorable core speeds is plausible, but cap q depends on `A=integral S dt` in the feed and `L=integral D dt` during transit, including lateral drift and numerical dilution. Even an inner D of .0026/s retains only `exp(-D*t)=.64–.54` over 170–240 s, before cap spreading. More importantly, `optics.wgsl` thresholds *total q* through the material basis before extinction, so nonzero upper q or q_i does not establish a visible cap. Add a selected Lagrangian/column witness recording feed-exit q, cap-entry q, phase fractions, and the common shaped optical coefficient or ray optical depth. Reject a cap whose source counters are positive but whose connected rendered coefficient vanishes. Use a seeded range, not only the easiest central trajectory.

2. **Separate the initial low body's ascent from renewed source success.** The initializer seeds q up to about 2.8 km at high density. That material can rise and reach the cap even if the broadened pulse source contributes too little. A cap image at 180–300 s alone would therefore not validate ongoing injection or a maintained tower. Include a clear-start Cb run with identical weather and seed, or a zero-new-source counterfactual / source-birth tracer. A valid result shows later pulses supplying a continuous lower-to-cap connection, followed by source-off weakening and anvil persistence. This stays within the card's bounded pilot and avoids changing the operator.

## Cap, phase, and consumers

The overlap is real in the shader: uplift fades across cap-600 to cap+700 m while radial outflow rises over that same interval; freeze is supported from cap-550 to cap+550 m and preserves `q_l+q_i` under its explicit min bounds. At the exact core axis radial outflow is zero by the regularized direction, so the test should also inspect off-axis transfer and a radial section; a central column alone cannot prove a spreading anvil. Ice loss is lower than liquid loss under ordinary and dry forcing. Report both ice fraction and connected optical extent after source-off, since `q_i>0` alone can coexist with a mainly liquid upper cloud.

The card's total-q morphology correction is **already present** in `optics.wgsl`: `shapeMatter` forms one envelope from `q_l+q_i`, then partitions shaped density by phase. Keep the proposed fixed-total midpoint/end-point coefficient fixture and inspect main, Sun/shadow, and reflected reads; do not count this as an outstanding shader edit. Distinct liquid/ice kappa still changes extinction during freezing by design.

No result here qualifies P1a morphology, the production sparse source, full consumers, or the 5 ms/M2 target.
