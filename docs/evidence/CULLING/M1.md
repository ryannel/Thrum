## Implementation and oracle

History on the member route is indexed by `InstanceRecord.ids[0]`, not member-list position. The final cull
clears the next side and atomically sets logical-slot bits, including when classes share a word. Each class's
depth pipeline shares the forward module, vertex entry, primitive and depth state. Both lists bind the bucket
member/table inputs; the prepass draws its own list, and the colour pass draws the final list in bucket order.
Direct remains the bucket-ordered oracle. The pyramid, depth tolerance and footprint widening are unchanged.

### Shadow-position invariance candidate

Direct and Frustum prepare separate shadow pipelines and draw different member lists, even though
their caster transform is shared. Unlike the forward position, neither caster entry point requested
[WGSL position invariance](https://www.w3.org/TR/2026/CRD-WGSL-20261021/#invariant). The bounded
initial broad candidate added `@invariant` to both caster outputs. This removed that missing arithmetic
guarantee; it does not establish that either uncaptured failure came from shadow positions.
The first two stress trials used the previous shader; trial 2 and the first exact-baseline verifier
built this broad candidate. The candidate stayed open until qualification; the scoped repair is now qualified above.

The [compiler diagnostic](m1-shadow-invariance-translation.json) validates both variants and confirms
that Naga 30.0.1 emits `[[position, invariant]]` for both candidate entry points; the previous outputs
lack it ([source](m1-shadow-invariance-translation.rs), [log](m1-shadow-invariance-translation.log)).
Its placeholder resource bindings are translation-only evidence; device output is qualified separately.

The broad candidate failed the exact native baseline at `portable-sdr-mirror-light`: two pixels
(six RGB bytes) changed at (853,304) and (799,324). No reference was changed. Source tracing shows
this valley proof overlay is `GameContent::Grid`, whose synthetic casters use the flat `vertex`
entry point; mixed-mesh field casters use `member_vertex`. The repair is therefore scoped to
`member_vertex`, the bucket path required by this milestone. The flat entry point retains its prior
arithmetic contract. The broad candidate's failure is retained; the scoped candidate subsequently passed
the complete exact-baseline run and named-target qualification linked above.

The [broad rejection record](m1-shadow-broad-rejection.json) retains exact pixel values and native
identity, with the [native manifest](m1-shadow-broad-native-manifest.json.gz). The selected-entry
[translation check](m1-shadow-scoped-translation.json) confirms the scoped flat-caster MSL is
byte-identical to the original, while scoped mesh-caster MSL is byte-identical to the broad
invariant mesh entry ([diagnostic source](m1-shadow-scoped-translation.rs)). This checks the scope
of the compiler change; it does not replace the new device comparisons.
