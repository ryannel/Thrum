# Independent final Q1 source review

1 November 2026. **Reject this source/product for Q1 acceptance.** The prepared optical source has a useful billowy body and visible local contour development, but the inspected raw source images do not establish the required convincing gap opening/detached-remnant breakup. The encoded stationary-carrier product additionally fails its unchanged numerical gates. Byte admission passes; Thrum runtime quality/cost remains unrun.

Thus this review does not prove that no brief event occurs at an uninspected age/view. It does establish that the supplied sample evidence is insufficient for the requested breakup cue. Normal/accelerated pacing, temporal seams and full lifecycle visual acceptance remain unrun.

## Visual finding and source-versus-reader diagnosis

At frame 20 the front view has substantial rounded lobes with opaque cores. The side view is a compact overlapping body. Frame 32 changes the upper contour and expands wispy peripheral structure; some actual local development is visible, rather than an exactly fixed shape whose gain merely changes.

By frame 45 the boundary becomes diffuse and the darker core contracts/softens. Frame 60 has much lower projected extinction in both directions and reads as a thinning wisp. The transmittance images retain one broad core while optical substance decreases. I cannot identify a clear interior opening between two persistent optically substantial bodies or a clearly detached substantial remnant in these sampled views. Fine turbulent texture and ragged edges are not the missing macroscopic separation cue.

This is a bounded visual judgment: the source is not literally a mathematically global fade, but its dominant sampled late-life experience is erosion/thinning. Some local change is insufficient to demonstrate the commissioned convincing growth-and-breakup lifecycle.

These renders sample actual raw float frames rather than the carrier reconstruction. Therefore carrier interpolation errors cannot explain the missing cue in these particular images. A better reader cannot add a missing source event. Source content and representation each have an independently identified rejection condition here.
