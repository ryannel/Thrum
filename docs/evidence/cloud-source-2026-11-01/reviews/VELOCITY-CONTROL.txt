For example, frame4→5 moves the centroid `[+0.227427,-0.157108,+0.082338]` cells. Its trapezoidal density-weighted
forward-centered raw field is `[+0.822514,-0.567713,+0.298230]`; multiplying by0.3125 predicts

The attempted background initializer did **not** create uniform flow: Blender weights gas source velocity spatially
and pressure projects it. A48.9% spatial velocity variation and nonconservative scalar transport leave the centroid
comparison confounded. Forward face average `(i,i+1)`, same-index samples and backward average `(i−1,i)` are all
reported; their errors are similar enough that this experiment cannot independently establish half-cell centering.
A clean followup would need a standalone Mantaflow uniform-grid initialization or a carefully qualified UNI restart;
that is outside this bounded commission. No further job is running.

The **declared**, nonempirical metric mapping is16local metres/BU and1.875source seconds/cache frame;
0.3125Manta internal time/cache frame implies6source seconds/internal time. If the source-derived raw-grid unit is
accepted, the conversion is `raw*(32/32)*16/6` at this control resolution, and `raw*(32/128)*16/6 = raw*2/3`
at production128³. These labels were supplied by the source recipe, not inferred from the observed displacement.
