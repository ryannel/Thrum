but the full recording was not watched/transcribed in this pass. No universal critical ranking of all atmospherics is

cache or scientific fluid/thermodynamic solver. This dynamic producer is a **Thrum adaptation**, not a demonstrated
Nubis³ feature. MacCormack advection is established; local source/shape/velocity recipes must prove actual clouds.
The source review's first moist-parcel/Stormscapes D0/D1 recommendation was narrowed after the author's visual-first/
nonphysical brief challenge; it is preserved, explicitly superseded by D2, not rewritten into purported consensus.

A2.5D weather-map method is a credible cheaper reduced-distance/profile alternative, not asserted equal to the chosen
close3D target. Full prepared animation remains an optional provider but cannot satisfy live-response capabilities
merely by playing. Compare complete cost only once defining appearance, motion, response and consumers match. No
source establishes a universal fastest equal-quality M2/browser method. Unsupported tiers stay explicitly unqualified.

## Whole-atmosphere meaning

Large-engine atmospherics is a coordinated collection of weather, wind, air, clouds, local media, celestial lights,
shadows, environment/reflections, display and surrounding effect/physical consumers. The coverage matrix gives every
required result an owner. This commissioned core proof includes live cloud weather and optical atmosphere/fog/light
coupling. Rain/lightning presentation, full Moon/stars, surface wetness, vegetation/water responses and audio remain
explicit separate implementations/integration boundaries. Typed causes and representative stubs are proved now;
actual product integration and budgets remain P8. No cloud shader or plan claims to supply those other implementations.
