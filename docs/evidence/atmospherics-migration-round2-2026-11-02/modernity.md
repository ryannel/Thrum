# Cloud system second-battery technique review — 2026-11-02

## Pre-plan criteria and credible technique families

Written before reading the current cloud migration plan or its retained technique survey. The commission is a coherent, beautiful, evolving Cu/Cb/Sc/St/Ci cloud and weather/air/Sun/shadow/ambient/reflection system, with an M2 WebGPU target of 720p, 5 ms cloud and 16 ms whole GPU, and 512 MiB whole browser. Native 3/10 targets are presently unqualified. This is a research and selection audit, not implementation or device certification.

### Neutral criteria

1. **Visible behaviour:** Each type needs characteristic silhouette, optical thickness, scale and transition; Cu/Cb must visibly develop, renew, break and merge, while layers and cirrus must move and evolve without merely translating or fading a static texture. Judge normal cadence, weather transitions and accelerated development, including views through, above and below the cloud field where supported.
2. **Causal coherence:** A named weather state and its dynamics must drive cloud formation/dissipation, advection, precipitation, humidity/fog, Sun extinction, ambient illumination, shadows and reflection inputs on compatible clocks and spatial supports. Rendering may use derived detail, but incompatible independent weather pictures fail the brief.
3. **Completeness of operators:** For any proposed structural dynamics, identify drivers, state, source/sink, transport, buoyancy or vertical organization, phase changes, instability or splitting, and boundary conditions. Separate authoritative weather quantities from artist-authored or statistical detail. A label such as fluid simulation or procedural noise is insufficient.
4. **Representational fit:** Compare volumetric fields, particle/primitive assemblies, procedural fields, hierarchical/sparse grids, simulation-conditioned synthesis and hybrids for all required cloud types and distances. Establish how coarse dynamics become fine geometry/optical density without inventing an unowned producer at implementation time.
5. **Lighting and consumers:** Trace one cloud event into view radiance, aerial perspective, surface/Sun shadows, sky and ambient light, fog and water/reflection, with explicit ownership, update rates, latency and approximations. Check temporal stability, multi-scattering approximation, phase/energy handling and discontinuities at LOD boundaries.
6. **System cost:** Account for GPU work by resolution, steps, volumes, tiles, views, lighting probes/shadow maps and temporal history; CPU prep, uploads and synchronization; persistent and transient bytes; and integrated browser memory and GPU time. The 5 ms cloud slice must include its attributable preparation and lighting, and any shared work must be counted once in the whole-frame budget.
7. **Executable migration:** Distinguish reusable observed assets/tests from old runtime structure. Identify dependency-ordered slices, API/authority changes, conversion, invalidation, fallback, quality floor and rollback, with an early complete scene that can reject a beautiful but static or incoherent substitute.
8. **Evidence strength:** Prefer primary papers, author code, substantive conference descriptions and official engine notes. Identify date/version, actual inspected material, target assumptions and unverified extrapolation. An isolated demo, unbuilt proposal or inaccessible abstract does not certify a browser assembly.

### Plausible 2026 approach families to compare

| Family | Strength or plausible role | Decisive selection question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Weather-driven sparse Eulerian or multiscale volume simulation, rendered by ray marching | Explicit evolving density, moisture/vertical motion and causal coupling; potentially suitable shared dynamic backbone | Can a sufficiently small, stable state plus source/closure operators produce the characteristic full range and meet memory/time? |
| Mesoscale/parcel/thermal or cellular growth model with procedural high-frequency density reconstruction | Efficient structural evolution and identity; explicit control of towers and breakup | Do the authored closures give genuine creation, merger and fragmentation rather than animated decorations, and can the fine detail stay anchored to the same event? |
| Procedural 3D fields conditioned by weather and time, with flow maps, domain warping and temporal reprojection | Mature fast appearance path and good large-area coverage | What actually changes topology and produces cloud life cycles, beyond advection/erosion of a fixed field? |
| Hierarchical implicit fields, sparse bricks or clipmaps with LOD/rate changes | World scale, view-dependent cost, possible physics/render separation | Are allocation, residency, validity and transitions affordable and deterministic enough on WebGPU/browser? |
| Cloud particles/splats/ellipsoids or other assembled primitives, possibly lit through volume transmittance | Efficient authored morphology, especially layered or distant clouds; may complement volumes | Can density, self-shadowing, continuity and close views match the brief without exploding primitive or sorting cost? |
| Neural or learned synthesis/transport conditioned on weather or simulation state | Could supply complex appearance or dynamics cheaply after training | Does it expose controllable weather causality, temporal stability, reproducible authoring and browser-size/runtime budgets, or only plausible images? |
| Hybrid typed system: different state/render detail for Cu/Cb, Sc/St and Ci, unified by weather and lighting contracts | May match distinct physics and visuals at manageable cost | Are shared causes and consumer outputs genuinely coherent, with no double accounting or missing handoffs? |

Cross-cutting rendering alternatives to compare are full/half/quarter-resolution ray marching; analytic or precomputed single/multiple-scatter approximations; temporal reprojection with validity masks; epipolar/slice methods; screen-space and world-space shadowing; atmosphere LUT integration; and physically approximate environment probes/reflection feeds. These are not substitutes for an evolution model.

## Evidence-backed findings

Inspected the 2 November `CLOUD-MIGRATION.md` and `ATMOSPHERICS-TECHNIQUES.md`, the canonical `CLOUDS.md`/`CLOUD-CAPABILITIES.md`, retained source notes, and the primary material below. ### M1 — Source lifecycle remains the decisive known missing proof (high; known missing decision/qualification)

**Location:** `CLOUD-MIGRATION.md` “Mathematical and content closure,” P1a/P1b, P5/P6; `CLOUD-CAPABILITIES.md` CF01–CF05 and CW01–CW04. **Evidence:** The plan selects recoverable time-varying optical masters and finite compatible continuation branches but explicitly has no accepted phase-complete Cu master, no Cu→Cb branch and no other family master. [Smolder 2026 speaker notes, pp. 51–64 and 101–104](https://www.realtimerendering.com/advances/s2026/content/AlexanderMueller_Smolder_Siggraph26.pdf) establish offline animated-volume rendering and packing, not five cloud genera or arbitrary weather response. The [2025 University of Ljubljana WebGPU paper, §§3.3–3.4](https://lgm.fri.uni-lj.si/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/250771715.pdf) explicitly scrolls noise beneath static coverage/type maps; its “dynamic” claim is internal appearance rather than this brief's structural lifecycle. This supports the plan's rejection of a quick procedural replacement, but does not prove the prepared route will meet controls, variety or memory.

**Consequence/action:** The selected route is a defensible *provisional* production path, not yet an executable solution to the defining behaviour. Keep P1a's recoverable Cu sample, mid-life forcing change, direct-frame interpolation, normal/accelerated motion and bounded implicit challenger as a hard selection gate. P1b must demonstrate continuous Cb development from a surviving body, and P5 must retain independent Sc/St/Ci gates. If a finite branch library cannot cover required authored weather transitions without reset or discontinuity, revise the source representation before P6; extra rendering polish cannot close that failure. This is already recognized by the plan, not an overlooked new paper.

### M2 — Test selective lighting-rate reduction before committing to whole-image reduction (moderate; bounded POC uncertainty)

**Location:** `ATMOSPHERICS-TECHNIQUES.md` “View cost”; `CLOUD-MIGRATION.md` “Budget account and reductions,” P3b/P4b. **Evidence:** [Smolder 2026 speaker notes, approximately physical pp. 89–100](https://www.realtimerendering.com/advances/s2026/content/AlexanderMueller_Smolder_Siggraph26.pdf) say whole-volume image half/quarter resolution caused spatial upsampling and TAA ghosting trouble. Profiling identified lighting as the dominant sample expense in that workload. Its alternative retains per-pixel march/depth/fog integration while sharing light evaluations within coherent waves after transmittance thresholds, with per-pixel blue-noise interpolation to restore TAA behaviour; it reports roughly 50% savings for dense smoke gadgets and explicitly weaker benefit for sparse volumes. These are console/engine and smoke-specific observations, **not** a WebGPU M2 speed prediction. The plan already has shared world-space light products, short local light rays and a spatial reference; its selected mixed-resolution view path is not yet measured.

**Consequence/action:** At P3b/P4b, attribute actual density vs local-light vs cached-light costs in Cu, overcast and storm, including fresh growth/cuts. If per-sample lighting dominates and whole-image reduction damages silhouettes/history, compare a portable per-pixel primary path with adaptive light sampling or grouped light reuse, preserving independent reflected and fog/geometry integration. WGSL subgroup features must be queried or a workgroup/texture alternative proved; do not presume console wave intrinsics or async shadow overlap. This is a **conditional challenger**, not a demand to replace the chosen path or to copy Smolder's algorithm.

### M3 — Add a contemporary WebGPU tiled-volume cost/LOD comparator, with strict applicability limits (low–moderate; missing comparison, not invalidation)

**Location:** `ATMOSPHERICS-TECHNIQUES.md` “Recent alternatives”; `CLOUD-MIGRATION.md` P3a far candidate and P3b atlas/occupancy. **Evidence:** [Muth, *Real-Time Volumetric Rendering of Meteorological Cloud Data*, March 2026, §§3.3–3.4 and 4.2–4.3](https://www.cg.tuwien.ac.at/research/publications/2026/muth-2026-clouds/muth-2026-clouds-thesis.pdf) is a primary, substantive WebGPU comparison absent from the technique watchlist. It converts hourly ICON-D2 fields into compressed tiles, uses conservative nonzero BC4 quantization/mips, a two-state coarse-search/fine-integration march, per-axis LOD and half-resolution TAAU. The measured ~2.25 ms *whole GPU frame* under high cover is on native Dawn/RTX 5070 Ti at 2560×1440 output and 1280×720 cloud pass, with resident local tiles; it is **not** a browser M2 cloud/whole-system figure. Its 512-slot atlas is ~1.14 GiB, over this brief's entire 512 MiB browser target. Its author reports inadequate sharp fog, small puffs and individual Cu texture, and its illumination has empirically scaled Sun, constant ambient and a camera-near density fade.

**Consequence/action:** Add it as a bounded source for conservative occupancy, anisotropic LOD and tile/paging stress cases in P3a/P3b, not as a replacement cloud/weather/lighting stack or performance target. Specifically, compare its coarse/fine search against the plan's conservative occupied-region traversal with matched density and empty-gap witnesses; reject if holes/silhouettes are missed. Avoid importing its atlas dimensions, BC4 portability assumption, constant ambient or camera fade. The omission does not invalidate current architecture because the thesis's own memory and fidelity limits are disqualifying here.

### M4 — Correct DDCL access/evidence scope; rejection remains sound (low; source-account correction)

**Location:** `ATMOSPHERICS-TECHNIQUES.md` “Recent alternatives and decision watchlist,” DDCL row. **Evidence:** The [author-hosted accepted 2025 manuscript](https://www.thomasbashfordrogers.com/Papers/DDCL.pdf) is now text-accessible beyond indexed excerpts. Inspected §§3.1–3.3, 4.1, 4.4 and 4.6. It predicts image-space sky movement/illumination on several time scales from LDR fisheye inputs; its unoptimized two-network/inverse-tone-map pipeline averages **3 seconds per generated frame** (§4.1). Section 3.3 says reconstructed HDR cannot recover the Sun and adds a separate Sun that is not attenuated when obscured. Section 4.4 shows generic frame interpolation can create implausible cloud movement/lighting in *image-space* sky sequences. These are not 3D optical source or directional-depth products.

**Consequence/action:** Update the source row from “indexed excerpts” to the now inspected manuscript and retain its exclusion as a runtime/physical consumer foundation. Its frame-interpolation failure is a useful caution for P1/P4 motion tests, but does **not** prove 3D optical-coefficient interpolation must fail; those are different representations and the plan already tests interval midpoints. No migration change required.

### M5 — Neural raster cloud shading is correctly bounded, not a 2026 lifecycle substitute (low; rejected finding)

**Location:** `ATMOSPHERICS-TECHNIQUES.md` EVNS watchlist and “Selection.” **Evidence:** [Olajos et al., HPG 2026 author abstract](https://fileadmin.cs.lth.se/graphics/research/papers/2026/environmental_volumetric_neural_shading_of_clouds_for_real_time_rendering/) describes a mesh/triplane neural shader trained from path-traced cloud assets and reports 3.7 ms relighting. The [author repository README](https://github.com/LUGGPublic/evns-clouds-rtr) describes a CMake native build and a 136 MB downloaded resource set. The Eurographics landing page lists a 19.51 MB PDF, but the page/PDF returned access errors here; I did **not** inspect the full paper. Existing retained review of pinned shaders is broader than this pass. The inspected material gives no five-family structural update operator, weather ownership or M2 WebGPU proof.

**Consequence/action:** Keep EVNS as a bounded visual/relighting challenger for a fixed admitted asset. Do not treat 3.7 ms native static asset shading as a 5 ms browser cloud-system result or as evidence against true evolving optical masters. Revisit only if an accessible dynamic sequence and portable implementation materially changes its scope.

### Rejected broader objection — selected assembly is coherent on paper

`CLOUD-MIGRATION.md` explicitly specifies one world clock, weather-owned identities, source-group partitioning, joint air/fog/cloud coefficients, nonrecursive environment production, independent reflected rays/histories, exact generation publication, offscreen Sun blockers and a P4a coupled shadow/ambient/reflection sentinel. The source survey appropriately labels Hillaire clear-air multiple scattering and browser RGB aerial adaptation as separate, and treats Smolder/Nubis as production precedents rather than lifecycle proof. I found no primary 2025–2026 source that makes a full runtime meteorological solver, neural shader, generic procedural noise or Gaussian splats a better established *whole-system* choice for this brief. This is an assessment of selection and planned gates, not execution quality.

## Current appropriateness and decisive gaps

The selected prepared-master plus weather-owned events plus shared optical/lighting contracts is **appropriate as a provisional 2026 target**. Its leading risk is whether actual recoverable masters and finite branch controls can produce the requested varied, renewing five-family weather at the available bytes. The second is whether the assembled source/light/views fit M2 browser time and memory; the current local graph already fails 5 ms. P1a/P1b and P4a/P3a are correctly early rejection points. P3b/P4b should conditionally compare selective lighting-rate reduction, and the source map should include the Muth WebGPU and now-accessible DDCL primary evidence. No design-invalidating modern omission was established. Native 3/10 ms, M2 browser 5/16 ms, 512 MiB process residency, motion quality and user acceptance remain **unqualified**.
