# Second-battery cloud/atmosphere coherence review

## Independent interaction model (recorded before reading the proposal)

This is the set of producer/consumer interactions implied by the commission, not a description of the proposed implementation.

1. Authored climate and weather controls, including moisture, stability, vertical motion, wind at cloud height, temperature and storm forcing, need a single world-time interpretation. A deterministic world/weather authority converts them into spatial, time-varying facts. Cu, Cb, Sc, St and Ci producers can use different morphology and evolution rules, but their occupancy, optical depth, heights and motion must remain mutually interpretable at transitions and blend boundaries.
2. Cloud morphology supplies wavelength-aware extinction and scattering along a ray, plus appropriately filtered summaries for direct Sun visibility, sky illumination, ground shadows, fog/air coupling and surface/reflection queries. Fine image detail may be view-only, but shared illumination and reflections must respond to the same large structures at the same world time and location.
3. The Sun and air have one shared state for solar direction/spectrum, air transmittance and clear-sky radiance. Cloud shadow/solar extinction modifies the direct term; cloud-scattered and air-scattered radiance contribute to the diffuse term. Cloud rendering, ground lighting, ambient probes and reflections must not apply an atmospheric/cloud term twice. Occlusion ordering on camera and reflected rays must be defined, including distant haze in front of and behind clouds.
4. In a normal frame: advance weather/cloud state at a defined simulation time; update changed cloud/atmosphere summaries; construct shared solar/sky/cloud lighting; update ground/volume shadow and ambient products; shade visible geometry and water; trace or approximate sky/cloud reflections against the same state; integrate air, fog and clouds for camera rays; resolve history and display. Update cadence and spatial support may differ, but each consumer needs a declared staleness bound and generation/version. A consumer must not read a newer shadow than its corresponding cloud or sky field without a stated temporal approximation.
5. In a weather transition: authored controls alter the world driver; cloud source, optical products, Sun visibility, sky diffuse, fog, shadows and ambient/reflection summaries must converge coherently. If a layer appears, disappears, rises or breaks apart, invalidation must include old and new footprints plus their swept/influence regions, including shadow displacement and view/reflection histories. Temporal filtering must preserve real growth/breakup rather than only image-space fade. Wind and transition clocks need clear ownership.
6. Disabled layers, low quality, device loss and failed resource allocation must select explicit substitutes for every retained service. Turning off cloud visuals should not silently remove weather lighting needed by other consumers, while disabling the cloud module entirely should avoid work and expose stable clear-air/clear-sky outputs. WebGPU and native backends need compatible units, encodings, texture/filter requirements, barriers and performance budgets.

### Full-chain trace and disposition

The intended producer chain is now visible: authored weather/front/event IDs and a committed world time drive phase/trajectory; prepared optical masters plus coefficient interpolation and source-group partition produce fields; all-layer Sun depth and clear-air profile supply direct/ambient light; one V3 integrator orders air/fog/cloud coefficients and terminal geometry for main, reflected and environment rays; the environment mode uses a direct-only ground terminal to avoid self-illumination; derived clouded irradiance feeds surfaces; V4 reconstructs independently per view and displays once. FrameOptics stages these products as one compatible generation, with regional read-set validity and an explicit missed-frame state. The transition example keeps event identity while dirtying old/new support and projected light influence. This is a coherent causal skeleton rather than a set of independent final-image layers.

The remaining uncertainties in field cadence, far summaries, air LUT representation, cloud-light fit, full-sky memory, fresh-sample cost and M2/native timing are **declared POC or admission gates**, not missing planning decisions by themselves. In particular, the plan correctly distinguishes point tau from filtered radiance, declares the clear-air higher-order term under cloud shadow an approximation, and rejects fabricated clear on out-of-capacity requests. No fluid-physics requirement is inferred.

### Findings

**R2-C1 — High, admission-scope blocker before calling P7 a complete-frame/browser pass.** [CLOUD-MIGRATION lines 303–319, 515–532](../../design/modules/CLOUD-MIGRATION.md) reserves clouds <=5 ms and whole GPU <=16 ms / browser <=512 MiB, then P7 says to *qualify* all three in the standalone full-sky proof. The same plan defers real rough-water BRDF, depth/transparency and Thrum runtime integration to P8; P4 uses an analytic mirror, and CLOUDS' reference scene uses matte/planar-mirror geometry ([CLOUDS lines 404–408, 662–668](../../design/modules/CLOUDS.md)). Counterexample: the P7 mirror320x180 path passes the 0.35 ms reflection allocation while a wave-normal rough-water path needs different sample/filter/history and interface composition work; the future engine also adds actual surface, scene and browser residency. The P7 fixture can validly prove its **cloud graph** on an M2 browser and measure that browser process, but cannot certify the integrated whole-frame16 ms or complete browser512 MiB, nor the production reflection cost. Otherwise a green P7 becomes an invalid admission premise for P8 and can force late representation rework. **Resolution:** label P7's5 ms as the fixture cloud allocation and its16 ms/512 MiB as measured fixture scope/comparison only; reserve complete product frame/memory admission for P8 with actual consumers. Before P3b/P7 representation freeze, budget a production-like rough reflection/depth/transparency proxy or bound its incremental sample/byte/latency envelope against the mirror witness. This is a written gate correction; actual device qualification remains unrun.

**R2-C2 — Medium-high, consequential source-control gate, not proof of a wrong algorithm.** [CLOUD-MIGRATION lines 160–165, 180–196, 414–430, 463–480, 505–513](../../design/modules/CLOUD-MIGRATION.md) selects prepared optical endpoint frames, shared by recipe/knot/LOD, with an instance phase/transform; P1a tests changing phase/loss, P1b tests a permitted Cu-to-Cb driver, and height shear is expanded in P6 after production source choices. [CLOUDS lines 128–139](../../design/modules/CLOUDS.md) requires material-height wind, subtracting baked wind exactly once, and bending an existing formation when authored wind changes. Counterexample: a growing Cb body reaches 12 km while an upper wind front rotates; a single affine instance transform or phase-only endpoint pair cannot bend old lower matter and new upper/anvil matter under different trajectories. If the master is baked in world-space wind, simply adding runtime wind doubles advection; if it is baked in local space, the cook needs a material-position/velocity/deformation map and conservative inverse/support for all V3 queries. The plan mentions wind/shear trajectory and correspondence but does not assign this *selected prepared-master* mapping or test it before the full family content investment. **Resolution:** before P1b/P3b source format selection, define the admitted deformation regime for prepared optical frames (e.g. bounded material-coordinate map and inverse with source-relative velocity, support and optical error, or separately authored wind-conditioned continuations) and demonstrate a mid-life height-wind change across one persistent Cu/Cb event in source, Sun-depth and reflected queries. Unsupported wind directions can be explicitly rejected at authoring/admission, but the admitted changing-wind weather route must be named. This can be a P1b/P3b acceptance gate; it does not require a fluid solver.

**R2-C3 — Medium, canonical decision ambiguity that could send implementation down two cost paths.** The current CLOUDS governing table calls CD03 current and says independent ages cost distinct evaluated source-field work ([CLOUDS lines 21–34, 283–294, 319–325, 673–678](../../design/modules/CLOUDS.md)); the migration explicitly selects shared immutable endpoint sampling across independently aged events, with current blended fields only an optional specialization ([CLOUD-MIGRATION lines 332–347](../../design/modules/CLOUD-MIGRATION.md)). These statements can be reconciled if “evaluated field” means a **blended current field**, but the canonical module text does not say that, while its initial atlas/current-field route still reads as the source baseline. Counterexample: two engineers independently implement P3a: one builds a current atlas for 512 differently aged Cu, the other samples two shared endpoint frames per ray. Their work, memory and invalidation slopes differ radically even though both can cite a governing selection. **Resolution:** update CD03 and the source-field/cost paragraphs to state the migration's endpoint baseline and label the per-age blended atlas as the measured challenger; keep the old statement only for current-field caching. This is a design-document reconciliation, not an objection to either sampling method or a new proof requirement.

The same canonical-document ambiguity extends beyond CD03: CLOUDS 169–281 still states the rejected Cu cohort and analytic Sc/St/Ci producers as concrete mechanisms, 396–403 calls single-scatter air the first closure, 420–554 describes the fitted liquid bank/K48/A0 schedule with explicit enormous endpoint ceilings, 554–590 treats history as a later option, and 590–614 describes per-time current-field preparation. The migration instead selects prepared lifecycle masters (167–198), a four-LUT air system with declared higher-order approximation (204–216, 467–472), a newly qualified bounded cloud-light closure (P3b 473–480), temporal production after a spatial reference (P4b 482–489), and endpoint-sharing as the first residency path. The CLOUDS header already says older detailed candidates are reconciliation material, so these are not separate new algorithm findings; the durable document should make that status unmistakable to implementers. Do not treat the historical A0 32,768-anchor/24-direction/512-endpoint ceiling as a selected migration cost or budget.

In that cleanup, retain the commissioned obligations rather than deleting them with the old mechanisms: CF01–CF05, CW01–CW04, FS01–FS04 and CL01–CL30; E1/W1/F4/V1–V4 authority and C01/C02/C04/C05/C07/C08/C09/C12/C13 invariants; stable weather/event IDs, once-only wind and typed precipitation/lightning seams; one-condensate partition versus genuinely additive constituents; SKY-G's common spherical frame, high-cloud/horizon altitude and virtual/offscreen source domain; ordered all-layer air/fog/cloud transport, direct Sun, ambient and independent reflection, plus clear/unavailable semantics; off/headless/history/device/provider boundaries; the four representative full-sky population/coverage/view recipes and target budgets. Preserve old derivations and failed-cost evidence as dated historical material or exact Git provenance, not active construction choices.

- **R2-C2: mechanism and gate supplied; two notation fixes remain before calling the math fully specified.** CLOUD-MIGRATION 217–235 chooses a local, wind-free prepared-master profile with `u(h,t)=u_b(t)+s(t)(h-h_b)`, `G=∫s dt`, affine `X=anchor+A(G)xi`, exact inverse and determinant 1. With `s` in s^-1, `G` dimensionless, and `xi` in metres, `X` and the inverse have metre units. If the ray integral retains world-metre arclength, scalar `sigma_t` remains m^-1 and `tau=∫sigma_t ds_world`; the map makes no mass-conservation claim.   The text should explicitly set `anchor_y=h_b` and `xi_y=h-h_b`. Otherwise the derivative of the written transform produces `u_b+s*xi_y`, which differs from the declared field at world height `h=anchor_y+xi_y` by `s*(anchor_y-h_b)`. It should also define `v_source` in `anchor_dot+A_dot*xi+A*v_source*phase_dot` as `d xi/d phase` (metres per phase unit), or write that derivative directly. A source velocity already in m/s must not be multiplied by a phase rate in s^-1. These are local notation/units fixes, not a need for a new solver or a rejection of the affine-shear approximation. ### Final narrow notation check

CLOUD-MIGRATION 217–237 now sets `h=X_y`, `anchor_y=h_b`, `xi_y=h-h_b`, and zero vertical `u_b` for the bounded Cartesian fixture. For fixed material `xi`, differentiating its `X=anchor+A(G)xi` gives horizontal `u_b+s*(h-h_b)` exactly, with vertical derivative zero. The stated inverse is exact, `det A=1`, `G=∫s dt` is dimensionless, and `sigma_t` retains m^-1 when integrated over world-metre arclength. The revised chain rule explicitly uses `d xi/d phi` in metres per phase unit times `phi_dot` in phase units/s, so its source-relative term is m/s. It rejects correspondence at birth/loss, preserves changed bounds/light/history invalidation and cost, and keeps the spherical chart profile separately unqualified. **R2-C2 is closed at the written-design level for this admitted local regime.** The P1b/P3b empirical inverse/support/optical/consumer and broader chart gates remain unrun; this check does not promote implementation or appearance.
