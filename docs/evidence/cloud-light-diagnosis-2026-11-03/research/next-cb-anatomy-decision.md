# Static Cb anatomy challenger: broad base, unequal rooted towers, covered major lobes, continuous anvil

3 November 2026. **Decision for root review before coding or cooking.** Keep the scalar/R16F consumer, frozen daab renderer, selected extra-scattering profile `(a,b,c)=(.5,.5,.5)`, high light reference for image discrimination, and held κ=.019 m⁻¹ calibration input. This proposes one new independently authored static field over the existing `12×14×12 km` box. It does not approve that κ, static source, live lifecycle, device budget, or art. No new source was generated for this decision.

## Why the current source remains a club

The current field has a deep positive core and 2,137 nominally covered lobes, but the outward candidates are dense and fairly uniform; the source-normal view shows fine wrinkling on one pear-like silhouette rather than multiple kilometre-scale crown masses. A shallow array of approximately 338 m median lobes can cover every original scaffold patch while its maximum-union envelope stays smooth at the body scale. The bottom narrows to a rounded point instead of a broad condensation base. The present cap's long dimension is about 6.9 km and its overall width is comparable to the body; its distal light source is mostly separate, nearly parallel Gaussian fibres. The native normal view makes those tubes obvious. More κ and a finer light grid cannot make the wide flat sheet and unequal major towers missing from the scalar. At held daab κ=.019 the body reads darker with some internal contrast but remains a pear/club; κ=.038 largely darkens it again.

## Single constructive producer

Use metres and positive-inside signed distance `D`. `s=+X` is downwind, `Z` is crosswind, `Y` altitude. All parts have closed support and a nonzero collar inside the existing source box. Example positions/ranges below are *pre-cook controls to be checked in analytic projections*, not measured output or final art tuning.

1. **Shared flat condensation base.** Make a broad, shallow closed footprint whose bottom `Y_b(X,Z)` stays within roughly 1.1–1.4 km over a central 4×3 km patch and whose lobed perimeter spans roughly X −4.4..+2.2 km and Z −3.1..+3.0 km. Set `D_base=min(Y-Y_b, Y_top(X,Z)-Y, signedPlanformDistance(X,Z))`, with a 1.8–2.7 km top that merges into tower roots. Anisotropic macro bumps shape the perimeter; the bottom interior remains recognizably level. This replaces the pointed lower loft, not merely covers it with small spheres.
2. **Several rooted rising cores.** Form three unequal closed swept elliptical tube SDFs `D_i=min(r_i(Y)-ellipticalDistance((X,Z)-C_i(Y)), Y-Y_root, Y_top_i-Y)` with smooth, bounded junctions to the base. A principal centerline rises from X≈−1.0 km to a shoulder near X≈−0.3 km and an overshooting crown near 11.5–11.8 km; an upwind core rises near X≈−2.8 km to about 10 km; a downwind/rear core rises near X≈+1.3 km to about 9–10 km. Offset their Z centers by roughly 0.8–1.5 km and tilt them differently. Overlap each root with the broad base and overlap neighboring mid-level shoulders without collapsing the silhouette into one central loft. The main crown may overshoot the flatter anvil by 0.4–0.7 km. `D_scaffold=smoothUnion(D_base,D_1,D_2,D_3)` with junction width ≤0.2 km, then re-distance. The exact closed control splines and radii must be printed in the recipe before a cook.
3. **Major cauliflower lobes before skin detail.** Place a *small authored hierarchy* across every exposed tower flank, rear and crown, with major approximately 0.8–1.5 km lobe radii and unequal protrusions roughly 0.35–0.7 radius beyond the scaffold. Large-lobe sites follow branch-specific growth rings and unequal azimuthal/height phase offsets; their centers are embedded deeply enough to retain a broad neck. Enforce separated major arcs and visible saddles rather than allowing a dense same-size sphere union to re-create a smooth hull. Re-distance the union. Only then attach medium 0.3–0.7 km lobes to major-lobe and valley regions, with smaller 0.15–0.3 km elements and bounded 3D displacement on the resulting surface. The latter may modify local curvature but must not set the kilometre-scale outline. Use exact signed-distance ownership labels for scaffold/major/medium/cap and keep a positive interior density floor. Any original scaffold arc left uncovered by the hierarchy receives a designed macro lobe or explicit broad valley displacement; no final exposed smooth loft wall is admitted. This is distinct from the prior 2,137 near-uniform coverage fill.
4. **One connected, broad downwind cap.** Create a closed swept sheet SDF using `D_cap=min(Y-Y_bottom(s,Z),Y_top(s,Z)-Y,w(s)-|Z-Z_c(s)|,s-s_start,s_end-s)`. Root it through the principal crown/upper shoulders for at least 0.6–1 km of 3D overlap. Initial planform runs roughly X −2.5..+5.5 km with crosswind half-width rising toward 3.2–3.8 km and tapering at the distal edge; top lies near 10.9–11.2 km, root thickness around 1–1.8 km, distal thickness around 0.25–0.6 km. Preserve a continuous low-density membrane across most crosswind sections; vary it with branched, *correlated* downwind filaments and edge fraying, not a maximum of isolated parallel Gaussian pipes. Fibres originate in the common root and merge/split within the sheet. The upper surface is broadly level with local undulations, whereas the main core may rise above it. The cap is connected to the same body SDF and density source; its far edge can become sparse without detaching the root.
5. **One optical field, unchanged renderer.** Re-distance the body/cap union and map it to nonnegative `q=F(D)Q(X,Y,Z)` with one finite boundary ramp and explicit body/cap interior profiles. Retain the current `σ=κq` reader and κ=.019 comparison setting; do not increase κ to compensate for missing geometry. Record actual per-feature and full-column τ alongside q because a larger base/cap may increase total opacity. If a later profile adjustment is needed to keep local and total optical depths in a usable range, treat it as an explicit new authored-source calibration with matched comparison captures, not a renderer change. Keep finest retained features ≥180 m at 256³ and no support beyond the source collar.

## Required geometry proof **before** the 256³ density cook

Evaluate the closed analytic SDF/control splines as 2D silhouettes, cross sections and owner-labeled surface samples without allocating the 256³ density or a GPU cache. Freeze an initial control table first. Reject the control table unless all tests below are measured and recorded at the held front, side and oblique cameras and at several altitudes. These are structural gates and suggested initial thresholds; root judges images before any candidate becomes art.

| Pre-cook gate | Measurement that would falsify this design |
| --- | --- |
| Base and root | Minimum broad underside width ≥4 km in front/side projection near Y≈1.3 km; central bottom elevation variation ≤0.3 km; each of three tower roots has an ≥0.5 km² connected cross section with the base at Y≈2 km. A narrow point or isolated bulb fails. |
| Multiple unequal towers | At Y≈5/7/9 km, record silhouette width, width maxima and the SDF branch-owner map. At least two offset major shoulders remain visible in two of the three camera projections, with crown heights differing by ≥1 km. A single monotone pear outline fails even if many small lobes are present. |
| Major lobe ownership | Sample the **final** body zero isosurface (not only the original scaffold) by height and azimuth. Major+medium lobes should own ≥75% of exposed body area outside flat base/cap, ≥60% in each nonempty flank quadrant, and no smooth scaffold arc >1 km. Record major-lobe radius/protrusion distributions and a count by branch; verify multiple ≥0.3 km saddles between major arcs. Dense pebble ownership with no large arcs fails. |
| Cap planform and attachment | At mid/downwind sections, the continuous sheet's occupied crosswind width should reach ≥6 km and be at least 1.8× the upper-body crown width at the root, while staying inside the 12 km collar. Record root overlap volume, minimum neck area, one 6-neighbour component at q=.03 after eventual rasterization, and width/thickness versus s. Narrow stem, disk pasted on top, or disconnected pipes fail. |
| Visual projections | Render *source-only* orthographic silhouettes and labeled SDF sections at front/side/oblique before volume cooking. Require a level broad base, unequal crown crests, kilometre-scale convex cauliflower arcs with valleys, one body-fed anvil sheet, and frayed distal edge. The analytic measures cannot substitute for these images. |

If the analytic fields pass, run one 256³ source cook with its exact recipe, controls, seed, coverage/owner map, central slices, per-column τ, support collar, topology and memory peaks; then compare native beauty under fixed daab selected profile and κ=.019 against the frozen f06/daab negatives. The previous cook's simultaneous RSS reached 763.75 MiB under a 768 MiB limit, so the new cook must derive a lower-memory slab/reuse plan *before* allocating additional owner volumes. Do not commission 384³, lifecycle, weather or GPU optimization from a static silhouette pass.

Predicted projection cues if those controls work: from the fixed +Z front view, the wide level base should support three non-equal towers, with the tallest near centre and a substantial cap extending to screen right; from +X, Z-offset cores should separate in depth/outline and the anvil should read as a wide sheet rather than a narrow side-on tube; from the held oblique camera, the former pointed base should be replaced by a broad underside, two or more large shoulders should interrupt the pear contour, and a single cap root should spread into a continuous flattened fan. These are falsifiable expectations, **not measured output**. If any projection still reads as one club with cosmetic wrinkles, reject the control table before density cooking.
