# M2 builder inspection list, written before viewing captures

1. Inspect native whole source frames and 1:1 crops in the initial hold. Sky, terrain, rock and sphere must be complete in the cooked valley, with opaque alpha, correct channel order and clean rows at the actual 1206x2622 portrait grid.
2. Inspect the 0-to-36-degree camera pan through its ordinary two-second hold, eight-second motion and final hold. Compare scene relationships across full frames and native terrain/rock edges at transitions. Check monotone yaw, coherent relative placement, silhouette continuity, repeats and gaps against recorded submission time. Judge the full sequence on the declared 1x timeline.
3. Inspect the running cooked diagnostic scene and moving grid at native size. Join alpha and adjacent committed endpoints to every source image. Verify ordinary semantic movement changes the committed controlled actor, and inspect visible moving-instance crops for continuity and history artifacts. Record the control method independently of UIKit touch.
4. Inspect final holds and the first image after surface, background and device recovery. Check complete geometry and current generation, with no stale source published into a replacement session.
5. Inspect the eight-second diagnostic render stall on its monotonic timeline and native before/after frames. Its actual gap/hold must persist through packaging, followed by fresh engine progress. Ordinary runs must contain no stall command.
6. Verify every submitted original hash with independent Zstd/XOR decoding and every PNG round trip. Join the exact source extent, row stride, view/history, world, surface and device identities. Capture omissions or incomplete finalization refuse continuous evidence.
7. Verify each requested callback's disposition, every dispatched opportunity and the independent submission/image count. Distinguish coalesced callbacks, renderer gaps and capture omissions. Native UI and UIKit controls are outside this engine stage.
8. Keep simulator function, semantic input, real UIKit regression gestures and physical-device evidence separately labelled. Assess normal-speed function from source frames plus host-time measurements when live video perception is unavailable.
