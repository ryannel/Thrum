## Visual mechanism and fit to G7

The shipped mesh supports the rim diagnosis. At the row nearest the lookout I read a 1.40 m drop between x = −40.38 and −60.31 m, followed by a 32.78 m drop to the next vertex. Giving the top and the descending face their own normals can remove the false highlight while keeping the depth edge readable. M9.1 judges that distinction at the original stop, at two other yaws and through the changing light. For M9.2 I sampled the shipped terrain toward the Sun from the two prop locations. At 6.1 h the solar centre was about 2.16° below the ridge. By 6.32 h it was about 0.36° above it, still low enough for long shadows. This CPU estimate establishes a plausible inspection window.
