# SHOWCASE M5 evidence

## Mesh sizing

Both runs used the exit layout and a 1920×1080 draw with Sun shadows and capture off. All three tiers used
stand-ins with the same local envelopes. Metal counted 4,376 visible entities at sunrise, noon and twilight,
3,559 at night upslope and 861 at each westward stop. Sunrise is the first of the tied stops in route order.
Chrome drew the same 4,376 entities at that paused tick-zero preset. Per-frame GPU times, build digests,
recipe identities, cooked roots and start/end load averages are in [the sweep rows](M5-sizing-sweep.json).
The timing adds the engine's measured GPU pass durations and the surface blit. The native sweep used the
portable texture representation, and Chrome's GPU timestamps are quantized. Both targets ran contended.

| Target and recipe | 48 triangles, p95 ms runs 1 / 2 | 224 triangles | 704 triangles |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Mac portable | 9.023 / 7.803 | 11.412 / 9.333 | 11.706 / 11.652 |
| Mac Apple tile | 9.289 / 9.285 | 11.420 / 9.463 | 11.620 / 14.019 |
| Chrome portable | 5.571 / 7.537 | 7.078 / 8.651 | 8.978 / 10.158 |

No tier stayed below 8.33 ms on both Mac recipes in both runs. The design therefore selects the lightest
tier. After those runs, the generator made five standalone glTF prop sources of 48 triangles each. The
balloon uses a tapered envelope and basket, and the four ground props use rooted shapes with a fixed
turn and scale per slot. The two native sweeps predate the Chrome preset-serving correction. Their
stand-in inputs and Metal view counts are identical to those in the corrected paired browser experiment.
The later lookout correction moved 244 still anchors, keeping all mover paths, the mesh and material mix,
and the per-instance turns and scales from the sweep.

The launcher's exit source overlays those five meshes onto the unchanged valley source. Its generation-1
cooked root is `3083a794f837963050dae3e566c788d6a9aad7fa7479309a46c1540cb0654643`, pinned by
[the flythrough](../../../examples/v1-game/routes/exit-flythrough.json).

## Layout and terrain clearance

`python3 scripts/run_v1_showcase.py layout` passed on the Apple M3 Max (macOS 26.6.2) at
`373780d6dc9bcb9757f84356236fbd04aefe4924`. It regenerated 10,000 entities with 1,500 drifts byte for byte.
After the lookout correction it again regenerated byte for byte. The shipped layout SHA-256 is
`e0f0a6db38e46502704ed580481fae4fbfefb4dc437d3d88995cd9e635f790fd`.

The verifier reads position and index accessors in the shipped [terrain](../../../examples/v1-game/assets/showcase/terrain.gltf)
and interpolates its actual triangles. The binary SHA-256 is `204713d67394e5ab6259c96035e4ab8815cee4439867cf0831eea3e78e6022ad`.
Each still anchor is within 0.051 mm of the triangle and clear of the lookout and the horizontal swept path.
The exclusion covers the entire flat lookout found by the independent review, with clearance for the prop's span.
The final verifier also transforms the vertices of the four shipped ground meshes and checks that each instance
crosses the surface while retaining an exposed top. All 8,500 ground props pass the contact check.
The verifier checks 360 points around each mover's loop against the same terrain. The smallest sampled clearance
is 35.22 m below the mover envelope. The maximum terrain slope and the mover's excursions give a continuous
lower bound of 33.36 m between samples. The original valley separately cooked to
`70d2baf823d09d85574f7cdfce7102648754544dd7d68659e9bd92dd5f30e21d`.

## Authoritative replay and drift

`cargo test --locked --offline -p v1-game --test exit_scene` passed on the same Mac and revision in 123.25 s.
Two 10,000-entity worlds consume input from four registered peers. The test amends an input after rewinding eight
ticks and compares every live identity, handle, gameplay value and authored loop against the straight world.
Position and velocity floating-point bits agree, and the full engine and peer metadata write streams agree byte
for byte. A second test executes all 216,000 fixed ticks through the game's authority. It checks the live and
moving counts at each tick, then verifies every mover's endpoint against its authored loop.

Source for both tests is in [exit_scene.rs](../../../examples/v1-game/tests/exit_scene.rs).

## Cooked draw and route comparison

The final Metal test resolves Frustum and reads indirect counts after the cooked rows and Sun-shadow passes
are active. Its CPU reference builds a world-space bounds test for each instance from that instance's
authored transform and the cooked row's vertices. Both sides reported 4,430 visible at sunrise, noon and
twilight, 815 at the two westward stops, and 3,580 at night upslope. [Metal test output](M5-native-counts.txt)

The first run on the final assets that passed the diagnostic, retinted texture and no-entities controls
set the route's three thresholds to 1.0 by the design's rule. Worst Mac–Chrome median, p95 and maximum
cell differences were 0.0274, 0.0775 and 0.1997. The retint reached 34.38 in the maximum cell.
The no-entities arm used the same cooked root and exceeded the threshold at all six stops, with maximum
cell differences of at least 21.59. The five available diagnostic phases each exceeded ten times the
threshold. [Calibration](M5-parity.json) · [Retained route baseline](M5-exit-baseline.json)

Chrome's page reported the fetched row publication and all 10,000 active slots. One tracked mover advanced
between ticks 8 and 15, then held the same position and tick throughout the pause. [Browser receipt](M5-chrome-function.json)

## Fixed visual inspection

The native shots used Metal and the portable recipe at 1280×720. The six presets used T2 fixed capture.
The six flythrough checkpoints used the native fixed route, one freshly committed game tick per step
and a half-tick presentation phase. Chrome's six fixed stops retain the submitted WebGPU frame and
metadata at the paused preset. All stills and receipts can be restored from the
[lossless inspection archive](M5-fixed-submitted.tar.gz).

The builder and a fresh visual reader inspected the native frames whole and in native-size near, middle
and far crops. The final plants and stones read as grounded, with irregular spacing beyond the reserved
lookout. The cactus that projects over the lip at pixel (263, 438) of `lookout` is 5.02 m beyond the
flat top and extends 0.148 m into its local terrain. The boulder near (175, 599) of `valley-floor`
and the cactus projected in front of it both intersect the terrain. The scene still shows stepped,
elongated mid-distance shadows in several flythrough views.

## Real-time motion inspection

The Mac portable recipe submitted 2,108 images over 24.53 seconds on the flythrough and 2,088
over 23.00 seconds from the held noon view down the valley. Chrome WebGPU submitted 1,376 images
over 24.54 seconds on the same route. All three captures used 1280×720 SDR with overlays off.
The recorder and exporter kept every submitted image in these runs. Both flythroughs had occasional
gaps between submissions. The longest gaps were 184 ms on the Mac route, 46 ms on the Mac hold,
and 268 ms on the Chrome route. The 1× playback decoder also skipped 958, 965 and 97 pictures
respectively. The review paired 1× playback with consecutive submitted PNGs to see the movement
within individual ticks. [Capture and playback receipts](M5-motion-receipts.json)

Whole frames and side-by-side near, middle and far native-size crops matched the visible props
and balloons at all six fixed stops. In the real-time samples, near balloons left the frame along
its edge and overlapping balloons separated again as the camera passed. The held view showed
balloons advancing between submissions within the same simulation tick, with their shadows moving
along the ground. For example, held submissions 879 and 880 show the same tick 378 at phases
0.330 and 0.830. After the opening hold, the independent visual reader found 30 isolated pairs
of identical endpoint frames on the Mac flythrough and 74 on the hold, each 2–9 ms apart at phase
1.0. The next submission continued the drift. Chrome had no repeated submissions after its hold.
The captures show motion across consecutive submissions. The whole-frame gaps limit what the
recordings establish about display cadence.
The flythroughs showed the same drift, with step edges still visible in some long shadows.
The motion archive includes consecutive submitted images, their crop coordinates,
timestamps, tick and interpolation phase, and all six paired fixed crops.
