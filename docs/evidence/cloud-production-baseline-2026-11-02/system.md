# Cloud production baseline — system review (2 November 2026)

## Selection criteria set before inspecting the current CLOUDS/MIGRATION proposal

This is an independent requirements/system check, not a claim of blind review of every prior cloud note: I saw the POC acceptance card first. A production baseline is acceptable only if it (1) makes Cu, Cb, Sc, St and Ci recognizably distinct, with local birth, continuing mature renewal and breakup rather than global fades or translating static volumes; (2) gives coherent wind and authored weather progression with stable survivors; (3) drives direct Sun attenuation, ambient/sky, air/fog, receiver shadows and independent reflection/environment consumers from one time/space event; (4) has a practical authored source/cook/update/streaming route and inspectable identities; (5) fits plausible combined CPU, GPU, upload and memory budgets on the stated browser/native targets, preserving quality with ranked reductions; and (6) supplies a proof path that can reject a beautiful but wrong still, a source-only illusion, or a fast isolated component that fails in the assembled scene. Physics fidelity and numerical weather forecasting are not prerequisites if these observable outcomes hold.

Likely traps to test: turning one demonstrator's controls into mandatory user requirements; making every cloud family obey one fluid solver; requiring exotic far-field queries when ordinary conservative optical products suffice; counting per-pass milliseconds as an additive frame budget; ignoring reflection/shadow/env work when main view is hidden; allowing stateless fades/crossfades or history to fake structural life; baking weather transitions as cuts with no survivor identity; treating a codec, field or parameter list as an authoring pipeline; and labeling a synthetic stress screen as M2 product qualification.

## Finding after reading the commission, CLOUDS/MIGRATION, capability/acceptance/performance cards and retained source map

The governing design correctly preserves the five families, four weather stories, four full skies and independent optical consumers. It correctly treats existing Cu stills, fitted light, Q1–Q3 transport and local M3 timing as bounded evidence. Its ordered medium transport, separate world authority, one condensate accounting, independent views, clear-air LUT direction and explicit fixture versus engine admission are sound production boundaries. The planned source route is **provisional**, despite the heading “selected production direction”: no accepted phase-complete master, tool/version/license, per-family cook, memory footprint or full coupled frame exists. Do not describe the system as production-proven.

## Recommended production baseline and source lock

1. **World and authoring.** E1/W1 owns deterministic weather/front tracks, per-event identity, time, altitude-aware wind and semantic rain/lightning causes. F4 stores editable source recipes and export provenance. Use family-specific authored evolving masters: volumetric convective Cu/Cb; connected shallow sheet/column fields for Sc/St; directional high ice/filament fields for Ci. A master must actually change local support and connectivity throughout birth, mature renewal and loss. Prepare multiple spatially varying exemplars with event offsets/phase rates and controlled local forcing so a full sky does not repeat one clip in lockstep. Neither a single procedural type slider nor a uniform alpha/crossfade passes.
2. **Cook contract.** Choose a real executable authoring tool and license after a **phase-complete Cu/Cb representative and one low-deck and one Ci representative** demonstrate export. Preserve native editable project, physical unit/coordinate/wind convention, source frame times, optical remap for extinction and phase-weighted scattering, velocity or correspondence validity, conservative swept bounds, LODs, hash and regeneration command. Cook sparse occupied bricks or 2.5D layer products as appropriate, with time chunks and quality tiers; build a cheap reference sampler from the same source. Positive interpolation of adjacent final optical frames is a defensible initial reconstruction if intermediate times pass watched local-cue and optical checks. Direct dense frames at a finer cadence are the fallback; flow maps are optional storage challengers. Lock product encoding **after** the three representative morphology regimes and a shared-consumer memory/cost screen, before producing the full variant library.
3. **Light and air.** V3 owns one ordered medium integral of RGB extinction and premultiplied in-scatter across overlapping air, fog and cloud. Use the Hillaire clear-air transmittance/multiple-scattering/sky-view/aerial family for compatible clear segments and a direct/reference segment path at spatial overlaps and unstable ratios. Build ordinary world-space Sun transmittance/shadow products for clouds, ground and air/fog, plus a bounded cloud single-scatter/multiple-scatter approximation and low-frequency clouded sky irradiance/environment. The Sun disk, direct surface light, ambient and reflection read the same source/light generation and declared footprints. Approximate cloud lighting is acceptable if silhouettes, backlit edges, thick undersides, thin ice, shadow alignment and temporal response pass; a fitted one-Cu light bank is only a comparator.
4. **Views and residency.** V2 requests main, shadow, reflected and environment domains independently; main visibility never decides source/event lifetime. V1 pools immutable endpoint/brick products by master/time/LOD, with compact per-event transforms and bounded resident windows, upload queues and old/new peaks. V3 uses conservative empty skipping and projected footprint LOD; V4 owns independent history/reconstruction for each view, with fresh samples at births and invalid correspondence. Keep a complete current spatial reference. Prepare fixed-demand shader variants; no per-cloud pipelines. Retire all cloud GPU work when no optical consumer requests it while preserving headless weather causes.

This combines established production families of techniques rather than asserting one studio ships this exact composition: Guerrilla's Nubis work supports authored weather/shape/detail, IOI's 2026 Smolder supports offline animated sparse-volume content but explicitly exposes whole-animation residency pressure, Epic documents approximate scattering, Beer shadow maps, conservative density skipping and per-reflection quality, and Hillaire supplies the clear-air LUT architecture. The exact Thrum source, cook, optical join, far products and WebGPU budget remain unproved. Primary references: [Nubis³](https://www.guerrilla-games.com/read/nubis-cubed), [Smolder 2026 course](https://www.advances.realtimerendering.com/s2026/), [Epic volumetric clouds](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/unreal-engine/volumetric-cloud-component-in-unreal-engine), [Hillaire 2020](https://sebh.github.io/publications/egsr2020.pdf). Epic's current documentation also describes distinct cloud tracing modes, shadow-map versus secondary-ray costs and independently scaled reflection samples; it does not certify M2 WebGPU.

## Cost and proof changes before production content expansion

The old one-source local graph measured 12.976/17.075 ms GPU p50/p95 on its recorded M3 fixture, already above the proposed 5 ms cloud target; it is not an M2 or new-design prediction. The 184 MiB internal reservation is only arithmetic: 64 MiB encoded copies + 48 MiB physical source + 8 MiB index + 8 MiB light + 32 MiB view/history + 16 MiB staging + 8 MiB air/environment. It omits actual browser/driver/engine contention; the remaining 328 MiB is not free memory. Do not assume all clips or all source instances are simultaneously resident. Count immutable content sharing, unique active phases/knot pairs, changed bricks, swept support, shadow and ambient influence, main/reflection/environment rays, decode/upload bytes, queues, shader cold work and quality-switch peaks at 1×/16×/64×. The 5 ms cloud allowance includes its retained consumers; the 16 ms whole frame and 512 MiB browser process, plus native 3/10 ms, require separate assembled admission. CPU 0.5/8 ms browser and 5 ms native objectives likewise need actual thread and critical-path accounting.

Keep P0/P1a/P2a and a **small P4a coupled sentinel** early, but simplify P3a to required source/multi-consumer integrity and a conservative far route; do not hold the sentinel for an unselected 16-subray summary. Before P3b representation freeze, add a minimal *Cu/Cb + low deck + Ci* source/authoring/cost pilot; P5 then expands to full five-family variants and watched lifecycle proof. Move an authored weather trace into the source pilot so scheduled control and survivor identity are demonstrated before the clip format freezes, while P6 retains full four-story acceptance. Use a reference spatial renderer and matched moving images for all selected reductions. P7 fixture success remains separate from P8 actual Thrum binding, rough-water/depth/transparency and native/browser complete admission.

The decisive gate is a **working source-to-consumer slice**: an editable, regenerable master shows real local change; the same ID/time drives main cloud, receiver shadow, clouded air/fog, ambient and an independent reflection; its source/cache and display ages remain inspectable; the spatial reference and optimized path agree within declared optical and visible-cue bounds; realistic shared-consumer work/physical peak has a credible route to the target. A failed source cue or structural cost model triggers source/representation revision before large library production. Success here is a scoped selection, not five-family, full-sky, M2 or engine admission.

## Review disposition and limits

Recommend **retaining** the optical/ownership contract, complete required capability ledger and adverse proof discipline; **revising** the hard P1b edit/branch and P3a far-summary gates, source-format freeze timing and exact-every-frame cadence; and **moving** representative non-Cu content plus one weather trace ahead of production atlas lock. The finite prepared-asset baseline remains conditional on actual art, controls, residency and coupled-cost proof. This was a read-only design review of the assigned worktree, with primary-source spot checks via current official Epic documentation; I did not run a build, inspect live images/motion, or measure devices. No independent runtime verification is claimed.
