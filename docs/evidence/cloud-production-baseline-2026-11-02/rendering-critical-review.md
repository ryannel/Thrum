# Rendering critical review of selected baseline

2026-11-02. Inspected `docs/design/modules/CLOUD-PRODUCTION-BASELINE.md` and rewritten `CLOUD-MIGRATION.md` at their 10:49 local revisions, plus pinned [Takram shadow](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/takram-design-engineering/three-geospatial/b012ad06d858fc035d88aacfd73f092f93c994e4/packages/clouds/src/shaders/clouds.frag), [Takram BSM writer](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/takram-design-engineering/three-geospatial/b012ad06d858fc035d88aacfd73f092f93c994e4/packages/clouds/src/shaders/shadow.frag), [Hillaire 2020](https://sebh.github.io/publications/egsr2020.pdf), [Epic cloud documentation](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/unreal-engine/volumetric-cloud-component-in-unreal-engine), and [Epic 5.8 sparse-volume documentation](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/unreal-engine/sparse-volume-textures-in-unreal-engine). This is a targeted mathematical/consumer/cost review by a contributor who had drafted an earlier baseline, not a blind independent architecture study. No build, runtime, GPU or content inspection.

## Blocking specification defects

1. **BSM column-to-point lookup is missing.** The selected map stores front metre depth, mean extinction, total optical depth and validity; later text asks for a tail at the 4-step local ray end. A 2D whole-column total is not a point prefix without an approximation. Define Sun-top depth `d(x)` along `-sunDirection`, local ray length `L`, and per-texel `d_end=max(0,d(x)-L)`. With `frontDepth` defined as first occupied cloud depth from Sun top, select `tau_hat(d_end)=min(tau_total, mean_sigma*max(0,d_end-frontDepth))` (or the exact pinned Takram weighted-front convention, explicitly). For octave `i`, evaluate `T_i,k=exp[-b^i*(tau_local+tau_hat_k(d_end))]` **separately for each valid bilinear tap**, then sum tap weights; blend cascade *visibility*, not optical depth. Empty valid column has tau=0; outside/invalid selects the bounded full-Sun fallback. This is a filtered radiance approximation, so validate multiple separated blockers and low Sun against direct rays. The chosen `RGBA32F` channels and `textureLoad` avoid the optional float-filtering dependency, but do not supply this formula automatically.
2. **Near/far result cannot be replaced after full integration.** The far pass and near pass must have disjoint path intervals: far integrates `[200 m, opaque/sky terminal]`; near integrates `[camera, 200 m]` for near-mask pixels, with clear-air prefix supplied for other pixels. Compose front-to-back `S=S_near+T_near*S_far`, `T=T_near*T_far`. If the far pass already integrated `[camera,terminal]`, its near cloud/air/fog cannot be removed by replacing pixels or cloud IDs. Define boundary crossing of one cloud at 200 m as two segments of the same source, with no double density and one common Sun field; disocclusion/history uses the assembled segment depth.
3. **Baked ambient validity conflicts with independent phase.** The 16³ accessibility bake includes *all episode cloud constituents*. Its value at source A depends on B's time/pose. The source sharing key in the baseline contains A's master/frame/story-wind revision, but not the complete other-source phase/pose state; `CLOUD-MIGRATION.md` P3a explicitly tests independent phases. Select one policy now: whole-story synchronized episode playback only (no independent phase/time-offset cloning except a separately baked complete story), or an accessibility product keyed by complete story-time/instance configuration and bounded residency. Wind-profile equivalence alone does not keep cross-cloud visibility valid. Do not quietly reuse A's baked visibility after B changes.

## Corrections required for a complete cost and light contract

4. **The 32 MiB view/history reservation lacks an allocation ledger.** At 1280×720, one RGBA16F image is 7.03125 MiB; RGB source plus RGB transmittance needs two images, 14.0625 MiB. Full-resolution ping-pong history for those two alone uses 28.125 MiB, before far/near/reflected current targets, depth moments, velocity/confidence, IDs and full-resolution resolve. Specify each format, physical resolution, history count and transient aliasing/lease lifetime; if histories stay low-resolution, name the required full-resolution final buffers and where they are charged. Increase reservation or reject the content/profile if actual peak exceeds it. The 184 MiB total arithmetic currently cannot substantiate the 512 MiB plan.
5. **Joint shadowed air/fog and fresh-silhouette work need cost ownership.** The view section permits up to 128 medium steps in shadowed air/fog, on top of 64/96 cloud steps, plus current native silhouette rays and independent reflection. State whether those steps occur at 320×180, 640×360, 1280×720 or 32³ aerial resolution, and charge them to the appropriate main/reflection/air ledger exactly once. The additional 0.8 ms clear-air/composition allowance can cover only genuinely clear LUT/composition work if joint steps are charged in cloud view. Cap or count native fresh pixels per frame, with failure status when the quality floor requires more than capacity; no silent truncation.
6. **Ambient radiance normalization and solar ownership are underspecified.** Define `L_sky_mean` as a radiance-valued quadrature over the *same upper-hemisphere directions* used for visibility, with weights summing to one, and say the direct Sun disk is excluded. If instead a physical 4π scattering integral is intended, include the 2π/(4π) solid-angle factor and appropriate phase weighting. As written, a 64-direction clear-sky mean multiplied by upper-hemisphere 16-direction visibility could use incompatible angular domains; if the mean includes the Sun disk, direct solar energy is included in both `j_amb` and `j_sun`. The 0.2 floor is acceptable as a declared look control once units/domain are fixed.
7. **Environment validity must name fog, geometry and region.** The six-face cube is current to source/Sun/air, but the selected joint capture can also see local fog and ground/geometry. Key capture and SH/roughness mips by fog/geometry revision, capture anchor and source/air/Sun generation, or explicitly omit those effects and state that narrower lighting contract. If surface terminal receives current cloud environment but a stale fog/geometry cube, the atomic `FrameOptics` claim is overstated. The captured cube correctly excludes the direct Sun disk and must not read its own surface irradiance.

## Sound closures and remaining qualification

The joint-medium/clear-LUT split and top-of-atmosphere terminal are coherent: a fully clear ray can use sky-view once; a ray already integrating air/cloud/fog terminates at space plus *unattenuated* solar radiance, so air and the Sun disk are filtered once. The `mu=dot(eye→sample,sample→Sun)` phase sign is correct for incoming Sun and outgoing eye directions. BSM at the local-ray end avoids double cloud attenuation if the prefix formula above is adopted. RGB aerial transmittance is a declared extension, not Hillaire's default scalar alpha. RG16F liquid/ice coefficient channels with material albedo/phase metadata can form nonnegative mixed scattering once, assuming occupancy and interpolation use those same channels. The reflection path is independent rather than main-history mirroring, and the six-face cube has no recursion.

The cited production methods do not certify the combined M2 5 ms/16 ms/512 MiB or native 3/10 targets. [Epic's sparse-volume documentation](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/unreal-engine/sparse-volume-textures-in-unreal-engine) supports two-frame interpolation and frame prefetch, but labels the UE feature Experimental and warns about streaming/quality limits. The 2048 km/128² far BSM has 16 km/texel before filtering; low-Sun and small offscreen blocker quality is a high-risk P4/P7 reference comparison, not a promised sharp result. The 32² current six-face capture and 64/96+128-step joint march are also unmeasured allocation/cost risks. Keep exact source/page/phase and image-motion/device gates; none of these observations demands a new algorithm experiment on the baseline critical path.
