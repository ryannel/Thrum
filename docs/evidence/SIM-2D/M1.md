## Device costs

[Mac](M1/costs-mac-metal.json) and [Chrome](M1/costs-chrome-webgpu.json) prove M1.8–M1.9.
Each has two independent release runs of the entire corrected trace, with 120 warm-up ticks,
accepted neighbours, build identity, load and tripwires. Rewind/replay use the final endpoint.
The current records were measured at `bf07e76e` after integrating the water-model workspace
package. Chrome uses a cross-origin-isolated page. The two targets were measured concurrently
as CPU workloads, with shared-host load retained for each repeat.

At 10,000 bodies, median ticks are 8.073–8.076 ms on the Mac and 8.49–8.59 ms in Chrome.
Eight-tick replay is 66.689–66.826 ms and 70.015–70.295 ms respectively.
All three rebuilt release artifacts match the retained conformance artifacts byte for byte,
including the simulator binary; the correctness receipts apply to these builds.

The 5,000-body neighbour-scaling flags were investigated. Bodies grow 2.5× and accepted
neighbours 2.21× from 2,000, while the grid table grows from 4,096 to 16,384 entries. Build
clears and scans that table. Its measured growth is 2.8–2.9× across these repeats.
This accounts for work that scales independently of accepted neighbours. The median clock
and chronological 25% regression flags are false. Both measured repeats are retained.

`measure` produces records. `costs` checks completeness, compiler and measured-source identity.
Completion depends on the complete source-matched record.
