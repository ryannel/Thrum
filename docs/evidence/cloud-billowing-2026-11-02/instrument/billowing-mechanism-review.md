## Disposition

Proceed as a prescribed VFX source trial, with the causal and accounting clarifications below. The cylindrical streamfunction is dimensionally correct (`A` in m/s, `psi` in m³/s), and the stated radial/vertical derivatives follow from it. The 48-head bound is conservative if selection is exactly one in three births: the current maximum source hazard is `.15*moist*lift <=.3/s`, so the first 420 s from reset admit at most 126 new pulses and 42 selected heads, plus at most two selected from the five reset pulses. A later sliding 420 s window may include one pulse from carried hazard, yielding at most 43 selected heads without the expired reset heads. A head must persist in its own bounded table after its parent pulse expires; rebuilding heads from the current pulse array would end most heads after roughly 34–75 s. Every 500 m band row can hold at most the 48 active IDs, with explicit overflow. The 32 bands cover the 500–15,000 m Cb source domain; intersect each head's `y_head±3H` support with that domain and include boundary-touching rows conservatively. The claimed prepared Cb velocity field is 128×80×128×16 bytes = 20 MiB.

## Consequential mechanism checks

1. **Head residence is a trial prediction, not proof of connected feed.** On the axis, the moving-frame fixed point satisfies `u_background + 2A exp(-zeta²/H²) = v_head`; at default values an upper fixed point near one `H` is plausible. It is stable axially but radially expansive: near `zeta≈H`, `u_r/r≈2A exp(-1)/H`, about `.025/s` for `A=24m/s,H=700m` (roughly a 40 s radial e-fold). That expansion can produce a billow or hollow a torus. Early in the 12 s activation, peak head uplift is below the 28 m/s head speed; feed capture begins only after sufficient activation. A head rises about 1.7 km in 60 s and 3.4 km in 120 s while the base injection remains near 1.3 km. Success therefore depends on carried q and overlap among successive heads, not their pose alone. Measure q-weighted head overlap, a base-to-head connected q path, radial cross sections, and the fraction reaching the cap before fade. Keep the card's rejection of detached strings and hollow rings. Define cap-height fade so an occupied head is handed into existing overlapping cap uplift/outflow before its force disappears.

2. **Specify one order for finite evaporation and source attribution.** Use, for example, `react(S,D) -> bounded same-cell freeze -> evaporate_j(q)=max(q-E_j dt,0)`. For the zero-source counterfactual, run the *same* freeze and evaporation from `react(0,D)` with the same controls, then define `amountAdded=max(sum(q_final)-sum(q_zero_source_final),0)`. If actual and counterfactual use different ordering, unequal liquid/ice evaporation can reintroduce phantom births. With `S=0`, the two paths are identical and `amountAdded=0`; age advances by `dt` while supported. With source on, this quantity is the net **surviving source-induced optical q**, not gross emitted q. Subtractive clipping makes a precise material-age interpretation unavailable; the weighted age remains a diagnostic. Keep a separate gross source counter only if proof needs emitted quantity.

3. **Carry the breakup pattern with the admitted height wind.** The shader's q velocity uses `W_x(y,t)=wind(t)*(1+.6*shear(y))` plus the existing 1.1 m/s z drift. For a fixed-height dry pocket use the corresponding integrated x displacement `(1+.6*shear(y))*windIntegral(t)` and z displacement `1.1*t`, or an explicitly integrated equivalent. Translating all heights by base `windIntegral` would slide kilometre-scale pockets relative to upper q under shear and introduce breakup not attributable to the declared dry field. Head centers likewise integrate the held weather wind at their actual height, without adding world wind twice in q velocity.

Retain immutable per-source-step head/band uploads across in-flight graph slots, including an explicit empty table in numerical fixtures.

## Addendum: occupied-core growth and revised operator order

The proposed below-cap occupied-liquid source has consistent units: `0.0008 m^-1 * max(v_y,0) m/s` is a nonnegative rate in s^-1. Multiplying by `q_adv,l` and `smoothstep(.01,.08,q_adv,l)` makes it exactly zero in an empty advected cell and strongly suppresses numerical wisps. With the final 100 m/s internal cap, this term is bounded by `.08/s` before moisture and occupancy factors. Evaluating those factors from the limited `q_adv` and current held controls, then freezing `S_extra` for the .25 s exponential reaction, is a valid positive first-order feedback step. It is not an exact continuous autocatalytic solution or a vapor-conserving microphysics law. At a representative occupied q=.5, wetness .85 and upward 40 m/s, `S_extra≈.0136/s`; this can replenish the rising core on a tens-of-seconds scale while the weaker 8–12 m/s background contributes much less. The same occupied-q feedback can saturate broad stationary support under persistent uplift, so reject a filled torch or loss of local gaps in the A/B and source-off images. The height mask must go to zero before the cap; no direct anvil q may be made by this term.

**Necessary lift-off gate:** the existing velocity field includes vertical curl even when lift=0. Therefore `max(v_y,0)*moist*q_adv,l` alone is still positive in some occupied cells after a lift-only shutdown. Multiply the term by an explicit nonnegative lift gate that is zero at `lift=0`, or use a separately named positive convective/head vertical component that is zero at `lift=0`. Moisture zero also removes the term. Weather controls relax toward their targets, so a runtime command to zero lift has a short declining tail unless the test explicitly starts from a committed zero-lift frame; report source-off timing accordingly.

The selected operator order is now **reaction → finite evaporation → bounded liquid-to-ice transfer**. Let `r=react(q_adv,S,D)`, `e_j=max(r_j-E_j dt,0)`, and `q_final=freeze(e)`. For the diagnostic counterfactual set `S=0`, use `r0_j=max(q_adv,j,0)*exp(-D_j dt)`, apply the **same** `E_j` to obtain `e0`, and compute `amountAdded=max(sum(q_final)-sum(e0),0)`. Freeze preserves the sum, so the counterfactual does not need a separate freeze for the scalar attribution; applying it would give the same total. With no base or occupied-core source, `r=r0`, hence `e=e0` and no phantom birth even with different liquid/ice evaporation. This supersedes the illustrative freeze-before-evaporation order in item 2 above. `amountAdded` remains net surviving source-induced q rather than gross emission, and age remains a weighted optical diagnostic.
