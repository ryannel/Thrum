# Charged baseline result-key scratch

1 November 2026. The charged replica baseline no longer builds a temporary `BTreeMap` of peers and `BTreeSet` of result identities. It validates the authority's sorted peer watermarks into its already charged confirmed-row buffer. A separate temporary vector holds `(PeerId, sequence)` for every retained result. Its requested `Layout::array` bytes and both progress buffers are reserved together before allocation. Sorting those keys in place detects duplicates, including results at or below the advertised prefix; row validation then checks peer membership, connection epoch and the fixed above-gap window. The key vector drops before its charge, and that charge releases before object-index setup. The two progress buffers remain charged for the replica lifetime. Prototype baseline admission keeps its prior arbitrary peer order and unbounded gap behavior.

Unit tests prove one-byte-short pool refusal before a replica becomes visible, exact-capacity admission and key-charge release, duplicate result rejection below the prefix, malformed charged peer order, an out-of-window gap, mixed epochs, valid delayed-gap closure and unchanged prototype acceptance of reordered peers and distant gaps. The full `RUSTC_WRAPPER= ./dev check` suite passes. The review-agent found no scoped semantics or charge-lifetime issue.

The [256-result fixture](../../../crates/thrum-core/examples/i1_baseline_results_cost.rs) retains one peer's maximum 256 rejected results, then times 100 warmups and 1,000 charged baseline admissions on macOS/aarch64 release builds. Identical fixture source ran sequentially against previous commit `99310e7` and this change, then in reverse order. Admission p50 was 9,500/7,291 ns before and 1,042/1,042 ns after; p99 was 12,166/7,667 ns before and 1,291/1,125 ns after. It excludes authority setup and uses an unlimited logical pool; finite-pool behavior comes from the tests above.

The existing [no-result baseline fixture](../../../crates/thrum-core/examples/i1_baseline_cost.rs) ran once before and after. At 4,096 objects, charged baseline-plus-replica p50 was 139,209 versus 138,291 ns. At 64 objects it was 2,208 versus 3,708 ns, while the prototype's same-run baseline cost also rose from 3,125 to 5,625 ns; these local samples do not isolate a no-result regression. Named-device timing, mutable world index charges, protected future output credits and complete authority-to-replica precommit byte overlap remain unqualified.
