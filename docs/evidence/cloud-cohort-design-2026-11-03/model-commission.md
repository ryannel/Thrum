## Why finite material, persistent formations

A permanent master under W_x=s*y has accumulated shear s*t. Clamping/rewinding that map breaks material wind or introduces wobble/reset. A persistent weather-cell ID instead owns a sequence of finite-lived, individually detailed 3D elements (cohorts). New material condenses locally while old elements erode and move away. Optical elements expire only after zero contribution; weather cells, storm causes and saved IDs outlive them. Not a canned lifecycle movie or replacement of the entire formation at once.

The exact anatomy and attachment programme are CONTENT, not a solver's hoped-for output. Static03 is one Cu ingredient, not four variants or a storm. Each recipe specifies low-body, off-axis unequal tower/shoulder regions, admissible birth sockets and mature target geometry. Sc/St require bank/sheet recipes; Ci requires actual filament-head/tail recipes. A frozen broad Cb composition is the next content falsifier, through unchanged optics. Its passing appearance would still not prove runtime assembly.

## One field and ownership

Each cohort j has immutable template rho_j(a)>=0, positive-inside d_j(a), zero collar, physical local transform, finite support, decoded maximum distance/error and fixed detail/channel fields. Current state: cell/cohort/parent IDs, birth/seed/recipe revision, growth, monotone age exposure, local weather exposure, spatial phase, current/retained material maps and bounds. No per-view state.

q_j(x)=A_seed*rho_j(a)*smoothstep(-w,w,d_j(a)-M_j(a)*(E_age_j(a)+E_weather_j(a))), a=Phi_j^-1(x). Outside actual domain return0, never edge-clamp unknown space. A_seed only ramps a buried sub-voxel seed while its minimum positive-scale inverse is well-conditioned; it is1 before the seed becomes exposed. Visible growth uses the local positive scale/shape map and boundary motion, not alpha fading a complete lobe. Existing geometry does not globally scale when a child grows.

Within one formation, m=max_j q_j. For m>0 use eta_j=(q_j/m)^4 and I=sum(eta_j*I_j)/sum(eta_j); qL=m*(1-I), qI=m*I. At m=0 both zero. The denominator>=1. This is one authored condensate, no physical mass claim. Independent formations add optical coefficients once. The max rule deliberately avoids optical buildup with arbitrary cohort overlap. Independent new lobes need no ownership transfer; there is NO complementary cap/fragment handoff under max.

I is spatial, not a scalar whole-cohort flag. It partitions the same amount. New amount uses the local condensation phase (liquid below the freezing band; admitted ice fraction in the cold band), then freezing converts only extant liquid. At a material control sample, if amount increases from q0 to q1, mix I1=(I0*q0+I_cond*(q1-q0))/q1 before freezing; amount loss preserves survivor phase. This applies to wet de-erosion too. Zero amount retains no phase reservoir. Native spatial interpolation is an admitted approximation, not a hidden conserved-water claim. Template density is already calibrated optical amount: never reapply the old procedural q/noise remap. All views/light/shadow/environment use one immutable effective field/time/provider revision.

## Birth, growth and sustained renewal

A recipe supplies named buried sites and a certified positive-density interior ball around each. Runtime chooses among them deterministically using weather lift/moisture and hashed irregular spacing, not an equal-period vertical lattice. Every candidate records the parent's current material site, world transform/velocity and the weather cause.

A child starts with a small positive scale (initial radius at least one reference sample) and zero amount, entirely inside a filled parent. Ramp its tiny seed to full density while buried, then use a C1 bounded size curve to expand the DETAILED 3D ingredient and expose a new billow. Normal support growth can add local relief; no extrapolation outside the qualified maximum ingredient. The initial parentless low-body seed is genuine local condensation and may use the same tiny-seed ramp. It must not reveal a faint full-size scaffold.

Before admitting an attachment, use certified actual parent/child interior bounds and relative motion/growth/erosion bounds to maintain a resolved positive connection through establishment. A sufficient ball intersection condition is min(rP,rC,(rP+rC-separation)/2)>=h_ref, with both interiors q>=q_attach throughout that interval. Tiny tails do not qualify. Initial subresolved seeds must be contained inside the actual parent interior or a deliberately qualified sufficient bound; do not assume a child template's whole support fits its own maximum interior ball. The local pilot pins and verifies an actual feasible certificate from the decoded master before code.

The child is NEW condensate: it receives fresh template rest/yaw/scales, age0, zero differential-wind history and a new cohortID. Do not inherit the parent's Jacobian/shear/exposure, which would accumulate strain across generations. Its center starts at the parent's current material socket and internal velocity u0=socketFullVelocity-W(socket,t). Thereafter Cdot=W(C,t)+u(t), with u evolving continuously toward the declared recipe uplift/outflow. This inherits the full initial path velocity without double weather wind. Every height label integrates only its own future differential wind. Surviving parent material never resets.

Attachment means certified GEOMETRIC overlap during establishment, not a recursive constraint following the parent forever. Birth admission includes future relative trajectories, growth, drying and all admitted live edits. The parent link becomes provenance after initialization. Optical neck separation changes neither ownership nor velocity; each existing cohort continues independently. No copied fragment or new map appears at split time. Holes inside one cohort do not imply separately tracked motion of every native connected component. Geometric burial alone does not guarantee zero optical change under phase mixing; phase/seed source transitions must be continuous and pass the declared optical error/pop gate.

Persistent wet/lift weather admits asynchronous replacements. Old material is not rejuvenated indefinitely. Saturation of the formation's current birth budget is a declared overload/error, never hidden suppression of required renewal. Changing dry/front/lift controls changes FUTURE birth, growth and loss; it does not replace history or replay a preauthored sky.

## Weather loss, finite life and phase

E=E_age+E_weather, all nonnegative in template-local metres. The age floor is irreversible; wet weather cannot reduce it. Fixed channel multiplier M(a) is in [.3,1]. Choose E_age to reach (maxDecodedD+w+margin)/.3 by the declared maximum life T_max. Weather drying can only accelerate this bound. E_weather may relax under wet weather, allowing local de-erosion while young; prospective support must include that expansion. It cannot cancel E_age.

Use material control lattices with world-projected pitch<=one quarter of admitted weather-front width (initial front minimum800m; pitch<=200m). Anisotropic extents use anisotropic control dimensions, not a universal3x3x3. Exact constant-rate nonnegative relaxation integrates E_weather over each causal source interval. Fixed fine material channels modulate boundary loss, not an independently scrolling noise field.

Spatial I control starts0. Under positive current amount above the freezing band, exact bounded freezing updates I; amount0 does not fabricate ice. Ice lowers local AGE/DRY loss rates, but every rate remains>=a documented floor sufficient to reach the same finite certified T_max. Track irreversible exposure itself, not E formed by directly blending liquid/ice exposures (which could decrease as phase changes). Weather labels do not generate a filled ice disk.

Initial workload candidates: Cu liquid life<=300s, lambda<=1/50s plus2establishment overlaps =>8cohorts/cell; Cb upper-ice life<=600s, lambda<=1/35s plus4overlaps =>22cohorts/cell. ## Material map and storm development
