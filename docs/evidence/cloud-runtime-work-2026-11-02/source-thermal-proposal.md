# Prescribed rising thermals for the Cu source — bounded proposal

2 November 2026. Analytical morphology diagnosis and candidate only; no shader/source edit, GPU result, accepted art or M2 claim. I read the current Cu source/velocity path and viewed native `cloud-0-{0,6000,18000,36000}-e2.png` captures from build `0ab4062170f6d491` with the same family, Sun, camera controls and 1280×720 output. The selected runtime 3D source and shared optics contracts remain governing.

## Diagnosed gap

At 60 s the current field reads as soft low puffs. At 180 s it grows a narrow upward form with separated small puffs; at 360 s it is a stringy tower with large internal holes and a soft base. The stills do not establish motion quality, but they are an adverse morphology witness. The shader has one broad, nearly stationary central updraft, plus modest repeating curl and finite base injection pulses. It can move q upward and erode it, but it has no pulse-associated rising/rolling head that expands laterally while a connected column feeds it. Altering light or merely increasing detail resolution cannot supply that missing velocity organization. The following is a **prescribed VFX velocity trial**, not a fluid solver, meteorology claim, or disclosed Nubis³ lifecycle method.

## Selected candidate field

Each existing finite seeded source pulse supplies a thermal control `(ID, birth, x_birth,z_birth,R,H,A,v_head,window)`. At source time `t`, put its head at `y_h=base+v_head(t-birth)`, capped/faded as it approaches the admitted Cu top. Move its horizontal center by the same **world wind integral** used to transport q, `x_h=x_birth+[I_W(t)-I_W(birth)]`; if height shear is active, integrate that declared W along the head's path or bound the error of the base-wind approximation. Include any prescribed z drift consistently if it moves q materially. These coordinates locate a velocity pattern and its source's lineage; they do not draw an ellipsoid, insert q at the head, retime q or apply wind a second time. Existing `S/D` still create/erase optical matter, and the same q snapshot feeds every consumer.

In cylindrical coordinates around the moving vertical axis, let `r=√[(x−x_h)²+(z−z_h)²]`, `ζ=y−y_h`, `E=exp[−r²/R²−ζ²/H²]`. The proposed axisymmetric Stokes streamfunction and its velocity are

`ψ=A r² E`, `u_r=−(1/r)∂ψ/∂y=2A r ζ E/H²`, `u_y=(1/r)∂ψ/∂r=2A(1−r²/R²)E`.

Use Cartesian radial components `u_x=2A ζ(x−x_h)E/H²`, `u_z=2A ζ(z−z_h)E/H²` to avoid `r_hat` at the axis. `A` is in m/s, `R,H` in m, `ψ` in m³/s and every velocity component in m/s. For each pulse, `u_r` is inward below its head and outward above it; `u_y` rises on-axis and has a weak return flow outside `r=R`. In cylindrical divergence, `(1/r)∂(r u_r)/∂r+∂u_y/∂y=0` exactly for this isolated uncut field. The formulas supplied in the commission have the correct signs/units. The peak upward speed is `2A`; maximum radial magnitude is `A R/(e H)`; the largest downward return magnitude is `2A e^-2≈0.271A`. The max vector speed is safely bounded by `sqrt[(2A)²+(A R/(eH))²]`. The moving center and time-only pulse amplitude preserve instantaneous divergence-free thermal terms, but the existing base velocity, spatial normalization and speed cap do not. Make no physical incompressibility claim for the assembled field.

Apply the pulse's finite smooth rise/hold/fall envelope to `A`. Sum thermal velocities with the existing one-time shared W and broad internal updraft/curl, then cap **internal** speed by a finite rule `u_internal←u_internal·min(1,100/max(|u_internal|,ε))`; add shared W once outside that cap. Record the fraction of occupied q cells hitting the cap. Frequent capping means the overlap/strength model is wrong, not a free stabilizer. The cap changes divergence, acceptable for an explicitly art-directed optical-control flow. For a compact smooth field, if profiling justifies it, multiply **ψ** by a C² radial/vertical cutoff whose value and first two derivatives vanish at its boundary, then derive velocities from the product. Multiplying the final velocity by a cutoff would break even the isolated thermal divergence identity and could create a visible ring seam. The first small proof can use Gaussian tails within the bounded Cu volume and skip negligible far pulses using an admitted error threshold.

A candidate starting point for the held A/B is `A=18m/s, R=600m, H=450m`, and `v_head≈35m/s`, with the current pulse envelope and an upper fade before the head outruns the Cu top. This gives 36m/s center uplift and ≈8.8m/s maximum radial motion per isolated thermal before the base field. It is a trial scale, not a look or cost pass. The important coupling condition is that transported q remains inside the moving head's `R/H` region for a substantial fraction of its active interval. If `v_head` is much faster than the material's vertical centroid, a beautiful-looking velocity field acts on empty air; if much slower, it trails q and may leave stringy columns or detached top fragments. Derive/measure the head–q overlap rather than asserting it from the formula. The initial q field and source pulse still need to produce a connected base-to-head path; velocity cannot create condensate above an empty gap.

## Cost and integration account

Precompute a world-space float32 velocity per 96×80×80 q cell **once per fixed source tick** from the shared WeatherFrame, current finite emitter buffer and static curl potential. An RGBA32Float 3D texture is 9.375 MiB for 614,400 cells before any old/new or staging overlap; use the three vector components and leave the fourth defined. Each q forward/reverse/corrected pass and the half-resolution carrier samples this one same-time field; no 32-emitter loop inside three q passes. One such precompute still evaluates up to ≈19.7 million cell–emitter pairs per tick, so support/indexing and complete source-pass timing decide whether it helps. Avoid a float32-filterability dependency: use `textureLoad` at q-cell centers and explicit bounded interpolation for the half-resolution carrier, or qualify a different filterable format. The thermal field must be computed before any q pass for that tick and remain leased through the carrier pass; only then may the next tick overwrite scratch. Count the velocity texture, precompute dispatch, any support list, bindings, in-flight overlap and old/new snapshot work in the source/memory ledger. A smaller shader after adding this pass is not proof of a cheaper complete graph.

The current Cu-only 8³ work mask's one-brick halo was justified for <85m/s total velocity and .25s steps. With `|u_internal|≤100m/s` plus admitted shared W≤19.2m/s, a conservative step displacement is <30m. This remains below the 65m minimum source-cell pitch and well inside one eight-cell brick plus donor/reverse stencil, but **recompute the certified bound** for the actual precompute/cap equation and any changed timestep or scale. The prospective source mask still follows base emitters, not thermal heads, because a thermal by itself has `S=0`; transported old q support plus halo must cover its deformation. A thermal field outside old/source support can be skipped only after that spatial proof.

## Focused discrimination and rejection

Hold the build, source seeds/pulses, weather commands, q calibration, camera, lighting and display fixed. A/B only the thermal velocity field (zero A versus the candidate). Capture native fixed/front/side/oblique q and beauty at 0/60/180/360 s, plus actual normal 1× and accelerated motion; an attractive still does not close the motion gate. Report the same-ID base/head connected components, new/lost occupied support, lobe width and vertical centroid over time, head–q overlap, source amount, loss, boundary occupancy, speed/cap-hit distribution, rest-coordinate resets and source/full-graph GPU time/physical bytes. Independent receiver shadow and reflected image must follow the same changed q/time; no private shader-only thermal silhouette.

The candidate is rejected if it produces a donut/hollow shell, repeated torus rings, an empty moving head, detached puffs, a single steady torch, synchronized growth, cut-looking holes, or the existing stringy/soft morphology at later times. Also reject if many active cells hit the 100m/s cap, the head leaves its fed q behind, or new rendering/shadow/history inconsistencies appear. A Cu source could still fail despite mathematically correct divergence and a visually better isolated head. If the trial fails, diagnose source–head overlap and q loss before increasing A or detail frequency; preserve the selected 3D optical/consumer contract while revising the responsible morphology mechanism.

**Decision status:** one bounded, source-owned prescribed thermal candidate for P1 Cu correction; unimplemented and unaccepted. It tests whether organized pulse-local roll/expansion can replace the current steady-torch behavior without a weather solver or prepared lifecycle frames.
