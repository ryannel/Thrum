11 November 2026. Chrome 157.0.8103.40, Apple M3 Max/Metal hardware WebGPU, headless Chrome on this Mac.
The command spelling chosen for the browser form is `./dev starter --web`, also available as
`./dev scene <folder> --web`. Source identities are in [source-files.json](m1/source-files.json).
Each run records the served Wasm, page, controls and initial store hashes, browser version and hardware facts.

Canvas proofs are fixed still-scene checkpoints from the renderer's submitted-output tap, recording submission,
source, history, device generation and `presented=true`. DOM screenshots are separate. The scope is function and
legibility. Display cadence, rendering quality and performance qualification remain with their owning gates.

Verification is recorded in [check.txt](m1/check.txt). The full repository check, Wasm checks and M1's three
named checks passed. One gate run failed in the V1 allocation suite. Its [gate record](m1/gate-retry-history.txt) identifies the suite, while its original panic was outside the loop’s retained output tail. Two focused runs of the exact binary passed ([first](m1/frame-alloc-exact.txt), [repeat](m1/frame-alloc-exact-repeat.txt)). The cause remains unconfirmed. The lead owns independent review.
