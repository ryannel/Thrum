# Review the commissioned artifact

Choose review depth from the consequence and uncertainty of the work. Design review, code correctness, experienced
output and performance claims answer different questions; record their actual scope and artifact identity.

## Independent challenge and discovery

Check that the module commission follows the engine's game requirements and neighbouring contracts. Challenge
boundaries and combined budgets as well as internal algorithms. Check whether the build queue follows consequential
design uncertainties, whether experiments actually qualify their claimed scope, and whether the current design was
updated when proofs changed its assumptions. More polished documents do not resolve design drift.

For substantial design work, use a fresh subagent reviewer when delegation is available and authorized. Supply original
requirements and representative use cases first. Ask it to derive the capability set and consequential interactions
independently, then compare with the saved inventory, design, evidence and proof plan. Keep findings about omissions,
unsupported additions and incompatible coupling distinct. Do not supply the author's desired techniques, expected
verdict or research conversation. Keep blue-sky reviewers independent of existing module code and old technical plans;
exposure limits must be disclosed, as for the author. Preserve the first proposal and actual authorship inputs. If a reviewer contributes
a new mechanism, another reader verifies that contribution. Self-checks are not independent review.

Review coverage before the design is treated as complete and revisit it when material scope or model changes could
invalidate that coverage. Use focused additional reviewers for a meaningful expertise/coverage gap, not a fixed quota.
If delegation is unavailable or unauthorized, disclose that limitation and self-check without claiming independence.

Ask reviewers to derive missing experiences, dependencies and operating conditions from the brief, rather than only
checking the proposal's polished sections. Match depth to the commissioned stage; a preliminary coverage review need
not settle every mechanism or repeat a literature survey. For consequential technique selections, inspect supporting
evidence and independently search current alternatives or omissions. Check substantive use of conference material, access claims, ownership, composition
and whether proposed experiments could reject the selected mechanism. A bibliography does not establish coverage.

Trace a defining outcome through its actual producer and dependencies. Challenge attractive substitutes that could
pass the author's checks: does the mechanism produce the requested state change, or only animate its presentation?
Check published limitations and hidden authoring inputs. A rigorous derivation of one component cannot support an
unimplemented link elsewhere in the assembly.

For an architectural selection audit, derive the consequential decisions and credible competing approaches before
reading the proposal. Then distinguish missing decisions, weak justification, known open choices, legitimate detailed
work and unsupported scope additions. Challenge findings against the actual design too: a concrete candidate is not
automatically a selected architecture, and an explicit safe deferral is not an omission. Preserve narrowed/rejected
findings and disagreements in the synthesis. Internal consistency alone does not establish selection quality.

Work through the proposal's normal operating cycle and coupled scenarios, including required nonvisual consumers
and feedback. Check that outputs become usable inputs at the declared location, time, fidelity and cost. Identify
where an implementer would still have to invent a core mechanism, query or update order. Review compatibility of
the full composition, not only plausibility of its individual techniques. An essential interaction deferred until
after the attractive component is complete is an incomplete design.

For a build-ready plan, try constructing a hard component and a coupled case using only its saved inputs and
instructions. Trace the creator's first use and one source or configuration change through preparation, loading and
invalidation. Record missing choices before supplying remedies; verify the revised plan against the actual saved
documents. Paper walkthroughs establish clarity, not running behaviour or visual quality.

Use targeted depth for consequential uncertainty or different expertise. Give neutral questions and relevant raw
artifacts; broad discovery and focused verification serve different roles. Divide independent work where useful,
while one owner integrates contracts. Do not prescribe a reviewer quota or repeat passes for ceremonial agreement.

## Read across boundaries and inspect the result

For implementation review, include callers, shared data, shaders, fixtures, lifecycle and tools needed to understand
the actual assembly. Look for duplicate producers/formulas, mismatched clocks or encodings, dead alternative paths,
resource scope, invalidation and fallbacks. Component-only reviews can miss cohesion. For a bounded module where
cross-cutting duplication is a concern, independent complete reads are useful; for larger systems map review coverage
and retain a whole-system reader alongside targeted reviews. Do not claim a full read of an uninspected scope.

Review ordinary output, motion and transitions separately from source. Reviewer agents inspect captured images and
sequences directly; that inspection is visual evidence. State whether reviewers watched motion or only
sampled stills, inspected native crops or downscaled sheets, and verified the actual loaded build/inputs. Neither
nonzero response nor a plausible still proves the commissioned experience. Diagnose the responsible layer using
[system diagnosis](system-diagnosis.md), rather than prescribing a cosmetic correction to the reported symptom.
Judge each indispensable cue separately; successful lighting, controls, numerical guards or performance do not
compensate for failed dynamics or content. Review normal pacing and accelerated development when both are relevant.

For performance-led design, challenge the workload multipliers, data movement, critical path and representation
choices before requiring measurements of unbuilt code. Check whether human implementation convenience has ruled out
credible specialization, and whether the proposed complexity has independent checks and an explicit supported domain.

For performance-sensitive work, challenge the [performance evidence](performance.md): benchmark identity and fairness,
long-run tails, equivalence claims, actual saved work, memory accounting and visual/profile regressions. Require
claims to match their measurement: isolated kernels, synthetic stress and end-to-end cadence are different evidence.

## Resolve findings and check consequences

A finding identifies the affected decision/location, concrete defect or unsupported claim, evidence or reproducible
counterexample, its consequence and a resolution condition. Separate invalidating defects, unsupported selections,
bounded uncertainties and optional improvements. Do not invent objections or require a particular paper without
showing why it changes the decision.

The author investigates and revises the model/contracts, supplies evidence, rejects the finding with reasons or
records a genuine open decision and discriminating experiment. More citations, vague renaming or moving an assumption
into an appendix do not resolve missing design. Restricting a claim can remove an overclaim without delivering the
excluded required experience. Keep such requirements open.

Reconcile discovered capabilities into the inventory: add justified omissions and their mechanism, dependencies and
proof; reject unsupported additions with reasons; keep genuine scope questions explicit. Update affected worked cases
and acceptance coverage. A review report alone does not close the gap it found.

Verify consequential resolutions against the revised assembly and affected consumers. Closure may receive the finding
and response, but must check rather than trust the disposition. Old review applies to its inspected revision; new
compositions and optimizations need their affected correctness, appearance and cost rechecked. Continue while
within-scope actionable defects can change the outcome; unavailable evidence and unrun qualification remain explicit.

Deliver the current recommendation/result, material changes, finding dispositions and remaining consequences. State
review limitations, disagreements and unavailable independent verification. Design review agreement does not establish
implementation validation, runtime viability or visual quality; those need running checks and agent visual review.
